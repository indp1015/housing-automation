How it works

Each axis is normalized to a percentile rank (0–100) across all U.S. counties with valid data. Your weighted average across available axes becomes the final Livability Score. If an axis has no data for a county, it's excluded from that county's calculation.

Sources: ACS 5-Year Estimates, FBI UCR (CDE), CDC PLACES, OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0).

Note: 11 counties are excluded from the ranking due to missing 5-year price data — primarily Connecticut planning regions, which transitioned from traditional counties to new planning boundaries in 2022.
