About These Rankings

Rankings are computed from U.S. Census ACS 5-Year Estimates, FHFA House Price Index, BLS QCEW, IRS migration data, and FRED mortgage rates. Each county is scored on 6 axes: Affordability, Stability, Momentum, Vitality, Tax Friendliness, and Rental Yield. Each axis is a percentile rank across all U.S. counties.

These rankings are editorial — designed to help users explore markets by their own priorities. They are not investment advice.
