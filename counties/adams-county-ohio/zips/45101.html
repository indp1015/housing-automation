ZIP code 45101 in Adams County, Ohio, exhibits a robust upward price trajectory over the recent five-year period. This sustained growth contrasts with the volatility observed in earlier decades, where the index fluctuated between 95.0 and 113.94 during the 2012–2014 period. The current trend indicates a maturing market that has moved past the post-recession recovery phase into a period of consistent value expansion. This outperformance relative to the state average suggests localized demand pressures or specific economic drivers within Adams County that are not reflected in the statewide median. This variance highlights that real estate performance in this region is highly hyperlocal rather than uniform.
