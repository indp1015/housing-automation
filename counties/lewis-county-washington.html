Market Analysis

The median home value in Lewis County is 14.1% below the Washington state average and 61.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,044/month is 13.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.3, Lewis County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lewis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
