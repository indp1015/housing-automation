Market Analysis

The median home value in Boise County is 36.0% above the Idaho state average and 100.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $854/month is 7.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 41.4, Boise County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Boise County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.0%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
