Market Analysis

The median home value in Winneshiek County is 48.8% above the Iowa state average and 13.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $800/month is 1.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Winneshiek County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
