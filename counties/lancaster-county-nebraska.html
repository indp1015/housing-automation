Market Analysis

The median home value in Lancaster County is 69.4% above the Nebraska state average and 22.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,045/month is 33.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lancaster County has an owner-occupancy rate of 58.8%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
