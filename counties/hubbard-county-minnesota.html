Market Analysis

The median home value in Hubbard County is 18.6% above the Minnesota state average and 24.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $905/month is 3.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Hubbard County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Hubbard County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.1%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
