Market Analysis

The median home value in Hubbard County is 15.0% above the Minnesota state average and 21.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $923/month is 0.9% above the state average.

Hubbard County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.6%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
