Market Analysis

The median home value in Taliaferro County is 65.2% below the Georgia state average and 69.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $638/month is 33.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.0, buying a home in Taliaferro County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Taliaferro County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.1%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
