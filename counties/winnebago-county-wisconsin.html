Market Analysis

The median home value in Winnebago County is 3.5% below the Wisconsin state average and 0.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $965/month is 5.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Winnebago County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Winnebago County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.8%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
