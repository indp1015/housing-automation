Market Analysis

The median home value in Tillman County is 47.5% below the Oklahoma state average and 65.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $733/month is 12.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.1, buying a home in Tillman County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Tillman County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.8%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
