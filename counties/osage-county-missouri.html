Market Analysis

The median home value in Osage County is 26.6% above the Missouri state average and 2.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $644/month is 16.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.6, Osage County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Osage County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 73.6% in Missouri and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
