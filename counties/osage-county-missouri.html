Market Analysis

The median home value in Osage County is 27.1% above the Missouri state average and 1.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $639/month is 18.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.1, Osage County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Osage County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.4%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
