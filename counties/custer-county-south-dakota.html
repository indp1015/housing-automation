Market Analysis

The median home value in Custer County is 97.0% above the South Dakota state average and 54.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,117/month is 47.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Custer County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.6%, compared to 72.7% in South Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
