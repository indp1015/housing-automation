Market Analysis

The median home value in Calumet County is 17.1% above the Wisconsin state average and 20.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,073/month is 17.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Calumet County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.1%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
