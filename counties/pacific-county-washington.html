Market Analysis

The median home value in Pacific County is 31.1% below the Washington state average and 29.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $962/month is 20.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Pacific County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
