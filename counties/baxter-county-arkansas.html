📝 Local Market Analysis

Baxter County, Arkansas, operates as a balanced market with distinct affordability challenges, characterized by a price-to-rent ratio of 17.2, which sits below the national average of 18.7, suggesting renting remains relatively competitive compared to buying. However, the median home value of $174,600 against a median household income of $48,452 creates a price-to-income ratio of 3.60, exceeding the national benchmark of 3.20. This disparity indicates that while entry-level costs are lower than the national median, local income levels lag behind, making homeownership less accessible relative to earnings. The rent burden stands at 21.0% of income, higher than the national average of 17.2%, further pressuring household budgets. The market exhibits strong momentum, with home values rising faster than rents, a trend that has persisted since 2020. Annual appreciation accelerated significantly, peaking at +12.3% in 2022 before moderating to +5.6% in 2024. Recent FHFA data confirms continued strength, showing a +2.82% year-over-year increase, outperforming the Arkansas state average of +1.09% by 1.73 percentage points. This outperformance is supported by positive net migration of +1,876 filers between 2021 and 2022, where incoming residents reported an average income of $64,316, slightly higher than those leaving. Despite this demand, new housing supply remains limited, with only 80 units authorized in 2025, comprising 69 single-family and 11 multi-family homes. Regional context highlights a complex positioning; while home values are 23.4% above the state average, they remain 23.3% below the national mean. This divergence is driven by local economic fundamentals, including an average weekly wage of $903 and total employment of 14,728 jobs. The current 7.40% mortgage rate further constrains purchasing power, exacerbating the affordability gap. Consequently, the market’s vitality is driven by robust price momentum and population growth, yet stability is tempered by the high cost of living relative to local wages. The composite assessment reflects this tension: high momentum and vitality scores are counterbalanced by low affordability metrics, resulting in a challenging environment for first-time buyers despite favorable rental economics.

The median home value in Baxter County is 23.4% above the Arkansas state average and 23.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $846/month is 9.3% above the state average.
