Market Analysis

The median home value in Pickett County is 20.1% below the Tennessee state average and 21.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $564/month is 36.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Pickett County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Pickett County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.4%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
