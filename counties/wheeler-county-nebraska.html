Market Analysis

The median home value in Wheeler County is 11.0% below the Nebraska state average and 35.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $509/month is 36.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Wheeler County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.7%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
