📝 Local Market Analysis

White County, Illinois, functions as a distinctly buyer’s market, characterized by extreme affordability metrics that diverge sharply from national norms. The price-to-income ratio stands at 1.97, significantly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is 15.7% of income compared to the national 17.2%. These figures indicate that housing costs consume a smaller portion of local household resources than in most other U.S. jurisdictions. Consequently, the market classification is driven by low entry barriers for homeownership rather than rental demand pressures, creating a structural advantage for prospective buyers seeking equity accumulation at lower initial capital outlays. The multi-year trend reveals a period of rapid appreciation followed by recent stabilization. Median home values surged from $69,700 in 2017 to $108,100 in 2024, with a peak year-over-year increase of 21.5% in 2022. However, this momentum has moderated, with the FHFA House Price Index showing a 4.05% year-over-year growth in 2025, underperforming the Illinois state average of 5.54% by 1.49 percentage points. This divergence suggests that while the county experienced a significant post-pandemic price correction, its recent growth trajectory is lagging behind broader state trends. The price-to-rent ratio of 12.5, well below the national 18.7, further confirms that ownership remains significantly cheaper relative to renting, a dynamic that has persisted despite the recent price surge. Regional context highlights a market that is 29.4% below the state average home value and 52.5% below the national average, reflecting its rural economic profile. Local vitality is constrained by limited economic fundamentals, evidenced by only 3,284 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $991. Housing supply is critically constrained, with just one new unit authorized in 2025, which likely contributes to the sustained price appreciation despite modest migration gains of 265 net filers. The current 7.40% mortgage rate exacerbates monthly payment burdens, yet the low base price mitigates this impact. The overall market assessment is driven by high affordability scores offset by low vitality and stability indicators, as the small population of 13,619 and limited employment base restrict long-term demand resilience. Potential buyers face a market where entry costs are low, but economic growth drivers are weak, resulting in a stable but slow-moving environment.

Market Analysis

The median home value in White County is 29.4% below the Illinois state average and 52.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $719/month is 17.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.5, buying a home in White County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

White County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.1%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
