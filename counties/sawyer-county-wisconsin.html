Market Analysis

The median home value in Sawyer County is 2.5% above the Wisconsin state average and 5.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $683/month is 23.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.2, Sawyer County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.
