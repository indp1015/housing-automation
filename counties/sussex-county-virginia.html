Market Analysis

The median home value in Sussex County is 37.9% below the Virginia state average and 19.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $907/month is 22.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Sussex County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Sussex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.7%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
