Market Analysis

The median home value in Mingo County is 38.9% below the West Virginia state average and 60.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $647/month is 16.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.7, buying a home in Mingo County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Mingo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.4%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
