Market Analysis

The median home value in Dawson County is 44.5% below the Texas state average and 54.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $777/month is 22.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.1, buying a home in Dawson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Dawson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.6%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
