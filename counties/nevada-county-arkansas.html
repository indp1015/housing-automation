Market Analysis

The median home value in Nevada County is 41.1% below the Arkansas state average and 63.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $869/month is 12.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 8.0, buying a home in Nevada County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Nevada County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.0%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
