Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 12.1% above the Oregon state average and 89.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,310/month is 15.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Jackson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.0%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
