Market Analysis

The median home value in Solano County is 1.4% above the California state average and 171.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,163/month is 28.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Solano County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.0%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
