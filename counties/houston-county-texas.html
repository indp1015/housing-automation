Market Analysis

The median home value in Houston County is 11.5% below the Texas state average and 27.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $887/month is 11.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Houston County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
