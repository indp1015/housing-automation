📝 Local Market Analysis

Lyon County, Kentucky, functions as a balanced market characterized by strong affordability relative to national standards, though supply constraints limit broader vitality. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.91, significantly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is only 13.8% of median income compared to the 17.2% national benchmark. These metrics indicate that housing costs remain manageable for local households, supporting the stability observed in the region. However, the price-to-rent ratio of 21.1 exceeds the national average of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing offers better long-term value than renting, despite the low absolute rent levels. Price trends reveal a period of rapid appreciation followed by recent stabilization. Median home values increased from $122,900 in 2017 to $189,500 in 2024, with peak year-over-year growth of 11.9% in 2023 moderating to 3.2% in 2024. Recent momentum remains positive, with the FHFA House Price Index showing a 4.81% year-over-year increase in 2025, outperforming the Kentucky state average of 4.26% by 0.55 percentage points. This performance is notable given the county’s small economic base, which includes only 1,764 total jobs and 250 establishments. The local economy supports this growth through net migration, with 333 more filers entering the county than leaving between 2021 and 2022, though the average income of incoming filers ($71,728) slightly exceeds that of outgoing filers ($70,615). The primary constraint on market vitality is housing supply, with only five new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits. This scarcity likely contributes to the sustained price appreciation despite the current 7.32% national mortgage rate, which typically suppresses demand. While the county’s median home value is 16.8% below the national average, it is 17.8% above the state average, positioning it as a premium market within Kentucky. The composite assessment reflects high stability and momentum, driven by consistent price gains and low affordability stress, but is tempered by low vitality scores due to the minimal new construction pipeline. For potential buyers, the current environment offers entry into a market with strong historical appreciation and favorable cost ratios, while renters benefit from low monthly burdens, though limited new inventory may restrict future options.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Lyon County is 17.8% above the Kentucky state average and 16.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $747/month is 5.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lyon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
