Market Analysis

The median home value in Oakland County is 74.2% above the Michigan state average and 50.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,376/month is 52.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Oakland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.7%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
