📝 Local Market Analysis

Pope County, Minnesota, presents a balanced market profile characterized by strong stability and moderate momentum, though affordability constraints persist relative to national norms. The price-to-income ratio of 3.35 slightly exceeds the national average of 3.20, indicating that home prices have outpaced local income growth. However, the rent burden stands at 14.2% of income, significantly below the national average of 17.2%, suggesting that renting remains a more financially accessible option than purchasing for many households. This divergence is highlighted by a price-to-rent ratio of 23.6, which is notably higher than the national average of 18.7, implying that the cost of ownership is disproportionately elevated compared to the cost of renting. The high owner-occupancy rate of 83.5% further underscores a market where homeownership is the dominant residential arrangement, yet the premium paid for equity is substantial. Recent momentum reveals a robust appreciation trend, with the FHFA House Price Index showing a year-over-year increase of 7.27% in 2025, outperforming the Minnesota state average of 2.42% by 4.85 percentage points. This recent acceleration follows a period of significant growth, including a peak year-over-year gain of 15.3% in 2022, which has since moderated to 5.1% in 2024. The multi-year trajectory indicates a sustained upward trend in home values, rising from $163,600 in 2017 to $255,100 in 2024, a cumulative increase of approximately 56%. In contrast, median gross rent has remained relatively stable, contributing to the widening gap between purchase and rental costs. This pattern suggests that the market is experiencing a supply-constrained environment where demand for ownership is driving prices higher without commensurate increases in rental alternatives. The economic context supports this appreciation through positive net migration of 237 filers and an average weekly wage of $1,051, which exceeds typical regional benchmarks. However, the local employment base is small, with only 3,397 jobs across 349 establishments, limiting the depth of the local labor market. Housing supply is tight, with only 33 new units authorized in 2025, predominantly single-family homes, which restricts the ability to meet growing demand. The national 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40% adds further pressure on buyer purchasing power, potentially slowing transaction volumes despite price increases. The composite assessment reflects high stability due to consistent income and occupancy levels, but lower vitality and affordability scores driven by the limited job market and elevated price-to-rent dynamics. For potential buyers, the high entry cost relative to rent makes timing and financing critical, while renters benefit from a lower immediate financial burden in this high-equity-cost environment.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Pope County is 7.0% above the Minnesota state average and 12.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $902/month is 3.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Pope County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.5%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
