Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 92.4% above the South Dakota state average and 53.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,260/month is 60.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.2%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
