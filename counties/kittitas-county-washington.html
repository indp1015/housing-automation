Market Analysis

The median home value in Kittitas County is 15.6% above the Washington state average and 117.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,253/month is 4.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.6, Kittitas County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Kittitas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.3%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
