Market Analysis

The median home value in Monroe County is 10.3% below the Wisconsin state average and 7.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $914/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Monroe County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Monroe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.8%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
