Market Analysis

The median home value in Bingham County is 17.3% below the Idaho state average and 22.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $845/month is 8.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.4, Bingham County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bingham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
