📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP code 38225, located in Weakley County, Tennessee, exhibits a robust but decelerating price trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) surged from 163.94 in 2021 to 273.38 in 2025, representing a cumulative appreciation of approximately 66.8%. This sharp ascent indicates a period of intense demand and limited inventory, characteristic of post-pandemic housing adjustments. However, the most recent year-over-year growth has moderated to +1.34%, signaling a stabilization phase after the rapid gains recorded in 2022 and 2023. This deceleration creates a notable divergence from broader regional metrics. While ZIP 38225 grew by 1.34%, the parent county expanded by 3.47%, and the state of Tennessee increased by 0.85%. The ZIP’s performance sits between these two benchmarks, suggesting that local market dynamics are currently aligning more closely with state-level cooling trends rather than the stronger momentum observed in the wider county. This alignment implies that the initial surge in local demand may be maturing, with price growth normalizing to reflect broader economic conditions, including the persistent 7.40% 30-year mortgage rate. The sub-market character of ZIP 38225 is defined by affordability relative to national averages, yet it shows signs of premiumization within its local context. The parent county’s median home value of $151,200 indicates an entry-level to moderate market, supported by a median household income of $51,880. With an owner-occupancy rate of 66.3% and median gross rent at $803, the area maintains a balanced mix of ownership and rental housing. For prospective buyers, the current HPI of 273.4 suggests that while prices have risen significantly from historical baselines, the recent slowdown in appreciation offers a more stable entry point compared to the volatile growth seen in 2022. Residents should note that the market is no longer in a rapid appreciation phase, potentially allowing for more predictable long-term equity accumulation without the extreme volatility of the previous three years.
