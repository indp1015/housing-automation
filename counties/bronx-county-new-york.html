Market Analysis

The median home value in Bronx County is 88.3% above the New York state average and 132.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,458/month is 24.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.3, Bronx County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bronx County has an owner-occupancy rate of 20.1%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
