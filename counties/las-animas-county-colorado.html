Market Analysis

The median home value in Las Animas County is 46.1% below the Colorado state average and 1.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $875/month is 30.2% below the state average.

Las Animas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.1%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
