📝 Editor's Insight

Las Animas County presents a balanced market structure, evidenced by a 70.1% owner-occupancy rate and a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, which sits only slightly above the national average of 18.0. Over the past five years, home values surged at a 9.2% CAGR, significantly outpacing the 5.3% CAGR in gross rents. This divergence indicates that equity accumulation is accelerating faster than rental cost inflation, favoring long-term ownership over leasing. However, affordability remains strained relative to national benchmarks. The price-to-income ratio stands at 4.26, substantially higher than the 3.09 national average, while the rent burden reaches 20.8% of income compared to the 17.2% norm. These metrics suggest that despite lower absolute prices, purchasing power is constrained by incomes 23.6% below the national mean. A critical insight for prospective residents is that the county’s home values are 46.1% below the state average, creating a significant discount relative to Colorado’s broader market. This gap implies that while local affordability is challenged by low incomes, the asset value offers a cheaper entry point into the state’s real estate market compared to urban centers.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Las Animas County is 44.4% below the Colorado state average and 4.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $891/month is 31.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Las Animas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.6%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
