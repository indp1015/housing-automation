📝 Local Market Analysis

Champaign County exhibits a distinct divergence between strong price momentum and deepening affordability constraints, characterizing it as a buyer’s market with significant entry barriers. The price-to-rent ratio of 16.8 sits below the national average of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing remains relatively more attractive than renting on a pure cost basis, despite the 3.32 price-to-income ratio slightly exceeding the national benchmark of 3.20. This structural advantage is reinforced by the rent burden, which stands at 19.8% of median household income compared to the national 17.2%, indicating that renters face disproportionate financial pressure relative to their earnings. Consequently, the market leans toward favoring buyers who can secure financing, although the high entry cost limits the pool of eligible purchasers. The multi-year trend reveals a sharp acceleration in home values, with the year-over-year growth spiking to 12.9% in 2022 before moderating to 3.1% in the 2024 Census estimates. However, recent FHFA data indicates a resurgence in momentum, with the 2025 HPI up 7.19%, outperforming the Illinois state average of 5.54% by 1.65 percentage points. This recent strength contrasts with the slower census-based growth, highlighting a lag in survey data versus transactional reality. Notably, home values are rising faster than rents, a pattern that typically signals a shift in demand toward ownership or a supply constraint in the rental sector. This divergence is supported by robust migration data, showing a net inflow of 3,002 filers between 2021 and 2022, with incoming residents earning an average of $89,151, significantly higher than the county’s median household income of $63,683. Economic fundamentals provide mixed support for this growth. While total employment stands at 67,040 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,194, the 7.40% national mortgage rate poses a substantial hurdle for affordability. The composite assessment reflects this tension: high momentum scores are offset by low stability and affordability metrics, resulting in an overall classification of "Stressed." Housing supply, with 860 new units authorized in 2025, including 593 multi-family units, suggests developers are responding to rental demand, yet the single-family supply of 267 units may be insufficient to meet the influx of higher-income migrants. For potential participants, the data indicates that while price appreciation is outpacing the state, the widening gap between asset values and local median incomes creates a fragile equilibrium where external economic shocks could rapidly impact buyer liquidity.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Champaign County is 38.2% above the Illinois state average and 7.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,051/month is 21.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Champaign County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Champaign County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.0%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
