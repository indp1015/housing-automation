Market Analysis

The median home value in Harris County is 48.5% above the Texas state average and 21.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,401/month is 40.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Harris County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Harris County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.7%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
