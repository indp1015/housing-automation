Market Analysis

The median home value in Tioga County is 14.6% below the Pennsylvania state average and 20.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $863/month is 12.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Tioga County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Tioga County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
