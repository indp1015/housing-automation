Market Analysis

The median home value in Weber County is 3.1% above the Utah state average and 84.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,269/month is 15.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.6, Weber County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Weber County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.6%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
