Market Analysis

The median home value in Gasconade County is 2.4% above the Missouri state average and 21.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $743/month is 5.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Gasconade County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.9%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
