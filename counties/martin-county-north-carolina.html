Market Analysis

The median home value in Martin County is 53.4% below the North Carolina state average and 51.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $758/month is 22.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.1, buying a home in Martin County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Martin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.8%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
