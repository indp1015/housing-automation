Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 13.6% above the Virginia state average and 49.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,199/month is 5.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.2%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
