Market Analysis

The median home value in Brazos County is 58.9% above the Texas state average and 30.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,193/month is 19.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Brazos County has an owner-occupancy rate of 46.8%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
