Market Analysis

The median home value in Carbon County is 50.8% above the Montana state average and 78.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $882/month is 6.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 35.8, Carbon County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Carbon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.1%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
