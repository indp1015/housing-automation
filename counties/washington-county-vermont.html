Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 7.6% above the Vermont state average and 38.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,094/month is 2.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.1%, compared to 76.9% in Vermont and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
