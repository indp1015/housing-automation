Market Analysis

The median home value in Gallatin County is 140.5% above the Montana state average and 186.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,574/month is 90.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.0, Gallatin County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Gallatin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.5%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
