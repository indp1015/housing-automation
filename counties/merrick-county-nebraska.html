Market Analysis

The median home value in Merrick County is 6.8% above the Nebraska state average and 22.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $750/month is 5.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Merrick County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
