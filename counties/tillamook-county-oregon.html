Market Analysis

The median home value in Tillamook County is 14.4% above the Oregon state average and 93.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,169/month is 2.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.1, Tillamook County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Tillamook County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.2%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
