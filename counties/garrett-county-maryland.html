Market Analysis

The median home value in Garrett County is 38.1% below the Maryland state average and 2.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $710/month is 49.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Garrett County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Garrett County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.0%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
