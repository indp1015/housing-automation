Market Analysis

The median home value in Del Norte County is 44.0% below the California state average and 51.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,182/month is 26.9% below the state average.

Del Norte County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.2%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
