Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 5.5% below the Nebraska state average and 31.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $645/month is 19.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.9%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
