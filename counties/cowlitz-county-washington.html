Market Analysis

The median home value in Cowlitz County is 7.6% below the Washington state average and 73.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,169/month is 2.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Cowlitz County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Cowlitz County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.2%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
