Market Analysis

The median home value in Orangeburg County is 42.0% below the South Carolina state average and 49.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $841/month is 14.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.5, buying a home in Orangeburg County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Orangeburg County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.0%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
