The housing market in ZIP code 45822, located within Mercer County, Ohio, exhibits a distinct pattern of volatility and recent acceleration. This divergence suggests that local demand factors are driving performance above the statewide baseline. However, the sub-market remains anchored by the parent county’s median home value of $210,600. The HPI level of 214.5, which is 114.5% above the 2000 baseline, indicates that prices have more than doubled over the last two decades, yet the absolute dollar values remain moderate relative to national averages. For prospective buyers and residents, the data indicates a market where affordability is increasingly tested by rapid price appreciation.
