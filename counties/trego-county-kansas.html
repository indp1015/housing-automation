📝 Local Market Analysis

Trego County, Kansas, operates as a distinctively affordable housing market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 1.48, significantly below the national average of 3.20. This extreme affordability is reinforced by a rent burden of 10.6%, well under the national benchmark of 17.2%, suggesting that housing costs impose minimal financial strain on residents. The market leans toward a buyer’s advantage, evidenced by a price-to-rent ratio of 14.0, which is lower than the national average of 18.7, indicating that purchasing is comparatively more economical than long-term renting. With an owner-occupancy rate of 82.2%, the community exhibits a strong preference for homeownership, further solidifying its status as a stable, low-cost residential area. The multi-year trend reveals a consistent appreciation pattern, with median home values rising from $84,400 in 2017 to $120,900 in 2024. Although the peak year-over-year growth of 10.2% occurred in 2019, recent momentum has stabilized at 4.8% in 2024, signaling a maturing market rather than a speculative bubble. Notably, home values are rising faster than rents, a divergence that suggests increasing demand for ownership relative to the rental sector. This trend is supported by a median household income of $81,528, which exceeds the national average by 19.9%, providing residents with the purchasing power to sustain local price growth despite the small population of 2,777. Regional context highlights that while Trego County’s home values are 46.9% below the national average, they are only 12.9% below the Kansas state average, indicating that the county’s affordability is partly driven by its rural isolation rather than statewide low costs. The local economy, comprising 599 jobs and 104 establishments, offers an average weekly wage of $967, which, combined with a net migration gain of 43 filers, suggests a stable but slowly growing demographic base. With only two new single-family units authorized in 2025 and no multi-family construction, housing supply is extremely constrained, which may limit future price volatility but also restricts new entry into the market. For potential buyers, the current 7.40% mortgage rate must be weighed against the exceptionally low entry price, while renters benefit from minimal cost burdens. The data indicates a market where affordability remains the primary driver of value, insulated from broader national housing fluctuations.
