Market Analysis

The median home value in Grant County is 14.3% below the Wisconsin state average and 11.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $813/month is 11.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grant County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
