Market Analysis

The median home value in Montezuma County is 22.6% below the Colorado state average and 45.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $974/month is 22.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Montezuma County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Montezuma County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.8%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
