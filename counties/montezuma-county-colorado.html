Market Analysis

The median home value in Montezuma County is 22.5% below the Colorado state average and 45.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $942/month is 27.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.3, Montezuma County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Montezuma County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.2%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
