Market Analysis

The median home value in Reeves County is 36.7% below the Texas state average and 48.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $981/month is 1.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.0, buying a home in Reeves County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Reeves County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.7%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
