Market Analysis

The median home value in Marin County is 143.5% above the California state average and 557.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,584/month is 59.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 44.8, Marin County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Marin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
