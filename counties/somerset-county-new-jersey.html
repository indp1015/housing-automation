Market Analysis

The median home value in Somerset County is 28.4% above the New Jersey state average and 142.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,033/month is 24.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Somerset County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
