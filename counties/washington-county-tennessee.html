Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 11.5% above the Tennessee state average and 9.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,005/month is 12.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.5%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
