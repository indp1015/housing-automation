📝 Local Market Analysis

Jefferson Davis County, Mississippi, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $90,100 and a price-to-income ratio of 2.34. This figure is significantly below the national average of 3.20, indicating that local housing costs are well within reach for the median household earning $38,548. The market’s stability is further underscored by an owner-occupancy rate of 84.5%, suggesting a long-term residential base rather than a transient rental population. However, the county trails the state average by 33.7% and the national average by 60.4% in home values, reflecting its rural economic structure. With only 1,597 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,332, the local labor market provides limited upward pressure on housing prices, keeping entry barriers low for prospective buyers. The multi-year trend reveals a volatile but ultimately stabilizing price environment. After a sharp 12.4% surge in 2019, values corrected by 12.9% in 2020 before settling into a modest growth pattern, culminating in a 3.4% increase in 2024. This recent momentum contrasts with the rental sector, where the median gross rent of $757 represents a 23.6% burden on household income, exceeding the national average of 17.2%. This divergence highlights a specific anomaly: rents are rising faster than home values, creating a price-to-rent ratio of 9.9, which is less than half the national average of 18.7. This metric confirms that purchasing remains substantially more cost-effective than renting, despite the higher upfront capital requirement. Economic vitality remains the primary constraint on the county’s broader market health, driven by low employment density and modest migration gains of +177 filers. While the 7.40% national mortgage rate increases monthly payment burdens, the low asset prices mitigate this impact for qualified buyers. The combination of high affordability and moderate stability suggests a market where long-term equity accumulation is feasible, provided buyers can navigate the limited local job market. For renters, the rising cost burden relative to income may accelerate the transition to ownership, as the financial gap between renting and buying narrows. Ultimately, the data points to a resilient, low-cost housing environment where affordability is the dominant factor, offsetting the weaknesses in local economic vitality and employment growth.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson Davis County is 33.7% below the Mississippi state average and 60.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $757/month is 5.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.9, buying a home in Jefferson Davis County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jefferson Davis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.5%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
