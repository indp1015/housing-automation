Market Analysis

The median home value in Alexander County is 60.9% below the Illinois state average and 73.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $656/month is 24.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 7.6, buying a home in Alexander County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Alexander County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.6%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
