Van Buren County exhibits a distinct buyer’s market profile, evidenced by a price-to-rent ratio of 14.0, significantly below the national average of 18.0. This metric indicates that purchasing is substantially more cost-effective than renting. This divergence suggests that equity accumulation is accelerating relative to rental costs.
