Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnson County is 34.1% above the Missouri state average and 3.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $932/month is 19.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Johnson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.5%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
