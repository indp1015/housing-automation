Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnson County is 36.3% above the Missouri state average and 4.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $905/month is 18.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Johnson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 73.6% in Missouri and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
