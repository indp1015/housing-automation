Market Analysis

The median home value in Buffalo County is 8.9% below the Wisconsin state average and 6.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $876/month is 4.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Buffalo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.5%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
