Market Analysis

The median home value in Powhatan County is 40.6% above the Virginia state average and 82.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,168/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.7, Powhatan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Powhatan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 93.2%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
