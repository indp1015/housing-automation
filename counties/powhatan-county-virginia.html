Market Analysis

The median home value in Powhatan County is 37.1% above the Virginia state average and 80.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,107/month is 2.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.7, Powhatan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Powhatan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 93.1%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
