📝 Editor's Insight

Monroe County operates as a distinct buyer’s market, evidenced by a price-to-rent ratio of 11.4, significantly below the national average of 18.0. This metric indicates that purchasing is substantially more cost-effective than renting for long-term residents. This suggests that equity accumulation through ownership is currently outpacing the cost of tenancy. A critical insight lies in the county’s relative positioning; despite being 37.7% below the state average in home values, the income gap is only 33.4% below the national mean. Market Analysis

The median home value in Monroe County is 37.7% below the Arkansas state average and 61.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $603/month is 20.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.4, buying a home in Monroe County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Monroe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.7%, compared to 70.5% in Arkansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
