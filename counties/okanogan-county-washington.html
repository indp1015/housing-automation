Market Analysis

The median home value in Okanogan County is 31.3% below the Washington state average and 31.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $887/month is 29.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.2, Okanogan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Okanogan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.4%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
