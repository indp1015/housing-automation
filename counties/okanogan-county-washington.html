Market Analysis

The median home value in Okanogan County is 28.5% below the Washington state average and 34.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $868/month is 27.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.3, Okanogan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Okanogan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.7%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
