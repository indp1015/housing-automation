Market Analysis

The median home value in Routt County is 90.0% above the Colorado state average and 257.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,845/month is 47.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 34.2, Routt County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Routt County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.9%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
