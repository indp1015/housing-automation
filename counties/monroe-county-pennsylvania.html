Market Analysis

The median home value in Monroe County is 26.2% above the Pennsylvania state average and 17.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,393/month is 40.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Monroe County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Monroe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.2%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
