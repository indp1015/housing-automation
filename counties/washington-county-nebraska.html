Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 80.8% above the Nebraska state average and 30.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $965/month is 21.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.7, Washington County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.2%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
