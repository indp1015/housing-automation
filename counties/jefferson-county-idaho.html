Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 7.4% above the Idaho state average and 58.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,098/month is 19.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.4, Jefferson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.4%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
