Market Analysis

The median home value in Deuel County is 39.1% below the Nebraska state average and 56.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $872/month is 9.5% above the state average.

Deuel County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
