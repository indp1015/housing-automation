Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 12.3% above the Kansas state average and 31.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $937/month is 16.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.9, buying a home in Clay County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
