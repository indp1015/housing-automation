Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 27.3% below the Kentucky state average and 48.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $631/month is 18.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Knox County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Knox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.6%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
