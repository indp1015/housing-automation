Market Analysis

The median home value in Boulder County is 79.3% above the Colorado state average and 237.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,893/month is 51.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.4, Boulder County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Boulder County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.5%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
