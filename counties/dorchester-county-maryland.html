Market Analysis

The median home value in Dorchester County is 28.1% below the Maryland state average and 19.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $959/month is 32.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dorchester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.3%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
