Market Analysis

The median home value in Lonoke County is 36.9% above the Arkansas state average and 15.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,025/month is 32.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lonoke County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lonoke County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.0%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
