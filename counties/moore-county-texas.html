Market Analysis

The median home value in Moore County is 18.1% below the Texas state average and 33.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $939/month is 5.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Moore County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Moore County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
