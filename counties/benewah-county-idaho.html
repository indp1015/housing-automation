Market Analysis

The median home value in Benewah County is 17.4% below the Idaho state average and 28.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $875/month is 9.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.0, Benewah County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Benewah County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.2%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
