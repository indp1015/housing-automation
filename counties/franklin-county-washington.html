Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 13.1% below the Washington state average and 63.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,171/month is 2.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.1%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
