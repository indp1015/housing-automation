Market Analysis

The median home value in Jasper County is 5.0% below the Missouri state average and 26.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $973/month is 24.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Jasper County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jasper County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.8%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
