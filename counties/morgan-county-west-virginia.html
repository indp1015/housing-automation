Market Analysis

The median home value in Morgan County is 62.6% above the West Virginia state average and 5.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $794/month is 2.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.3, Morgan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Morgan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.1%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
