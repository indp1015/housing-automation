Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 9.5% below the Michigan state average and 21.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,132/month is 25.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.1, buying a home in Wayne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.8%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
