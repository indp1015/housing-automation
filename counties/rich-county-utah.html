Market Analysis

The median home value in Rich County is 22.9% below the Utah state average and 37.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $743/month is 32.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.7, Rich County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Rich County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
