Market Analysis

The median home value in Bonner County is 38.9% above the Idaho state average and 104.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,059/month is 15.2% above the state average.

Bonner County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
