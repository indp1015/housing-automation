Market Analysis

The median home value in Floyd County is 51.9% below the Texas state average and 60.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $676/month is 32.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.0, buying a home in Floyd County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Floyd County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.3%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
