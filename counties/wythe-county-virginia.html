Market Analysis

The median home value in Wythe County is 42.1% below the Virginia state average and 24.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $764/month is 34.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Wythe County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Wythe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
