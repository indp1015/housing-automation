📝 Local Market Analysis

Humphreys County, Mississippi, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $87,800 and a price-to-income ratio of 2.60, which sits significantly below the national average of 3.20. The price-to-rent ratio of 11.4 further underscores the economic advantage of homeownership over renting, where the national average stands at 18.7. This structural affordability is reinforced by a median household income of $33,731, which, while 50.4% below the national mean, aligns closely with the low cost of entry into the housing stock. The market’s classification is further supported by the fact that home values are 35.4% below the state average and 61.4% below the national average, creating a deep discount relative to broader economic benchmarks. Recent trends reveal a period of accelerated appreciation followed by stabilization. Home values surged by 11.7% in 2022 and 11.1% in 2023, reaching a peak of $88,800 before moderating to a 1.1% decline in 2024. This sharp deceleration contrasts with the steady, lower-growth trajectory observed between 2017 and 2021, suggesting that the recent spike was likely driven by transient factors rather than sustained fundamental demand. The divergence between rapid price appreciation and relatively stable rent burdens, currently at 22.8% of income compared to the 17.2% national average, indicates that while housing costs are rising, they remain manageable within the local income structure. The net migration of +107 filers between 2021 and 2022, with inflow incomes averaging $40,953, suggests a modest influx of higher-earning residents that may support future price stability, although the small population of 7,395 limits the depth of this demand pool. The economic context presents a mixed signal for market vitality. With only 1,510 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $725, the local labor market is constrained, limiting the purchasing power of the majority of residents. The current 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40% further pressures affordability, yet the low absolute price point mitigates this impact. The composite assessment of the market reflects strong momentum from recent price gains but low vitality due to the limited economic base and small population. Stability remains low, as evidenced by the volatile price swings in the last two years, while affordability remains the primary driver of the market’s attractiveness. For potential buyers, the data suggests an entry point well below national norms, but the limited local employment base and recent price volatility warrant careful consideration of long-term residency and income stability. Renter’s burden is moderate, but the significant discount in ownership costs makes purchasing a more financially advantageous strategy for those with stable local employment.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Humphreys County is 35.4% below the Mississippi state average and 61.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $641/month is 20.3% below the state average.
