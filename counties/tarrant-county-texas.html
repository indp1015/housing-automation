Market Analysis

The median home value in Tarrant County is 73.9% above the Texas state average and 42.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,547/month is 55.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Tarrant County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Tarrant County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.3%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
