Market Analysis

The median home value in Summit County is 113.7% above the Colorado state average and 302.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,851/month is 47.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.3, Summit County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Summit County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.2%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
