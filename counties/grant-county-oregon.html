Market Analysis

The median home value in Grant County is 40.6% below the Oregon state average and 0.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $789/month is 33.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grant County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
