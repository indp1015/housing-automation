Market Analysis

The median home value in De Witt County is 14.8% below the Illinois state average and 42.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $903/month is 4.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.0, buying a home in De Witt County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

De Witt County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
