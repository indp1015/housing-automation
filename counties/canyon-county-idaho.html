Market Analysis

The median home value in Canyon County is 9.8% above the Idaho state average and 71.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,347/month is 39.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Canyon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
