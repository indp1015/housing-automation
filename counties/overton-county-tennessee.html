Market Analysis

The median home value in Overton County is 22.5% below the Tennessee state average and 23.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $832/month is 6.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Overton County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Overton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
