Market Analysis

The median home value in Alexandria city is 148.5% above the Virginia state average and 222.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,089/month is 78.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.3, Alexandria city is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Alexandria city has an owner-occupancy rate of 42.1%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
