Market Analysis

The median home value in Chattooga County is 41.6% below the Georgia state average and 49.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $790/month is 18.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.2, buying a home in Chattooga County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Chattooga County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.8%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
