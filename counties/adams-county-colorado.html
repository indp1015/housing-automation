Market Analysis

The median home value in Adams County is 15.2% above the Colorado state average and 116.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,713/month is 36.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Adams County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.9%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
