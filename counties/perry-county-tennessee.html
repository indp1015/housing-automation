Market Analysis

The median home value in Perry County is 43.2% below the Tennessee state average and 44.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $758/month is 15.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.0, buying a home in Perry County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Perry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.8%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
