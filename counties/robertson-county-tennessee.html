Market Analysis

The median home value in Robertson County is 47.2% above the Tennessee state average and 40.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,176/month is 36.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Robertson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
