Market Analysis

The median home value in Columbia County is 23.4% above the New York state average and 52.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,260/month is 7.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Columbia County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.3%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
