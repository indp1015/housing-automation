This consistent appreciation indicates a robust demand environment that has outpaced broader regional trends. The most recent year-over-year growth of 4.55% continues this momentum, although the rate of increase has moderated slightly compared to the double-digit gains observed in previous annual intervals. When compared to broader geographic benchmarks, ZIP 28712 demonstrates distinct performance characteristics. This divergence from the state average signals that local demand in 28712 remains significantly stronger than the statewide norm, driven by specific local factors rather than general economic conditions. The sub-market character of ZIP 28712 aligns with a premium, owner-occupied segment.
