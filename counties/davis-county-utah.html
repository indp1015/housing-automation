Market Analysis

The median home value in Davis County is 24.6% above the Utah state average and 122.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,516/month is 37.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.9, Davis County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Davis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
