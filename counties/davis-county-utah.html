Market Analysis

The median home value in Davis County is 20.7% above the Utah state average and 122.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,621/month is 41.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Davis County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Davis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.3%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
