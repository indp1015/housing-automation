Market Analysis

The median home value in Ottawa County is 12.0% above the Ohio state average and 3.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $930/month is 2.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Ottawa County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Ottawa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.6%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
