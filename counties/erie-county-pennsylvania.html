Market Analysis

The median home value in Erie County is 16.5% below the Pennsylvania state average and 22.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $904/month is 8.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Erie County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Erie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.1%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
