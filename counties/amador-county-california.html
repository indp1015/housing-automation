Market Analysis

The median home value in Amador County is 25.9% below the California state average and 99.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,380/month is 14.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Amador County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Amador County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.1%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
