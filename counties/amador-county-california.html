📝 Local Market Analysis

Amador County presents a seller's market characterized by severe affordability constraints and persistent price appreciation. The price-to-income ratio of 5.11 significantly exceeds the national 3.20 benchmark, while the price-to-rent ratio of 26.4 surpasses the 18.7 national average, indicating ownership costs substantially outpace rental economics. Rent burden at 19.4% of income exceeds the 17.2% national rate, though owner occupancy remains elevated at 80.4%. Median home values surged 61% from $278,600 (2017) to $449,800 (2024), driven by a 17.2% spike in 2022 before moderating to 6.4% in 2024. This trajectory reflects pandemic-era demand rather than local wage growth, as median income of $88,044 trails home value appreciation. Regionally, Amador homes trade at a 26.2% discount to California's median while commanding a 97.5% premium over national levels, positioning the county as a relative value destination within the state. Market Analysis

The median home value in Amador County is 26.2% below the California state average and 97.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,421/month is 15.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Amador County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Amador County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
