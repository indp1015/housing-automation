Market Analysis

The median home value in Forsyth County is 6.4% above the North Carolina state average and 10.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,098/month is 12.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Forsyth County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Forsyth County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.6%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
