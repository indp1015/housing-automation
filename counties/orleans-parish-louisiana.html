Market Analysis

The median home value in Orleans Parish is 83.5% above the Louisiana state average and 40.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,211/month is 38.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Orleans Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 50.5%, compared to 71.7% in Louisiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
