Market Analysis

The median home value in Orleans Parish is 88.6% above the Louisiana state average and 38.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,251/month is 36.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Orleans Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 51.2%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
