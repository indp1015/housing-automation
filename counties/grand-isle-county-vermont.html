Market Analysis

The median home value in Grand Isle County is 34.5% above the Vermont state average and 73.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,425/month is 26.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grand Isle County has an owner-occupancy rate of 89.9%, compared to 77.1% in Vermont and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
