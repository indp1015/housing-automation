📝 Local Market Analysis

Nez Perce County, Idaho, exhibits characteristics of a buyer’s market despite significant recent price appreciation, primarily driven by a price-to-rent ratio of 27.5, which substantially exceeds the national average of 18.7. This metric suggests that renting is relatively more economical than purchasing for many households, particularly given the median gross rent of $1,015 and a rent burden of 16.8%, which is slightly below the national average of 17.2%. However, the affordability landscape is complex; the price-to-income ratio stands at 4.61, well above the national benchmark of 3.20, indicating that home purchases remain expensive relative to local earnings of $72,599. The market’s classification is further nuanced by the fact that home values are rising faster than rents, a trend that typically favors sellers in the long term but currently creates a disparity between the cost of entry and the cost of occupancy. The multi-year trend reveals a period of aggressive appreciation, with median home values increasing from $172,500 in 2017 to $334,700 in 2024, representing a cumulative gain of nearly 94%. The most rapid growth occurred between 2022 and 2024, with year-over-year increases of 17.4%, 12.2%, and 14.9%, respectively. This surge contrasts with the recent FHFA HPI data for 2025, which shows a modest year-over-year change of +0.87%. This recent slowdown places the county underperforming relative to the Idaho state average of +3.48%, a divergence of -2.61 percentage points. This deceleration suggests that the initial post-pandemic boom has moderated, potentially stabilizing prices after a period of overheating. The economic context supports this stabilization, with total employment reaching 17,725 jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,090. Regional comparisons highlight that while Nez Perce County home values are 47.0% above the national average, they remain 5.8% below the state average, indicating that the county is not the most expensive market in Idaho. Migration data shows a net inflow of 1,002 filers between 2021 and 2022, with incoming residents earning an average of $74,248, slightly lower than those who left. Housing supply remains constrained relative to demand, with only 272 new units authorized in 2025, comprising 122 single-family and 150 multi-family homes. For potential buyers, the high price-to-income ratio and elevated mortgage rates of 7.40% present significant barriers to entry, whereas renters benefit from a manageable rent burden. The divergence between the strong historical price growth and the recent HPI slowdown indicates a market in transition, where the initial speculative pressure is giving way to more fundamental economic drivers.

The median home value in Nez Perce County is 5.8% below the Idaho state average and 47.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,015/month is 4.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.5, Nez Perce County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Nez Perce County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.4%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
