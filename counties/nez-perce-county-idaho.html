Market Analysis

The median home value in Nez Perce County is 5.8% below the Idaho state average and 47.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,015/month is 4.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.5, Nez Perce County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Nez Perce County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.4%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
