Market Analysis

The median home value in Cache County is 4.0% above the Utah state average and 85.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,137/month is 3.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.8, Cache County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Cache County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.6%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
