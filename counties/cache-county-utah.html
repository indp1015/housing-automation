Market Analysis

The median home value in Cache County is 6.1% above the Utah state average and 95.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,213/month is 5.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.5, Cache County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Cache County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.7%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
