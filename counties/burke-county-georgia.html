Market Analysis

The median home value in Burke County is 23.4% below the Georgia state average and 33.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $758/month is 21.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Burke County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Burke County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.0%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
