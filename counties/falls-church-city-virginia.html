Market Analysis

The median home value in Falls Church city is 256.9% above the Virginia state average and 363.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,190/month is 87.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 40.2, Falls Church city is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Falls Church city has an owner-occupancy rate of 52.5%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
