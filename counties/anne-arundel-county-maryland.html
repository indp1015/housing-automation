Market Analysis

The median home value in Anne Arundel County is 26.5% above the Maryland state average and 105.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,071/month is 41.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Anne Arundel County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
