Market Analysis

The median home value in McKenzie County is 98.9% above the North Dakota state average and 53.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,284/month is 66.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

McKenzie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 53.4%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
