Market Analysis

The median home value in Iroquois County is 9.9% below the Illinois state average and 39.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $799/month is 7.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Iroquois County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Iroquois County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.0%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
