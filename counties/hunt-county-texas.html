Market Analysis

The median home value in Hunt County is 22.2% above the Texas state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,184/month is 22.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Hunt County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hunt County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.8%, compared to 73.0% in Texas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
