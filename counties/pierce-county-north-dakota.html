Market Analysis

The median home value in Pierce County is 14.9% below the North Dakota state average and 32.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $807/month is 4.9% above the state average.

Pierce County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.9%, compared to 74.1% in North Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
