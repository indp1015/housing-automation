Market Analysis

The median home value in Wasco County is 6.9% below the Oregon state average and 57.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $978/month is 14.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.3, Wasco County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Wasco County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.2%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
