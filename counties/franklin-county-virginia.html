Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 19.9% below the Virginia state average and 5.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $797/month is 30.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.7%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
