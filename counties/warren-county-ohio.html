Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 75.2% above the Ohio state average and 51.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,293/month is 48.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 73.4% in Ohio and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
