📝 Local Market Analysis

Stearns County, Minnesota, functions as a moderately priced market with distinct affordability pressures, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 3.55, which exceeds the national average of 3.20. Despite this elevated ratio, the rent burden stands at 16.6% of income, slightly below the national benchmark of 17.2%, suggesting that rental housing remains relatively accessible compared to homeownership. The market is neither a strict buyer’s nor renter’s environment but leans toward a balanced dynamic where ownership costs are high relative to local earnings, yet rental burdens are manageable. This classification is reinforced by an owner-occupancy rate of 67.9%, indicating a stable residential base, while the median home value of $273,400 sits 14.7% above the state average and 20.1% above the national mean, reflecting a premium status within the region. The trajectory of home values reveals a period of rapid appreciation followed by stabilization. After a peak year-over-year increase of 17.3% in 2022, growth has moderated to 6.6% in 2024, aligning more closely with the state’s recent momentum. The FHFA House Price Index indicates a 2.20% year-over-year change for 2025, slightly lagging the state average of 2.42% by 0.22 percentage points. This deceleration suggests that the initial surge in demand has cooled, creating a more stable pricing environment. However, the divergence between price and rent growth is notable; home values have risen significantly faster than rents, resulting in a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, well above the national average of 18.7. This gap implies that renting may be a more cost-effective strategy for many households in the near term, as the financial outlay for ownership is disproportionately higher relative to rental costs. Economic fundamentals support the market’s resilience, with a median household income of $77,066, which is 13.3% higher than the national average. Local employment stands at 76,313 jobs, with an average weekly wage of $1,239, providing a solid income base that partially offsets high home prices. Housing supply remains active, with 648 new units authorized in 2025, including 371 single-family and 277 multi-family homes, which helps mitigate potential shortages. Net migration of +1,934 filers indicates sustained demographic growth, further supporting demand. The high mortgage rate of 7.40% adds a layer of friction for buyers, potentially extending the period of price stabilization. Consequently, while momentum remains positive, the affordability constraints and elevated price-to-rent multiples suggest that the market is adjusting to higher financing costs, with stability currently driven by strong local incomes and consistent supply rather than speculative price increases.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Stearns County is 14.7% above the Minnesota state average and 20.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,068/month is 13.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Stearns County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.9%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
