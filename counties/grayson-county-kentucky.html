Market Analysis

The median home value in Grayson County is 2.3% above the Kentucky state average and 27.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $658/month is 17.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grayson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.6%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
