Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 42.4% above the Mississippi state average and 14.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,087/month is 39.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.9, buying a home in Jackson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.0%, compared to 71.7% in Mississippi and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
