Market Analysis

The median home value in Shelby County is 2.7% below the Iowa state average and 26.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $810/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Shelby County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
