Market Analysis

The median home value in Suwannee County is 39.6% below the Florida state average and 24.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $884/month is 32.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Suwannee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Suwannee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.9%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
