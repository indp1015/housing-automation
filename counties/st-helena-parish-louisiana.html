📝 Local Market Analysis

St. Helena Parish operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 1.84, significantly below the national average of 3.20. With a median home value of $77,300 and a median household income of $41,934, housing costs remain accessible relative to local earnings. However, the rental sector presents a tighter squeeze, with a rent burden of 24.9% of income compared to the national benchmark of 17.2%. This divergence is highlighted by a price-to-rent ratio of 7.4, far below the national 18.7, suggesting that purchasing remains substantially more cost-effective than renting. The market’s classification is further supported by a high owner-occupancy rate of 79.0%, indicating a stable, long-term residential base rather than a transient rental population. Recent data reveals significant volatility and a notable divergence between price trends and rental momentum. While the FHFA HPI recorded a robust +20.32% year-over-year increase in 2023, outperforming the Louisiana state average of +1.59% by 18.73 percentage points, the broader ACS data shows a sharp 27.3% decline in median home values in 2024. This discrepancy likely reflects the lag in Census reporting versus real-time mortgage transaction data, yet both sources confirm a market in flux. Rents are rising faster than home values, signaling shifting demand dynamics. The local economic foundation is fragile, with only 1,022 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,085, which constrains purchasing power despite low home prices. Housing supply is minimal, with just 14 new single-family units authorized in 2025, limiting inventory growth. The overall market assessment reflects high affordability and momentum but low vitality and stability. The composite evaluation is driven by the stark contrast between exceptional price accessibility and weak local economic indicators. Migration data shows a net inflow of 81 filers, with incoming residents averaging $47,217 in income, slightly higher than outflowing residents at $46,562, suggesting a marginal demographic upgrade. However, the limited employment base of 122 establishments and the current 7.40% national mortgage rate complicate financing for local buyers. For potential stakeholders, the data indicates that while entry costs are low, the lack of economic diversification and the volatility in value metrics require careful consideration of long-term stability. The market is currently defined by low barriers to entry but constrained by the region's small-scale economic activity and inconsistent valuation signals.

Market Analysis

The median home value in St. Helena Parish is 53.8% below the Louisiana state average and 66.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $870/month is 4.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 7.4, buying a home in St. Helena Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

St. Helena Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.0%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
