Market Analysis

The median home value in Juab County is 2.1% below the Utah state average and 74.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $917/month is 16.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.6, Juab County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Juab County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.0%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
