Market Analysis

The median home value in Orange County is 21.3% above the Virginia state average and 59.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,145/month is 0.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Orange County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.8%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
