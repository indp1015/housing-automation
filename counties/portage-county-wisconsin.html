Market Analysis

The median home value in Portage County is 3.9% above the Wisconsin state average and 6.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $940/month is 2.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Portage County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.7%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
