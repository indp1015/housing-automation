Market Analysis

The median home value in Levy County is 33.0% below the Florida state average and 16.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $850/month is 34.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Levy County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Levy County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.4%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
