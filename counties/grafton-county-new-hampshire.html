Market Analysis

The median home value in Grafton County is 1.4% below the New Hampshire state average and 51.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,301/month is 1.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grafton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 74.3% in New Hampshire and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
