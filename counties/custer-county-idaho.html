Market Analysis

The median home value in Custer County is 7.4% above the Idaho state average and 67.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $691/month is 28.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 46.0, Custer County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Custer County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.8%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
