Market Analysis

The median home value in Bedford County is 0.5% above the Virginia state average and 30.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $952/month is 18.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Bedford County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bedford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.7%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
