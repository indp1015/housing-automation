Market Analysis

The median home value in Sevier County is 25.7% below the Utah state average and 36.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $912/month is 20.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.4, Sevier County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sevier County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.8%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
