Market Analysis

The median home value in Sevier County is 27.4% below the Utah state average and 29.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $878/month is 20.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Sevier County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sevier County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.9%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
