ZIP 46121 exhibits a pronounced price appreciation cycle followed by a recent correction. However, the most recent data for 2025 indicates a reversal, with the HPI declining to 266.59. This represents a year-over-year decrease of 2.24%, signaling a stabilization or softening phase after the rapid expansion observed in the preceding three years. This negative variance suggests that local supply-demand dynamics in 46121 are currently decoupling from the general upward trajectory of the county and state. Contextualized by parent county data, ZIP 46121 appears to be a mid-to-upper-tier owner-occupied sub-market.
