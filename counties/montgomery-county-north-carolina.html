Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 31.3% below the North Carolina state average and 29.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $766/month is 21.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Montgomery County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.0%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
