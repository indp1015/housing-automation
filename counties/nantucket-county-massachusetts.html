Market Analysis

The median home value in Nantucket County is 151.3% above the Massachusetts state average and 599.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,213/month is 37.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 60.0, Nantucket County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Nantucket County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.8%, compared to 67.2% in Massachusetts and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
