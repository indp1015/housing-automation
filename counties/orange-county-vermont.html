Market Analysis

The median home value in Orange County is 7.4% below the Vermont state average and 19.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,173/month is 3.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Orange County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Orange County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.2%, compared to 77.1% in Vermont and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
