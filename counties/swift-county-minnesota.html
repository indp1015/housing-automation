Market Analysis

The median home value in Swift County is 32.3% below the Minnesota state average and 29.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $920/month is 1.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Swift County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Swift County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.4%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
