Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 39.9% below the Pennsylvania state average and 44.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $755/month is 23.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Warren County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.5%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
