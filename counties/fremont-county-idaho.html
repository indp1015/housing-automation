Market Analysis

The median home value in Fremont County is 11.6% below the Idaho state average and 38.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $864/month is 10.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.3, Fremont County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fremont County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
