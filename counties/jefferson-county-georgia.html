Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 48.5% below the Georgia state average and 56.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $723/month is 21.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.7, buying a home in Jefferson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
