Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 6.3% below the Florida state average and 17.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,363/month is 4.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Polk County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.5%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
