Market Analysis

The median home value in Buncombe County is 66.5% above the North Carolina state average and 72.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,362/month is 39.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Buncombe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.2%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
