📝 Local Market Analysis

Buncombe County operates as a distinctly renter-favorable market, driven by a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, which significantly exceeds the national average of 18.7. This structural imbalance suggests that purchasing equity is less cost-effective than renting for many residents. The market exhibits a clear divergence between asset appreciation and rental costs; while home values surged 20.1% year-over-year in 2022 and continued rising 8.8% in 2024, the median gross rent stands at $1,362, resulting in a rent burden of 22.0% of income. This contrasts sharply with the national rent burden average of 17.2%, indicating that while housing costs are high, the rental sector provides a relatively more accessible entry point than homeownership. Affordability remains the primary constraint, with a price-to-income ratio of 5.26 compared to the national benchmark of 3.20. Home values are 72.1% above the national average and 66.5% above the state average, yet median household income is only 9.5% above the national mean. This disparity creates significant financial stress for potential buyers. Recent momentum shows signs of stabilization, as the FHFA House Price Index indicates a modest 1.19% year-over-year increase in 2025, outperforming the state’s 0.38% growth. This slowdown from the peak 20.1% gain in 2022 suggests the market is cooling, though prices remain elevated. Economic fundamentals support sustained demand, with net migration adding 7,846 filers between 2021 and 2022, many earning above the county median. The local economy supports 119,099 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,157, providing a solid income base. However, new construction of 2,028 units in 2025 may not fully offset demand, particularly with 1,036 multi-family units suggesting a shift toward denser housing. The 7.40% mortgage rate further suppresses buyer purchasing power, reinforcing the preference for renting. Consequently, the market’s vitality is high due to strong migration and employment, but stability is compromised by extreme affordability metrics. The overall assessment reflects a market where high economic activity collides with prohibitive entry costs, favoring those with existing equity or rental flexibility over new entrants seeking to purchase.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Buncombe County is 66.5% above the North Carolina state average and 72.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,362/month is 39.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Buncombe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.2%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
