Market Analysis

The median home value in Watauga County is 50.1% above the North Carolina state average and 52.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,066/month is 13.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Watauga County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Watauga County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.2%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
