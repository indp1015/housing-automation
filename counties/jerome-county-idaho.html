Market Analysis

The median home value in Jerome County is 23.5% below the Idaho state average and 19.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $966/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Jerome County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.2%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
