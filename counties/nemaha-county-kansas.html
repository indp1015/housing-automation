Market Analysis

The median home value in Nemaha County is 56.5% above the Kansas state average and 4.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $774/month is 3.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Nemaha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
