Market Analysis

The median home value in Bradley County is 17.2% above the Tennessee state average and 15.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,030/month is 15.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Bradley County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.3%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
