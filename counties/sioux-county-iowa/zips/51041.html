ZIP code 51041 in Sioux County, Iowa, exhibits a robust upward price trajectory over the past five years. This sustained growth indicates a consistent tightening of the local housing inventory and strong demand relative to supply. The most recent year-over-year appreciation of 4.68% continues this pattern, though it marks a slight moderation from the steeper gains observed in 2022 and 2023. While the county-wide year-over-year change stands at 6.13%, the ZIP code’s 4.68% growth suggests a potential stabilization or a lag in price acceleration compared to other areas within Sioux County. However, both figures significantly outpace the state average of 2.25%, confirming that the local market remains a distinct outlier of strength within Iowa. This divergence implies that while the broader county is experiencing rapid appreciation, specific sub-markets like 51041 may be entering a phase of more measured growth, potentially due to local inventory cycles or demographic shifts.
