Market Analysis

The median home value in Mountrail County is 32.4% above the North Dakota state average and 2.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $950/month is 23.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Mountrail County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.5%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
