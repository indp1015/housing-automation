Market Analysis

The median home value in Suffolk County is 105.7% above the New York state average and 154.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,255/month is 93.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Suffolk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.2%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
