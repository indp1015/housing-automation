Market Analysis

The median home value in Cook County is 32.1% above the Minnesota state average and 38.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $886/month is 3.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.7, Cook County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Cook County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
