Market Analysis

The median home value in Suffolk County is 15.9% above the Massachusetts state average and 221.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,069/month is 32.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.4, Suffolk County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Suffolk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 36.6%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
