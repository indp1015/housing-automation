The housing market in ZIP code 47024 exhibits a pronounced upward trajectory over the past five years. The FHFA House Price Index (HPI) for this area surged from 134.29 in 2020 to 285.89 in 2024. This represents a cumulative increase of approximately 113% over four years, indicating a period of rapid appreciation that significantly outpaces broader market trends. The consistent year-over-year growth suggests sustained demand pressure within this specific geographic segment of Fayette County. A critical divergence exists between ZIP 47024 and its surrounding regions. This substantial gap signals that 47024 is not moving in lockstep with the general Fayette County market. Instead, it functions as a distinct sub-market with unique demand drivers. For comparison, nearby ZIP 47331 saw a more moderate 3.99% increase, further isolating 47024’s exceptional performance. The HPI is an index, not a direct price, but the steep rise implies that the properties within 47024 are appreciating at a rate far exceeding the county’s entry-level baseline. Prospective buyers in ZIP 47024 should recognize that they are entering a premium segment of Fayette County.
