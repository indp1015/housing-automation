Market Analysis

The median home value in Granite County is 35.0% above the Montana state average and 60.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $734/month is 11.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.5, Granite County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Granite County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
