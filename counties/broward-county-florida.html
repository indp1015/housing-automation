Market Analysis

The median home value in Broward County is 45.8% above the Florida state average and 82.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,907/month is 46.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Broward County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Broward County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.6%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
