Market Analysis

The median home value in Broward County is 47.9% above the Florida state average and 79.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,804/month is 46.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Broward County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
