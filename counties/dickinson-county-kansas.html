Market Analysis

The median home value in Dickinson County is 5.7% above the Kansas state average and 35.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $901/month is 12.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Dickinson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Dickinson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.6%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
