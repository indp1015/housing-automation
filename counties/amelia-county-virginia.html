Market Analysis

The median home value in Amelia County is 4.5% below the Virginia state average and 24.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,164/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Amelia County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.5%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
