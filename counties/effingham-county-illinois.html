Market Analysis

The median home value in Effingham County is 23.8% above the Illinois state average and 16.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $733/month is 15.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Effingham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
