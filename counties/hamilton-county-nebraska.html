Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 56.2% above the Nebraska state average and 12.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $776/month is 0.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.6, Hamilton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.0%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
