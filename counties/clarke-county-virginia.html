Market Analysis

The median home value in Clarke County is 93.8% above the Virginia state average and 154.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,360/month is 19.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.0, Clarke County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clarke County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.1%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
