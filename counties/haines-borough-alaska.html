Market Analysis

The median home value in Haines Borough is 3.5% above the Alaska state average and 28.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,087/month is 12.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Haines Borough has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 64.7% in Alaska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
