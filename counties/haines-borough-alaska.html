Market Analysis

The median home value in Haines Borough is 18.6% above the Alaska state average and 50.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $914/month is 25.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.1, Haines Borough is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Haines Borough has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 64.3% in Alaska and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
