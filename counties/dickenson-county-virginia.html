Market Analysis

The median home value in Dickenson County is 65.3% below the Virginia state average and 54.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $738/month is 36.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.6, buying a home in Dickenson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Dickenson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
