Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 8.7% below the Kansas state average and 43.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $778/month is 1.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Hamilton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 74.0% in Kansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
