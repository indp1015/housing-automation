Market Analysis

The median home value in Floyd County is 30.8% above the Indiana state average and 10.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,036/month is 13.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Floyd County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.2%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
