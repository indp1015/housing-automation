Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 8.2% above the Rhode Island state average and 122.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,303/month is 4.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.0, Washington County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.8%, compared to 69.3% in Rhode Island and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
