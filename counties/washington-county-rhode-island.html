Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 7.6% above the Rhode Island state average and 124.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,327/month is 7.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.1, Washington County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.5%, compared to 69.4% in Rhode Island and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
