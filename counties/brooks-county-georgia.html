Market Analysis

The median home value in Brooks County is 29.7% below the Georgia state average and 38.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $809/month is 16.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Brooks County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Brooks County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.7%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
