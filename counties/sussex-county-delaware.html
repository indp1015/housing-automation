Market Analysis

The median home value in Sussex County is 8.9% above the Delaware state average and 67.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,221/month is 5.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sussex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 73.6% in Delaware and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
