Market Analysis

The median home value in Sussex County is 9.2% above the Delaware state average and 68.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,238/month is 8.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.8, Sussex County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sussex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 74.4% in Delaware and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
