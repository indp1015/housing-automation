Market Analysis

The median home value in Butler County is 17.4% below the Missouri state average and 36.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $780/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Butler County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Butler County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.6%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
