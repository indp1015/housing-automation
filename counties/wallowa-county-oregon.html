Market Analysis

The median home value in Wallowa County is 2.1% above the Oregon state average and 73.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $981/month is 17.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.5, Wallowa County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Wallowa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.5%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
