Market Analysis

The median home value in Stevens County is 19.6% below the Washington state average and 54.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $898/month is 28.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.5, Stevens County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Stevens County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.8%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
