📝 Local Market Analysis

Shelby County, Missouri, functions as a highly affordable buyer’s market, characterized by significant price-to-income disparities relative to national benchmarks. The median home value of $99,900 stands at 43.1% below the state average and 56.1% below the national mean, while the median household income of $51,594 remains 24.1% lower than the U.S. norm. Consequently, the price-to-income ratio is a remarkably low 1.94, compared to the national average of 3.20, and the rent burden sits at 14.5% of income, well under the 17.2% national standard. These metrics indicate that housing costs impose minimal financial strain on local residents, creating a strong foundation for ownership despite the county’s small population of 5,986. Recent data reveals a volatile yet upward trajectory in property values, driven by sharp year-over-year increases of 17.0% in 2022 and 15.9% in 2024. This momentum is corroborated by the FHFA House Price Index, which recorded a 50.31% county-level increase in 2022, vastly outpacing the 3.71% state growth. However, this price appreciation contrasts with modest rent growth, suggesting a divergence where equity gains are outpacing rental income potential. The low supply of new construction, with only three single-family permits authorized in 2025, likely contributes to this tightness, while positive net migration of 59 filers indicates sustained demand from individuals with average incomes near $60,000. The economic context remains constrained by a small labor base of 1,367 jobs and an average weekly wage of $936, which limits broader economic vitality despite strong housing momentum. With a 77.5% owner-occupancy rate, the market leans heavily toward existing homeowners rather than new entrants. The combination of extreme affordability and rapid price appreciation creates a unique dynamic where entry costs are low, but recent buyers may face rapid equity fluctuations. Potential participants should note that while the price-to-rent ratio of 13.3 remains favorable for buyers compared to the national 18.7, the high volatility in recent price changes suggests that stability is currently a weaker component of the market profile than its affordability or growth metrics.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Shelby County is 43.1% below the Missouri state average and 56.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $625/month is 20.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.3, buying a home in Shelby County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Shelby County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.5%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
