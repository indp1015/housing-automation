📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 38751, located in Sunflower County, Mississippi, exhibits a volatile yet ultimately upward price trajectory over the past five years. However, a significant correction occurred in 2022, with the index dropping to 123.88. This dip was followed by a sharp rebound in 2023, where the HPI reached 139.04. This pattern suggests that while the area experienced temporary price adjustments, the underlying value has recovered to new highs, surpassing pre-correction levels. A notable divergence exists between the local ZIP code and broader regional trends. This substantial outperformance signals that specific local factors are driving demand and price appreciation in this specific sub-market, independent of the slower growth observed in the surrounding county and state. The disparity highlights that ZIP 38751 is not merely following regional trends but is experiencing distinct, localized momentum. With an owner-occupancy rate of 54.9%, the area maintains a balanced mix of homeowners and renters, though the median gross rent of $694 per month remains relatively low. For prospective buyers, the combination of a high HPI relative to the 2000 baseline and strong recent growth suggests increasing competitive pressure.
