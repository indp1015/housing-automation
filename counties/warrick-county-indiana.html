Market Analysis

The median home value in Warrick County is 26.4% above the Indiana state average and 6.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,061/month is 16.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Warrick County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Warrick County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.4%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
