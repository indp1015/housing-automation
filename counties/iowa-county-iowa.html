Market Analysis

The median home value in Iowa County is 18.2% above the Iowa state average and 10.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $722/month is 11.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Iowa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
