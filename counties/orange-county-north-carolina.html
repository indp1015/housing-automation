Market Analysis

The median home value in Orange County is 95.3% above the North Carolina state average and 101.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,496/month is 53.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.6, Orange County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Orange County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.8%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
