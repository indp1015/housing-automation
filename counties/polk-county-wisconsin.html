Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 16.2% above the Wisconsin state average and 19.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $957/month is 4.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.0%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
