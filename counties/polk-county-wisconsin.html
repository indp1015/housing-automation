Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 10.7% above the Wisconsin state average and 14.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $958/month is 7.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.1%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
