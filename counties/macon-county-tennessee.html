Market Analysis

The median home value in Macon County is 1.0% above the Tennessee state average and 0.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $846/month is 5.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Macon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.6%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
