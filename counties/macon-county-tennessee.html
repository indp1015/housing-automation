Market Analysis

The median home value in Macon County is 4.8% above the Tennessee state average and 0.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $805/month is 6.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Macon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.8%, compared to 73.7% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
