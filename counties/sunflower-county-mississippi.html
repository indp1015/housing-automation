Market Analysis

The median home value in Sunflower County is 26.2% below the Mississippi state average and 56.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $694/month is 13.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.0, buying a home in Sunflower County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Sunflower County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.9%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
