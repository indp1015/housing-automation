Market Analysis

The median home value in Valley County is 28.3% below the Montana state average and 12.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $767/month is 8.9% below the state average.

Valley County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
