Market Analysis

The median home value in Charleston County is 148.5% above the South Carolina state average and 112.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,506/month is 59.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Charleston County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.5%, compared to 72.9% in South Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
