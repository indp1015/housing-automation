Market Analysis

The median home value in Lee County is 17.1% above the Georgia state average and 1.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,184/month is 22.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
