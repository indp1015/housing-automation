Market Analysis

The median home value in Berrien County is 33.5% below the Georgia state average and 42.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $782/month is 19.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.0, buying a home in Berrien County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
