📝 Local Market Analysis

Decatur County, Iowa, functions as a highly affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 1.93, significantly below the national average of 3.20. With a median home value of $113,900 and a median household income of $59,044, the county offers substantial financial leverage for homeowners. The rent burden stands at 12.4% of income, well under the national benchmark of 17.2%, while the price-to-rent ratio of 15.5 suggests that purchasing is more cost-effective than renting compared to broader national trends. This affordability profile is the primary driver of the high stability metrics associated with the region, as low entry barriers reduce the risk of severe price corrections. The market has experienced a pronounced upward trajectory, with home values rising from $76,300 in 2017 to $113,900 in 2024, representing a cumulative increase of approximately 49.3%. However, recent momentum is moderating; the year-over-year growth rate slowed from a peak of 12.9% in 2023 to 4.0% in 2024. The FHFA House Price Index further confirms this deceleration, showing a 1.44% annual increase in 2025, which underperforms the Iowa state average of 2.25%. This divergence indicates that while the county remains in an expansion phase, the velocity of price appreciation is cooling relative to the state. A notable pattern is the divergence between asset values and rental costs, as home values are rising faster than rents, reinforcing the buyer’s advantage. Economic fundamentals present a mixed picture that constrains long-term vitality. Total employment stands at only 1,770 jobs with an average weekly wage of $805, reflecting a limited local labor base. Housing supply is extremely tight, with only one new unit authorized in 2025, which may sustain price floors but limits market liquidity. Migration data shows a net gain of 214 filers, with inflows averaging slightly lower incomes ($57,635) than outflows ($58,402), suggesting a demographic shift toward lower-income residents. The current 7.40% mortgage rate increases monthly carrying costs, yet the low absolute home prices mitigate this impact. Consequently, the market is defined by exceptional affordability and slowing momentum, rather than robust economic vitality or high transaction volume.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Decatur County is 33.4% below the Iowa state average and 50.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $611/month is 26.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Decatur County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
