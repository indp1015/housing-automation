Market Analysis

The median home value in Bee County is 40.3% below the Texas state average and 51.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,065/month is 6.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 8.7, buying a home in Bee County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Bee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.8%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
