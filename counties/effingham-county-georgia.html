📝 Local Market Analysis

Effingham County, Georgia, operates as a balanced market with a slight tilt toward buyers, characterized by strong economic fundamentals and moderate affordability. The price-to-income ratio of 3.12 sits marginally below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden of 15.6% is lower than the national 17.2%. However, the price-to-rent ratio of 20.1 exceeds the national benchmark of 18.7, indicating that purchasing offers a more favorable long-term value proposition than renting. This structural balance is supported by a median household income of $88,438, which is 30.1% above the national average, providing residents with greater purchasing power relative to their local cost of living. The market’s trajectory reveals a significant acceleration in home values, which rose from $155,500 in 2017 to $276,000 in 2024. This period saw peak year-over-year growth of 22.5% in 2022, moderating to 12.5% by 2024. Recent FHFA HPI data confirms this cooling trend, showing a 2.66% year-over-year increase that outpaces the state average of 1.83%. This divergence between slowing price appreciation and robust income growth suggests a stabilizing market. Notably, home values have risen faster than rents, widening the price-to-rent gap and further incentivizing homeownership. The county’s vitality is underscored by net migration of +2,865 filers between 2021 and 2022, driven by an inflow of 26,243 individuals with an average income of $74,074. Economic context further supports this stability. Local employment stands at 10,871 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,082, while new housing supply remains constrained, with 212 single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits. This limited new supply, combined with a 7.40% national mortgage rate, creates friction for entry-level buyers but supports price resilience for existing owners. The composite assessment reflects high vitality driven by migration and income levels, offset by moderate stability and momentum scores due to the recent sharp price corrections. Affordability remains a key driver, as the county’s metrics align closely with national norms, suggesting that while entry costs have surged, the underlying economic capacity of the population remains strong enough to sustain current price levels without significant distress.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Effingham County is 39.4% above the Georgia state average and 21.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,147/month is 18.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Effingham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
