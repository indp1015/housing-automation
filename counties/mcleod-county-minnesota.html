Market Analysis

The median home value in McLeod County is 3.3% above the Minnesota state average and 8.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $941/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

McLeod County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
