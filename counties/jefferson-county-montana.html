Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 61.4% above the Montana state average and 96.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $925/month is 9.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 40.3, Jefferson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.2%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
