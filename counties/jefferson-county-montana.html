Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 62.2% above the Montana state average and 92.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $886/month is 7.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.4, Jefferson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.1%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
