Market Analysis

The median home value in Atchison County is 39.2% below the Missouri state average and 53.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $631/month is 19.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Atchison County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Atchison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.8%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
