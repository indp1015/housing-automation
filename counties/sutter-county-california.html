Market Analysis

The median home value in Sutter County is 30.0% below the California state average and 88.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,364/month is 15.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sutter County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.2%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
