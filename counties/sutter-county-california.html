Market Analysis

The median home value in Sutter County is 28.8% below the California state average and 90.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,439/month is 14.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Sutter County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sutter County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.3%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
