Market Analysis

The median home value in Christian County is 56.4% above the Missouri state average and 20.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,058/month is 35.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Christian County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.9%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
