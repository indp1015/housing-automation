Market Analysis

The median home value in Power County is 40.1% below the Idaho state average and 6.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $767/month is 20.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Power County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.7%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
