Market Analysis

The median home value in Park County is 68.8% above the Montana state average and 105.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $976/month is 15.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 39.9, Park County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Park County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.4%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
