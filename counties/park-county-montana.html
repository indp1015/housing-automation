Market Analysis

The median home value in Park County is 70.8% above the Montana state average and 103.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $927/month is 12.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.6, Park County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Park County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.6%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
