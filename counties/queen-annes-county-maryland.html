Market Analysis

The median home value in Queen Anne's County is 26.0% above the Maryland state average and 108.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,611/month is 13.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Queen Anne's County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
