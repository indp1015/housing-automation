Market Analysis

The median home value in Queen Anne's County is 25.0% above the Maryland state average and 103.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,619/month is 11.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Queen Anne's County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.9%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
