Market Analysis

The median home value in Santa Cruz County is 77.8% above the California state average and 379.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,172/month is 34.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 39.0, Santa Cruz County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Santa Cruz County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.0%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
