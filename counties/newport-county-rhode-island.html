Market Analysis

The median home value in Newport County is 28.9% above the Rhode Island state average and 164.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,652/month is 21.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.2, Newport County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Newport County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.4%, compared to 69.3% in Rhode Island and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
