Market Analysis

The median home value in Monroe County is 24.0% below the New York state average and 6.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,173/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Monroe County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Monroe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.7%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
