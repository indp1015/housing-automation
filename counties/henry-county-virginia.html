Market Analysis

The median home value in Henry County is 56.7% below the Virginia state average and 43.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $705/month is 38.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Henry County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Henry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
