Market Analysis

The median home value in Kent County is 46.9% above the Michigan state average and 27.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,243/month is 37.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Kent County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Kent County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.3%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
