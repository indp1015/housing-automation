Market Analysis

The median home value in Wichita County is 17.5% below the Texas state average and 32.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,006/month is 0.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.7, buying a home in Wichita County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wichita County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.4%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
