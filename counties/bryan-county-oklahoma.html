Market Analysis

The median home value in Bryan County is 15.1% above the Oklahoma state average and 22.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $914/month is 12.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Bryan County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Bryan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.6%, compared to 72.1% in Oklahoma and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
