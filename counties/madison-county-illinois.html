Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 13.1% above the Illinois state average and 24.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,024/month is 18.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Madison County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
