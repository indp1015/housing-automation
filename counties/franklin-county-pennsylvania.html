Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 17.8% above the Pennsylvania state average and 9.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,093/month is 10.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Franklin County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.5%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
