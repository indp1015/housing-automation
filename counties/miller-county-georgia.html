Market Analysis

The median home value in Miller County is 37.4% below the Georgia state average and 45.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $810/month is 16.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Miller County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Miller County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.5%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
