📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP code 29431 exhibits a distinct price trajectory characterized by rapid appreciation followed by recent stabilization. The FHFA Home Price Index (HPI) for the area surged from 213.97 in 2021 to a peak of 306.23 in 2023, reflecting the broader national housing boom. However, the index corrected to 295.1 in 2024 before settling at 296.68 in 2025. This pattern indicates a market that has cooled significantly after a period of extreme volatility, with current price levels remaining well above pre-2021 baselines despite the recent plateau. Current year-over-year performance reveals a notable divergence from broader regional trends. While the state of South Carolina reports a 2.32% increase and Berkeley County shows a 2.04% rise, ZIP 29431 has grown by only 0.54%. This underperformance relative to the county and state averages suggests that the local market has reached a maturity phase or is experiencing specific supply-demand constraints that limit further rapid escalation. Unlike neighboring ZIP 29410, which is growing at 5.76%, 29431 is moving in lockstep with its immediate neighbor 29483 (3.11%), though slightly slower, indicating a localized stabilization rather than a broader regional downturn. The sub-market character of 29431 aligns with the upper-middle segment of Berkeley County. With a county median home value of $310,300 and a median household income of $84,358, the area supports a strong owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%. The HPI level of 296.7 suggests that home values in this specific ZIP have outpaced the county median, positioning it as a premium sub-market rather than an entry-level or renter-heavy zone. For prospective buyers, the practical takeaway is that 29431 offers price stability in a high-income area. The 7.40% national mortgage rate, combined with the 0.54% local appreciation, creates a favorable environment for equity preservation without the risk of rapid price inflation seen in other parts of the county.
