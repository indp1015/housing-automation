Market Analysis

The median home value in Charlotte County is 15.7% above the Florida state average and 44.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,464/month is 12.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Charlotte County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Charlotte County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.8%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
