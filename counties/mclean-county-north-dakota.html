Market Analysis

The median home value in McLean County is 38.3% above the North Dakota state average and 10.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $768/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.3, McLean County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

McLean County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.4%, compared to 74.1% in North Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
