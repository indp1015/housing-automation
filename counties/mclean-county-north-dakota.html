Market Analysis

The median home value in McLean County is 29.2% above the North Dakota state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $784/month is 1.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

McLean County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.2%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
