Market Analysis

The median home value in New York County is 315.1% above the New York state average and 424.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,132/month is 88.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 43.3, New York County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

New York County has an owner-occupancy rate of 24.7%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
