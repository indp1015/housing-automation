📝 Local Market Notes

Telfair County, Georgia, operates as a distinctively affordable market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.22, significantly below the national average of 3.20. The median home value of $113,300 sits 50.2% below the national mean and 42.8% below the Georgia state average, establishing a clear buyer’s advantage in terms of entry cost. This affordability is reinforced by a rent burden of 16.8%, which is marginally lower than the national benchmark of 17.2%. However, the market structure leans toward a balanced dynamic rather than a pure buyer’s market, as evidenced by the 64.9% owner-occupancy rate and a price-to-rent ratio of 13.3. This ratio, well below the national average of 18.7, indicates that purchasing remains a mathematically favorable alternative to renting for long-term residents, yet the low absolute price points limit significant equity accumulation compared to higher-cost regions. The multi-year trend reveals a dramatic acceleration in housing values, with median prices surging from $55,600 in 2017 to $113,300 in 2024. This period included a peak year-over-year increase of 30.2% in 2022, followed by a 20.2% rise in 2023, before moderating to 4.7% in 2024. This rapid appreciation contrasts sharply with the relatively stable median gross rent of $712, creating a notable divergence where asset values are rising faster than rental costs. The local economic foundation remains modest, supported by 1,322 jobs and an average weekly wage of $845, which translates to an annualized income well below the national average. Despite this, net migration of +237 filers between 2021 and 2022 suggests sustained demographic interest, with inflow incomes averaging $44,111. For potential buyers, the primary consideration is the recent momentum in price appreciation relative to stagnant local wage growth. The 7.40% national mortgage rate further impacts borrowing costs, making the low absolute price of $113,300 a mitigating factor for monthly payments. The data indicates that while the market is currently stabilizing after a period of exponential growth, the underlying affordability metrics remain robust. Renters face a low burden, but the widening gap between rising home values and flat rents may further incentivize the transition to homeownership among those with stable income. The combination of low price-to-income ratios and positive net migration suggests a market that is accessible but experiencing structural shifts in demand, driven by external factors rather than local industrial expansion.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Telfair County is 42.8% below the Georgia state average and 50.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $712/month is 26.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.3, buying a home in Telfair County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Telfair County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.9%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
