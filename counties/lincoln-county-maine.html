Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 27.6% above the Maine state average and 46.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,055/month is 3.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.3, Lincoln County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.5%, compared to 76.6% in Maine and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
