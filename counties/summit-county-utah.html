Market Analysis

The median home value in Summit County is 164.9% above the Utah state average and 372.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,969/month is 79.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 42.3, Summit County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Summit County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
