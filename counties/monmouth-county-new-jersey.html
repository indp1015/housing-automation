Market Analysis

The median home value in Monmouth County is 40.9% above the New Jersey state average and 166.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,827/month is 11.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.6, Monmouth County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Monmouth County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.6%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
