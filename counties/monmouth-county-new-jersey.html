Market Analysis

The median home value in Monmouth County is 40.6% above the New Jersey state average and 167.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,771/month is 12.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.7, Monmouth County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Monmouth County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.4%, compared to 68.0% in New Jersey and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
