Market Analysis

The median home value in Hawaii County is 35.0% below the Hawaii state average and 128.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,510/month is 18.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.7, Hawaii County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Hawaii County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.0%, compared to 67.0% in Hawaii and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
