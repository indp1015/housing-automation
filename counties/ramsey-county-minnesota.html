📝 Local Market Analysis

Ramsey County, Minnesota, functions as a distinctly buyer’s market, characterized by significant affordability constraints despite robust economic fundamentals. The price-to-income ratio stands at 4.00, substantially exceeding the national average of 3.20, while the price-to-rent ratio of 20.5 surpasses the national benchmark of 18.7. These metrics indicate that home equity accumulation is increasingly difficult relative to local earnings, pushing the affordability dimension of the market into a stressed category. Consequently, the overall market assessment reflects low vitality and stability, driven primarily by these high entry barriers rather than a lack of demand. Multi-year data reveals a sharp acceleration in home values, with year-over-year growth peaking at 13.5% in 2022 before moderating to 7.1% in 2024. This trajectory contrasts with the recent FHFA HPI index, which shows a slower 2.92% year-over-year increase in 2025, slightly outpacing the state’s 2.42% growth. This divergence suggests that while long-term appreciation has been strong, immediate transactional momentum is cooling. Notably, home values are rising faster than rents, creating a widening gap that pressures potential first-time buyers who may find renting more financially accessible in the short term, even though the rent burden of 19.6% remains above the national average of 17.2%. The regional context highlights Ramsey County’s premium status, with median home values 37.0% above the state average and 43.4% above the national mean. This premium is supported by a median household income of $81,568, which is 20.0% higher than the national figure. Local employment data shows 274,775 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,541, providing a stable economic foundation. However, housing supply remains constrained, with only 708 new units authorized in 2025, split between 273 single-family and 435 multi-family homes. Positive net migration of 2,817 filers further tightens inventory. Against a national 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40%, the high price points and limited new construction create a challenging environment for buyers, while the strong income base continues to support rental demand, keeping the market resilient despite affordability stress.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Ramsey County is 37.0% above the Minnesota state average and 43.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,329/month is 41.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Ramsey County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.6%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
