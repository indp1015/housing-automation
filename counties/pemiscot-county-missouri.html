Market Analysis

The median home value in Pemiscot County is 44.8% below the Missouri state average and 57.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $695/month is 11.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.6, buying a home in Pemiscot County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Pemiscot County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.6%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
