Market Analysis

The median home value in Christian County is 24.5% below the Illinois state average and 49.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $785/month is 9.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, buying a home in Christian County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Christian County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.2%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
