Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 25.0% below the Mississippi state average and 55.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $725/month is 9.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.7, buying a home in Franklin County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.7%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
