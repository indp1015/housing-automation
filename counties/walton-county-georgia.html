Market Analysis

The median home value in Walton County is 66.2% above the Georgia state average and 42.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,231/month is 32.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Walton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.2%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
