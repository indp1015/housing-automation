Market Analysis

The median home value in Chester County is 129.0% above the Pennsylvania state average and 113.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,763/month is 78.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Chester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.1%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
