Market Analysis

The median home value in Platte County is 21.1% below the Wyoming state average and 17.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $745/month is 20.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.8, Platte County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Platte County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.5%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
