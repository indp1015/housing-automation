Market Analysis

The median home value in Linn County is 3.5% below the Oregon state average and 62.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,273/month is 12.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Linn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.0%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
