Market Analysis

The median home value in Rappahannock County is 76.3% above the Virginia state average and 131.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,384/month is 21.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.5, Rappahannock County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Rappahannock County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.9%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
