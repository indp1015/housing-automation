Market Analysis

The median home value in Rappahannock County is 71.2% above the Virginia state average and 122.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,290/month is 10.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.7, Rappahannock County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Rappahannock County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.4%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
