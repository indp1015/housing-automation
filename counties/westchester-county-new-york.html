Market Analysis

The median home value in Westchester County is 135.8% above the New York state average and 191.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,951/month is 67.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.3, Westchester County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Westchester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.8%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
