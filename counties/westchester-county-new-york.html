Market Analysis

The median home value in Westchester County is 139.0% above the New York state average and 201.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,876/month is 65.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.4, Westchester County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Westchester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.2%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
