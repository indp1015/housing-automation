Market Analysis

The median home value in Waldo County is 1.2% below the Maine state average and 13.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,021/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Waldo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.9%, compared to 76.6% in Maine and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
