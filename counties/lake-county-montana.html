Market Analysis

The median home value in Lake County is 46.8% above the Montana state average and 74.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $884/month is 7.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 34.8, Lake County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.8%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
