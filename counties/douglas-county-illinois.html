📝 Local Market Notes

Douglas County, Illinois, functions as a distinctively affordable market that currently favors buyers, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 1.95, significantly below the national average of 3.20. The median home value of $150,400 sits 33.9% below the national mean, while the median household income of $77,320 exceeds the national benchmark by 13.7%. This favorable income-to-price dynamic is further supported by a rent burden of 13.2%, which is lower than the national average of 17.2%. Consequently, the price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, compared to the national 18.7, suggests that ownership costs are relatively low, reinforcing the market’s classification as buyer-friendly rather than a renter’s market. The multi-year trend reveals a period of accelerated appreciation that has recently stabilized. Home values surged 14.4% year-over-year in 2022, moderating to 5.5% in 2024, while the FHFA House Price Index indicates a recent 7.16% annual increase, outperforming the state average of 5.54% by 1.62 percentage points. This momentum is driven by a net inflow of 278 tax filers between 2021 and 2022, with incoming residents earning an average of $73,326. However, housing supply remains constrained, with only 28 new units authorized in 2025, all single-family, and zero multi-family construction. Despite the local economic base comprising 6,598 jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,166, the limited new inventory supports continued price resilience. A notable pattern emerges in the divergence between asset values and rental costs. While home values have risen sharply, the median gross rent of $848 remains modest, indicating that rental demand has not kept pace with the appreciation of owned properties. This disparity highlights a market where equity accumulation is the primary driver of value rather than speculative rental yields. For potential buyers, the combination of high affordability metrics and strong recent price momentum suggests a competitive environment for the limited available inventory. Conversely, renters benefit from low relative costs, though the lack of new multi-family development implies limited future supply expansion. The current 7.40% national mortgage rate adds a fixed cost layer that must be weighed against the significant price discount relative to national averages. Overall, Douglas County presents a stable, low-cost entry point for homeownership, underpinned by positive migration and outperforming state-level price trends, despite the broader macroeconomic headwinds of elevated interest rates.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 1.7% below the Illinois state average and 33.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $848/month is 2.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Douglas County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
