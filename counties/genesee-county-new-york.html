📝 Local Market Analysis

Genesee County, New York, operates as a distinctively affordable, buyer-leaning market characterized by strong value metrics relative to national benchmarks. The median home value of $170,500 sits 25.1% below the national average and 39.4% below the New York state mean, while the median household income of $73,314 exceeds the national figure by 7.8%. This dynamic produces a price-to-income ratio of 2.33, significantly lower than the national average of 3.20, and a rent burden of 15.2%, which is 2.0 percentage points below the national norm of 17.2%. These figures indicate that housing costs remain manageable for local earners, positioning the county as a high-affordability outlier within a typically expensive state. The market exhibits robust momentum, with home values rising 8.0% year-over-year in 2024 and 3.80% in the latest FHFA HPI data, slightly outpacing the state’s 3.55% growth. A notable divergence exists between asset appreciation and rental costs; while home values have compounded from $111,900 in 2017 to $170,500 in 2024, the median gross rent remains modest at $929. This results in a price-to-rent ratio of 15.3, well below the national average of 18.7, suggesting that homeownership offers superior long-term value compared to renting. The rapid appreciation phase, peaking at 14.2% in 2022, has moderated but remains positive, supported by a net migration inflow of 700 filers and an average weekly wage of $1,097. However, housing supply is constrained, with only 102 new units authorized in 2025, predominantly single-family, which may limit future inventory growth. Economic vitality remains a relative weakness, reflected in the low employment base of 18,052 jobs and limited multi-family construction. The current 7.40% mortgage rate increases monthly payment burdens, potentially tempering demand despite low entry prices. The overall market profile is driven by high affordability and steady price momentum, offsetting lower economic vitality scores. For potential buyers, the low price-to-income ratio and sub-national rent burden present a favorable entry point, particularly given the historical trend of home values rising faster than rents. Renter’s equity potential is limited by the low rent burden, but the significant discount to state averages makes the county an attractive option for income-driven buyers seeking leverage in a high-cost state. The data suggests a stable, value-oriented environment where affordability drives demand rather than speculative investment.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Genesee County is 39.4% below the New York state average and 25.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $929/month is 20.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Genesee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Genesee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.8%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
