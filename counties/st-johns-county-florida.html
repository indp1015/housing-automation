Market Analysis

The median home value in St. Johns County is 77.9% above the Florida state average and 116.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,775/month is 44.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

St. Johns County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.1%, compared to 74.0% in Florida and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
