Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 36.5% below the Illinois state average and 57.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $723/month is 16.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.2, buying a home in Cass County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.9%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
