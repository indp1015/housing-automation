Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 1.8% above the Illinois state average and 31.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $818/month is 5.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Washington County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.1%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
