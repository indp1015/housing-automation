📝 Local Market Analysis

Linn County, Iowa, presents a balanced market leaning slightly toward buyers, characterized by strong affordability relative to national standards. The price-to-income ratio of 2.76 is significantly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden stands at 14.6% of income, well under the 17.2% national benchmark. Although the price-to-rent ratio of 18.9 is nearly identical to the national average of 18.7, the substantial gap between home value and income levels indicates that purchasing remains more accessible than renting for many residents. This affordability is reinforced by a median household income of $77,649, which exceeds the national average by 14.2%, providing a solid economic foundation for homeownership despite rising asset values. The market has experienced a distinct acceleration in home values, with year-over-year growth spiking to 13.1% in 2022 before moderating to 4.8% in 2024. This trajectory contrasts with the more stable rent environment, where the median gross rent remains at $945 per month. Recent FHFA data confirms this deceleration, showing a 3.02% year-over-year increase in the Home Price Index, which outperforms the state’s 2.25% growth rate by 0.77 percentage points. This divergence suggests that while home equity gains remain robust, the pace of appreciation is normalizing. The county’s home values are 25.3% higher than the state average, positioning Linn County as a premium market within Iowa, yet it remains 5.9% below the national median, indicating it has not yet reached the price levels of major metropolitan areas. Local economic indicators support continued stability in housing demand. With 111,917 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,381, the labor market provides consistent purchasing power. Housing supply is active, with 941 new units authorized in 2025, including 418 single-family homes, helping to mitigate potential scarcity. Positive net migration of 3,747 filers further sustains demand, although the current 7.40% mortgage rate poses a headwind for new buyers. The combination of moderate price growth, strong local employment, and favorable affordability metrics suggests a resilient market where buyers can still secure value, while renters face moderate financial obligations. The sustained outperformance of home prices compared to state trends highlights Linn County’s relative strength, driven by a combination of demographic inflow and economic stability.

The median home value in Linn County is 25.3% above the Iowa state average and 5.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $945/month is 13.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Linn County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Linn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.5%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
