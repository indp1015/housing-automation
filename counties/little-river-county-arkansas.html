Market Analysis

The median home value in Little River County is 19.8% below the Arkansas state average and 50.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $697/month is 9.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Little River County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Little River County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.1%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
