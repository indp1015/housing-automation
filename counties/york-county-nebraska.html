Market Analysis

The median home value in York County is 15.0% above the Nebraska state average and 16.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $877/month is 10.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in York County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

York County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
