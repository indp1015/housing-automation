Market Analysis

The median home value in White County is 40.9% above the Georgia state average and 22.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,032/month is 6.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

White County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
