Market Analysis

The median home value in Pope County is 6.0% below the Illinois state average and 36.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $449/month is 48.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.7, Pope County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Pope County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.3%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
