Market Analysis

The median home value in Pope County is 13.1% above the Illinois state average and 21.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $438/month is 47.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.4, Pope County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Pope County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.1%, compared to 75.7% in Illinois and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
