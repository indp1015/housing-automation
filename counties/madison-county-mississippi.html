Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 124.4% above the Mississippi state average and 35.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,097/month is 40.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.4%, compared to 71.7% in Mississippi and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
