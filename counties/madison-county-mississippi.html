Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 129.1% above the Mississippi state average and 36.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,186/month is 47.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
