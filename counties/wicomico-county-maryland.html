📝 Local Market Analysis

Wicomico County, Maryland, operates as a balanced market leaning slightly toward buyers, characterized by robust price appreciation that outpaces state trends. The price-to-rent ratio of 16.0 sits below the national average of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing remains relatively more economical than long-term renting compared to broader national standards. However, this affordability is tempered by affordability metrics that lag behind national norms. The price-to-income ratio stands at 3.34, slightly above the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden consumes 20.9% of household income, exceeding the national benchmark of 17.2%. These figures indicate that while entry barriers are manageable relative to rent, the cost of ownership and rental living pressures are intensifying for local households. The market’s trajectory reveals a significant acceleration in home values, with the year-over-year increase moderating from a peak of 18.5% in 2022 to 6.6% in 2024. Current FHFA data confirms this momentum, showing a 5.70% year-over-year gain that significantly outperforms the state’s 0.95% growth rate. This divergence highlights a localized strength, likely driven by positive net migration of 2,043 filers and a median household income of $76,210, which is 12.1% above the national average. The local economy supports this demand, with 37,838 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,134. Despite this economic vitality, housing supply remains constrained, with only 170 new units authorized in 2025, including 129 single-family homes. This limited supply, coupled with a national 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40%, creates a challenging environment for new entrants, as high borrowing costs offset the relative price advantages seen in the price-to-rent comparison. Regional context further complicates the affordability picture. While home values are 11.9% above the national average, they remain 31.2% below the state average, positioning Wicomico as a more accessible option within Maryland. The primary drivers of market stress are affordability and stability, with scores of 25.0 and 19.2 respectively, reflecting the strain of rising prices against static income growth. Conversely, momentum is strong at 77.6, driven by consistent price gains and outperformance relative to the state. Potential buyers must navigate a market where strong demand and limited supply keep prices elevated, while renters face a higher income burden than the national norm. The data suggests a market in transition, where historical affordability is eroding under the pressure of sustained price appreciation and high interest rates.

The median home value in Wicomico County is 31.2% below the Maryland state average and 11.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,327/month is 9.0% below the state average.
