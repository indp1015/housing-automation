Market Analysis

The median home value in Susquehanna County is 2.0% above the Pennsylvania state average and 5.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $933/month is 5.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Susquehanna County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Susquehanna County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
