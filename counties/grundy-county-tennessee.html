Market Analysis

The median home value in Grundy County is 31.1% below the Tennessee state average and 32.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $636/month is 28.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grundy County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.7%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
