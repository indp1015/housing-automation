Market Analysis

The median home value in Carroll County is 17.3% above the Maryland state average and 90.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,427/month is 2.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.3, Carroll County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Carroll County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.3%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
