📝 Local Market Analysis

Montgomery County, Arkansas, presents a balanced market leaning toward affordability, characterized by a median home value of $151,900 and a median household income of $48,906. The price-to-income ratio stands at 3.11, slightly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is 15.6%, well under the national benchmark of 17.2%. These metrics indicate that housing costs remain manageable relative to local earnings, supporting a stable ownership rate of 80.6%. The market is not strictly a buyer’s or renter’s domain but rather a balanced environment where both sides benefit from low entry barriers compared to national norms. The multi-year trend reveals significant momentum, with home values increasing from $96,900 in 2017 to $151,900 in 2024, representing a cumulative gain of approximately 56.8%. This growth has been consistent, with year-over-year increases ranging from 4.7% to 11.7% in recent years, notably accelerating to 11.7% in 2024. This upward trajectory contrasts with the relatively static median gross rent of $636, suggesting a divergence where asset values are appreciating faster than rental costs. The price-to-rent ratio of 19.9 exceeds the national average of 18.7, indicating that purchasing may become increasingly attractive compared to renting as the gap widens. This pattern reflects strong local demand outpacing rental market adjustments, a dynamic often seen in regions experiencing population inflows. Regional context further clarifies this dynamic. While home values are 33.3% below the national average, they are 7.4% above the Arkansas state average, positioning the county as a premium market within the state. Local economic indicators support this resilience, with net migration adding 348 filers between 2021 and 2022 and an average weekly wage of $646. However, the small employment base of 809 jobs and 153 establishments suggests limited economic diversification, which may constrain long-term vitality. The current 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.32% adds financing pressure, yet the low absolute price point mitigates monthly payment burdens. Consequently, the market exhibits high stability and momentum, driven by affordability and consistent price appreciation, while vitality remains moderate due to the small economic footprint. Potential buyers benefit from below-national price levels and strong equity growth, whereas renters face a stable but slowly appreciating cost structure.

The median home value in Montgomery County is 7.4% above the Arkansas state average and 33.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $636/month is 17.8% below the state average.
