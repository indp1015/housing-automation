Market Analysis

The median home value in Collin County is 155.3% above the Texas state average and 108.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,859/month is 86.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Collin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
