Market Analysis

The median home value in Texas County is 9.4% above the Oklahoma state average and 26.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $939/month is 15.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.8, buying a home in Texas County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Texas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.4%, compared to 72.1% in Oklahoma and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
