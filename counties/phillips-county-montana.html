Market Analysis

The median home value in Phillips County is 30.4% below the Montana state average and 17.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $623/month is 24.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Phillips County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.8%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
