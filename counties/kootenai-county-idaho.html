Market Analysis

The median home value in Kootenai County is 49.8% above the Idaho state average and 121.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,330/month is 44.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.3, Kootenai County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Kootenai County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.2%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
