Market Analysis

The median home value in Juneau City and Borough is 60.9% above the Alaska state average and 104.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,462/month is 19.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Juneau City and Borough has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.4%, compared to 64.3% in Alaska and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
