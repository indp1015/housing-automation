Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 11.4% above the Minnesota state average and 16.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,019/month is 8.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.9%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
