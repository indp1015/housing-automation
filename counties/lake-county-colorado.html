Market Analysis

The median home value in Lake County is 0.8% above the Colorado state average and 89.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,599/month is 27.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.0%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
