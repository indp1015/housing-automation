📝 Local Market Notes

The steepest move occurred in 2021–2022, when the index rose 25.0% in a single year; appreciation then decelerated to 5.0% in 2023, 5.4% in 2024, and 5.4% in 2025. That pattern indicates a market that repriced sharply during the pandemic-era boom and has since settled into a steadier, mid-single-digit trajectory. The ZIP's +5.44% year-over-year change trails the parent county's +6.53% but outpaces Virginia's +3.03%, suggesting demand in this ZIP remains firmer than statewide norms while slightly lagging its own county's momentum. The sub-market character is decidedly entry-level and owner-occupied rather than luxury or renter-heavy. With median household income at $49,883, price points here remain accessible relative to coastal Virginia markets, and the HPI level of 242.8 reflects sustained but moderate appreciation on a low base.
