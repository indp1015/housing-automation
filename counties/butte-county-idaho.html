Market Analysis

The median home value in Butte County is 37.2% below the Idaho state average and 7.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $525/month is 42.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.1, Butte County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Butte County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
