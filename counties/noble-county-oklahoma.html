Market Analysis

The median home value in Noble County is 3.7% below the Oklahoma state average and 35.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $826/month is 1.5% below the state average.

Noble County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.7%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
