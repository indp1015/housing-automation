Market Analysis

The median home value in Guilford County is 10.4% above the North Carolina state average and 14.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,163/month is 19.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Guilford County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Guilford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.9%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
