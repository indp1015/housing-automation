Market Analysis

The median home value in Mobile County is 18.0% above the Alabama state average and 16.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,021/month is 27.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Mobile County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Mobile County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.9%, compared to 72.9% in Alabama and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
