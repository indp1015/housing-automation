Market Analysis

The median home value in Hopkins County is 11.2% below the Kentucky state average and 37.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $837/month is 5.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Hopkins County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hopkins County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.7%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
