Market Analysis

The median home value in Middlesex County is 7.6% above the New Jersey state average and 103.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,871/month is 14.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Middlesex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.6%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
