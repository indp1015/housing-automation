Market Analysis

The median home value in Hall County is 53.8% below the Texas state average and 62.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $529/month is 46.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Hall County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hall County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.6%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
