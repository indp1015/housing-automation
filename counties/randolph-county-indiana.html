Market Analysis

The median home value in Randolph County is 40.9% below the Indiana state average and 50.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $723/month is 20.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.1, buying a home in Randolph County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Randolph County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.3%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
