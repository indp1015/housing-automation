Market Analysis

The median home value in Currituck County is 64.0% above the North Carolina state average and 66.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,126/month is 20.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Currituck County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Currituck County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.5%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
