Market Analysis

The median home value in Berkshire County is 51.7% below the Massachusetts state average and 34.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,042/month is 33.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Berkshire County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.0%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
