Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 19.8% below the Arkansas state average and 50.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $888/month is 14.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.6, buying a home in Jefferson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.8%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
