Market Analysis

The median home value in Camden County is 66.7% above the Missouri state average and 28.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $838/month is 9.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.9, Camden County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Camden County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.1%, compared to 73.6% in Missouri and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
