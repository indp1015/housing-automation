Market Analysis

The median home value in Northampton County is 6.9% above the Virginia state average and 38.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $829/month is 29.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.8, Northampton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Northampton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.1%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
