Market Analysis

The median home value in Northampton County is about equal to the Virginia state average and 31.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $780/month is 31.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.8, Northampton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Northampton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.2%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
