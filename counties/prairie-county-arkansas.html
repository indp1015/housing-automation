Market Analysis

The median home value in Prairie County is 31.9% below the Arkansas state average and 57.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $625/month is 19.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Prairie County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Prairie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.8%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
