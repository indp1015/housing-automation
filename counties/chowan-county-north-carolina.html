Market Analysis

The median home value in Chowan County is 4.6% below the North Carolina state average and 3.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $766/month is 18.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Chowan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.7%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
