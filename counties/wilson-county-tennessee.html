Market Analysis

The median home value in Wilson County is 96.9% above the Tennessee state average and 87.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,370/month is 59.3% above the state average.

Wilson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.8%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
