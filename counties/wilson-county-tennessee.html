Market Analysis

The median home value in Wilson County is 91.6% above the Tennessee state average and 88.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,486/month is 66.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Wilson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
