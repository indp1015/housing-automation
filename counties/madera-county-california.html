Market Analysis

The median home value in Madera County is 35.6% below the California state average and 73.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,307/month is 19.2% below the state average.

Madera County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.5%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
