Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is about equal to the Alabama state average and 29.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $655/month is 20.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.8%, compared to 73.2% in Alabama and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
