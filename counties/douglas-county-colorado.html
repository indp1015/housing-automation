Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 69.3% above the Colorado state average and 218.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,095/month is 67.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.8, Douglas County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.8%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
