Market Analysis

The median home value in Cumberland County is 73.1% above the Maine state average and 98.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,589/month is 55.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cumberland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 76.6% in Maine and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
