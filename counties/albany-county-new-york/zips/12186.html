The housing market in ZIP code 12186 exhibits a volatile but ultimately appreciating trajectory over the last five years. This decline reversed sharply in 2024, with the index rising to 270.54, and further increasing to 281.95 in 2025. This pattern indicates a market that experienced rapid inflationary pressure, a subsequent cooling period, and a recent recovery that has now surpassed pre-correction levels.
