Market Analysis

The median home value in Sherburne County is 51.6% above the Minnesota state average and 58.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,127/month is 20.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.7, Sherburne County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sherburne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.2%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
