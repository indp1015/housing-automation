Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 18.3% above the North Carolina state average and 22.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $804/month is 17.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.9, Clay County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
