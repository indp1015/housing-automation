Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 22.9% above the North Carolina state average and 24.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $791/month is 15.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.7, Clay County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.8%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
