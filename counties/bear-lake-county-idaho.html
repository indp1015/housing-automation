Market Analysis

The median home value in Bear Lake County is 22.6% below the Idaho state average and 20.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $632/month is 34.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 36.3, Bear Lake County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bear Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 89.5%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
