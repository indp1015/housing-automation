Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 51.8% above the Colorado state average and 185.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,792/month is 43.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.1, Jefferson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.6%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
