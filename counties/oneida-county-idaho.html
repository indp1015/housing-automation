Market Analysis

The median home value in Oneida County is 23.4% below the Idaho state average and 19.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $859/month is 11.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Oneida County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Oneida County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.3%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
