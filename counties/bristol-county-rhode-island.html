Market Analysis

The median home value in Bristol County is 7.8% above the Rhode Island state average and 120.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,348/month is 1.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.9, Bristol County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bristol County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.3%, compared to 69.2% in Rhode Island and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
