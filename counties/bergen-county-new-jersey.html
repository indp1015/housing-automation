Market Analysis

The median home value in Bergen County is 44.9% above the New Jersey state average and 173.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,914/month is 17.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.1, Bergen County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bergen County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.3%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
