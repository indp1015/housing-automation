Market Analysis

The median home value in Union County is 27.1% below the Oregon state average and 22.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $982/month is 13.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Union County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.6%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
