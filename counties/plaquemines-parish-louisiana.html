📝 Local Market Analysis

Plaquemines Parish functions as a distinctively balanced market, characterized by a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, significantly below the national average of 18.7. This metric indicates that purchasing is relatively more attractive than renting, despite the area’s premium valuation. The median home value of $282,900 sits 69.0% above the state average and 24.2% above the national mean, reflecting a high-cost environment driven by localized wealth rather than broad regional trends. With an owner-occupancy rate of 75.8%, the market exhibits strong residential stability, though the recent FHFA HPI decline of 1.84% signals a temporary cooling phase that lags behind the state’s 1.59% growth. This divergence suggests that while the broader Louisiana market continues to appreciate, Plaquemines is undergoing a period of price consolidation after a surge. Affordability remains the primary constraint, with a price-to-income ratio of 3.28 slightly exceeding the national benchmark of 3.20. Although the median household income of $86,315 is 26.9% higher than the national average, the rapid appreciation of home values has outpaced income growth, resulting in a low affordability score of 24.3. Renters face a burden of 21.9% of income, well above the national 17.2% average, further limiting rental flexibility. The economic foundation is modest, with only 11,487 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,863, which supports the high income levels only through specialized sectors rather than broad-based employment expansion. Housing supply remains tight, with just 47 new units authorized in 2025, insufficient to offset the 553 net positive migration recorded between 2021 and 2022. The market’s vitality score of 67.4 reflects sustained demand driven by in-migrants with average incomes near $79,000, yet this demand is currently checked by high mortgage rates of 7.40%. The multi-year trend reveals a shift from aggressive appreciation, peaking at 19.4% in 2022, to a stabilized 2.6% growth in 2024. This moderation, combined with the recent HPI contraction, indicates a market transitioning from speculative momentum to fundamental stability. For stakeholders, the data suggests that while entry prices are high relative to local wages, the long-term value retention is supported by limited supply and steady demographic inflows. The overall assessment of 42.6 highlights a market where high vitality and stability are offset by significant affordability pressures and slowing momentum.
