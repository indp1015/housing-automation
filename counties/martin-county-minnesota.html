Market Analysis

The median home value in Martin County is 31.4% below the Minnesota state average and 28.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $761/month is 18.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Martin County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
