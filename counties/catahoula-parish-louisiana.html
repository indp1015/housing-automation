📝 Local Market Analysis

Catahoula Parish, Louisiana, functions as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $101,800, which sits 55.3% below the national average. The price-to-income ratio of 2.02 is significantly lower than the national benchmark of 3.20, indicating that local household incomes of $50,275 are proportionally strong relative to housing costs. This affordability is reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 10.3, well below the national average of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing offers a more favorable long-term financial position than renting. However, the market is not without constraints. The rent burden stands at 19.6% of income, slightly exceeding the national average of 17.2%, implying that while ownership is accessible, rental units may place a heavier relative strain on lower-income households. The owner-occupancy rate of 78.0% reflects a community that leans heavily toward homeownership, consistent with the low entry price point. Trend analysis reveals a volatile but ultimately appreciating trajectory. After a dip to $87,100 in 2021 and a further decline to $88,000 in 2023, the median home value surged 15.7% to $101,800 in 2024. This recent spike contrasts with the FHFA HPI data from 2021, which showed only a 1.08% year-over-year increase, underperforming the state average by 0.51 percentage points. The divergence between the modest historical mortgage-based index and the sharp recent valuation jump suggests that the 2024 increase may reflect broader valuation adjustments or specific transaction outliers rather than sustained quarterly momentum. Meanwhile, rent growth has outpaced home value appreciation in recent periods, signaling a shifting dynamic where rental demand remains robust despite the availability of low-cost ownership. The local economic context remains fragile, with total employment limited to 1,752 jobs and an average weekly wage of $774. This low vitality is the primary driver of the market’s challenging overall assessment, as the economic base lacks the depth to support sustained housing demand. Migration data shows a net inflow of 129 filers, with incoming residents earning an average of $72,849, which is higher than the outgoing average of $74,356, though the margin is narrow. With 30-year mortgage rates at 7.40%, the high interest rate environment further complicates affordability for new buyers, even at low price points. Consequently, while the market offers exceptional value relative to national standards, its stability is compromised by a thin employment base and recent price volatility, requiring careful consideration of long-term economic resilience by potential stakeholders.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Catahoula Parish is 39.2% below the Louisiana state average and 55.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $821/month is 10.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.3, buying a home in Catahoula Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Catahoula Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
