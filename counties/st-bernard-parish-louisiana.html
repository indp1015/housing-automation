Market Analysis

The median home value in St. Bernard Parish is 21.0% above the Louisiana state average and 11.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,082/month is 18.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in St. Bernard Parish is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
