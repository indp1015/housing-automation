Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 7.9% below the Ohio state average and 20.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,026/month is 13.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.7, buying a home in Montgomery County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.5%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
