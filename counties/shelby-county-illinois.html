Market Analysis

The median home value in Shelby County is 10.4% below the Illinois state average and 39.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $814/month is 6.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.0, buying a home in Shelby County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Shelby County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
