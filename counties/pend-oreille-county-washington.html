Market Analysis

The median home value in Pend Oreille County is 15.7% below the Washington state average and 61.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $855/month is 31.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 35.9, Pend Oreille County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Pend Oreille County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.8%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
