Market Analysis

The median home value in Oconee County is 19.7% above the South Carolina state average and 2.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $876/month is 7.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Oconee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.4%, compared to 72.9% in South Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
