Market Analysis

The median home value in San Luis Obispo County is 36.2% above the California state average and 267.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,899/month is 17.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 34.1, San Luis Obispo County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

San Luis Obispo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.9%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
