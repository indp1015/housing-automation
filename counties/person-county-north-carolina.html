Market Analysis

The median home value in Person County is 9.1% below the North Carolina state average and 6.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $864/month is 11.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Person County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.3%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
