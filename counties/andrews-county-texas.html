Market Analysis

The median home value in Andrews County is 5.1% above the Texas state average and 14.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,305/month is 30.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.5, buying a home in Andrews County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Andrews County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
