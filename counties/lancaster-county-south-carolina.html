Market Analysis

The median home value in Lancaster County is 65.2% above the South Carolina state average and 44.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $959/month is 2.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.7, Lancaster County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lancaster County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.3%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
