Market Analysis

The median home value in Scott County is 76.0% above the Minnesota state average and 84.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,568/month is 67.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Scott County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.8%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
