Market Analysis

The median home value in Scott County is 76.8% above the Minnesota state average and 86.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,485/month is 62.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Scott County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.3%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
