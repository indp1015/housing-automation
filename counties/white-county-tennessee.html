Market Analysis

The median home value in White County is 7.2% below the Tennessee state average and 9.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $849/month is 4.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

White County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
