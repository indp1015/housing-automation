Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 22.6% above the Washington state average and 135.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,522/month is 21.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.3, Jefferson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.0%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
