Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 24.5% above the Washington state average and 134.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,291/month is 7.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.0, Jefferson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.7%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
