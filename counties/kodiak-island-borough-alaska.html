Market Analysis

The median home value in Kodiak Island Borough is 45.4% above the Alaska state average and 81.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,765/month is 41.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Kodiak Island Borough is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
