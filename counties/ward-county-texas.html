Market Analysis

The median home value in Ward County is 19.1% below the Texas state average and 33.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,118/month is 12.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.2, buying a home in Ward County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Ward County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
