Market Analysis

The median home value in Baker County is 26.1% below the Oregon state average and 25.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $865/month is 27.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.5, Baker County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Baker County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.5%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
