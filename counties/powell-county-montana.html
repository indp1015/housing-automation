Market Analysis

The median home value in Powell County is 1.7% above the Montana state average and 20.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $770/month is 6.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.7, Powell County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Powell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.5%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
