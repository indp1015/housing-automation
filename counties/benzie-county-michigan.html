📝 Local Market Analysis

Benzie County, Michigan, operates as a balanced market with distinct affordability constraints, characterized by an 87.8% owner-occupancy rate and a median home value of $285,700. The market exhibits strong momentum, with the FHFA House Price Index rising 8.42% year-over-year, significantly outpacing the state average of 3.68%. This appreciation is supported by robust net migration of 583 filers and a local economy generating 4,219 jobs with an average weekly wage of $873. However, the high price-to-rent ratio of 23.2, compared to the national average of 18.7, suggests that ownership costs are escalating faster than rental alternatives, creating a divergence where home values are rising more rapidly than rents. Affordability remains the primary challenge in this coastal county. The price-to-income ratio stands at 3.82, exceeding the national benchmark of 3.20, indicating that median household incomes of $74,834 are lagging behind housing costs. Despite this, the rent burden is slightly below the national average at 16.5%, suggesting that rental housing remains relatively accessible compared to ownership. The county’s home values are 44.8% higher than the state average and 25.5% above the national mean, reflecting its premium status as a desirable location. This premium is sustained by limited housing supply, with only 136 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits, restricting new inventory growth. The market’s stability is high, driven by consistent price appreciation from 2017 to 2024, which saw values increase from $162,200 to $285,700. This long-term upward trend contrasts with the current 7.40% national mortgage rate, which may temper immediate demand but does not appear to have halted price growth. The economic context is favorable for existing homeowners, with inflow filers averaging incomes of $77,584, slightly higher than outflow filers, indicating that incoming residents possess the financial capacity to support elevated price points. For potential buyers, the combination of low new construction and high migration inflows suggests continued pressure on inventory, while renters may find the current rent burden manageable relative to national norms. The market’s vitality is further underscored by the outperformance of state-level price indices, signaling a resilient local demand base that is decoupled from broader regional economic trends.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Benzie County is 44.8% above the Michigan state average and 25.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,026/month is 13.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Benzie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.8%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
