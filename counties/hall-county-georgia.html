Market Analysis

The median home value in Hall County is 77.0% above the Georgia state average and 53.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,341/month is 39.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hall County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.6%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
