📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 45601, located in Jackson County, Ohio, demonstrates a consistent upward price trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) has risen from 152.12 in 2021 to 209.86 in 2025, indicating a significant appreciation of approximately 38% during this period. This sustained growth reflects a robust local demand environment that has outpaced broader regional trends in recent quarters. In terms of relative performance, ZIP 45601 exhibits a distinct divergence from both its parent county and the state of Ohio. The ZIP code recorded a year-over-year increase of 3.08%, which exceeds the county’s 2.25% growth rate but lags behind the state average of 4.33%. This specific positioning suggests that while the local market is outperforming the immediate Jackson County environment, it is not experiencing the same momentum as the wider Ohio market. Compared to nearby ZIPs, such as 45692 with a 12.17% surge, 45601 appears to be stabilizing at a higher valuation level rather than entering a speculative boom phase. The sub-market character of 45601 aligns with an affordable, owner-occupied residential segment. With a parent county median home value of $150,700 and a median household income of $57,106, the area serves primarily entry-level and mid-range buyers. The high owner occupancy rate of 73.9% further underscores its stability as a traditional housing market rather than a renter-heavy or luxury enclave. Residents should note that while prices have risen sharply since 2021, the recent slowdown in year-over-year growth compared to the state average may indicate a market reaching a temporary equilibrium, offering a more predictable landscape for long-term investment or settlement.
