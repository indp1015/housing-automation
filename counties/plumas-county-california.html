Market Analysis

The median home value in Plumas County is 42.6% below the California state average and 54.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,034/month is 36.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Plumas County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Plumas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.8%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
