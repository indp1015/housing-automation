Market Analysis

The median home value in Hudson County is 25.5% above the New Jersey state average and 137.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,894/month is 15.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hudson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 30.7%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
