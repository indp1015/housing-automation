Market Analysis

The median home value in Pike County is 30.4% below the Kentucky state average and 50.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $858/month is 8.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.9, buying a home in Pike County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Pike County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.3%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
