Market Analysis

The median home value in Anoka County is 45.4% above the Minnesota state average and 52.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,464/month is 56.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Anoka County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Anoka County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.2%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
