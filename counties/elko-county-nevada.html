Market Analysis

The median home value in Elko County is 1.7% below the Nevada state average and 32.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,164/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Elko County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.4%, compared to 73.0% in Nevada and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
