Market Analysis

The median home value in Elko County is 6.1% below the Nevada state average and 33.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,126/month is 1.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Elko County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.4%, compared to 70.8% in Nevada and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
