Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 10.5% below the Indiana state average and 24.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $858/month is 5.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Montgomery County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.3%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
