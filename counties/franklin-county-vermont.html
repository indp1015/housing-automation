Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 4.7% above the Vermont state average and 34.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,180/month is 4.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 77.1% in Vermont and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
