Market Analysis

The median home value in Albany County is 2.1% above the Wyoming state average and 52.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $936/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.7, Albany County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Albany County has an owner-occupancy rate of 49.4%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
