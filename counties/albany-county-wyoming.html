Market Analysis

The median home value in Albany County is 4.0% below the Wyoming state average and 46.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $931/month is 1.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.8, Albany County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Albany County has an owner-occupancy rate of 50.0%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
