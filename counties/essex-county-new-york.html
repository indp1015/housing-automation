Market Analysis

The median home value in Essex County is 24.6% below the New York state average and 6.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $934/month is 20.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Essex County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Essex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.3%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
