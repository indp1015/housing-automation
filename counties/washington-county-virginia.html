Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 27.7% below the Virginia state average and 6.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $854/month is 26.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.8%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
