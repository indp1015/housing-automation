Market Analysis

The median home value in Worcester County is 33.5% below the Massachusetts state average and 84.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,347/month is 13.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Worcester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.7%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
