Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 17.6% above the Oregon state average and 99.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,299/month is 9.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.2, Polk County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.7%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
