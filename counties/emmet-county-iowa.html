Market Analysis

The median home value in Emmet County is 33.3% below the Iowa state average and 49.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $733/month is 11.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.0, buying a home in Emmet County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Emmet County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.4%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
