Market Analysis

The median home value in Gaston County is 13.0% above the North Carolina state average and 16.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,153/month is 18.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Gaston County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Gaston County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.8%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
