Market Analysis

The median home value in Tate County is 53.9% above the Mississippi state average and 8.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $963/month is 19.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Tate County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Tate County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.0%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
