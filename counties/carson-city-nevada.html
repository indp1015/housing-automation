Market Analysis

The median home value in Carson City is 47.7% above the Nevada state average and 98.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,248/month is 6.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.2, Carson City is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Carson City has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.3%, compared to 73.0% in Nevada and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
