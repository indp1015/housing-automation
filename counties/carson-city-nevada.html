Market Analysis

The median home value in Carson City is 41.9% above the Nevada state average and 101.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,172/month is 3.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.3, Carson City is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Carson City has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.2%, compared to 70.8% in Nevada and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
