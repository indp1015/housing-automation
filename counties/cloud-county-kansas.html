Market Analysis

The median home value in Cloud County is 26.5% below the Kansas state average and 54.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $689/month is 12.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.6, buying a home in Cloud County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cloud County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 74.0% in Kansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
