Market Analysis

The median home value in Frederick County is 25.6% above the Maryland state average and 104.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,774/month is 21.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Frederick County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
