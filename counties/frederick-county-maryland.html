Market Analysis

The median home value in Frederick County is 24.8% above the Maryland state average and 106.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,706/month is 20.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Frederick County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
