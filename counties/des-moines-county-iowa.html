Market Analysis

The median home value in Des Moines County is 14.7% below the Iowa state average and 35.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $908/month is 11.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.6, buying a home in Des Moines County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Des Moines County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.7%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
