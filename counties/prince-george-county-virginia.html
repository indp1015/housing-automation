Market Analysis

The median home value in Prince George County is 3.5% below the Virginia state average and 26.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,563/month is 37.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Prince George County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Prince George County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.9%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
