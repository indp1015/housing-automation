Market Analysis

The median home value in Wetzel County is 27.1% below the West Virginia state average and 52.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $688/month is 11.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.1, buying a home in Wetzel County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wetzel County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.2%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
