Market Analysis

The median home value in Williamson County is 140.3% above the Texas state average and 96.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,720/month is 78.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Williamson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.1%, compared to 73.0% in Texas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
