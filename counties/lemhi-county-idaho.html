Market Analysis

The median home value in Lemhi County is 8.2% below the Idaho state average and 43.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $754/month is 22.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 36.0, Lemhi County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lemhi County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.3%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
