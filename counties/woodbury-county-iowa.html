Market Analysis

The median home value in Woodbury County is 7.1% above the Iowa state average and 19.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $981/month is 17.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Woodbury County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Woodbury County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.2%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
