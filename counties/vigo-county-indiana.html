Market Analysis

The median home value in Vigo County is 21.2% below the Indiana state average and 33.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $879/month is about equal to the state average.

Vigo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.4%, compared to 76.0% in Indiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
