Market Analysis

The median home value in Vigo County is 22.6% below the Indiana state average and 34.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $924/month is 1.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.4, buying a home in Vigo County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Vigo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.7%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
