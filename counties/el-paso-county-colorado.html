Market Analysis

The median home value in El Paso County is 8.3% above the Colorado state average and 103.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,609/month is 28.4% above the state average.

El Paso County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.4%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
