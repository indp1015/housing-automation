Market Analysis

The median home value in El Paso County is 7.8% above the Colorado state average and 102.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,696/month is 30.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

El Paso County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.2%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
