Market Analysis

The median home value in Benton County is 6.5% below the Washington state average and 79.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,306/month is 4.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Benton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Benton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.6%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
