The housing market in ZIP code 43219, located in Franklin County, Ohio, exhibits a consistent upward price trajectory over the past five years. This sustained growth indicates a robust appreciation trend, with the index reaching a level 2.57 times higher than the 2000 baseline. This divergence signals that the specific sub-market within 43219 is experiencing slightly slower momentum than the wider Franklin County and Ohio housing sectors. Nearby ZIP codes, such as 43137 and 43227, report significantly higher YoY increases of 8.92% and 6.71%, respectively, further highlighting that 43219 is not at the forefront of the current regional price surge. The sub-market character of 43219 aligns with the broader Franklin County profile, which features a median home value of $288,400 and a median household income of $75,176. With an owner-occupancy rate of 52.8%, the area demonstrates a balanced mix of homeowners and renters, avoiding the extremes of either a purely rental-driven or exclusive luxury market. The HPI level of 257.9 places the area in a mid-tier segment, accessible to the median income earner but reflecting significant equity growth for existing owners. For prospective buyers, the lower YoY growth rate compared to neighboring ZIPs may offer more stability and potentially less competitive pressure than in rapidly appreciating adjacent areas. However, the 7.40% national mortgage rate remains a significant factor in affordability calculations. Residents should note that while prices are stabilizing, they remain well above historical baselines, requiring careful budgeting relative to the county’s median income levels.
