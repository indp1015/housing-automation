Market Analysis

The median home value in Fulton County is 138.5% above the Georgia state average and 103.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,635/month is 76.6% above the state average.

Fulton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.1%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
