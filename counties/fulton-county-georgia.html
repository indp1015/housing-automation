Market Analysis

The median home value in Fulton County is 131.8% above the Georgia state average and 101.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,732/month is 79.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Fulton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.3%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
