Market Analysis

The median home value in Ramsey County is 17.1% above the North Dakota state average and 6.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $687/month is 10.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Ramsey County has an owner-occupancy rate of 58.7%, compared to 74.1% in North Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
