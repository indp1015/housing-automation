📝 Editor's Insight

Clear Creek County exhibits a strong buyer’s market, evidenced by a price-to-rent ratio of 38.8, nearly double the national average of 18.0. This metric indicates that purchasing is significantly more economical than renting for long-term stability. The five-year trend reveals aggressive appreciation, with home values growing at a 10.9% CAGR, outpacing the 7.0% rent increase. This divergence suggests capital gains are driving value rather than rental demand. Affordability remains strained for buyers, as the price-to-income ratio stands at 5.93, nearly double the national benchmark of 3.09. Conversely, renters face moderate pressure, with a 15.3% rent burden slightly below the national average of 17.2%. A critical, non-obvious insight is the county’s high owner occupancy rate of 82.6%. This indicates a stable, established residential base rather than a transient rental market. For potential residents, this implies limited new housing inventory and a community oriented toward long-term ownership, which may constrain rental availability despite lower relative rent costs.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Clear Creek County is 42.3% above the Colorado state average and 167.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,467/month is 12.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 34.6, Clear Creek County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clear Creek County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
