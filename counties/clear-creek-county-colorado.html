Market Analysis

The median home value in Clear Creek County is 43.9% above the Colorado state average and 170.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,230/month is 1.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.8, Clear Creek County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clear Creek County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.6%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
