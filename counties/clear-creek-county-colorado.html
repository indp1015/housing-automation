Market Analysis

The median home value in Clear Creek County is 42.3% above the Colorado state average and 167.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,467/month is 12.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 34.6, Clear Creek County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clear Creek County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
