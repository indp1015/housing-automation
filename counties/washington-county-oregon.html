Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 52.1% above the Oregon state average and 158.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,849/month is 55.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.5, Washington County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.8%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
