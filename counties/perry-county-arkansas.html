Market Analysis

The median home value in Perry County is 8.0% below the Arkansas state average and 42.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $805/month is 4.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Perry County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Perry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
