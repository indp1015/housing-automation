Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 30.1% below the Illinois state average and 51.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $715/month is 14.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.9, buying a home in Montgomery County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.3%, compared to 75.7% in Illinois and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
