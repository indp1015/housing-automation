📝 Local Market Analysis

Montgomery County, Illinois, functions as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $112,300, which sits 50.7% below the national average. The market exhibits strong affordability metrics, with a price-to-income ratio of 1.77 compared to the national benchmark of 3.20, and a rent burden of 13.6% against the national average of 17.2%. These figures indicate that housing costs consume a significantly smaller portion of local household income, which averages $63,620, than in most other U.S. jurisdictions. The high owner-occupancy rate of 77.4% further underscores the stability and accessibility of homeownership in this region, where the price-to-rent ratio of 12.9 suggests that purchasing remains a more financially viable option than long-term renting. Recent data reveals a complex trend dynamic, marked by significant price appreciation followed by a sharp correction. Home values surged from $81,000 in 2017 to $112,300 in 2024, driven by double-digit growth in 2022 and 2024. However, the most recent FHFA House Price Index indicates a year-over-year decline of 3.24%, causing the county to underperform the state average by 8.78 percentage points. This divergence is notable because while home prices have recently corrected, the broader state market grew by 5.54%, highlighting a localized softening in demand. This price adjustment contrasts with the historical pattern of rising values outpacing rent growth, suggesting a potential rebalancing of the local real estate cycle. The economic context provides limited support for sustained price growth, as the local economy supports only 6,998 jobs with an average weekly wage of $888. Housing supply is extremely constrained, with only 11 new units authorized in 2025, all of which are single-family homes. Despite this scarcity, net migration of +382 filers suggests modest population growth, though incoming residents earn slightly less ($69,427) than those departing ($70,009). With national mortgage rates at 7.40%, the low absolute price point remains the primary driver of affordability, offsetting the high cost of borrowing. For potential buyers, the current 3.24% price decline presents a tactical entry point, while renters benefit from a low rent burden that preserves disposable income relative to the national average. The market’s trajectory is currently defined by this tension between tight supply and cooling price momentum.

The median home value in Montgomery County is 26.6% below the Illinois state average and 50.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $723/month is 16.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Montgomery County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
