Market Analysis

The median home value in Cumberland County is 48.5% below the New Jersey state average and 2.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,282/month is 21.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Cumberland County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cumberland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.5%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
