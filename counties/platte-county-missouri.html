Market Analysis

The median home value in Platte County is 95.5% above the Missouri state average and 50.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,247/month is 62.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Platte County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.0%, compared to 73.6% in Missouri and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
