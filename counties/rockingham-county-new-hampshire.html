Market Analysis

The median home value in Rockingham County is 46.0% above the New Hampshire state average and 118.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,619/month is 27.2% above the state average.

Rockingham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.6%, compared to 73.7% in New Hampshire and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
