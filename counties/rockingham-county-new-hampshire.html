Market Analysis

The median home value in Rockingham County is 42.3% above the New Hampshire state average and 118.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,687/month is 28.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Rockingham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 74.3% in New Hampshire and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
