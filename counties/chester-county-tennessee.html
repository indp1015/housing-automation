Market Analysis

The median home value in Chester County is 26.4% below the Tennessee state average and 27.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $756/month is 15.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Chester County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Chester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.4%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
