Market Analysis

The median home value in Monongalia County is 80.9% above the West Virginia state average and 17.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $987/month is 27.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Monongalia County has an owner-occupancy rate of 57.9%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
