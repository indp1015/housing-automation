Market Analysis

The median home value in Kalamazoo County is 22.6% above the Michigan state average and 6.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,093/month is 20.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Kalamazoo County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Kalamazoo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.5%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
