Market Analysis

The median home value in Pottawatomie County is 77.1% above the Kansas state average and 8.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $924/month is 15.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Pottawatomie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.2%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
