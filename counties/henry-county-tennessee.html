Market Analysis

The median home value in Henry County is 28.3% below the Tennessee state average and 29.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $761/month is 14.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Henry County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
