Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 48.2% above the Missouri state average and 14.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $995/month is 27.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.2%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
