Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 48.5% above the Missouri state average and 14.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $913/month is 19.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.4%, compared to 73.6% in Missouri and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
