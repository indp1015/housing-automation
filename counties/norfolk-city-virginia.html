Market Analysis

The median home value in Norfolk city is 2.0% below the Virginia state average and 27.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,321/month is 13.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Norfolk city is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Norfolk city has an owner-occupancy rate of 46.3%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
