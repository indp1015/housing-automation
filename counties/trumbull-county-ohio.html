Market Analysis

The median home value in Trumbull County is 27.8% below the Ohio state average and 37.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $797/month is 11.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Trumbull County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Trumbull County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
