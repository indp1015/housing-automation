Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 23.2% above the Ohio state average and 6.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,075/month is 18.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Hamilton County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.3%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
