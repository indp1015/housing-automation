📝 Local Market Analysis

Hamilton County, Illinois, operates as a distinctly buyer’s market, characterized by extreme affordability metrics that far exceed national benchmarks. With a median home value of $113,700, the price-to-income ratio stands at 1.73, significantly lower than the national average of 3.20. Similarly, the rent burden is only 10.4% of income, well below the 17.2% national norm. These figures indicate that housing costs impose minimal financial strain on residents, creating a strong foundation for owner occupancy, which currently sits at 76.4%. The market’s classification is reinforced by the price-to-rent ratio of 16.7, suggesting that purchasing remains a more economically rational choice than renting for qualified buyers. The trend data reveals a volatile but ultimately upward trajectory in home values. After experiencing significant fluctuations, including a 10.7% decline in 2021, the market stabilized with an 8.5% increase in 2023 and a 4.5% rise in 2024. Recent FHFA HPI data indicates a 10.44% year-over-year increase, outperforming the state average of 5.54% by 4.90 percentage points. This divergence between accelerating price growth and relatively stable rents suggests a tightening supply of available homes rather than broad-based inflationary pressure across all housing types. While the local economy supports this growth with an average weekly wage of $1,144, the limited employment base of 1,517 jobs and 176 establishments constrains long-term vitality. Net migration of +120 filers indicates modest population growth, but the low establishment count limits job diversity. The market’s stability is currently the weakest component, reflected in historical price volatility, while momentum and affordability drive its overall strength. The 7.32% national mortgage rate remains a headwind for entry-level buyers, yet the low absolute price point mitigates this impact. For potential buyers, the current environment offers exceptional value, with home values 50.1% below the national average and 25.7% below the state average. Renters may find the low rent burden appealing, but the rising price-to-rent dynamics suggest that the cost of entry for homeownership is becoming more competitive. The combination of high affordability and strong recent momentum positions Hamilton County as an attractive option for those prioritizing low entry costs and equity potential over high-volume urban amenities. The data supports a market where financial barriers to entry are low, but economic depth remains limited.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 25.7% below the Illinois state average and 50.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $568/month is 34.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Hamilton County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
