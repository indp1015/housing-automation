📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP 28560's FHFA HPI climbed from 180.74 in 2021 to 275.06 in 2025, a gain of roughly 52% over four years, with annual appreciation decelerating from the sharp 2021–2022 surge (180.74 to 213.39) to a more moderate +2.22% in 2025. The index now sits at 275.1, the highest of the three Craven County ZIPs cited—well above 28532 (235.8) and 28562 (237.2)—indicating that 28560 has absorbed a larger cumulative price increase than its neighbors since the 2000 base year. On a year-over-year basis, 28560's +2.22% trails the county's +2.80% but substantially exceeds North Carolina's +0.38%. That pattern—positive but below-county growth—suggests the ZIP is still appreciating, though slightly less briskly than the county aggregate, while the state's near-flat reading places 28560 in a comparatively firmer local market. The divergence is modest and does not indicate outright weakness. The parent county's ACS profile—median home value of $231,600, median gross rent of $1,110, median household income of $65,873, and 68.4% owner occupancy—describes a middle-income, owner-dominant sub-market rather than a luxury or renter-heavy one. A value-to-income ratio near 3.5 and rent consuming roughly 20% of median household income point to moderate affordability pressure. Prospective buyers should note that with mortgage rates at 7.40%, entry costs remain elevated relative to the 2021 baseline, even as appreciation has cooled.
