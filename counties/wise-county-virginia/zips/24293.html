📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP 24293 in Wise County, Virginia, shows a housing market that peaked and has since softened. The FHFA HPI climbed from 149.61 in 2020 to 172.27 in 2022, a 15.1% surge, then fell to 165.25 by 2025 — a 4.1% retreat from that peak. Measured across the last five years, the index is up 10.5% (149.61 to 165.25), but essentially all of that gain was captured before 2023; the 2025 reading sits only 8.4% above its 2019 level of 152.48. That trajectory diverges sharply from broader trends. Wise County is down 1.27% year over year while Virginia is up 3.03%, a roughly 4.3-percentage-point gap. The ZIP's decline therefore reflects local weakness rather than statewide conditions, signaling demand softness specific to this rural Appalachian sub-market. The parent county's ACS profile confirms an entry-level, owner-occupied market: a median home value of $118,600, median gross rent of $790 per month, median household income of $52,943, and 74.2% owner occupancy. With an HPI of 165.2 (2000 = 100), prices have outpaced the general price level over 26 years, yet absolute values remain low.
