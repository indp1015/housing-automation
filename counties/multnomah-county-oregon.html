Market Analysis

The median home value in Multnomah County is 42.9% above the Oregon state average and 142.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,646/month is 38.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.0, Multnomah County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Multnomah County has an owner-occupancy rate of 53.4%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
