Market Analysis

The median home value in Bastrop County is 69.0% above the Texas state average and 38.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,410/month is 41.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Bastrop County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Bastrop County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
