Market Analysis

The median home value in Wake County is 96.0% above the North Carolina state average and 102.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,623/month is 66.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Wake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.1%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
