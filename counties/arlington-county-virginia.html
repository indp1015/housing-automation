Market Analysis

The median home value in Arlington County is 202.6% above the Virginia state average and 293.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,322/month is 98.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.1, Arlington County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Arlington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 41.3%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
