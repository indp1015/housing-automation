Market Analysis

The median home value in Arlington County is 211.0% above the Virginia state average and 308.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,275/month is 99.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.7, Arlington County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Arlington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 41.8%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
