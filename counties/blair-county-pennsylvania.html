Market Analysis

The median home value in Blair County is 24.0% below the Pennsylvania state average and 29.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $885/month is 10.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Blair County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Blair County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.4%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
