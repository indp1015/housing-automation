Market Analysis

The median home value in Carbon County is 45.4% below the Utah state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $870/month is 24.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Carbon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.9%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
