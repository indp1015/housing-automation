Market Analysis

The median home value in Colfax County is 12.1% below the Nebraska state average and 36.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $980/month is 23.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, buying a home in Colfax County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Colfax County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.3%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
