Market Analysis

The median home value in Kanawha County is about equal to the West Virginia state average and 35.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $906/month is 16.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Kanawha County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Kanawha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.1%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
