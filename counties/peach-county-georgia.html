Market Analysis

The median home value in Peach County is 5.0% above the Georgia state average and 8.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $940/month is 2.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Peach County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Peach County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.6%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
