Market Analysis

The median home value in Morton County is 50.1% above the North Dakota state average and 15.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,076/month is 39.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Morton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
