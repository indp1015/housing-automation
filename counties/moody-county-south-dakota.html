Market Analysis

The median home value in Moody County is 25.1% above the South Dakota state average and 0.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $758/month is 3.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Moody County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.5%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
