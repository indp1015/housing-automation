Market Analysis

The median home value in Moody County is 24.2% above the South Dakota state average and 2.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $723/month is 4.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Moody County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.9%, compared to 72.7% in South Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
