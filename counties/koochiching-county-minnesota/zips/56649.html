📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 56649, located in Koochiching County, Minnesota, exhibits a volatile yet aggressive upward trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) rose from 179.48 in 2021 to 247.46 in 2025, representing a cumulative increase of approximately 37.9%. This growth was not linear; the index surged 19.1% from 2021 to 2022, dipped 4.6% in 2023, and then accelerated sharply with gains of 12.8% in 2024 and 7.56% in 2025. This pattern suggests a market that experienced a significant correction before re-entering a phase of robust appreciation, outpacing the broader state trend of 2.42% YoY. While the ZIP’s 7.56% YoY growth is strong, it lags slightly behind the county-wide average of 8.82%. This minor divergence indicates that while Koochiching County as a whole is experiencing heightened demand, ZIP 56649 is tracking closely with regional trends rather than leading them. The absence of other ZIP codes in the county data implies that 56649 likely covers a significant portion of the county’s residential inventory, making its performance a primary indicator of local market health. The high HPI value of 247.5, relative to the 2000 baseline, signals substantial long-term equity accumulation for owners, despite the recent volatility. Characterizing the sub-market, the parent county’s median home value of $156,600 and median household income of $66,940 suggest an affordable, owner-occupied community rather than a luxury or high-density rental market. With an owner occupancy rate of 80.4%, the area is predominantly residential. However, the rapid HPI growth relative to the modest median income suggests that affordability may be tightening. For prospective buyers, the current 30-year mortgage rate of 7.40% combined with the ZIP’s steep recent price increases implies that entry costs have risen significantly. Residents should be aware that while long-term value has appreciated strongly, the pace of recent gains may outstrip local income growth, impacting future affordability and resale liquidity.
