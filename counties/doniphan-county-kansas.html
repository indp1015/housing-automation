Market Analysis

The median home value in Doniphan County is 2.6% below the Kansas state average and 40.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $776/month is 3.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in Doniphan County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Doniphan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.1%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
