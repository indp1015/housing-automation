Market Analysis

The median home value in Pennington County is 18.0% below the Minnesota state average and 14.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $862/month is 8.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Pennington County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Pennington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.2%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
