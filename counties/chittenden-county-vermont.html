Market Analysis

The median home value in Chittenden County is 49.9% above the Vermont state average and 92.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,646/month is 45.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Chittenden County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 77.1% in Vermont and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
