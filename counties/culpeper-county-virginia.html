Market Analysis

The median home value in Culpeper County is 38.3% above the Virginia state average and 79.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,423/month is 21.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Culpeper County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.0%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
