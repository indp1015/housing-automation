Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 12.1% below the Wisconsin state average and 9.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $909/month is 0.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Douglas County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.9%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
