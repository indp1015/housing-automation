Market Analysis

The median home value in Davie County is 7.9% above the North Carolina state average and 11.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $867/month is 11.2% below the state average.

Davie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.2%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
