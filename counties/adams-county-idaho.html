Market Analysis

The median home value in Adams County is 4.9% above the Idaho state average and 54.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $823/month is 10.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.1, Adams County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Adams County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.1%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
