📝 Local Market Analysis

Vernon Parish operates as a distinctively affordable market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.72, which sits well below the national average of 3.20. This affordability is further underscored by a price-to-rent ratio of 12.2, significantly lower than the national benchmark of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing remains a more financially efficient strategy than renting for qualified buyers. However, the rental sector presents a different dynamic, with a rent burden of 22.4% of median income, exceeding the national average of 17.2%. This divergence indicates that while homeownership is accessible, the rental market is comparatively strained, pushing the overall classification toward a buyer’s market despite the higher relative cost of renting. The most striking feature of the local data is the sharp divergence between recent price appreciation and current momentum. Home values surged 8.7% in 2024 to $156,000, following a 13.0% jump in 2022, creating a strong upward trend signal. Yet, this historical momentum contrasts sharply with the latest FHFA HPI data, which shows a 0.03% year-over-year decline, underperforming the Louisiana state average of +1.59% by 1.62 percentage points. This deceleration suggests the market has cooled from its recent peak, a trend likely influenced by the high 7.32% national mortgage rate which dampens buyer demand. The stability of the market is further challenged by minimal new supply, with only 18 single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits, limiting inventory growth despite a net migration inflow of 2,525 filers. Economic fundamentals provide a mixed backdrop for this housing environment. With a median household income of $57,270, which is 15.8% below the national average, and an average weekly wage of $948, local purchasing power is constrained. The parish’s home values are 31.5% below the national average and 6.8% below the state average, reinforcing its status as a low-cost region. However, the low vitality and stability metrics are driven by the small employment base of 8,355 jobs and the recent flattening of price growth. For potential stakeholders, the data implies that while entry points are low, the recent negative momentum and high interest rates create a cautious environment where price appreciation is no longer guaranteed, and the limited new construction supply may sustain existing asset values despite broader economic headwinds.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Vernon Parish is 6.8% below the Louisiana state average and 31.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,068/month is 16.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.2, buying a home in Vernon Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Vernon Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 56.0%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
