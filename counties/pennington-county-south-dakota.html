Market Analysis

The median home value in Pennington County is 62.8% above the South Dakota state average and 27.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,066/month is 41.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Pennington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.1%, compared to 72.7% in South Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
