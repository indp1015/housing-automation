Market Analysis

The median home value in Wasatch County is 91.9% above the Utah state average and 242.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,905/month is 73.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.7, Wasatch County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Wasatch County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.9%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
