Market Analysis

The median home value in Prince William County is 79.2% above the Virginia state average and 132.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,067/month is 76.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Prince William County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
