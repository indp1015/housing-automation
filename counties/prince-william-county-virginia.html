Market Analysis

The median home value in Prince William County is 80.0% above the Virginia state average and 136.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,002/month is 75.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Prince William County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.4%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
