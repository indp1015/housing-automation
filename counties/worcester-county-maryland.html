Market Analysis

The median home value in Worcester County is 1.3% above the Maryland state average and 64.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,159/month is 20.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.9, Worcester County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Worcester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
