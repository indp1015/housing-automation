Market Analysis

The median home value in Snohomish County is 59.5% above the Washington state average and 205.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,949/month is 55.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.8, Snohomish County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Snohomish County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.2%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
