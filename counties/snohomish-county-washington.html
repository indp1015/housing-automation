Market Analysis

The median home value in Snohomish County is 62.1% above the Washington state average and 204.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,866/month is 55.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.8, Snohomish County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Snohomish County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.4%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
