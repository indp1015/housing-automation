Market Analysis

The median home value in Cheboygan County is 2.4% below the Michigan state average and 15.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $773/month is 14.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cheboygan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.6%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
