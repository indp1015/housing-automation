Market Analysis

The median home value in Rio Grande County is 44.0% below the Colorado state average and 5.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $945/month is 27.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Rio Grande County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.1%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
