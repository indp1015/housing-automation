Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 66.4% above the Missouri state average and 28.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,205/month is 54.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.6%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
