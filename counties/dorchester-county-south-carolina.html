Market Analysis

The median home value in Dorchester County is 64.9% above the South Carolina state average and 44.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,463/month is 48.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Dorchester County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Dorchester County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
