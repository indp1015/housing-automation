Market Analysis

The median home value in Medina County is 46.2% above the Ohio state average and 26.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,143/month is 26.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Medina County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.3%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
