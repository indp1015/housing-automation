📝 Local Market Analysis

Calhoun County, Illinois, exhibits a distinct buyer’s market profile, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.05, significantly below the national average of 3.20. This affordability is further underscored by a rent burden of 7.5% of household income, compared to the 17.2% national benchmark. However, the price-to-rent ratio stands at 27.3, exceeding the national average of 18.7, suggesting that while purchasing is relatively inexpensive, renting is comparatively costly relative to home values. The market is defined by high owner occupancy at 88.7%, indicating a stable residential base with limited rental inventory. The multi-year trend reveals aggressive appreciation, with median home values rising from $110,900 in 2017 to $190,900 in 2024, representing a cumulative increase of approximately 71.9%. Annual growth rates have remained robust, peaking at 13.2% in 2021 and maintaining a 9.7% increase in 2024. This trajectory contrasts sharply with the modest median gross rent of $583, highlighting a significant divergence where home values are rising substantially faster than rental costs. The median household income of $93,203 is 37.1% above the national average, providing the economic foundation for this price growth. Despite the small population of 4,330, the county’s home values are 24.8% higher than the Illinois state average, positioning it as a premium micro-market within the state. Notable data anomalies include the BLS reporting zero total employment and average weekly wage, likely reflecting a data collection limitation or unique local economic structure rather than true economic absence, given the high income levels. Housing supply remains extremely constrained, with only five new single-family units authorized in 2025 and no multi-family construction, which likely contributes to sustained price pressure. Net migration of +96 filers indicates modest population growth, with inflow and outflow incomes nearly identical at approximately $74,500. With 30-year fixed mortgage rates at 7.40%, the high cost of borrowing offsets some of the local affordability advantages. Potential buyers should note that the low price-to-income ratio is a strong indicator of relative value, but the limited new construction and rapid historical appreciation suggest that entry prices will continue to reflect the area’s high-income demographic rather than typical rural Illinois pricing.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Calhoun County is 24.8% above the Illinois state average and 16.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $583/month is 32.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.3, Calhoun County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Calhoun County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.7%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
