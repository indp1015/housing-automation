Market Analysis

The median home value in Calhoun County is 24.8% above the Illinois state average and 16.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $583/month is 32.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.3, Calhoun County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Calhoun County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.7%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
