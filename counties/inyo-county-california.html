Inyo County exhibits a balanced market structure, evidenced by a 24.7 price-to-rent ratio that exceeds the national average of 18.0. This metric suggests that purchasing offers a more favorable long-term financial position than renting, despite the high entry cost. This near-identical growth rate indicates that asset values and rental costs are tracking closely, preventing a significant divergence in the relative attractiveness of ownership versus tenancy. Furthermore, the rent burden of 18.9% slightly exceeds the national average of 17.2%, indicating that tenants face moderate financial pressure. Market Analysis

The median home value in Inyo County is 40.7% below the California state average and 60.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,140/month is 29.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Inyo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.1%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
