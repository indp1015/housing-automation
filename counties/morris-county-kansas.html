Market Analysis

The median home value in Morris County is 7.8% below the Kansas state average and 42.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $711/month is 9.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Morris County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Morris County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.2%, compared to 74.0% in Kansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
