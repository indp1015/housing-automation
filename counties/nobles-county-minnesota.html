📝 Local Market Analysis

Nobles County, Minnesota, functions as a balanced market leaning toward affordability, characterized by a median home value of $197,200 and a median household income of $66,101. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.98, which is notably lower than the national average of 3.20, indicating that local purchasing power remains relatively robust despite lower absolute income levels. Similarly, the rent burden is 16.3% of income, slightly below the national benchmark of 17.2%, suggesting that rental costs do not disproportionately strain household budgets. These metrics confirm that the county offers a cost-effective entry point into homeownership and rental living compared to broader U.S. standards. The market has experienced significant appreciation, with home values rising from $118,300 in 2017 to $197,200 in 2024, representing a cumulative increase of approximately 66.7%. This trajectory includes sharp annual gains of 14.4% in 2022 and 11.3% in 2023. However, recent momentum has decelerated; the FHFA HPI indicates a year-over-year increase of only 1.61% in 2025, underperforming the Minnesota state average of 2.42% by 0.81 percentage points. This divergence suggests that the rapid post-pandemic price surge is stabilizing, with growth rates normalizing relative to the state. The price-to-rent ratio of 18.3 is marginally below the national average of 18.7, reinforcing that buying remains competitively priced against renting. Regional context reveals that Nobles County home values are 17.3% below the state average and 13.4% below the national mean, while incomes are only 2.8% below the national average. This disparity highlights the county’s relative affordability. Local economic indicators show 8,371 total jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,148, supporting a steady demand base. Housing supply is skewed toward multi-family units, with 84 of 94 new permits designated for multi-family use, potentially influencing rental availability. Net migration of +282 filers indicates modest population growth, with inflow incomes averaging $71,925, slightly below outflow incomes of $72,893. With mortgage rates at 7.40%, high borrowing costs temper demand, but the low price-to-income ratio and stable rental burden provide a cushion. The overall market stability is driven by these affordability metrics, while momentum is tempered by the recent slowdown in price appreciation relative to the state.
