Market Analysis

The median home value in Grand Traverse County is 72.0% above the Michigan state average and 49.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,288/month is 42.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grand Traverse County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
