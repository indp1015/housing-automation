Market Analysis

The median home value in Powder River County is 30.6% below the Montana state average and 15.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $533/month is 36.7% below the state average.

Powder River County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
