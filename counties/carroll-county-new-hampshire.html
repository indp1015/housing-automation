Market Analysis

The median home value in Carroll County is 10.4% above the New Hampshire state average and 64.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,179/month is 7.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Carroll County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 73.7% in New Hampshire and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
