Market Analysis

The median home value in Carroll County is 11.3% above the New Hampshire state average and 70.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,195/month is 9.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.1, Carroll County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Carroll County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.4%, compared to 74.3% in New Hampshire and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
