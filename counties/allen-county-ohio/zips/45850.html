📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 45850, located in Allen County, Ohio, exhibits a robust and accelerating price trajectory. The Home Price Index (HPI) has climbed steadily from 169.51 in 2021 to 251.79 in 2025, representing a cumulative appreciation of approximately 48.5% over four years. This consistent upward trend indicates sustained demand within the sub-market. Notably, the recent year-over-year growth of 4.89% for 2025 suggests a slight deceleration in the rate of appreciation compared to the steeper gains observed in 2023 and 2024, although the absolute price levels remain at historic highs relative to the 2000 baseline. When compared to broader regional metrics, ZIP 45850 currently trails the county and state averages in annual growth. The parent county recorded a 7.75% YoY increase, while the state average stood at 4.33%. This divergence suggests that while 45850 remains a strong performer, its growth rate has moderated relative to neighboring areas like ZIP 45844 and 45810, which posted higher YoY gains of 8.86% and 10.30%, respectively. This pattern may indicate a market adjustment phase where initial rapid appreciation is normalizing, or it could reflect specific local inventory dynamics that differ from the broader county trends. In terms of sub-market character, the data points to an established, owner-occupied residential area. With a median home value of $165,300 in the parent county and an owner occupancy rate of 67.7%, the market is firmly rooted in middle-income homeownership rather than luxury or high-density rental sectors. The median household income of $64,038 supports this characterization, indicating a stable demographic base. For prospective buyers, the practical takeaway is clear: while entry prices have risen significantly since 2021, the current 7.40% mortgage rate remains a substantial barrier to affordability. Residents and investors should note that although 45850’s growth has slowed slightly compared to the county average, the long-term appreciation trend remains positive, offering stability for long-term holders despite the high interest rate environment.
