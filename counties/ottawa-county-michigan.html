Market Analysis

The median home value in Ottawa County is 59.8% above the Michigan state average and 37.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,148/month is 31.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Ottawa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 79.9% in Michigan and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
