📝 Local Market Analysis

Clay County, Arkansas, operates as a distinctly buyer’s market, characterized by extreme affordability relative to national standards. The median home value of $94,600 sits 58.5% below the national average and 33.1% below the state average, resulting in a price-to-income ratio of 1.84, significantly lower than the national benchmark of 3.20. Similarly, the price-to-rent ratio of 11.2 is well below the national average of 18.7, indicating that purchasing is a substantially more cost-effective long-term strategy than renting. The rent burden of 16.5% of income is also slightly below the national average of 17.2%, further reinforcing the financial advantage of homeownership in this rural economy. Recent market momentum is robust, with the FHFA House Price Index showing a 9.67% year-over-year increase, outperforming the Arkansas state average of 1.09% by 8.58 percentage points. This surge follows a volatile period where home values dipped 4.8% in 2021 before rebounding 13.2% in 2022 and continuing to rise 7.4% in 2024. The stability of the market is currently challenged by this rapid appreciation, which contrasts with the high affordability scores. While the low entry price point provides a buffer against rising interest rates, the current 7.40% mortgage rate still imposes significant monthly payment obligations relative to the median household income of $51,481. Economic fundamentals reveal a constrained supply environment that likely supports price growth. With only nine new housing units authorized in 2025, all single-family, and total local employment limited to 2,105 jobs, the market lacks the depth to absorb significant population shifts. However, net migration of 240 filers between 2021 and 2022 suggests modest demographic growth. The average weekly wage of $821 aligns with the low-income profile of the region, limiting the pool of high-earning buyers. Consequently, the market exhibits high momentum driven by low baseline prices and limited new construction, but low vitality due to the small economic base. Potential buyers benefit from the current price-to-income advantage, yet must account for the rapid pace of recent appreciation which has narrowed the gap between local and state price trajectories.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 33.1% below the Arkansas state average and 58.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $707/month is 8.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.2, buying a home in Clay County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
