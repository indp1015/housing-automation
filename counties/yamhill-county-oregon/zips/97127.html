📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 97127 exhibits a distinct deceleration in price appreciation, contrasting sharply with broader regional trends. The Home Price Index (HPI) for this area reached a peak of 326.43 in 2024 before retreating to 319.83 in 2025. This reversal represents a year-over-year decline of 2.02%, a significant deviation from the positive momentum observed in preceding years. While the HPI rose steadily from 268.22 in 2021 to its 2024 high, the latest data indicates a stabilization or slight correction phase specific to this sub-market. This local contraction stands in direct opposition to the surrounding macroeconomic environment. Yamhill County recorded a year-over-year increase of 3.08%, while the state of Oregon saw a modest 0.24% gain. The divergence suggests that ZIP 97127 is currently underperforming relative to both its county and state benchmarks. Unlike nearby ZIPs 97115 and 97148, which are experiencing double-digit growth rates of 13.06% and 10.11% respectively, 97127 is navigating a period of price adjustment. This isolation from the county's upward trajectory may indicate localized inventory shifts or specific demand dynamics within this particular postal code. In terms of sub-market character, the area aligns with the upper-middle segment of Yamhill County’s housing inventory. The parent county’s median home value of $471,700 and median household income of $90,063 suggest a market accessible to established professionals rather than entry-level buyers. With an owner-occupancy rate of 69.4%, the area leans toward ownership, though the median gross rent of $1,480 per month indicates a robust rental sector. For prospective buyers, the current 2.02% price dip offers a potential entry point below the 2024 peak, provided they can secure financing in a high-interest environment where the 30-year mortgage rate stands at 7.32%. This market presents a value opportunity for those seeking stability in a high-income corridor, distinct from the rapid appreciation seen in adjacent ZIP codes.
