Market Analysis

The median home value in Carlisle County is 25.1% below the Kentucky state average and 47.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $792/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.7, buying a home in Carlisle County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Carlisle County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
