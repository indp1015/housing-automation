Market Analysis

The median home value in Marquette County is 7.7% below the Wisconsin state average and 4.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $800/month is 10.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Marquette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.9%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
