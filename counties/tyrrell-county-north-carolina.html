Market Analysis

The median home value in Tyrrell County is 30.1% below the North Carolina state average and 27.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $644/month is 34.0% below the state average.

Tyrrell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.8%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
