Market Analysis

The median home value in Hampden County is 52.9% below the Massachusetts state average and 30.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,105/month is 29.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hampden County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.2%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
