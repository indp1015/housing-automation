Market Analysis

The median home value in Sequatchie County is 4.1% above the Tennessee state average and 2.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $818/month is 8.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sequatchie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
