Market Analysis

The median home value in Hughes County is 32.9% below the Oklahoma state average and 55.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $660/month is 21.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Hughes County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hughes County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.1%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
