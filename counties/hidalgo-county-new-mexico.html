Market Analysis

The median home value in Hidalgo County is 42.4% below the New Mexico state average and 51.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $814/month is 10.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.4, buying a home in Hidalgo County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hidalgo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.8%, compared to 74.2% in New Mexico and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
