Market Analysis

The median home value in Skamania County is 18.8% above the Washington state average and 123.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,024/month is 14.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.5, Skamania County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Skamania County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.6%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
