Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 19.8% below the Idaho state average and 18.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $919/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.3%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
