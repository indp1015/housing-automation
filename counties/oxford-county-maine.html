Market Analysis

The median home value in Oxford County is 16.7% below the Maine state average and 4.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $836/month is 18.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Oxford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.6%, compared to 76.6% in Maine and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
