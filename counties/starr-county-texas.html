Market Analysis

The median home value in Starr County is 48.9% below the Texas state average and 58.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $726/month is 27.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.9, buying a home in Starr County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Starr County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
