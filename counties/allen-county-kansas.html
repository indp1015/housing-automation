Market Analysis

The median home value in Allen County is 22.5% below the Kansas state average and 52.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $720/month is 10.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.5, buying a home in Allen County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Allen County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.4%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
