Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 22.9% above the Maine state average and 37.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,079/month is 11.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Knox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 76.3% in Maine and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
