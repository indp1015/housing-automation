Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnston County is 25.0% above the North Carolina state average and 26.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,007/month is 7.4% above the state average.

Johnston County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.1%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
