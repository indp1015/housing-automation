Market Analysis

The median home value in Clark County is 22.7% above the Washington state average and 129.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,668/month is 38.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Clark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.2%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
