Market Analysis

The median home value in Clark County is 19.8% above the Washington state average and 129.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,748/month is 39.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Clark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.4%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
