Market Analysis

The median home value in Lane County is 8.4% below the Kansas state average and 44.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $590/month is 26.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lane County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lane County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.9%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
