Market Analysis

The median home value in Sheridan County is 11.6% above the Wyoming state average and 66.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $950/month is 1.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.9, Sheridan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sheridan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.9%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
