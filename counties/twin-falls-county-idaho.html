Market Analysis

The median home value in Twin Falls County is 4.9% below the Idaho state average and 48.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,089/month is 12.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.9, Twin Falls County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Twin Falls County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.5%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
