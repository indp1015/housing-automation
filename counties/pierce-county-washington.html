Market Analysis

The median home value in Pierce County is 20.7% above the Washington state average and 131.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,806/month is 44.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Pierce County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.7%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
