Market Analysis

The median home value in Nassau County is 36.5% above the Florida state average and 66.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,335/month is 8.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Nassau County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.0%, compared to 74.0% in Florida and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
