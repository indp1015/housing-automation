Market Analysis

The median home value in Aitkin County is 5.6% above the Minnesota state average and 11.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $912/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Aitkin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.3%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
