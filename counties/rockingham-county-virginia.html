Market Analysis

The median home value in Rockingham County is 3.5% above the Virginia state average and 36.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,125/month is 1.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Rockingham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.2%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
