Market Analysis

The median home value in Lyon County is 8.5% above the Kansas state average and 33.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $817/month is 1.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lyon County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lyon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 57.9%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
