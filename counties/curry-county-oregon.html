Market Analysis

The median home value in Curry County is 2.7% above the Oregon state average and 73.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,156/month is 1.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Curry County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Curry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.8%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
