Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 6.1% above the Tennessee state average and 4.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $894/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
