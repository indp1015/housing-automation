Market Analysis

The median home value in Yakima County is 29.3% below the Washington state average and 32.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,068/month is 11.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Yakima County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.4%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
