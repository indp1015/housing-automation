Market Analysis

The median home value in Kent County is 22.9% below the Rhode Island state average and 60.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,327/month is 7.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Kent County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.1%, compared to 69.4% in Rhode Island and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
