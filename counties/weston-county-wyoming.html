Market Analysis

The median home value in Weston County is 36.1% below the Wyoming state average and 2.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $698/month is 25.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.5, Weston County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Weston County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.8%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
