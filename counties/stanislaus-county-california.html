Market Analysis

The median home value in Stanislaus County is 25.3% below the California state average and 101.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,528/month is 5.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Stanislaus County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.0%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
