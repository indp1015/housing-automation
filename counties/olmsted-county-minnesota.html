Market Analysis

The median home value in Olmsted County is 39.2% above the Minnesota state average and 45.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,370/month is 46.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Olmsted County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.6%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
