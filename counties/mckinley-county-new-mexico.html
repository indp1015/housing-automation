Market Analysis

The median home value in McKinley County is 59.2% below the New Mexico state average and 65.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $859/month is 5.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 7.6, buying a home in McKinley County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

McKinley County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 74.2% in New Mexico and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
