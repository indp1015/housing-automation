Market Analysis

The median home value in Garfield County is 23.2% above the Colorado state average and 131.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,515/month is 20.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.0, Garfield County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Garfield County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.7%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
