Market Analysis

The median home value in Garfield County is 23.5% above the Colorado state average and 131.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,637/month is 25.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.9, Garfield County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Garfield County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.6%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
