Market Analysis

The median home value in Vilas County is 28.2% above the Wisconsin state average and 31.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $847/month is 7.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.5, Vilas County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Vilas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.0%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
