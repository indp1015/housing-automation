Market Analysis

The median home value in LaGrange County is 33.4% above the Indiana state average and 12.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $839/month is 7.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.4, LaGrange County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

LaGrange County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.0%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
