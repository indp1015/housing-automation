Market Analysis

The median home value in Lowndes County is 20.8% above the Mississippi state average and 27.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $930/month is 15.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.7, buying a home in Lowndes County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lowndes County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.3%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
