Market Analysis

The median home value in Luzerne County is 17.9% below the Pennsylvania state average and 23.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,004/month is 1.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in Luzerne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Luzerne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.8%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
