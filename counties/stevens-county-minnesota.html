Market Analysis

The median home value in Stevens County is 14.9% below the Minnesota state average and 10.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $951/month is 1.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Stevens County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Stevens County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.4%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
