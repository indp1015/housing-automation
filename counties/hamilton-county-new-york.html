Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 9.4% below the New York state average and 14.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $860/month is 24.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.5%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
