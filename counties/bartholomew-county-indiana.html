Market Analysis

The median home value in Bartholomew County is 21.3% above the Indiana state average and 2.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,127/month is 23.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Bartholomew County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Bartholomew County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.3%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
