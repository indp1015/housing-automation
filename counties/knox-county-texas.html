Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 61.3% below the Texas state average and 68.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $661/month is 31.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 8.4, buying a home in Knox County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
