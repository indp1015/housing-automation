Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 17.0% above the Idaho state average and 72.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $965/month is 5.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.5, Madison County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 40.2%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
