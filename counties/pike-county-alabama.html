Market Analysis

The median home value in Pike County is about equal to the Alabama state average and 29.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $855/month is 7.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in Pike County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Pike County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.9%, compared to 72.9% in Alabama and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
