Market Analysis

The median home value in Fremont County is 19.9% below the Wyoming state average and 19.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $858/month is 8.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Fremont County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.7%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
