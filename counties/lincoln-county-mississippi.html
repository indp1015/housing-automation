Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 14.7% above the Mississippi state average and 31.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $760/month is 5.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lincoln County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.9%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
