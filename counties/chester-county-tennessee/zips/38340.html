📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP code 38340 in Chester County, Tennessee, exhibits a robust upward price trajectory over the past five years. The FHFA Home Price Index (HPI) has surged from 164.9 in 2021 to 259.4 in 2025. This represents a cumulative appreciation of approximately 57.3% in just four years. The annual growth rates have remained consistently strong, with the index rising from 164.9 to 202.44 in 2022, 227.24 in 2023, and 239.2 in 2024. Such sustained momentum indicates a market experiencing significant demand pressure relative to its historical baseline. In 2025, the ZIP code recorded a year-over-year increase of 8.46%. The divergence from the state trend highlights that local factors are driving value appreciation more than regional economic conditions. Compared to nearby ZIP 38313, which posted a 9.36% increase, ZIP 38340 remains slightly more conservative in its recent growth, while ZIP 38356 experienced a sharp 25.60% decline. This variance suggests that housing values within Chester County are not uniform, with specific sub-markets exhibiting distinct volatility patterns. The sub-market character of ZIP 38340 aligns with the broader county profile, which reports a median home value of $164,400 and a median household income of $59,341. With an owner-occupancy rate of 75.4%, the area leans toward ownership rather than renting, despite a median gross rent of $756 per month. The current HPI level of 259.4, which is 2.59 times the 2000 baseline, reflects substantial long-term equity accumulation. For prospective buyers, the data indicates a market where prices have risen rapidly but remain below the county’s recent peak growth rates. Residents should note that while the ZIP is appreciating faster than the state, it is lagging behind the county average, suggesting potential for continued alignment with local county trends rather than isolated spikes.
