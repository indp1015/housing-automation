Market Analysis

The median home value in Rutherford County is 14.2% below the North Carolina state average and 11.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $783/month is 19.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Rutherford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.9%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
