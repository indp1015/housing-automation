Market Analysis

The median home value in Boyle County is 30.5% above the Kentucky state average and 7.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $778/month is 1.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Boyle County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.0%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
