Market Analysis

The median home value in Boyle County is 33.7% above the Kentucky state average and 5.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $770/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Boyle County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.5%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
