Market Analysis

The median home value in Arapahoe County is 31.3% above the Colorado state average and 146.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,891/month is 45.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Arapahoe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.7%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
