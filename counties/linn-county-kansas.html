Market Analysis

The median home value in Linn County is 25.5% above the Kansas state average and 22.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $682/month is 13.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Linn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 74.0% in Kansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
