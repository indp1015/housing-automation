Market Analysis

The median home value in Linn County is 25.1% above the Kansas state average and 23.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $691/month is 13.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Linn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.1%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
