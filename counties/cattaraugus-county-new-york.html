📝 Local Market Analysis

Cattaraugus County, New York, functions as a distinctly buyer’s market, characterized by significant affordability advantages relative to national benchmarks. The median home value of $113,100 is 50.3% below the national average and 59.8% below the state average, resulting in a price-to-income ratio of 1.90, nearly half the national standard of 3.20. Similarly, the rent burden stands at 15.5%, lower than the 17.2% national average, while the price-to-rent ratio of 12.2 indicates that purchasing is financially more advantageous than renting compared to most U.S. regions. These metrics confirm a market where entry costs are low, driven largely by values that remain suppressed despite recent appreciation. Recent momentum reveals a sharp acceleration in home values, with the FHFA House Price Index showing an 8.42% year-over-year increase, outpacing the 3.55% state growth rate by 4.87 percentage points. This surge follows a 13.6% jump in 2022, suggesting a sustained upward correction rather than a temporary spike. However, this price appreciation is not yet matched by equivalent rent growth, as indicated by the similar rise rates in the broader trend data. The local economy supports this stability through a 75.2% owner-occupancy rate and positive net migration of 1,008 filers in 2021-2022, which adds demand pressure. Yet, the economic base remains modest, with only 19,116 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,030, limiting the purchasing power of the local workforce. The primary constraint on this market is vitality, evidenced by a low score of 38.6, which reflects limited new housing supply and a smaller economic engine. Only 150 new units were authorized in 2025, with 124 being single-family homes, indicating a tight supply pipeline that may sustain price gains. High momentum scores of 93.0 are driven by the recent price acceleration, while stability remains moderate at 58.0 due to the disparity between rapid value increases and slower income growth. For potential buyers, the current 7.40% mortgage rate complicates affordability, but the low baseline home values mitigate this impact significantly. Renters face a stable but non-urgent market, with rents rising in tandem with values. The data suggests a market in transition, where affordability remains the dominant feature, but the recent price momentum signals a narrowing gap between Cattaraugus County and the broader New York State market.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Cattaraugus County is 59.8% below the New York state average and 50.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $770/month is 34.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.2, buying a home in Cattaraugus County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cattaraugus County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.2%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
