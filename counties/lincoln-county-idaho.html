Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 33.7% below the Idaho state average and 2.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $860/month is 6.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
