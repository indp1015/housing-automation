Market Analysis

The median home value in Nueces County is 12.1% above the Texas state average and 8.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,283/month is 28.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Nueces County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Nueces County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.7%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
