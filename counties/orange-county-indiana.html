Market Analysis

The median home value in Orange County is 19.2% below the Indiana state average and 32.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $838/month is 5.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Orange County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Orange County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 76.0% in Indiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
