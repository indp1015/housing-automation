📝 Local Market Analysis

Montgomery County, Iowa, presents a distinct buyer’s market characterized by significant affordability advantages relative to national benchmarks. The median home value of $121,400 stands 46.7% below the national average, while the price-to-income ratio of 1.91 is substantially lower than the national mean of 3.20. This affordability is further supported by a rent burden of 15.3%, which is below the national average of 17.2%. Consequently, the price-to-rent ratio of 12.4 indicates that purchasing is economically more favorable than renting when compared to the national standard of 18.7. These metrics collectively suggest that home equity accumulation is more accessible for local households, provided they possess stable income streams. The market is experiencing a pronounced upward trajectory in home values, diverging sharply from historical norms. Between 2022 and 2024, median home values surged by over 23%, with annual increases of 12.6%, 10.7%, and 10.5% respectively. This rapid appreciation contrasts with the relatively stable rent trajectory, where the median gross rent remains at $814. The FHFA House Price Index confirms this momentum, showing a year-over-year county change of +15.50%, which outpaces the state’s +2.25% growth by 13.25 percentage points. This divergence suggests that capital gains are currently driven by limited supply and high demand rather than broad-based income growth, as the average weekly wage of $955 has not kept pace with the 10-12% annual home price increases observed in recent years. Regional context and economic fundamentals shape this dynamic. The county’s population of 10,214 supports a modest housing supply, with only 29 new units authorized in 2025, including just 6 single-family homes. This constrained supply likely contributes to the sustained price appreciation. Migration data indicates a net inflow of 327 filers, with incoming residents earning an average of $66,037, slightly below the outflow average, suggesting a shift in demographic composition. Despite the national 30-year mortgage rate of 7.40%, which typically suppresses demand, the local market continues to see strong price gains. For potential buyers, the current environment offers entry points well below national averages, though the rapid price escalation necessitates careful consideration of long-term carrying costs relative to static wage growth. Renters may find that the low rent burden remains a viable alternative, but the widening gap between rental costs and home prices may eventually pressure rents upward.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 29.0% below the Iowa state average and 46.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $814/month is 2.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.4, buying a home in Montgomery County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.1%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
