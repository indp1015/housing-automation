Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 31.6% below the Iowa state average and 48.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $782/month is 3.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.7, buying a home in Montgomery County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.6%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
