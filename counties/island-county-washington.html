Market Analysis

The median home value in Island County is 34.6% above the Washington state average and 153.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,631/month is 35.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.4, Island County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Island County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.6%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
