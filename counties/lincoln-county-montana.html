Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 2.9% above the Montana state average and 22.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $810/month is 1.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.6, Lincoln County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.0%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
