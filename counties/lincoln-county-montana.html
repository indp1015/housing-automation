Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 7.1% above the Montana state average and 30.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $762/month is 9.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.4, Lincoln County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.6%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
