Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 25.4% above the Tennessee state average and 23.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,308/month is 46.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Montgomery County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.9%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
