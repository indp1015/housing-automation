Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 39.9% above the Tennessee state average and 33.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,163/month is 35.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.3%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
