Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 40.0% above the Tennessee state average and 37.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,253/month is 40.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
