📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP 62948's FHFA HPI rose from 157.63 in 2021 to a peak of 207.82 in 2024, a cumulative gain of roughly 32% over three years, before declining to 198.46 in 2025. That 2025 reading represents a 4.50% year-over-year contraction and leaves the index about 4.5% below its 2024 high, though still well above its 2021 level. The trajectory therefore shows a strong pandemic-era appreciation phase followed by a notable single-year reversal. That reversal diverges sharply from the surrounding market. Williamson County posted +5.04% year-over-year and Illinois +5.54%, while the neighboring ZIP 62959 recorded +7.60% at a nearly identical HPI level of 199.0. A roughly 9.5-percentage-point gap between ZIP 62948 and its county indicates the decline is localized rather than regional, and it occurs against a 7.40% 30-year mortgage rate that constrains affordability broadly. The sub-market profile is moderate and owner-occupied rather than luxury. The county's $156,400 median home value, $930 median gross rent, and $65,604 median household income, combined with 70.7% owner occupancy, describe a middle-income, primarily owner-occupied market. At an HPI of 198.5, ZIP 62948 sits essentially level with ZIP 62959, suggesting comparable pricing rather than a premium or discount. Prospective buyers should note that this ZIP is currently moving opposite to its county and state, and that the 2025 softening follows an unusually steep run-up, making local price discovery and recent comparable sales more informative than county-level averages.
