Market Analysis

The median home value in Portage County is 15.6% above the Ohio state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,064/month is 17.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Portage County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Portage County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.1%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
