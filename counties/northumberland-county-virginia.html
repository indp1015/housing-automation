Market Analysis

The median home value in Northumberland County is 19.8% above the Virginia state average and 57.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,053/month is 7.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Northumberland County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Northumberland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 89.3%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
