Market Analysis

The median home value in Banks County is 40.5% above the Georgia state average and 22.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $827/month is 14.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.0, Banks County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Banks County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.7%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
