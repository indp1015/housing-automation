Market Analysis

The median home value in Dubois County is 19.3% above the Indiana state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $801/month is 9.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dubois County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.0%, compared to 76.0% in Indiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
