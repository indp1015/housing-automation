Market Analysis

The median home value in Martin County is 46.0% below the Kentucky state average and 61.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $450/month is 43.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Martin County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Martin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.9%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
