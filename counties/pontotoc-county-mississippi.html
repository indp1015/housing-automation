📝 Local Market Analysis

Pontotoc County, Mississippi, exhibits characteristics of a buyer’s market, driven by a price-to-rent ratio of 15.2, which sits significantly below the national average of 18.7. This metric suggests that purchasing is more financially efficient than renting, a structural advantage reinforced by the county’s median home value of $157,100. Despite this relative affordability, the market faces underlying stressors. The price-to-income ratio stands at 3.05, marginally below the national benchmark of 3.20, indicating that while homes are cheaper than the national norm, local incomes of $51,484 remain 24.3% below the national average. Consequently, the rent burden is elevated at 20.1% of income compared to the 17.2% national standard, suggesting that while buying is preferable, the overall cost of living remains tight for average households. The multi-year trend reveals a period of rapid appreciation followed by recent stabilization. Home values surged from $102,100 in 2017 to $157,100 in 2024, with a peak year-over-year increase of 17.1% in 2022. However, recent momentum has cooled; the FHFA House Price Index shows a year-over-year decline of 2.13% in 2025, underperforming the state average by 2.73 percentage points. This divergence between the long-term upward trajectory and short-term correction signals a market adjusting to high interest rates, currently at 7.40%. The economic foundation is modest, with total employment at 10,070 jobs and an average weekly wage of $828. Housing supply remains limited, with only 94 new units authorized in 2025, split between 26 single-family and 68 multi-family homes. This constrained supply, coupled with positive net migration of 394 filers, supports price resilience despite the recent index dip. The composite assessment reflects a market with moderate vitality but significant stability concerns. The low affordability score stems from the disparity between rising asset values and stagnant local wages, while the stability metric is dampened by the recent negative price momentum. Nevertheless, the county outperforms the state average in home value by 15.7%, indicating relative strength within Mississippi. For potential buyers, the current environment offers entry points below national price levels, yet the high mortgage rate and limited job growth necessitate careful financial planning. Renter’s alternatives are less attractive due to the favorable price-to-rent dynamic, making ownership the more viable long-term strategy for those with stable income, despite the current cooling of price appreciation.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Pontotoc County is 15.7% above the Mississippi state average and 31.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $864/month is 7.5% above the state average.
