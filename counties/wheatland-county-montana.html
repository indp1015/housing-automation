Market Analysis

The median home value in Wheatland County is 34.7% below the Montana state average and 20.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $730/month is 13.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Wheatland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
