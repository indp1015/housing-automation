Market Analysis

The median home value in Bath County is 19.3% below the Kentucky state average and 43.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $659/month is 16.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Bath County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Bath County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
