Market Analysis

The median home value in Delaware County is 27.4% below the Indiana state average and 38.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $906/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Delaware County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Delaware County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.5%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
