Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 5.0% below the Oregon state average and 60.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $957/month is 15.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.5, Jefferson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.4%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
