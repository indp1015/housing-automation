📝 Local Market Analysis

Tallahatchie County operates as a distinct renter’s market, characterized by a price-to-rent ratio of 9.4, significantly below the national average of 18.7. This metric, combined with a median home value of $82,300 and a median gross rent of $732, indicates that rental costs are disproportionately high relative to asset values. The affordability landscape is mixed; while the price-to-income ratio of 2.20 is favorable compared to the national benchmark of 3.20, the rent burden stands at 23.4% of median household income, exceeding the national average of 17.2%. This divergence suggests that while purchasing remains accessible, the cost of renting consumes a substantial portion of local earnings, particularly given a median household income of $37,466. The market exhibits a sharp structural shift following a 20.0% surge in home values in 2022, which has since stabilized with a 0.1% increase in 2024. This recent price plateau contrasts with the trend signal indicating that rents are rising faster than home values, creating a narrowing gap between the two housing sectors. Economically, the county faces constraints, with only 1,824 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $945. Housing supply is critically low, with just four new units authorized in 2025, all of which are multi-family. This scarcity likely contributes to elevated rental pressures. Migration data shows a net gain of 176 filers, but the average income of incoming households ($43,405) is only marginally higher than outflowing residents ($43,721), suggesting limited demographic upgrading power. The overall market assessment reflects high affordability but low stability and vitality. The low vitality score is driven by the minimal construction activity and modest employment base, while the stability metric is undermined by the volatility seen in the 2022 price spike. For potential buyers, the current environment offers entry points at values 63.9% below the national average, yet the high rent burden implies that leasing is not a low-cost alternative. The 7.40% national mortgage rate further complicates financing, though the low home prices mitigate some of this impact. Ultimately, the market is defined by a static price environment where rental costs are outpacing asset appreciation, signaling a shift in value dynamics that favors those who can secure ownership before further supply constraints tighten.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Tallahatchie County is 39.4% below the Mississippi state average and 63.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $732/month is 9.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.4, buying a home in Tallahatchie County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Tallahatchie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.5%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
