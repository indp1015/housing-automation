Market Analysis

The median home value in Sioux County is 51.4% below the North Dakota state average and 62.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $444/month is 42.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Sioux County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Sioux County has an owner-occupancy rate of 46.6%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
