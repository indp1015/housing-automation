Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 7.4% above the Wyoming state average and 63.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $960/month is 2.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.3, Lincoln County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
