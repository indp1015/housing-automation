Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 6.0% above the Wyoming state average and 58.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $868/month is 7.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.1, Lincoln County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.1%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
