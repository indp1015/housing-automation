Market Analysis

The median home value in Dakota County is 12.8% above the Nebraska state average and 18.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,069/month is 34.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in Dakota County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Dakota County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
