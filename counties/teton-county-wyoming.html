Market Analysis

The median home value in Teton County is 334.8% above the Wyoming state average and 548.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,758/month is 87.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 65.0, Teton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Teton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.5%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
