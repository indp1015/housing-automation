Market Analysis

The median home value in Teton County is 370.9% above the Wyoming state average and 617.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,904/month is 102.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 71.5, Teton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Teton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 58.3%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
