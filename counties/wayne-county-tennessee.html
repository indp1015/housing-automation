Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 41.5% below the Tennessee state average and 42.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $619/month is 30.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Wayne County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.2%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
