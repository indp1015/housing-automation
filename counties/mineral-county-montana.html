Market Analysis

The median home value in Mineral County is 24.7% above the Montana state average and 48.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $746/month is 9.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 35.0, Mineral County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Mineral County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.3%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
