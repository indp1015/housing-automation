Market Analysis

The median home value in Mineral County is 31.6% above the Montana state average and 60.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $801/month is 4.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 37.9, Mineral County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Mineral County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.1%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
