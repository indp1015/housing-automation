Market Analysis

The median home value in Lancaster County is 10.5% above the Virginia state average and 43.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,033/month is 11.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Lancaster County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lancaster County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.5%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
