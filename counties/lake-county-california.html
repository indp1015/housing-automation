Market Analysis

The median home value in Lake County is 44.5% below the California state average and 49.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,292/month is 20.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.9%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
