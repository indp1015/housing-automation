Market Analysis

The median home value in Kauai County is 9.3% above the Hawaii state average and 283.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,870/month is 1.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.9, Kauai County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Kauai County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.6%, compared to 67.0% in Hawaii and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
