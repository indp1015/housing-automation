Market Analysis

The median home value in DeKalb County is 59.2% above the Illinois state average and 7.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,049/month is 21.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in DeKalb County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

DeKalb County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.4%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
