Market Analysis

The median home value in Los Angeles County is 36.9% above the California state average and 266.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,954/month is 15.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 35.6, Los Angeles County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Los Angeles County has an owner-occupancy rate of 45.9%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
