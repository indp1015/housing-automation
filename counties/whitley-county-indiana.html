Market Analysis

The median home value in Whitley County is 14.3% above the Indiana state average and 3.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $934/month is 2.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Whitley County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Whitley County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.8%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
