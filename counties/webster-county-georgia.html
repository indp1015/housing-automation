Market Analysis

The median home value in Webster County is 58.3% below the Georgia state average and 63.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $658/month is 31.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.5, buying a home in Webster County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Webster County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
