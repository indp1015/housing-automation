Market Analysis

The median home value in Lumpkin County is 53.3% above the Georgia state average and 31.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,090/month is 17.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lumpkin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
