Market Analysis

The median home value in East Feliciana Parish is 36.3% above the Louisiana state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $911/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

East Feliciana Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.6%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
