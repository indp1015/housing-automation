Market Analysis

The median home value in East Feliciana Parish is 32.3% above the Louisiana state average and 1.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $745/month is 14.7% below the state average.

East Feliciana Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.8%, compared to 71.7% in Louisiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
