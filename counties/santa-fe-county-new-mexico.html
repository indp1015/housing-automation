Market Analysis

The median home value in Santa Fe County is 129.5% above the New Mexico state average and 97.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,318/month is 50.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Santa Fe County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Santa Fe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.6%, compared to 73.3% in New Mexico and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
