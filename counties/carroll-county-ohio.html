Market Analysis

The median home value in Carroll County is 6.7% below the Ohio state average and 19.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $863/month is 4.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Carroll County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Carroll County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.7%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
