Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 12.1% above the Ohio state average and 3.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $849/month is 2.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 73.4% in Ohio and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
