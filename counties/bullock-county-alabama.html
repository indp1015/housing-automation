Market Analysis

The median home value in Bullock County is 48.8% below the Alabama state average and 64.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $556/month is 32.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.2, buying a home in Bullock County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Bullock County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.6%, compared to 73.2% in Alabama and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
