Market Analysis

The median home value in Sangamon County is 15.2% above the Illinois state average and 22.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $983/month is 13.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Sangamon County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Sangamon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
