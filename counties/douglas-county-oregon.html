Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 20.7% below the Oregon state average and 33.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $995/month is 12.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.8%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
