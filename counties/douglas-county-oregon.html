Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 19.8% below the Oregon state average and 36.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,055/month is 11.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.7%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
