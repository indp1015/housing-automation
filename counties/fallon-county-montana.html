Market Analysis

The median home value in Fallon County is 5.8% below the Montana state average and 12.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $772/month is 6.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.6, Fallon County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fallon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
