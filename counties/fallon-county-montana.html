Market Analysis

The median home value in Fallon County is 11.6% below the Montana state average and 7.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $779/month is 7.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Fallon County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fallon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.4%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
