Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 9.5% above the Texas state average and 10.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,065/month is 6.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Jackson County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.8%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
