Market Analysis

The median home value in Bernalillo County is 52.0% above the New Mexico state average and 28.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,144/month is 26.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Bernalillo County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.5%, compared to 74.2% in New Mexico and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
