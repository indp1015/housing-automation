📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 45426 exhibits a distinct pattern of recent stabilization after a period of rapid appreciation. The Home Price Index (HPI) for this area rose from 120.27 in 2021 to 169.33 in 2025, indicating a cumulative increase of approximately 40% over four years. However, the most recent year-over-year change of +0.22% suggests that price growth has effectively plateaued. This sharp deceleration contrasts with the broader regional trends, signaling a localized market correction or a shift in demand dynamics specific to this sub-market. This performance diverges significantly from both the county and state averages. While Montgomery County recorded a +5.66% year-over-year increase and the state of Ohio grew by +4.33%, ZIP 45426 remained nearly flat. This underperformance relative to the surrounding macro-environment indicates that the area is not currently benefiting from the same momentum driving other parts of the county. Nearby ZIP codes, such as 45405 and 45439, are experiencing robust double-digit or high single-digit growth, further highlighting 45426’s relative stagnation. The sub-market character of 45426 aligns with entry-level or mid-range housing stock. The parent county’s median home value stands at $180,900, and the ZIP’s HPI of 169.3 (indexed to 2000) reflects a moderate price point rather than a luxury segment. With an owner-occupancy rate of 62.5% and a median household income of $66,139, the area serves a typical suburban demographic. For prospective buyers, the current environment offers a potential entry point, as price appreciation has paused while the broader county continues to rise.
