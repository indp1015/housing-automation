Market Analysis

The median home value in Okmulgee County is 20.3% below the Oklahoma state average and 46.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $834/month is 0.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.1, buying a home in Okmulgee County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Okmulgee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.1%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
