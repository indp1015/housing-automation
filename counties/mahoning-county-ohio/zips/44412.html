📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market within ZIP code 44412 exhibits a pronounced upward trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) has surged from 139.12 in 2021 to 215.98 in 2025, representing a cumulative appreciation of approximately 55 percent. This rapid expansion was punctuated by a minor contraction in 2024, where the index dipped slightly to 183.16, before rebounding sharply in the latest data. Such volatility suggests a market experiencing significant recent momentum, likely driven by localized demand surges that outpaced broader regional trends. ZIP 44412’s year-over-year growth of 17.92 percent stands in stark contrast to the state average of 4.33 percent. This substantial divergence indicates that the sub-market is decoupling from general Ohio housing dynamics, potentially reflecting unique local economic drivers or inventory constraints specific to this area. While neighboring ZIP 44437 also shows strong growth at 12.95 percent, ZIP 44412 is outpacing both its immediate peers and the broader state, signaling a distinct micro-market phenomenon rather than a uniform county-wide trend. Contextualizing this growth with Mahoning County’s American Community Survey data reveals a mid-range sub-market. With a county median home value of $154,700 and a median household income of $56,942, the area remains accessible to middle-income buyers rather than serving as a luxury enclave. The 70.3 percent owner-occupancy rate further supports a stable, homeownership-oriented community. However, the current 30-year mortgage rate of 7.32 percent complicates entry for new buyers, as high interest rates may dampen future demand despite recent price gains. Prospective residents should note that while price appreciation has been robust, the entry price point remains moderate relative to national standards. The market character suggests a competitive environment for existing homeowners, but potential affordability challenges for first-time buyers navigating high borrowing costs in a rapidly appreciating zone.
