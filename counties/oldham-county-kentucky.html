Market Analysis

The median home value in Oldham County is 149.7% above the Kentucky state average and 77.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,142/month is 48.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.4, Oldham County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Oldham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.1%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
