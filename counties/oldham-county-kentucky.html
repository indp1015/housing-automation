Market Analysis

The median home value in Oldham County is 144.4% above the Kentucky state average and 72.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,219/month is 53.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.9, Oldham County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Oldham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.1%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
