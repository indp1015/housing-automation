Market Analysis

The median home value in Somerset County is 53.2% below the Maryland state average and 22.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $938/month is 33.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Somerset County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Somerset County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.8%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
