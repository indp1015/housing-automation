Market Analysis

The median home value in Somerset County is 53.2% below the Maryland state average and 23.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,013/month is 30.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Somerset County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Somerset County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.1%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
