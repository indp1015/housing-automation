Market Analysis

The median home value in Linn County is 29.8% below the Missouri state average and 45.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $620/month is 20.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Linn County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Linn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
