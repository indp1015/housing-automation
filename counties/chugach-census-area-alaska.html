Market Analysis

The median home value in Chugach Census Area is 22.1% above the Alaska state average and 52.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,369/month is 9.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Chugach Census Area has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.8%, compared to 64.7% in Alaska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
