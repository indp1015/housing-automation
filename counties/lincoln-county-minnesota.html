Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 31.0% below the Minnesota state average and 27.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $674/month is 28.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.4%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
