Market Analysis

The median home value in Fremont County is 29.1% below the Colorado state average and 33.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,036/month is 17.3% below the state average.

Fremont County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.2%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
