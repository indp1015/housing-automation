Market Analysis

The median home value in Sherman County is 45.2% below the Oregon state average and 7.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,032/month is 13.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Sherman County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Sherman County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.9%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
