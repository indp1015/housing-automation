Market Analysis

The median home value in Avery County is 13.0% above the North Carolina state average and 16.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $773/month is 20.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.7, Avery County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Avery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.0%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
