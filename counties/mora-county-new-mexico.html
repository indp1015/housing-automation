Market Analysis

The median home value in Mora County is 36.4% below the New Mexico state average and 46.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $887/month is 1.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.5, buying a home in Mora County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Mora County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.7%, compared to 74.2% in New Mexico and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
