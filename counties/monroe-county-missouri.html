Market Analysis

The median home value in Monroe County is 15.0% below the Missouri state average and 34.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $689/month is 11.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Monroe County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Monroe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
