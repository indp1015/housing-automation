Market Analysis

The median home value in Richland Parish is 15.2% below the Louisiana state average and 37.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $903/month is 1.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.1, buying a home in Richland Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Richland Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.8%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
