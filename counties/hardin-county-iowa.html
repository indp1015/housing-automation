Market Analysis

The median home value in Hardin County is 29.6% below the Iowa state average and 46.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $814/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.6, buying a home in Hardin County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hardin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.3%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
