Market Analysis

The median home value in Darlington County is 20.8% below the South Carolina state average and 30.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $896/month is 8.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.7, buying a home in Darlington County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Darlington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.9%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
