Market Analysis

The median home value in Boone County is 37.1% below the West Virginia state average and 59.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $837/month is 7.7% above the state average.

Boone County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
