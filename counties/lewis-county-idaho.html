Market Analysis

The median home value in Lewis County is 36.6% below the Idaho state average and 1.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $737/month is 23.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Lewis County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lewis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
