Market Analysis

The median home value in Lewis County is 35.1% below the Idaho state average and 4.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $708/month is 23.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lewis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
