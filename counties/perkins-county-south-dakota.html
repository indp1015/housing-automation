Market Analysis

The median home value in Perkins County is 36.7% below the South Dakota state average and 49.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $738/month is 5.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Perkins County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Perkins County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
