Market Analysis

The median home value in Brewster County is 21.9% above the Texas state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $839/month is 15.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Brewster County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
