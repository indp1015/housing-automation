Market Analysis

The median home value in Sioux County is 46.7% above the Iowa state average and 11.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $888/month is 9.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sioux County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.8%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
