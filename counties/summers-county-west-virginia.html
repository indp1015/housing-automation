Market Analysis

The median home value in Summers County is 5.3% below the West Virginia state average and 38.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $915/month is 17.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Summers County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Summers County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
