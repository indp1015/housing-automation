Market Analysis

The median home value in Jay County is 38.8% below the Indiana state average and 48.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $770/month is 15.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.7, buying a home in Jay County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.9%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
