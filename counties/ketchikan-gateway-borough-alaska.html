Market Analysis

The median home value in Ketchikan Gateway Borough is 38.7% above the Alaska state average and 72.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,505/month is 20.6% above the state average.

Ketchikan Gateway Borough has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.6%, compared to 64.7% in Alaska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
