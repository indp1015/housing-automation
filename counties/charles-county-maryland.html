📝 Local Market Analysis

Charles County, Maryland, functions as a distinctively balanced market, characterized by strong stability and moderate momentum rather than extreme buyer or renter advantages. The price-to-rent ratio of 18.6 aligns almost perfectly with the national average of 18.7, suggesting that the relative cost of owning versus renting remains standard for the region. However, the market exhibits a clear ownership bias, evidenced by an 82.1% owner-occupancy rate and a price-to-income ratio of 3.49. This ratio exceeds the national benchmark of 3.20, indicating that while the market is not strictly a renter’s haven, it presents higher entry barriers for first-time buyers compared to the broader national landscape. The rent burden of 18.8% of income is slightly elevated relative to the national average of 17.2%, further tightening the financial constraints for tenants. The multi-year trend reveals a period of accelerated appreciation that has recently moderated. Home values surged 12.9% in 2022, followed by continued growth of 6.5% in 2024, bringing the median home value to $428,500. This trajectory significantly outpaces the state’s recent 0.95% year-over-year growth, with the county outperforming the state by 0.60 percentage points according to FHFA data. This sustained appreciation is supported by robust demographic inflows, with net migration adding 3,677 filers between 2021 and 2022. The local economy underpins this demand, offering an average weekly wage of $1,037 and a median household income of $122,816, which is 80.6% above the national average. Despite this economic strength, housing supply remains constrained, with 935 new units authorized in 2025, all of which are single-family, leaving a void in the multi-family sector that likely contributes to rental pressure. Affordability remains the primary constraint in this market, driving the lower composite assessment despite high stability. The median home value is 88.2% above the national average, a premium justified by the county’s superior income levels but still resulting in a tighter affordability profile than the national norm. The current 7.40% mortgage rate further complicates purchasing power, as high interest costs amplify the impact of the elevated price-to-income ratio. For prospective buyers, the data suggests a market where high incomes do not fully offset the premium pricing, requiring careful budgeting. Renter options are limited by the lack of new multi-family construction, potentially sustaining higher rental costs. The market’s vitality is sustained by consistent job growth and population inflows, yet the divergence between high price appreciation and moderate rent growth indicates that ownership equity is accumulating faster than rental costs, favoring long-term holders over new entrants. Stability remains high, reflecting the county’s resilient economic base and consistent demand patterns.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Charles County is 15.8% above the Maryland state average and 88.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,920/month is 31.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Charles County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Charles County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.1%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
