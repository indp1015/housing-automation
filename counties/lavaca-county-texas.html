Market Analysis

The median home value in Lavaca County is 22.6% above the Texas state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $910/month is 8.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lavaca County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.3%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
