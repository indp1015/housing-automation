Market Analysis

The median home value in Manitowoc County is 20.2% below the Wisconsin state average and 17.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $797/month is 12.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Manitowoc County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Manitowoc County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.1%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
