Market Analysis

The median home value in Bristol County is 28.9% below the Massachusetts state average and 98.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,245/month is 22.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.2, Bristol County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bristol County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.3%, compared to 67.2% in Massachusetts and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
