Market Analysis

The median home value in Bristol County is 28.2% below the Massachusetts state average and 99.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,181/month is 24.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.8, Bristol County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bristol County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.2%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
