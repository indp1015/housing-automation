Market Analysis

The median home value in Uintah County is 28.4% below the Utah state average and 27.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $914/month is 16.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Uintah County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.0%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
