Market Analysis

The median home value in Uintah County is 28.7% below the Utah state average and 31.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $941/month is 18.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Uintah County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Uintah County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.5%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
