Market Analysis

The median home value in Greater Bridgeport Planning Region is 12.8% above the Connecticut state average and 88.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,546/month is 6.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Greater Bridgeport Planning Region has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.3%, compared to 69.0% in Connecticut and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
