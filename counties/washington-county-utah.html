Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 22.0% above the Utah state average and 124.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,566/month is 36.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.2, Washington County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.6%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
