Market Analysis

The median home value in Brown County is 40.2% above the Indiana state average and 18.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $895/month is 1.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Brown County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Brown County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.6%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
