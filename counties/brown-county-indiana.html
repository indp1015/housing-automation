Market Analysis

The median home value in Brown County is 36.2% above the Indiana state average and 14.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $960/month is 8.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Brown County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.9%, compared to 76.0% in Indiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
