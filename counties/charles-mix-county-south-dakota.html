Market Analysis

The median home value in Charles Mix County is 5.8% below the South Dakota state average and 25.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $704/month is 10.2% below the state average.

Charles Mix County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.3%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
