Lamar County exhibits a distinct buyer’s advantage, evidenced by a 74.0% owner-occupancy rate and a price-to-rent ratio of 17.1, which sits below the national average of 18.0. This metric suggests that purchasing offers better long-term value than renting. Prospective buyers must act promptly to secure properties before the widening gap between income growth and asset appreciation erodes current purchasing power advantages.
