Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 33.9% above the North Carolina state average and 38.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,178/month is 20.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
