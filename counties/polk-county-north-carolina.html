Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 31.3% above the North Carolina state average and 32.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,089/month is 16.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.6%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
