📝 Local Market Analysis

The median home value of $156,100 and median rent of $869 per month, together with a price‑to‑income ratio of 2.18 and a rent‑burden of 14.6 % of household income, indicate a balanced market where buyer demand and rental demand are both present but constrained by limited new supply. Multi‑year price trends show a rise from $104,500 in 2017 to $156,100 in 2024, punctuated by a modest decline in 2020 and a sharp 13.9 % increase in 2022, while rent growth has remained modest, resulting in a price‑to‑rent ratio of 15.0, below the national 18.7. Net migration of +244 filers between 2021 and 2022, combined with only 11 building permits in 2025, suggests sustained demand outpaces construction, reinforcing price appreciation. Affordability is strong relative to national benchmarks: the price‑to‑income ratio is 31.4 % lower than the national average and rent burden is 15.2 % below the national 17.2 %, yielding an affordability rating well above the composite average. In contrast, stability scores are depressed because price volatility and a 7.40 % mortgage rate limit transaction fluidity, while vitality lags due to minimal new‑unit authorizations and a modest 5.1 % higher household income than the national mean. The county outperforms Minnesota, where home values are 34.5 % higher and the state’s year‑over‑year HPI change is only 2.42 % versus the county’s 11.38 %, indicating superior recent momentum. For a prospective buyer, the combination of low price‑to‑income, limited inventory, and rising prices implies careful budgeting and possible competition, whereas a renter benefits from a rent burden that remains well under national levels despite modest rent growth. The strong HPI gain signals continued price pressure, and the local employment base of 3,850 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,014 provides a stable income foundation for financing decisions. The limited new‑unit authorizations of only 11 in 2025 further constrain supply, amplifying price pressure.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Chippewa County is 34.5% below the Minnesota state average and 31.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $869/month is 7.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Chippewa County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Chippewa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.3%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
