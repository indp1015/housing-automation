Market Analysis

The median home value in Eastland County is 27.6% below the Texas state average and 40.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $866/month is 13.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.0, buying a home in Eastland County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Eastland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.6%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
