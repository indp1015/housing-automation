Market Analysis

The median home value in Morgan County is 14.5% below the Illinois state average and 42.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $722/month is 16.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Morgan County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Morgan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.8%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
