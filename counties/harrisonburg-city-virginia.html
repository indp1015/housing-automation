Market Analysis

The median home value in Harrisonburg city is 1.1% above the Virginia state average and 31.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,184/month is 1.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Harrisonburg city has an owner-occupancy rate of 39.5%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
