Market Analysis

The median home value in Williamson County is about equal to the Illinois state average and 31.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $897/month is 7.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Williamson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Williamson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.1%, compared to 75.7% in Illinois and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
