Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 28.9% below the West Virginia state average and 53.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $771/month is 0.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.4, buying a home in Lincoln County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
