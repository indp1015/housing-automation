Market Analysis

The median home value in Talbot County is 13.6% above the Maryland state average and 88.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,248/month is 11.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.6, Talbot County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Talbot County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.7%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
