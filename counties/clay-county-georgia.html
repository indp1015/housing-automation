Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 52.3% below the Georgia state average and 58.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $536/month is 44.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.7, buying a home in Clay County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.3%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
