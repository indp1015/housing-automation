Market Analysis

The median home value in Niobrara County is 36.2% below the Wyoming state average and 2.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $605/month is 35.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.5, Niobrara County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Niobrara County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.8%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
