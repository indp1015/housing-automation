Market Analysis

The median home value in Roosevelt County is 57.1% below the Montana state average and 47.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $705/month is 16.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.0, buying a home in Roosevelt County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Roosevelt County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.6%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
