Market Analysis

The median home value in Craig County is 24.3% below the Virginia state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $728/month is 36.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Craig County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.0%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
