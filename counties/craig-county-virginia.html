Market Analysis

The median home value in Craig County is 36.1% below the Virginia state average and 17.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $713/month is 39.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Craig County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.7%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
