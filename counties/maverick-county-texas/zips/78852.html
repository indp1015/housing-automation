📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP 78852's FHFA HPI climbed from 183.84 in 2021 to 262.39 in 2025, a cumulative gain of approximately 42.7% across four years. Annual appreciation ran 16.0% in 2022, 6.2% in 2023, 8.2% in 2024, and 7.03% in 2025, indicating a post-2021 deceleration followed by a renewed acceleration in the most recent two years. At 262.4, the index sits roughly 2.6 times its 2000 baseline. The ZIP's +7.03% year-over-year change slightly exceeds Maverick County's +6.70% and far outpaces Texas's +0.43%. That divergence signals that appreciation in 78852 is driven by local conditions rather than statewide momentum; Texas as a whole was essentially flat over the period while this ZIP continued to post mid-single-digit gains. The parent county's ACS profile places 78852 in entry-level, owner-dominant territory. Median home value is $158,700 against median household income of $49,568, a price-to-income ratio near 3.
