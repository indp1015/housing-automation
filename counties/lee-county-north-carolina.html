Market Analysis

The median home value in Lee County is 2.1% above the North Carolina state average and 5.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,017/month is 4.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.3%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
