Market Analysis

The median home value in Gove County is 14.7% below the Kansas state average and 48.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $705/month is 12.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.0, buying a home in Gove County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Gove County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.4%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
