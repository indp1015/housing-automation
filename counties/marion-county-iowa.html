Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 36.2% above the Iowa state average and 2.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $884/month is 6.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.7%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
