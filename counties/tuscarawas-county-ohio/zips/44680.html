📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 44680 exhibits a volatile yet ultimately upward price trajectory over the past five years. The FHFA Home Price Index (HPI) for this area rose from 164.19 in 2021 to 219.92 in 2025, representing a cumulative increase of approximately 34%. This growth was not linear; after a significant surge to 195.02 in 2022, the index experienced a contraction to 187.7 in 2023. However, the market rebounded sharply in 2024 and 2025, indicating a recovery from the mid-cycle dip and establishing new local price highs. Regarding relative performance, ZIP 44680 posted a year-over-year increase of 3.83% in 2025. This figure trails both the Tuscarawas County average of 4.52% and the Ohio state average of 4.33%. This slight underperformance suggests that the specific sub-market is cooling marginally compared to broader regional trends. Nevertheless, the absolute HPI level of 219.9 remains robust, signaling sustained demand despite the slower recent pace of appreciation. The sub-market character of 44680 aligns with the broader economic profile of Tuscarawas County. With a median household income of $65,044 and a median home value of $185,400, the area functions as an affordable, owner-occupied market rather than a luxury or high-growth tech hub. The 68.9% owner-occupancy rate further confirms a stable residential base. Compared to nearby ZIP 44622, which has a lower HPI of 198.2, ZIP 44680 commands a premium, suggesting slightly higher perceived value or desirability within the county. For prospective buyers, the current environment offers a balance of affordability and established equity. While mortgage rates stand at 7.40%, limiting purchasing power, the recent price stabilization provides a clearer entry point than the volatile 2022-2023 period. Residents should note that while the market is not outpacing the state, it maintains solid structural integrity, making it a viable option for those seeking long-term stability in a mid-priced Ohio market.
