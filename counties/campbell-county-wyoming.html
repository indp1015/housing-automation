Market Analysis

The median home value in Campbell County is 15.6% below the Wyoming state average and 25.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $952/month is 1.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Campbell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
