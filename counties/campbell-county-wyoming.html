Market Analysis

The median home value in Campbell County is 19.6% below the Wyoming state average and 22.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,009/month is 7.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Campbell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.7%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
