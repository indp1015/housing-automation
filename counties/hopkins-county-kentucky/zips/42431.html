📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 42431, located in Hopkins County, Kentucky, exhibits a robust upward price trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) has climbed from 179.85 in 2021 to 248.7 in 2025, representing a cumulative appreciation of approximately 38.3%. This consistent growth indicates sustained demand within this specific geographic segment, with the most recent year-over-year increase recorded at 4.75%. While this growth is positive, it lags slightly behind the broader Hopkins County average of 5.94% and the state-wide figure of 4.26%, though it remains above the state mean. This minor divergence suggests that while the local market is healthy, it is not experiencing the explosive, outlier growth seen in some neighboring areas, such as ZIP 42413, which reported a significantly higher year-over-year gain of 11.21%. The sub-market character of ZIP 42431 aligns with an affordable, entry-level residential segment. The parent county’s median home value stands at $142,800, and the median household income is $56,815, indicating a price point accessible to middle-income buyers. With an owner-occupancy rate of 70.7%, the area maintains a strong base of homeownership, though the median gross rent of $837 per month suggests a viable rental alternative for those not yet ready to purchase. The HPI level of 248.7, when contextualized against the county’s median value, reflects typical appreciation patterns for lower-priced housing stocks that have benefited from broader rural and suburban demand shifts. Prospective buyers in ZIP 42431 should note that current entry costs remain moderate relative to national standards, despite the recent appreciation. However, the prevailing 30-year mortgage rate of 7.40% significantly impacts monthly carrying costs, potentially offsetting the affordability of the initial purchase price. Residents and investors should recognize that while the market is growing, it does not currently display the volatility or extreme premium pricing often associated with luxury sub-markets. The data supports a stable, growing environment suitable for long-term residential investment, provided that buyers account for the high interest rate environment when calculating total housing expenses.
