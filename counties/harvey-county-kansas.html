Market Analysis

The median home value in Harvey County is 29.9% above the Kansas state average and 20.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $900/month is 12.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Harvey County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Harvey County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
