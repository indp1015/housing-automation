Market Analysis

The median home value in Crook County is 18.5% above the Oregon state average and 100.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,224/month is 7.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.8, Crook County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Crook County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.6%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
