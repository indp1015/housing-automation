Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 3.9% below the Florida state average and 20.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,049/month is 19.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.9%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
