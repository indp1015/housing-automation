Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 1.1% below the Florida state average and 20.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,044/month is 15.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 74.0% in Florida and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
