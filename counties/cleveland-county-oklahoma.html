Market Analysis

The median home value in Cleveland County is 55.1% above the Oklahoma state average and 3.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,167/month is 39.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Cleveland County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Cleveland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
