Market Analysis

The median home value in Louisa County is 14.3% below the Iowa state average and 34.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $797/month is 1.8% below the state average.

Louisa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.1%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
