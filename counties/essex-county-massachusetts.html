Market Analysis

The median home value in Essex County is 0.5% below the Massachusetts state average and 176.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,673/month is 7.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.1, Essex County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Essex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.0%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
