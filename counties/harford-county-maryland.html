Market Analysis

The median home value in Harford County is 4.4% above the Maryland state average and 69.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,586/month is 8.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Harford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.7%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
