Market Analysis

The median home value in Caldwell County is 5.2% below the Missouri state average and 26.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $640/month is 18.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Caldwell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
