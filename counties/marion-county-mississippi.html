Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 8.3% below the Mississippi state average and 44.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $700/month is 10.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.9, buying a home in Marion County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 71.7% in Mississippi and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
