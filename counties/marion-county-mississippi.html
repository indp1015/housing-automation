Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 9.6% below the Mississippi state average and 46.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $818/month is 1.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.5, buying a home in Marion County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
