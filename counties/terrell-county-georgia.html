Market Analysis

The median home value in Terrell County is 40.2% below the Georgia state average and 48.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $745/month is 22.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.2, buying a home in Terrell County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Terrell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.6%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
