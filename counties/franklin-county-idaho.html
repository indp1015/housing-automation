Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 2.5% below the Idaho state average and 43.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $831/month is 9.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.5, Franklin County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.2%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
