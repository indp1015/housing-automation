Market Analysis

The median home value in McMullen County is 47.9% below the Texas state average and 57.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $758/month is 24.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.7, buying a home in McMullen County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

McMullen County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.3%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
