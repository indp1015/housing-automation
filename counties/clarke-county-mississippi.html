Market Analysis

The median home value in Clarke County is 13.0% below the Mississippi state average and 48.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $801/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, buying a home in Clarke County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clarke County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.3%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
