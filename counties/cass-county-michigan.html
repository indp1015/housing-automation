Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 10.5% above the Michigan state average and 4.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $916/month is 1.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Cass County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
