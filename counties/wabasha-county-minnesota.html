Market Analysis

The median home value in Wabasha County is 10.9% above the Minnesota state average and 16.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $830/month is 11.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.5, Wabasha County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Wabasha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.3%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
