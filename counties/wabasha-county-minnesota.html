Market Analysis

The median home value in Wabasha County is 10.5% above the Minnesota state average and 16.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $798/month is 12.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.7, Wabasha County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Wabasha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
