Market Analysis

The median home value in Hayes County is 22.2% below the Nebraska state average and 43.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $508/month is 36.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hayes County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.9%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
