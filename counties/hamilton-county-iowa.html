Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 17.6% below the Iowa state average and 37.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $853/month is 5.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Hamilton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.8%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
