Market Analysis

The median home value in Butts County is 21.6% above the Georgia state average and 5.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $941/month is 2.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Butts County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
