Market Analysis

The median home value in Fulton County is 1.7% below the Pennsylvania state average and 8.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $809/month is 18.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Fulton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.6%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
