Market Analysis

The median home value in Smith County is 9.1% above the Tennessee state average and 7.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $922/month is 3.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Smith County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
