📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP 44827's FHFA HPI rose from 125.24 in 2021 to 212.53 in 2025, a gain of roughly 70% over four years. Annual appreciation accelerated from 2022 through 2024, when the index climbed from 150.31 to 200.26, before moderating in 2025 with a +6.13% year-over-year increase. The index now sits at 212.5 against a 2000 baseline of 100, meaning typical home price appreciation in this ZIP has slightly more than doubled over the 40-year series. That +6.13% YoY gain trails Crawford County's +8.90% but exceeds Ohio's +4.33%. The divergence is modest rather than structural: 44827 is appreciating faster than the state but slower than its own county, suggesting demand here is real but less intense than in hotter county pockets such as ZIP 44882 (+16.22%) or ZIP 44865 (+26.91%). With a county median home value of $136,700, median gross rent of $794, and owner occupancy of 73.1%, 44827 functions as an entry-level, owner-occupied sub-market rather than a luxury or renter-heavy one. For prospective buyers or residents, the practical picture is a low-priced, ownership-oriented market where values have risen sharply but remain below county-level momentum. Affordability is the defining feature, though the 7.40% mortgage rate raises the carrying cost of any purchase. Buyers should note that this ZIP's appreciation is decelerating relative to its own recent history and to the county, which matters for both budgeting and resale expectations.
