Market Analysis

The median home value in Gray County is 48.4% above the Kansas state average and 9.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $808/month is 0.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Gray County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.1%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
