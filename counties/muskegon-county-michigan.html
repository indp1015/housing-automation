📝 Local Market Analysis

Muskegon County operates as a balanced market with distinct affordability advantages, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 3.05, which sits below the national average of 3.20. The rent burden stands at 19.0% of income, slightly exceeding the national norm of 17.2%, yet the price-to-rent ratio of 16.1 remains favorable compared to the national 18.7, suggesting that purchasing offers better value than renting. This classification is reinforced by a 78.1% owner-occupancy rate and a median home value of $198,300, which is 12.9% below the national average. The market exhibits strong stability, evidenced by consistent positive migration of 2,344 filers and robust local employment totaling 54,648 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,114. Trend analysis reveals significant appreciation, with median home values rising from $105,300 in 2017 to $198,300 in 2024. This trajectory includes a peak year-over-year increase of 17.0% in 2022, moderating to 9.6% by 2024. Recent FHFA data indicates a 6.69% year-over-year price increase in 2025, outperforming the state average of 3.68% by 3.01 percentage points. A notable divergence exists between asset classes, as home values are rising faster than rents, which currently stand at a median $1,028 per month. This price appreciation outpaces the 7.40% national mortgage rate, creating a complex environment where rising equity is offset by higher financing costs. The economic context is shaped by limited housing supply, with only 451 new units authorized in 2025, comprising 351 single-family and 100 multi-family homes. This constrained supply likely contributes to the sustained price momentum and the county’s outperformance relative to the broader Michigan market. While the median household income of $65,024 is 4.4% below the national average, the county’s home values remain in line with the state average, indicating a localized premium. The composite assessment reflects high stability and moderate momentum, driven by the strong year-over-year price growth and positive net migration. However, affordability metrics lag behind vitality indicators, as the rising cost of entry and financing costs temper the otherwise favorable rental-to-purchase dynamics. Potential buyers face a market with strong equity growth potential but must navigate elevated interest rates and a supply-constrained environment that supports sustained price appreciation.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Muskegon County is 0.5% above the Michigan state average and 12.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,028/month is 13.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Muskegon County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Muskegon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.1%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
