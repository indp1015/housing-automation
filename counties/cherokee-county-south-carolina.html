Market Analysis

The median home value in Cherokee County is 28.9% below the South Carolina state average and 37.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $869/month is 11.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Cherokee County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cherokee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.8%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
