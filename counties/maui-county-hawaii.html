Market Analysis

The median home value in Maui County is 13.1% above the Hawaii state average and 305.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,863/month is 4.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.4, Maui County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Maui County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.0%, compared to 66.2% in Hawaii and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
