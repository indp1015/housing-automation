Market Analysis

The median home value in Norfolk County is 10.6% above the Massachusetts state average and 207.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,072/month is 33.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.1, Norfolk County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Norfolk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.5%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
