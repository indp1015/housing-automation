Market Analysis

The median home value in Logan County is 64.7% above the Oklahoma state average and 10.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $895/month is 10.0% above the state average.

Logan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.8%, compared to 72.1% in Oklahoma and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
