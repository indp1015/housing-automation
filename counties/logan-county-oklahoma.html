Market Analysis

The median home value in Logan County is 62.3% above the Oklahoma state average and 8.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $891/month is 6.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Logan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.2%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
