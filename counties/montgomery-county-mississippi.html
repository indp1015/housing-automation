Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 13.2% below the Mississippi state average and 47.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $682/month is 12.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Montgomery County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.1%, compared to 71.7% in Mississippi and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
