Market Analysis

The median home value in Somervell County is 55.4% above the Texas state average and 27.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $773/month is 22.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.2, Somervell County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Somervell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.0%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
