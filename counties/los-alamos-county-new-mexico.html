Market Analysis

The median home value in Los Alamos County is 149.1% above the New Mexico state average and 113.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,308/month is 49.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.8, Los Alamos County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Los Alamos County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%, compared to 73.3% in New Mexico and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
