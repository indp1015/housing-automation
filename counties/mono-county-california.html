Market Analysis

The median home value in Mono County is 5.0% above the California state average and 180.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,629/month is 3.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.7, Mono County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Mono County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.4%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
