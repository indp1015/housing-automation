Market Analysis

The median home value in Hancock County is 19.1% above the Maine state average and 36.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,061/month is 4.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hancock County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 76.6% in Maine and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
