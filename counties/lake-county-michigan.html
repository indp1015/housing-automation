Market Analysis

The median home value in Lake County is 32.1% below the Michigan state average and 41.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $875/month is 3.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Lake County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.0%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
