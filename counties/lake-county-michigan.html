📝 Local Market Analysis

Lake County, Michigan, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $133,900 and a robust 82.0% owner-occupancy rate. The price-to-income ratio of 2.64 remains significantly below the national average of 3.20, indicating strong relative affordability despite a median household income of $50,805. This affordability is further supported by a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, well under the national benchmark of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing remains a more financially efficient long-term strategy than renting for qualified buyers. The market’s stability is underscored by consistent ownership rates and a moderate rent burden of 20.7%, which, while slightly above the national average of 17.2%, does not offset the substantial equity potential available to homeowners. Recent momentum presents a complex picture of diverging signals. While the FHFA Home Price Index indicates a recent 1.26% year-over-year decline, underperforming the state’s 3.68% growth by 4.94 percentage points, the longer-term trend reveals significant appreciation. Home values surged 19.1% in 2022 and 9.0% in 2024, outpacing rent growth and driving the price-to-rent ratio down. This divergence suggests that the current softening in mortgage-backed prices may reflect broader national interest rate pressures rather than local demand collapse. The local economy supports this resilience through positive net migration of 510 filers and an average weekly wage of $735, though total employment of 1,256 jobs indicates a small, specialized labor base. Housing supply remains tight, with only 30 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family construction, limiting new inventory to existing sales. The market’s vitality is constrained by its small population of 12,563 and limited commercial establishments, yet its affordability metrics drive strong overall performance. The composite assessment reflects high stability due to low volatility in ownership and moderate affordability, balanced by lower momentum scores stemming from the recent HPI contraction. Potential buyers benefit from entry prices 41.2% below the national average and 32.1% below the state average, while renters face a higher cost burden relative to income. The combination of low supply, steady inbound migration, and deep affordability creates a market where equity accumulation is likely, provided buyers navigate the current high mortgage rate environment of 7.40%. The data indicates a market that is stabilizing after a period of rapid inflation, with fundamental support from demographic inflows and structural housing scarcity.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Lake County is 32.1% below the Michigan state average and 41.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $875/month is 3.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Lake County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.0%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
