📝 Local Market Analysis

Franklin County, Illinois, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 1.57, significantly below the national average of 3.20. The median home value of $89,000 sits 60.9% below the national mean and 41.8% below the state average, creating a structural advantage for potential homeowners. This affordability is reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 9.2, less than half the national benchmark of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing offers superior long-term value compared to renting. With a rent burden of 17.0%, closely aligned with the national average of 17.2%, rental costs remain manageable, yet the low entry price for ownership makes the market particularly attractive for income-constrained buyers. Recent momentum indicates a stabilization phase following a sharp correction. After peaking at 10.5% year-over-year growth in 2022, home value appreciation has moderated to 1.5% in 2024 and further decelerated to 0.45% according to the 2025 FHFA HPI. This recent stagnation contrasts sharply with the state’s 5.54% growth, marking a 5.09 percentage point underperformance. The divergence between the county’s flat recent growth and its historical upward trajectory suggests a market digesting prior gains. Meanwhile, housing supply remains extremely constrained, with only 19 new units authorized in 2025, including just 15 single-family homes. This limited inventory likely supports price stability despite weak local economic indicators, such as an average weekly wage of $920 and total employment of only 7,002 jobs. The market’s primary weakness lies in its vitality, driven by a small economic base and modest migration. While net migration of 635 filers provides some demographic support, the inflow average income of $57,644 offers limited purchasing power to drive significant price appreciation. The composite assessment reflects high affordability and moderate stability but low vitality, as the small labor market and low wage levels limit broader economic expansion. For potential buyers, the current environment offers a low-risk entry point with strong affordability metrics, though investors should note that recent price growth has lagged significantly behind state trends. The combination of minimal new construction and stable, albeit slow, price appreciation creates a static but affordable landscape, where the primary challenge is not cost, but the limited economic engine driving long-term value increases.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 41.8% below the Illinois state average and 60.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $803/month is 7.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.2, buying a home in Franklin County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.1%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
