Market Analysis

The median home value in Columbia County is 21.0% above the Wisconsin state average and 24.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $978/month is 6.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Columbia County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
