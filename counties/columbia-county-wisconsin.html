Market Analysis

The median home value in Columbia County is 18.8% above the Wisconsin state average and 22.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $926/month is 4.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Columbia County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
