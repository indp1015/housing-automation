Market Analysis

The median home value in Collier County is 89.2% above the Florida state average and 130.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,752/month is 42.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Collier County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.0%, compared to 74.0% in Florida and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
