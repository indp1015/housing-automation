Market Analysis

The median home value in King County is 104.0% above the Washington state average and 283.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,035/month is 69.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.2, King County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

King County has an owner-occupancy rate of 56.1%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
