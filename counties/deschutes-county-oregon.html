Market Analysis

The median home value in Deschutes County is 66.9% above the Oregon state average and 180.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,674/month is 47.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.7, Deschutes County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Deschutes County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.7%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
