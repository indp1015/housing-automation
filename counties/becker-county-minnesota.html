Market Analysis

The median home value in Becker County is 22.1% above the Minnesota state average and 27.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $920/month is 1.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Becker County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Becker County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
