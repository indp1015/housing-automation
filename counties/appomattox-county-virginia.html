Market Analysis

The median home value in Appomattox County is 29.8% below the Virginia state average and 8.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $855/month is 26.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Appomattox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.8%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
