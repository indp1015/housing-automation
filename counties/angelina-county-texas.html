Market Analysis

The median home value in Angelina County is 12.2% below the Texas state average and 28.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,076/month is 7.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.7, buying a home in Angelina County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Angelina County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.0%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
