Market Analysis

The median home value in Billings County is 88.3% above the North Dakota state average and 45.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $621/month is 19.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 44.4, Billings County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Billings County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.9%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
