Market Analysis

The median home value in Billings County is 91.3% above the North Dakota state average and 52.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $630/month is 18.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 42.7, Billings County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Billings County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 74.1% in North Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
