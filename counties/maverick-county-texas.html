Market Analysis

The median home value in Maverick County is 14.8% below the Texas state average and 30.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $859/month is 13.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Maverick County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Maverick County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
