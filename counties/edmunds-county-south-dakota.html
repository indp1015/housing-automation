Market Analysis

The median home value in Edmunds County is 9.8% below the South Dakota state average and 28.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $992/month is 26.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.7, buying a home in Edmunds County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Edmunds County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.1%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
