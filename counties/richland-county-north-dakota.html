Market Analysis

The median home value in Richland County is 4.4% above the North Dakota state average and 19.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $740/month is 4.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Richland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.1%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
