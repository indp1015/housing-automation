Market Analysis

The median home value in Colorado County is 12.6% above the Texas state average and 7.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $926/month is 7.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Colorado County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Colorado County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
