Market Analysis

The median home value in Hot Spring County is 6.8% above the Arkansas state average and 33.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $768/month is 0.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Hot Spring County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Hot Spring County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.5%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
