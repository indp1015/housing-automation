Market Analysis

The median home value in Merrimack County is 5.2% above the New Hampshire state average and 61.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,360/month is 3.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Merrimack County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.5%, compared to 74.3% in New Hampshire and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
