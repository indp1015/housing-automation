Market Analysis

The median home value in Gilpin County is 28.8% above the Colorado state average and 142.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,340/month is 6.9% above the state average.

Gilpin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
