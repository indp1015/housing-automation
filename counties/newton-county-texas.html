Market Analysis

The median home value in Newton County is 44.7% below the Texas state average and 54.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,001/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 8.6, buying a home in Newton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Newton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.9%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
