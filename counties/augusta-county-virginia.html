Market Analysis

The median home value in Augusta County is 1.2% above the Virginia state average and 33.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,057/month is 7.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Augusta County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.9%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
