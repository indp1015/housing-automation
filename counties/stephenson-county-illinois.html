Market Analysis

The median home value in Stephenson County is 11.8% below the Illinois state average and 40.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $798/month is 8.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Stephenson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Stephenson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.9%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
