Market Analysis

The median home value in Vinton County is 23.6% below the Ohio state average and 34.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $728/month is 19.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Vinton County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Vinton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
