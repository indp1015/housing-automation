Market Analysis

The median home value in Perry County is 42.9% below the Kentucky state average and 59.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $798/month is 0.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.6, buying a home in Perry County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Perry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.4%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
