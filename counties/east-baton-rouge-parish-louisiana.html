📝 Local Market Analysis

East Baton Rouge Parish operates as a balanced market with distinct affordability pressures, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 3.94 compared to the national average of 3.20. The market is neither exclusively a buyer’s nor a renter’s domain, as the price-to-rent ratio of 18.1 aligns closely with the national benchmark of 18.7, suggesting equivalent economic efficiency for both housing types. However, the rent burden stands at 21.8% of income, significantly exceeding the national average of 17.2%, indicating that rental costs consume a larger share of local household resources than typical. This cost structure is driven by a median gross rent of $1,147 against a median household income of $63,071, creating a tight squeeze for lower-income demographics despite the overall stability of the housing stock. Multi-year data reveals a pronounced acceleration in home values, with year-over-year growth spiking to 12.7% in 2022 before moderating to 2.9% in 2024. This recent stabilization is corroborated by the FHFA House Price Index, which shows a 2.93% annual increase, outperforming the state average of 1.59% by 1.34 percentage points. While home values are rising faster than rents, the divergence is not extreme, maintaining relative parity between the two sectors. The county’s home values are 48.7% higher than the state average, reflecting its status as the economic hub of Louisiana, yet this premium is partially offset by incomes that sit 7.2% below the national mean. This regional disparity highlights a structural challenge where local wealth generation has not kept pace with asset appreciation. Economic fundamentals provide a mixed backdrop for this trajectory. With 224,248 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,323, the labor market supports demand, yet the 7.40% national mortgage rate significantly dampens buyer purchasing power. Housing supply remains robust, with 2,631 new units authorized in 2025, including 1,420 single-family homes, which helps mitigate inventory shortages. Net migration of +2,684 filers indicates sustained demographic inflow, though the average income of incoming residents ($85,906) exceeds the local median, suggesting that new entrants are less price-sensitive. Consequently, the market’s vitality is driven by strong momentum and employment stability, but these positives are heavily counterweighted by low affordability metrics. The primary driver of market stress is the inability of local incomes to match the pace of historical price appreciation, resulting in a scenario where momentum is high but accessibility is constrained.

Market Analysis

The median home value in East Baton Rouge Parish is 48.7% above the Louisiana state average and 9.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,147/month is 25.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in East Baton Rouge Parish is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

East Baton Rouge Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.3%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
