Market Analysis

The median home value in Llano County is 86.8% above the Texas state average and 52.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $995/month is 3.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.0, Llano County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Llano County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 73.0% in Texas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
