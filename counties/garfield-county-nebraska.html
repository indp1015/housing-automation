Market Analysis

The median home value in Garfield County is 7.3% above the Nebraska state average and 22.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $490/month is 38.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.0, Garfield County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Garfield County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.0%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
