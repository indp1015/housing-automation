Market Analysis

The median home value in Beaverhead County is 11.3% above the Montana state average and 35.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $837/month is 0.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.7, Beaverhead County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Beaverhead County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.8%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
