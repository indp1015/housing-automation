Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 77.1% above the Minnesota state average and 85.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,710/month is 82.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
