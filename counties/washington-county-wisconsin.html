Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 47.3% above the Wisconsin state average and 51.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,153/month is 26.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.1%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
