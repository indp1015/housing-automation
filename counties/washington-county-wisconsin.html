Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 45.2% above the Wisconsin state average and 49.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,126/month is 26.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.1%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
