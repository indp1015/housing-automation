Market Analysis

The median home value in Oswego County is 46.1% below the New York state average and 33.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $967/month is 17.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.1, buying a home in Oswego County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Oswego County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.4%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
