Market Analysis

The median home value in Montrose County is 9.1% below the Colorado state average and 70.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,188/month is 8.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.2, Montrose County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Montrose County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.4%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
