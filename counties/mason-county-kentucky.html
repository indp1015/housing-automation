Market Analysis

The median home value in Mason County is about equal to the Kentucky state average and 29.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $727/month is 8.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Mason County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Mason County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.1%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
