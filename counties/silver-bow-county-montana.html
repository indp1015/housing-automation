Market Analysis

The median home value in Silver Bow County is 7.8% below the Montana state average and 12.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $863/month is 2.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Silver Bow County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.8%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
