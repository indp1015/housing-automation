Market Analysis

The median home value in Marshall County is 1.2% above the West Virginia state average and 34.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $837/month is 7.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Marshall County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Marshall County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
