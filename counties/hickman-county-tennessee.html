Market Analysis

The median home value in Hickman County is about equal to the Tennessee state average and 1.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $893/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hickman County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.2%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
