Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 5.2% below the Georgia state average and 17.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $918/month is 4.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Polk County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.0%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
