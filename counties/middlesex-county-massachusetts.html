Market Analysis

The median home value in Middlesex County is 14.7% above the Massachusetts state average and 219.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,201/month is 36.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.6, Middlesex County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Middlesex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.3%, compared to 67.2% in Massachusetts and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
