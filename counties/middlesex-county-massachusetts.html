Market Analysis

The median home value in Middlesex County is 17.0% above the Massachusetts state average and 224.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,126/month is 36.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.9, Middlesex County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Middlesex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.6%, compared to 67.0% in Massachusetts and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
