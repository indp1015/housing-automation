Market Analysis

The median home value in Jennings County is 15.8% below the Indiana state average and 29.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $988/month is 8.6% above the state average.

Jennings County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
