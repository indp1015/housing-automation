Market Analysis

The median home value in Baltimore County is 5.9% below the Maryland state average and 56.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,566/month is 10.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Baltimore County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Baltimore County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.3%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
