Market Analysis

The median home value in Fayette County is 52.6% above the Tennessee state average and 49.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $902/month is 1.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.5, Fayette County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fayette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.4%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
