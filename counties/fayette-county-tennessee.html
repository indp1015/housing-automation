Market Analysis

The median home value in Fayette County is 56.7% above the Tennessee state average and 49.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $883/month is 2.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.8, Fayette County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fayette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
