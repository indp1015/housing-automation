Market Analysis

The median home value in Marinette County is 32.2% below the Wisconsin state average and 30.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $795/month is 13.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Marinette County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Marinette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
