Market Analysis

The median home value in Lyon County is 13.2% below the Minnesota state average and 8.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $742/month is 18.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lyon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.5%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
