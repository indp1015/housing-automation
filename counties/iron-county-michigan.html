Market Analysis

The median home value in Iron County is 44.7% below the Michigan state average and 52.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $716/month is 20.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.7, buying a home in Iron County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Iron County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.5%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
