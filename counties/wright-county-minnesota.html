Market Analysis

The median home value in Wright County is 49.6% above the Minnesota state average and 57.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,246/month is 36.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Wright County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.3%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
