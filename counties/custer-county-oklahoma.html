Market Analysis

The median home value in Custer County is 19.4% above the Oklahoma state average and 20.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $824/month is 1.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Custer County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Custer County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.2%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
