Market Analysis

The median home value in Stillwater County is 46.6% above the Montana state average and 78.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $961/month is 14.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 35.2, Stillwater County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Stillwater County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.9%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
