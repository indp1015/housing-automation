Market Analysis

The median home value in Stillwater County is 41.4% above the Montana state average and 67.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $968/month is 17.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.6, Stillwater County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Stillwater County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.8%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
