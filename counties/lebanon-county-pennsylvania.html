Market Analysis

The median home value in Lebanon County is 14.1% above the Pennsylvania state average and 6.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,110/month is 12.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lebanon County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
