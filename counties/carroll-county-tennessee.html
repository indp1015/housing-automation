Market Analysis

The median home value in Carroll County is 36.3% below the Tennessee state average and 37.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $755/month is 15.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Carroll County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Carroll County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
