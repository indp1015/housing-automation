Market Analysis

The median home value in Queens County is 157.4% above the New York state average and 217.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,956/month is 67.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.8, Queens County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Queens County has an owner-occupancy rate of 44.9%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
