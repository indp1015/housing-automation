Market Analysis

The median home value in Falls County is 40.5% below the Texas state average and 51.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $731/month is 26.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.6, buying a home in Falls County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Falls County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
