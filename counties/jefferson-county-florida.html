Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 22.7% below the Florida state average and 6.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $790/month is 35.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.1%, compared to 74.0% in Florida and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
