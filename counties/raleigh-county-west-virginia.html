Market Analysis

The median home value in Raleigh County is 2.0% above the West Virginia state average and 33.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $902/month is 16.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.9, buying a home in Raleigh County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Raleigh County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.3%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
