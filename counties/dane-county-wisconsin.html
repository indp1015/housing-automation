Market Analysis

The median home value in Dane County is 68.7% above the Wisconsin state average and 73.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,398/month is 52.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dane County has an owner-occupancy rate of 57.1%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
