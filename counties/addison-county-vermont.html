Market Analysis

The median home value in Addison County is 23.3% above the Vermont state average and 58.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,177/month is 4.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.6, Addison County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Addison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.3%, compared to 77.1% in Vermont and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
