Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 13.6% below the Tennessee state average and 15.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $827/month is 7.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Warren County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.4%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
