Market Analysis

The median home value in Ziebach County is 45.0% below the South Dakota state average and 56.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $734/month is 6.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.3, buying a home in Ziebach County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Ziebach County has an owner-occupancy rate of 57.1%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
