Market Analysis

The median home value in Flathead County is 93.4% above the Montana state average and 135.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,142/month is 35.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 39.1, Flathead County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Flathead County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.4%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
