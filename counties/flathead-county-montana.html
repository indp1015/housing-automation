Market Analysis

The median home value in Flathead County is 86.0% above the Montana state average and 121.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,094/month is 32.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 35.6, Flathead County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Flathead County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.0%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
