Market Analysis

The median home value in Stutsman County is 18.6% above the North Dakota state average and 8.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $774/month is about equal to the state average.

Stutsman County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.1%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
