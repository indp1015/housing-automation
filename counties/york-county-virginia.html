Market Analysis

The median home value in York County is 46.4% above the Virginia state average and 90.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,827/month is 56.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in York County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

York County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.6%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
