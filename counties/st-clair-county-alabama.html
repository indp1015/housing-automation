📝 Local Market Analysis

St. Clair County, Alabama, presents a balanced market profile, characterized by strong affordability relative to national benchmarks and stable ownership structures. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.92, significantly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is 16.8%, marginally lower than the national 17.2%. These metrics indicate that housing costs remain manageable for local households, supported by a median household income of $77,463, which exceeds the national average by 13.9%. The high owner-occupancy rate of 82.6% further underscores the stability of the residential sector, suggesting a community with deep roots and consistent housing demand. The multi-year trend reveals a period of rapid appreciation followed by recent moderation. Home values surged from $142,800 in 2017 to $226,300 in 2024, with a peak year-over-year increase of 18.2% in 2022. However, growth has decelerated to 3.6% in 2024, aligning with a recent FHFA HPI change of just +0.64% in 2025. This recent slowdown represents a significant underperformance relative to the Alabama state average of +2.79%, creating a 2.15 percentage point divergence. This deceleration suggests that the initial post-pandemic price shock has normalized, though the county still commands a premium, sitting 41.7% above the state median home value. The price-to-rent ratio of 17.4 remains below the national 18.7, indicating that buying remains a financially competitive alternative to renting, a dynamic reinforced by the similar growth rates observed in both home values and rents. Economic fundamentals provide a solid backdrop for this stability. Local employment supports 18,861 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,110, while net migration of +2,226 filers between 2021 and 2022 indicates sustained population growth. Housing supply remains limited, with only 411 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and no multi-family construction, which likely contributes to the resilience of home values despite the national 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40%. The market’s vitality is driven by this constrained supply and steady income growth, while stability is anchored by the low debt-to-income ratios implied by the affordable price-to-income metrics. Consequently, the overall assessment reflects a market where affordability and economic vitality offset the recent momentum slowdown, creating a resilient environment for long-term housing investment.
