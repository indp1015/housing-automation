Market Analysis

The median home value in Crook County is 12.3% below the Wyoming state average and 30.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $881/month is 6.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Crook County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Crook County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.0%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
