📝 Local Market Analysis

Meade County, Kansas, functions as a distinct buyer’s market, characterized by significant affordability advantages relative to national benchmarks. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.07, substantially lower than the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is only 13.2% of income compared to the national 17.2%. Furthermore, the price-to-rent ratio of 15.7 indicates that purchasing is financially more efficient than renting, a structural feature reinforced by the 74.0% owner-occupancy rate. This classification is further supported by the fact that median home values are 34.6% below the national average, despite median household income being 5.7% higher than the national norm. The multi-year trend reveals extreme volatility in home values, diverging sharply from rent stability. After steady growth of 4.4% to 5.2% annually from 2018 to 2020, home values surged 20.4% in 2021 and 12.2% in 2022. This was followed by an 11.3% correction in 2023 before a dramatic 22.0% rebound in 2024. In contrast, median gross rent remains modest at $790, suggesting that the sharp fluctuations in asset values are not mirrored by rental demand pressures. This divergence highlights a market where equity gains are volatile, while rental costs remain fixed and low. The recent 22.0% year-over-year increase in 2024 represents significant recent momentum, indicating a strong recovery from the 2023 dip, though no FHFA HPI data is provided to validate this specific index movement. Regional context shows Meade County outperforming the state average by 7.3% in home value, yet it remains deeply affordable due to the small population of 3,903. The local economic foundation is fragile, with only 1,173 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,133. Housing supply is critically constrained, with just one new unit authorized in 2025, which likely contributes to price sensitivity. Migration data from 2021-2022 shows a net inflow of 125 filers, but the average income of outflowing residents ($89,155) exceeds that of inflowing residents ($85,773), suggesting a potential wealth leakage. With 30-year mortgage rates at 7.40%, the high cost of financing partially offsets the low price-to-income ratio. For potential buyers, the market offers exceptional entry-level affordability but carries high volatility risk due to thin liquidity and minimal new supply. Renters benefit from low burdens but face limited options in a predominantly owner-occupied community.
