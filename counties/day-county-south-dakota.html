Market Analysis

The median home value in Day County is 4.1% below the South Dakota state average and 23.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $660/month is 15.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Day County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.6%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
