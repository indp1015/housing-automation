📝 Local Market Analysis

Grundy County, Missouri, functions as a highly affordable buyer’s market, characterized by significant price suppression relative to national and state averages. The median home value of $112,500 sits 50.6% below the national average and 36.0% below the state average, creating a price-to-income ratio of 2.16 compared to the national benchmark of 3.20. This extreme affordability is reinforced by a rent burden of 16.1%, which is lower than the national average of 17.2%. Consequently, the market exhibits strong affordability metrics despite a challenging overall economic environment, driven primarily by low entry costs rather than robust local income growth. The multi-year trend reveals a volatile but ultimately appreciating trajectory. After a sharp 7.3% decline in 2021, home values surged 18.8% in 2022 before moderating to a 5.1% increase in 2024. Recent FHFA data confirms this stabilization, with a 3.63% year-over-year change that slightly lags the state’s 3.71% growth. A notable divergence exists between asset classes: while home values have recovered and grown, the price-to-rent ratio of 13.5 remains well below the national average of 18.7, suggesting that rental yields are relatively high for investors, or that rental demand is not outpacing ownership costs. This pattern indicates that the housing market is not experiencing the rent-driven inflation seen in many urban centers, keeping the barrier to entry low for prospective homeowners. Economic fundamentals present a mixed picture that limits broader market vitality. Local employment is constrained, with only 1,922 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $847, reflecting a median household income of $51,975 that is 23.6% below the national mean. Housing supply is critically low, with only three new single-family units authorized in 2025, which could theoretically support future price appreciation but currently limits market depth. Migration data shows a net inflow of 283 filers, with incoming residents earning slightly less than those departing, suggesting in-migration is driven by affordability rather than high-wage opportunities. The combination of low supply, stable migration, and high affordability drives strong momentum metrics, even as stability and vitality scores remain depressed due to the weak local labor market and high mortgage rates of 7.40%. For potential buyers, the primary risk is not overpayment but rather the limited local economic base that may constrain long-term income growth and resale liquidity.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Grundy County is 36.0% below the Missouri state average and 50.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $696/month is 11.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Grundy County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Grundy County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.7%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
