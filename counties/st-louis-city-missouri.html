Market Analysis

The median home value in St. Louis city is 12.4% above the Missouri state average and 13.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $997/month is 27.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in St. Louis city is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

St. Louis city has an owner-occupancy rate of 45.3%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
