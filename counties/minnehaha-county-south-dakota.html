Market Analysis

The median home value in Minnehaha County is 58.9% above the South Dakota state average and 26.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $978/month is 24.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Minnehaha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.1%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
