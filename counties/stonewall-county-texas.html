Market Analysis

The median home value in Stonewall County is 73.9% below the Texas state average and 78.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $613/month is 38.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 6.6, buying a home in Stonewall County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Stonewall County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.6%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
