Market Analysis

The median home value in Milwaukee County is 1.4% below the Wisconsin state average and 1.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,101/month is 20.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Milwaukee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Milwaukee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 49.9%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
