Market Analysis

The median home value in Mercer County is 7.3% above the Ohio state average and 7.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $804/month is 11.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Mercer County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
