Market Analysis

The median home value in Dawson County is 94.6% above the Georgia state average and 66.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,425/month is 53.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dawson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
