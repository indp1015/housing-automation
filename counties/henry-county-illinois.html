📝 Local Market Analysis

Henry County, Illinois, functions as a highly affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.11, significantly below the national average of 3.20. The median home value of $151,600 is 33.4% lower than the national benchmark, while the median household income of $71,911 exceeds the national mean by 5.8%. This favorable income-to-price dynamic is reinforced by a rent burden of only 14.1%, well under the 17.2% national average, and a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, indicating that ownership costs remain substantially lower than long-term rental commitments. Consequently, the market exhibits strong stability and affordability metrics, which drive its overall positive assessment despite limited local economic vitality. Trend analysis reveals a period of accelerated appreciation that has recently moderated. Home values rose from $115,800 in 2017 to $151,600 in 2024, with a peak year-over-year increase of 10.7% in 2022. This growth has slowed to 4.3% in 2024, aligning closely with the 5.21% year-over-year change reported by the FHFA HPI for 2025. Notably, the county is tracking 0.33 percentage points below the Illinois state average of 5.54%, suggesting that while momentum persists, it is slightly lagging broader state trends. The parallel rise in home values and rents indicates a balanced adjustment in housing costs, preventing significant divergence between ownership and rental markets. The local economic context presents a constrained supply environment that supports price stability. With only 18 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits, new construction is minimal. This scarcity is partially offset by positive net migration of 664 filers, though average inflow income of $77,484 is slightly lower than outflow income of $78,174. Employment stands at 10,299 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,032, providing a steady but modest economic base. The current 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.32% impacts monthly carrying costs, yet the low entry price point maintains accessibility. The market’s high scores in affordability, stability, and momentum are counterbalanced by a low vitality score, reflecting the limited job growth and housing supply expansion. For potential buyers, the data suggests a stable, low-cost entry point with consistent, moderate appreciation, whereas renters face a market where long-term ownership remains financially advantageous due to the low price-to-rent ratio.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Henry County is 0.9% below the Illinois state average and 33.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $842/month is 2.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Henry County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Henry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.9%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
