Market Analysis

The median home value in Taylor County is 55.7% below the Georgia state average and 61.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $630/month is 34.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.6, buying a home in Taylor County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Taylor County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.9%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
