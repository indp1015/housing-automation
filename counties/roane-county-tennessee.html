Market Analysis

The median home value in Roane County is 5.3% above the Tennessee state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $767/month is 10.8% below the state average.

Roane County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.6%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
