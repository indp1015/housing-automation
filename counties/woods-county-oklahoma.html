Market Analysis

The median home value in Woods County is 8.2% below the Oklahoma state average and 38.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $850/month is 1.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.7, buying a home in Woods County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Woods County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.3%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
