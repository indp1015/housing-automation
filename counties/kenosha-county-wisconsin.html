Market Analysis

The median home value in Kenosha County is 13.4% above the Wisconsin state average and 16.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,219/month is 33.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Kenosha County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Kenosha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.7%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
