Market Analysis

The median home value in Santa Barbara County is 29.8% above the California state average and 247.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,131/month is 26.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.9, Santa Barbara County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Santa Barbara County has an owner-occupancy rate of 52.4%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
