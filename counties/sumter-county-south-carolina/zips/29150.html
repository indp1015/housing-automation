📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP code 29150's FHFA HPI rose from 160.66 in 2021 to 222.72 in 2025, a cumulative gain of roughly 38.6% over four years. The advance was front-loaded: +15.8% in 2022 and +11.4% in 2023, followed by a near-flat 2024 (207.86, up 0.28%) and a renewed +7.15% in 2025. That pause-and-resume pattern indicates the ZIP absorbed the mortgage-rate shock of 2023–2024 without sustained price declines. The 2025 divergence is notable. ZIP 29150's +7.15% year-over-year gain exceeds Sumter County's +4.50% and South Carolina's +2.32%, and it outpaces neighboring ZIP 29154 (+2.80%) despite that ZIP sitting at a nearly identical index level (222.4 versus 222.7). The spread suggests demand concentrating in 29150 specifically rather than a countywide or statewide tide, though a single year of outperformance does not establish a durable trend. The sub-market character is entry-level to mid-market, not luxury. Sumter County's median home value of $165,300 against median household income of $56,693 implies a price-to-income ratio near 2.9x, and median gross rent of $1,057 consumes about 22% of median monthly income. With 68.1% owner occupancy, the county is owner-dominated but retains a substantial rental segment. At a 7.40% mortgage rate, a buyer at the county median price faces meaningful payment pressure. Prospective residents should note that 29150 is pricing above its county and state peers, and that affordability—not appreciation—is the binding constraint.
