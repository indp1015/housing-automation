Market Analysis

The median home value in Smith County is 32.2% below the Kansas state average and 58.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $635/month is 20.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, buying a home in Smith County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Smith County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.8%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
