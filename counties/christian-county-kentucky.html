Market Analysis

The median home value in Christian County is 7.9% above the Kentucky state average and 23.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,040/month is 31.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.9, buying a home in Christian County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Christian County has an owner-occupancy rate of 50.0%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
