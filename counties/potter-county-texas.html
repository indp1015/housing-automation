Market Analysis

The median home value in Potter County is 20.9% below the Texas state average and 35.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,022/month is 2.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.0, buying a home in Potter County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Potter County has an owner-occupancy rate of 57.3%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
