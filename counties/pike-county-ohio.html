Market Analysis

The median home value in Pike County is 18.6% below the Ohio state average and 29.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $833/month is 4.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Pike County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Pike County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.8%, compared to 73.4% in Ohio and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
