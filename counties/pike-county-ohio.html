Market Analysis

The median home value in Pike County is 16.6% below the Ohio state average and 28.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $907/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Pike County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Pike County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.5%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
