Market Analysis

The median home value in Barton County is 22.3% below the Missouri state average and 40.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $805/month is 2.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Barton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Barton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.1%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
