Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 43.1% above the Kansas state average and 12.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,042/month is 29.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Franklin County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.2%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
