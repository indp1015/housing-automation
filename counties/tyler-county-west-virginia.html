Market Analysis

The median home value in Tyler County is 18.0% below the West Virginia state average and 46.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $748/month is 3.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Tyler County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Tyler County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.1%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
