Market Analysis

The median home value in Pike County is 54.4% above the Georgia state average and 34.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $993/month is 2.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.6, Pike County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Pike County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.6%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
