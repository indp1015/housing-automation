Market Analysis

The median home value in Morgan County is 26.5% below the Kentucky state average and 48.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $663/month is 16.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Morgan County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Morgan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.9%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
