📝 Local Market Analysis

Wilcox County, Alabama, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.43, significantly below the national average of 3.20. The median home value of $98,800 sits 56.6% below the national average and 38.1% below the state average, reflecting deep affordability relative to the median household income of $40,640. This affordability is further underscored by a price-to-rent ratio of 11.1, well under the national benchmark of 18.7, indicating that purchasing remains a more economical long-term strategy than renting. However, the rental sector presents a different dynamic; the median gross rent of $745 consumes 22.0% of median income, exceeding the national rent burden average of 17.2%, suggesting that while ownership is accessible, rental costs are proportionally higher for local households. The market exhibits a volatile yet recovering trajectory. After a sharp 15.4% decline in 2020, home values rebounded with an 8.3% increase in 2021 and a 14.3% surge in 2022, culminating in a 17.3% year-over-year jump to $98,800 in 2024. Despite this recent appreciation in the Census data, the FHFA House Price Index indicates a 2.58% year-over-year decline in 2025, underperforming the state’s 2.79% growth by 5.37 percentage points. This divergence suggests that the recent Census spike may reflect lagging statistical adjustments or a specific subset of transactions, while current mortgage-backed transaction data points to a cooling or stabilizing market. The local economy, with only 2,012 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,236, provides limited fundamental support for sustained high growth, although positive net migration of 153 filers in 2021-2022 indicates modest demographic interest. For potential stakeholders, the high owner-occupancy rate of 77.4% suggests a stable, established housing stock with limited new supply pressure. The 7.40% national mortgage rate significantly impacts carrying costs, yet the low entry price mitigates this burden compared to higher-priced markets. The primary risk lies in the disconnect between the reported 2024 price surge and the 2025 HPI decline, which may signal a market correction or data inconsistency. Renter’s markets are less attractive due to the high rent burden, whereas buyers benefit from the low price-to-income ratio. However, the weak local employment base and average wages constrain the pool of qualified buyers, potentially limiting liquidity in the resale market. The combination of low absolute prices and recent volatility requires careful scrutiny of individual property valuations, as county-level aggregates may mask significant variance within this small, rural population of 10,172 residents.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Wilcox County is 38.1% below the Alabama state average and 56.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $745/month is 9.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.1, buying a home in Wilcox County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wilcox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 73.2% in Alabama and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
