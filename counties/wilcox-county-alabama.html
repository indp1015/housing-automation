📝 Local Market Analysis

Wilcox County, Alabama, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $98,800, which is 56.6% below the national average and 38.1% below the state mean. The local price-to-income ratio stands at 2.43, significantly lower than the national benchmark of 3.20, indicating high purchasing power relative to asset prices for the median household earning $40,640. Conversely, the rental sector exhibits tighter constraints, with a rent burden of 22.0% of income compared to the national average of 17.2%. This divergence highlights a market where ownership remains accessible while rental costs strain household budgets, supported by a price-to-rent ratio of 11.1, well below the national figure of 18.7. The multi-year trend reveals significant volatility, with home values dipping to $67,200 in 2020 before rebounding 17.3% in 2024 to reach $98,800. However, recent FHFA HPI data for 2025 shows a 2.59% year-over-year decline, underperforming the state’s 2.79% growth by 5.38 percentage points. This suggests that the sharp 2024 recovery has stalled, creating a divergence where rents are rising faster than home values. The economic foundation remains fragile, with only 2,012 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,236, limiting broader demand. While net migration was positive at 153 filers, the small population of 10,172 and 77.4% owner occupancy rate indicate limited liquidity and inventory turnover. The current 7.32% mortgage rate further pressures affordability, though the low absolute price point mitigates some of this impact for qualified buyers. The overall market assessment reflects a stressed environment driven primarily by low vitality and moderate stability. The vitality score of 30.3/100 underscores the constrained local economy and limited job growth, which dampens long-term demand drivers. Stability remains moderate at 45.1/100, reflecting the historical price volatility seen between 2019 and 2022. Momentum is slightly stronger at 51.2/100, capturing the recent price surge, but this is offset by the current negative HPI trend. Affordability scores of 44.8/100 confirm that while entry-level costs are low, the burden on renters and the high interest rate environment create friction. For potential buyers, the low price-to-income ratio offers a favorable entry point, but the declining HPI and weak employment base suggest limited appreciation potential in the near term. Renters face a challenging landscape where cost-of-living pressures are outpacing income growth, making the transition to ownership a financially prudent, albeit risky, strategy given the current market conditions.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Wilcox County is 38.1% below the Alabama state average and 56.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $745/month is 9.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.1, buying a home in Wilcox County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wilcox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 73.2% in Alabama and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
