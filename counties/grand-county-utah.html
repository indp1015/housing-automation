Market Analysis

The median home value in Grand County is 28.7% above the Utah state average and 136.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,118/month is 2.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 40.2, Grand County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Grand County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.3%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
