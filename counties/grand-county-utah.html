Market Analysis

The median home value in Grand County is 27.4% above the Utah state average and 127.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,021/month is 7.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 39.3, Grand County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Grand County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
