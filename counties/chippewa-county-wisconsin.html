Market Analysis

The median home value in Chippewa County is 9.1% above the Wisconsin state average and 12.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $963/month is 8.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Chippewa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.6%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
