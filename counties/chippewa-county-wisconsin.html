Market Analysis

The median home value in Chippewa County is 10.2% above the Wisconsin state average and 13.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $963/month is 5.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Chippewa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
