📝 Local Market Analysis

Muscogee County presents a distinct buyer’s advantage, characterized by a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, significantly below the national average of 18.7. This metric indicates that purchasing is substantially more cost-effective than renting, a structural feature reinforced by a 50.8% owner-occupancy rate. The market has experienced a pronounced acceleration in home values, with the median price surging from $140,200 in 2017 to $193,900 in 2024. This trajectory includes a peak year-over-year increase of 15.3% in 2022, which has since moderated to 6.4% in 2024. Recent FHFA HPI data confirms this continued but slowing momentum, showing a 4.98% year-over-year gain in 2025, which outpaces the Georgia state average of 1.83% by 3.15 percentage points. This divergence suggests local demand is stronger than the broader state trend, likely driven by net migration of 3,821 filers and an inflow of households with an average income of $71,969. Affordability remains a primary constraint, as the price-to-income ratio of 3.34 slightly exceeds the national benchmark of 3.20. Furthermore, the rent burden stands at 22.9% of median household income, well above the national average of 17.2%, indicating that renters face significant financial pressure. The median gross rent of $1,106 against a median household income of $58,073 highlights this disparity. Despite these affordability challenges, the county’s economic foundation supports sustained demand. Local employment totals 71,307 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,088, providing a stable income base. Housing supply remains limited, with only 260 new units authorized in 2025, comprising 230 single-family and 30 multi-family homes. This low construction volume, combined with a national 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40%, creates a competitive environment where existing inventory is highly sought after. The overall market assessment reflects a tension between strong momentum and limited stability. The high momentum score is driven by the consistent appreciation of home values and outperformance relative to the state. However, stability is compromised by the sharp volatility seen in the 2022 price spike and the current high interest rates. Vitality is moderate, supported by population growth and migration, yet affordability remains the weakest link in the market structure. For potential buyers, the lower price-to-rent ratio offers a long-term value proposition, but the immediate cost of entry is elevated by high mortgage rates. Renters, conversely, face a constrained market with high burdens, though the rapid appreciation of home values suggests that the cost of waiting may increase as supply fails to meet the growing demand indicated by recent migration patterns.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Muscogee County is 2.0% below the Georgia state average and 14.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,106/month is 14.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Muscogee County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Muscogee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 50.8%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
