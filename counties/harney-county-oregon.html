Market Analysis

The median home value in Harney County is 37.4% below the Oregon state average and 6.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $684/month is 42.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.5, Harney County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Harney County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.4%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
