Market Analysis

The median home value in Honolulu County is 15.0% above the Hawaii state average and 312.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,054/month is 15.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 35.4, Honolulu County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Honolulu County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.5%, compared to 66.2% in Hawaii and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
