Market Analysis

The median home value in Otero County is 60.5% below the Colorado state average and 25.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $839/month is 35.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Otero County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Otero County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.9%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
