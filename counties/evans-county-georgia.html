📝 Local Market Analysis

Evans County, Georgia, functions as a distinctively affordable market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.59, significantly below the national average of 3.20. With a median home value of $138,200 and a median household income of $53,259, the county offers substantial value relative to national benchmarks, where home values are 39.3% lower than the U.S. mean. The rent burden stands at 16.2%, slightly under the national 17.2% average, and the price-to-rent ratio of 16.0 indicates that purchasing is more cost-effective than renting compared to the national standard of 18.7. These metrics suggest a buyer’s advantage in terms of entry cost, although the market’s overall stability is compromised by recent volatility. The composite assessment highlights strong momentum, driven by aggressive price appreciation, but low vitality and stability reflect the county’s limited economic base and recent market turbulence. The multi-year trend reveals a dramatic shift from steady growth to a sharp correction. After modest gains of 2.0% to 3.1% between 2017 and 2019, home values surged 11.1% in 2020, 13.3% in 2021, and peaked at 17.9% in 2022. This rapid escalation has since reversed, with a 7.0% increase in 2023 followed by a 1.1% decline in 2024. However, recent FHFA data for 2025 indicates a resurgence, with a 19.81% year-over-year increase, outperforming the Georgia state average of 1.83% by nearly 18 percentage points. This divergence suggests that while the broader state market stabilizes, Evans County experiences heightened volatility, likely driven by specific local transaction dynamics rather than broad regional trends. Economic context further explains this volatility. The county supports only 3,916 jobs with an average weekly wage of $868, indicating a constrained local economy that may not fully sustain rapid price growth. Housing supply remains limited, with only 25 new units authorized in 2025, exacerbating scarcity pressures. Net migration of +136 filers suggests modest population growth, but the small sample size limits its impact on market depth. For potential buyers, the current mortgage rate of 7.32% impacts monthly payments, yet the low price-to-income ratio mitigates this burden. Renter’s advantages are less pronounced given the favorable purchase metrics, but the recent price correction offers a more stable entry point than the 2022 peak. The market’s high momentum score contrasts with its low stability rating, reflecting a dynamic but unpredictable environment where affordability remains the primary draw despite economic limitations.

The median home value in Evans County is 30.2% below the Georgia state average and 39.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $719/month is 25.5% below the state average.
