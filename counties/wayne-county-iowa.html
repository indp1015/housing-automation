Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 34.9% below the Iowa state average and 51.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $659/month is 20.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Wayne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.9%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
