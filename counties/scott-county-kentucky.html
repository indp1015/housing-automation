Market Analysis

The median home value in Scott County is 79.4% above the Kentucky state average and 26.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,247/month is 57.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Scott County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Scott County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.7%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
