Market Analysis

The median home value in Morgan County is 56.4% above the Utah state average and 187.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,492/month is 29.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 36.6, Morgan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Morgan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.8%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
