Market Analysis

The median home value in Morgan County is 59.1% above the Utah state average and 184.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,493/month is 35.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.5, Morgan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Morgan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 90.2%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
