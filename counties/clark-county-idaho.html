Market Analysis

The median home value in Clark County is 34.9% below the Idaho state average and 4.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $771/month is 16.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Clark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
