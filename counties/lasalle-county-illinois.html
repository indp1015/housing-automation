Market Analysis

The median home value in LaSalle County is 5.8% above the Illinois state average and 28.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $928/month is 7.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in LaSalle County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

LaSalle County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.4%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
