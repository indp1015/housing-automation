Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 22.1% below the Georgia state average and 32.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $710/month is 26.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Marion County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.9%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
