Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 21.3% below the Maryland state average and 30.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,100/month is 22.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.9%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
