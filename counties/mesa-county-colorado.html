Market Analysis

The median home value in Mesa County is 11.4% below the Colorado state average and 66.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,182/month is 9.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.7, Mesa County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Mesa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.5%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
