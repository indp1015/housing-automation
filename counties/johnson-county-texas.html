Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnson County is 53.5% above the Texas state average and 25.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,398/month is 40.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Johnson County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Johnson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
