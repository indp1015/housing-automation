📝 Local Market Analysis

Pettis County, Missouri, functions as a balanced market with distinct affordability advantages, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.75, which is significantly lower than the national average of 3.20. The price-to-rent ratio of 15.7 further supports this classification, falling below the national benchmark of 18.7, indicating that purchasing remains more financially efficient than renting for qualified buyers. While the median home value of $166,300 sits 27.0% below the national average, it is only 5.4% below the state average, positioning the county as a relatively accessible entry point within Missouri’s broader housing landscape. The rent burden of 17.6% aligns closely with the national average of 17.2%, suggesting that rental costs are proportionate to local incomes, though the low occupancy rate of 69.1% implies a robust owner-occupier base that stabilizes the local investment environment. Multi-year trends reveal a period of rapid appreciation followed by recent stabilization. Home values surged from $111,400 in 2017 to $166,300 in 2024, with the peak year-over-year growth of 12.0% occurring in 2022. This momentum is currently moderating, as evidenced by the 3.0% year-over-end increase in 2024, contrasting sharply with the 11.83% year-over-year change reported in the latest FHFA HPI data. This divergence between the slower ACS census data and the faster HPI index suggests that recent mortgage transactions are reflecting a sharper, more immediate price adjustment than the five-year census estimates capture. The local economy supports this activity through a net migration gain of 814 filers and an average weekly wage of $975, which sustains demand despite a median household income of $60,430 being 11.1% below the national mean. Supply constraints and economic fundamentals drive the current market dynamics. With only 117 new units authorized in 2025, including just 29 single-family homes, the limited new construction pipeline fails to outpace the inflow of residents, contributing to sustained price pressure. The national 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40% acts as a significant barrier for new entrants, yet the county’s lower absolute price point mitigates this burden compared to higher-cost regions. Consequently, the market exhibits high momentum due to strong price appreciation and positive migration, but lower stability and vitality scores are driven by the modest employment base of 16,027 jobs and the lagging income growth relative to national standards. Buyers benefit from the county’s superior affordability metrics, while renters face a market where rising home values are not yet fully mirrored in rent increases, maintaining a favorable price-to-rent dynamic.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Pettis County is 5.4% below the Missouri state average and 27.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $885/month is 13.2% above the state average.
