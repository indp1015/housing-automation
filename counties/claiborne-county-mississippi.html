Market Analysis

The median home value in Claiborne County is 39.1% below the Mississippi state average and 63.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $660/month is 17.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.4, buying a home in Claiborne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Claiborne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.5%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
