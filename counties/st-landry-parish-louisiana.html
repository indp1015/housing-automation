Market Analysis

The median home value in St. Landry Parish is 14.9% below the Louisiana state average and 37.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $748/month is 18.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in St. Landry Parish is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
