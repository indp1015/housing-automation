Market Analysis

The median home value in Van Zandt County is 19.2% above the Texas state average and 2.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,066/month is 6.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Van Zandt County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Van Zandt County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
