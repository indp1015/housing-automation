Market Analysis

The median home value in Lake County is 56.2% below the Tennessee state average and 57.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $532/month is 40.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lake County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 47.8%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
