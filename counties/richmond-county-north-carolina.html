Market Analysis

The median home value in Richmond County is 45.7% below the North Carolina state average and 43.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $724/month is 25.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.7, buying a home in Richmond County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Richmond County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.9%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
