📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 65032, located within Cole County, Missouri, exhibits a robust and accelerating price trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) for this area has risen from 171.57 in 2020 to 274.51 in 2025, representing a cumulative increase of approximately 60%. This growth is not linear; while the index climbed moderately between 2020 and 2022, a significant surge occurred in 2023, jumping from 189.23 to 268.98. This sharp appreciation suggests a period of intense demand or limited inventory that fundamentally reset the local price baseline before stabilizing at higher levels in 2025. Current market dynamics reveal a notable divergence from broader regional trends. The 6.62% year-over-year appreciation in Cole County outpaces the Missouri state average of 3.71%, indicating that local fundamentals are driving stronger gains than statewide economic factors. However, this growth is more measured than the explosive 20.83% increase seen in neighboring ZIP 65023. This relative moderation suggests that 65032 is stabilizing after its 2023 spike, positioning it as a mature segment of the county market rather than a speculative frontier. The HPI level of 274.5, significantly above the 2000 baseline, reflects substantial long-term equity accumulation for existing homeowners. In terms of sub-market character, 65032 aligns with the broader Cole County profile, which features a median home value of $229,600 and a median household income of $74,876. With an owner-occupancy rate of 67.9% and a median gross rent of $834, the area functions as a balanced, middle-income residential market rather than a luxury enclave or a renter-heavy zone. The proximity to ZIP 65074, which shows a more conservative 3.92% growth, further contextualizes 65032 as a high-performing yet accessible sub-market. For prospective buyers, the data indicates a market with strong historical appreciation but currently moderating growth rates. Residents should note that while entry barriers have risen significantly since 2020, the current 7.40% mortgage rate requires careful budgeting to maintain affordability relative to the county’s median income levels.
