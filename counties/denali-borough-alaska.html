Market Analysis

The median home value in Denali Borough is 3.0% below the Alaska state average and 20.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $825/month is 33.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.8, Denali Borough is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Denali Borough has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.0%, compared to 64.7% in Alaska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
