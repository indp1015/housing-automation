Market Analysis

The median home value in Box Elder County is 5.3% below the Utah state average and 69.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,028/month is 6.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.0, Box Elder County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Box Elder County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.7%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
