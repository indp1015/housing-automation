Market Analysis

The median home value in Edgecombe County is 46.8% below the North Carolina state average and 45.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $858/month is 12.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.2, buying a home in Edgecombe County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Edgecombe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.8%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
