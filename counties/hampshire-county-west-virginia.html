Market Analysis

The median home value in Hampshire County is 48.1% above the West Virginia state average and 3.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $720/month is 7.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.4, Hampshire County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Hampshire County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.7%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
