Market Analysis

The median home value in Rusk County is 8.0% below the Texas state average and 24.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,015/month is 1.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Rusk County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Rusk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
