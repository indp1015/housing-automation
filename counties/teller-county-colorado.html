📝 Editor's Insight

Teller County exhibits a strong buyer’s market profile, evidenced by a high 82.2% owner-occupancy rate and a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, significantly exceeding the national average of 18.0. This suggests substantial long-term equity potential relative to rental costs. Over the past five years, the market demonstrated aggressive appreciation, with home values growing at an 11.0% CAGR to reach $445,000. Notably, rents accelerated faster at 12.2%, indicating that rental demand outpaced housing supply growth during this period. Despite incomes rising to $80,666, affordability remains strained. The price-to-income ratio stands at 5.52, nearly double the national benchmark of 3.09, while the rent burden reaches 26.0% compared to the 17.2% national average. A critical insight is that the county’s median home value is 109.4% above the national mean, yet only 11.8% above the Colorado state average. This disparity highlights that Teller County’s premium is driven by regional dynamics rather than statewide outliers, making it a high-cost environment relative to broader Colorado standards.
