Market Analysis

The median home value in Mecklenburg County is 72.9% above the North Carolina state average and 78.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,627/month is 66.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Mecklenburg County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.1%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
