Market Analysis

The median home value in Miami-Dade County is 65.4% above the Florida state average and 101.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,731/month is 40.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Miami-Dade County has an owner-occupancy rate of 52.2%, compared to 74.0% in Florida and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
