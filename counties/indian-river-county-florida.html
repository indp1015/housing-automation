Market Analysis

The median home value in Indian River County is 23.6% above the Florida state average and 54.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,357/month is 4.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Indian River County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.1%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
