Market Analysis

The median home value in Wilson County is 65.9% above the Texas state average and 35.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,069/month is 11.0% above the state average.

Wilson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.7%, compared to 73.0% in Texas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
