Market Analysis

The median home value in Lawrence County is 91.4% above the South Dakota state average and 52.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $938/month is 19.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.8, Lawrence County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lawrence County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.1%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
