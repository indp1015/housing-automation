Market Analysis

The median home value in Hartley County is 40.9% above the Texas state average and 15.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,028/month is 3.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hartley County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.4%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
