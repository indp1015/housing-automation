Market Analysis

The median home value in Chelan County is 14.4% above the Washington state average and 115.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,182/month is 1.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.1, Chelan County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Chelan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.3%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
