📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 62656 exhibits a robust and accelerating price trajectory over the past five years. With a current Home Price Index (HPI) of 181.3 as of 2025, the area has nearly doubled its value relative to the 2000 baseline. The recent trend is particularly steep, showing a jump from 135.9 in 2021 to 181.3 in 2025. This represents a significant compounding effect, with the most recent year-over-year increase of 7.03% following a substantial 12.16% gain in the prior period. Such consistent upward movement indicates sustained demand pressure within this specific Logan County sector, distinguishing it from a flat or volatile market. When compared to broader geographic metrics, ZIP 62656’s 7.03% year-over-year growth slightly lags the Logan County average of 7.91% but significantly outpaces the Illinois state average of 5.54%. This divergence suggests that while the county is experiencing a strong regional boom, 62656 maintains a solid, though slightly more moderate, pace of appreciation. It does not display the extreme overheating seen in nearby ZIP 61723, which recorded a 13.68% increase, nor the contraction observed in ZIP 62693. The alignment with the state trend, albeit at a higher magnitude, indicates that local fundamentals are driving growth beyond general statewide inflationary pressures. The sub-market character of 62656 is defined by strong owner-occupancy and moderate income levels. With a parent county median home value of $134,400 and a median household income of $66,358, the area functions as an accessible, entry-to-mid-tier market rather than a luxury enclave. The 68.5% owner-occupancy rate further confirms a stable, residential community structure. For prospective buyers, the practical takeaway is that 62656 offers a stable appreciation profile without the extreme volatility of neighboring areas. However, with mortgage rates at 7.40%, the rising HPI implies that monthly carrying costs are increasing. Residents and buyers should recognize that while the market is healthy, the rapid HPI growth since 2021 has significantly raised the entry price point compared to historical norms.
