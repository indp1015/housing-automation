Market Analysis

The median home value in Benton County is 21.7% above the Iowa state average and 8.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $826/month is 0.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Benton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.0%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
