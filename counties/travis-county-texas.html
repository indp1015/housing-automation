Market Analysis

The median home value in Travis County is 182.6% above the Texas state average and 130.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,669/month is 73.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Travis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 53.0%, compared to 73.0% in Texas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
