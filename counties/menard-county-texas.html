Market Analysis

The median home value in Menard County is 41.8% below the Texas state average and 52.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $729/month is 26.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.4, buying a home in Menard County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Menard County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
