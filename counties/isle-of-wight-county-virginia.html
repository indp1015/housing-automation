Market Analysis

The median home value in Isle of Wight County is 20.5% above the Virginia state average and 56.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,359/month is 16.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Isle of Wight County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.1%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
