Market Analysis

The median home value in Allen County is 15.8% below the Ohio state average and 27.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $922/month is 2.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Allen County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Allen County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.7%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
