Market Analysis

The median home value in Juneau County is 24.1% below the Wisconsin state average and 22.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $879/month is 3.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Juneau County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Juneau County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.1%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
