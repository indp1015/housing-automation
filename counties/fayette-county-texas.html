Market Analysis

The median home value in Fayette County is 57.8% above the Texas state average and 29.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $988/month is 0.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Fayette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.4%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
