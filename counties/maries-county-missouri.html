Market Analysis

The median home value in Maries County is 24.7% above the Missouri state average and 3.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $590/month is 24.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.9, Maries County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Maries County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.3%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
