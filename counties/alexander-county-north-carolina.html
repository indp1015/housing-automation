Market Analysis

The median home value in Alexander County is 10.0% below the North Carolina state average and 7.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $764/month is 21.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Alexander County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
