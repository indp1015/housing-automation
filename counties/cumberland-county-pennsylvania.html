Market Analysis

The median home value in Cumberland County is 30.9% above the Pennsylvania state average and 21.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,249/month is 26.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Cumberland County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Cumberland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
