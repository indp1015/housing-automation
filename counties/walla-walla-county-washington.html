Market Analysis

The median home value in Walla Walla County is 6.3% below the Washington state average and 79.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,218/month is 2.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.0, Walla Walla County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Walla Walla County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.0%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
