Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 54.7% above the Texas state average and 26.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,165/month is 16.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
