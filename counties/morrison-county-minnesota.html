Market Analysis

The median home value in Morrison County is 6.5% above the Minnesota state average and 12.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $782/month is 14.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.3, Morrison County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Morrison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.9%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
