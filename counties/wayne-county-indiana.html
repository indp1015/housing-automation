Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 29.2% below the Indiana state average and 40.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $813/month is 7.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Wayne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.7%, compared to 76.0% in Indiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
