Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 26.9% below the Indiana state average and 38.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $815/month is 10.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Wayne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.2%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
