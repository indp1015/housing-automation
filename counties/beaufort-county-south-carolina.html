Market Analysis

The median home value in Beaufort County is 128.1% above the South Carolina state average and 100.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,653/month is 68.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Beaufort County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.9%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
