Market Analysis

The median home value in Beaufort County is 124.7% above the South Carolina state average and 92.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,484/month is 57.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Beaufort County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.7%, compared to 72.9% in South Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
