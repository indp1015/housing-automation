Market Analysis

The median home value in Graham County is 27.5% below the North Carolina state average and 25.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $676/month is 30.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Graham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
