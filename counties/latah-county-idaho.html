Market Analysis

The median home value in Latah County is 9.8% above the Idaho state average and 61.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $905/month is 1.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.5, Latah County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Latah County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
