Market Analysis

The median home value in Ashe County is 3.3% above the North Carolina state average and 6.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $859/month is 12.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Ashe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.4%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
