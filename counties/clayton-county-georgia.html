Market Analysis

The median home value in Clayton County is 12.3% above the Georgia state average and 2.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,433/month is 48.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Clayton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clayton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.5%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
