Market Analysis

The median home value in Clayton County is 7.6% above the Georgia state average and 8.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,307/month is 41.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.4, buying a home in Clayton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clayton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 53.2%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
