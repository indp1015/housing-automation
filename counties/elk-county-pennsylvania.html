Market Analysis

The median home value in Elk County is 37.0% below the Pennsylvania state average and 41.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $719/month is 27.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Elk County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Elk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.5%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
