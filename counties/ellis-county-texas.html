Market Analysis

The median home value in Ellis County is 87.1% above the Texas state average and 53.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,594/month is 59.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Ellis County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Ellis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.8%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
