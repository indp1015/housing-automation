Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 28.2% below the Indiana state average and 39.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $796/month is 12.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Knox County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Knox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.8%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
