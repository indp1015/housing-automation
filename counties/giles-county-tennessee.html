Market Analysis

The median home value in Giles County is 4.9% above the Tennessee state average and 2.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $862/month is 3.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Giles County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.3%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
