Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 19.2% above the Virginia state average and 56.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,048/month is 8.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.4, Madison County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
