Market Analysis

The median home value in Union County is 62.2% above the South Dakota state average and 27.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,055/month is 39.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Union County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 72.7% in South Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
