Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 28.4% below the Nevada state average and 3.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $919/month is 21.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lincoln County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.0%, compared to 73.0% in Nevada and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
