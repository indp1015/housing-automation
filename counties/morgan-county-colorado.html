Market Analysis

The median home value in Morgan County is 24.8% below the Colorado state average and 41.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,092/month is 12.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Morgan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.1%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
