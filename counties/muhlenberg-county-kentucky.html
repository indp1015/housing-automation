Market Analysis

The median home value in Muhlenberg County is 20.8% below the Kentucky state average and 44.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $718/month is 9.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Muhlenberg County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Muhlenberg County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
