Market Analysis

The median home value in Eau Claire County is 11.3% above the Wisconsin state average and 14.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $977/month is 9.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Eau Claire County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.8%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
