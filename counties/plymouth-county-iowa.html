Market Analysis

The median home value in Plymouth County is 36.1% above the Iowa state average and 3.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $865/month is 6.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Plymouth County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.7%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
