📝 Local Market Analysis

Plymouth County, Iowa, presents a balanced market structure with distinct affordability advantages relative to national standards. With a median home value of $228,900 and a median household income of $83,251, the price-to-income ratio stands at 2.75, significantly below the national average of 3.20. This metric indicates that housing costs remain manageable for local earners. Furthermore, the rent burden is only 13.1% of income, well under the national average of 17.2%, suggesting that rental housing is also highly accessible. However, the price-to-rent ratio of 21.0 exceeds the national average of 18.7, implying that purchasing offers a more favorable long-term financial position than renting, despite the overall affordability of both options. The market has experienced a pronounced appreciation cycle, with home values rising from $150,200 in 2017 to $228,900 in 2024. This represents a cumulative increase of approximately 52% over seven years. The pace of growth moderated from a peak of 13.0% in 2022 to 4.7% in 2024, indicating a stabilization phase. Recent FHFA data confirms this cooling trend, showing a year-over-year increase of 4.27% in 2025, which outpaces the Iowa state average of 2.25% by 2.02 percentage points. This outperformance suggests that Plymouth County retains stronger demand fundamentals than the broader state, likely driven by its proximity to Des Moines and higher local incomes. Economic indicators support continued stability, though supply constraints are evident. The county supports approximately 9,994 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,222, contributing to the 22.4% income premium over national averages. Migration data shows a net inflow of 622 filers between 2021 and 2022, with incoming residents earning an average of $88,149, slightly below the outgoing residents' $90,724, yet still above the county median. Housing supply remains tight, with only 45 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits, limiting new inventory. Against a backdrop of a 7.40% national mortgage rate, the low price-to-income ratio provides a buffer for buyers. For potential purchasers, the market offers strong value relative to income levels, while renters benefit from low cost burdens, though the limited new construction suggests that existing inventory will remain the primary driver of price dynamics.
