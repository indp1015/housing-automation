Market Analysis

The median home value in Haskell County is 8.7% below the Oklahoma state average and 39.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $770/month is 8.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Haskell County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Haskell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.3%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
