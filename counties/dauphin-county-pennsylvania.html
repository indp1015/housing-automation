Market Analysis

The median home value in Dauphin County is 11.5% above the Pennsylvania state average and 3.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,217/month is 22.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Dauphin County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Dauphin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.1%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
