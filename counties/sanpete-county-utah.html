Market Analysis

The median home value in Sanpete County is 17.1% below the Utah state average and 52.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $954/month is 17.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.3, Sanpete County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sanpete County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 77.2% in Utah and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
