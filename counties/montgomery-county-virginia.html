Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 3.0% above the Virginia state average and 33.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,240/month is 6.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.3%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
