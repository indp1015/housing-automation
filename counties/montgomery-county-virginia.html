Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 2.0% above the Virginia state average and 34.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,206/month is 5.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Montgomery County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.7%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
