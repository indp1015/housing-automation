Market Analysis

The median home value in Lipscomb County is 33.3% below the Texas state average and 45.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $876/month is 12.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.8, buying a home in Lipscomb County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lipscomb County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.4%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
