Market Analysis

The median home value in Greene County is 16.1% above the Virginia state average and 50.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,309/month is 12.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Greene County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.4%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
