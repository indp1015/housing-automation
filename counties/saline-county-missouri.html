Market Analysis

The median home value in Saline County is 14.3% below the Missouri state average and 33.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $739/month is 5.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Saline County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Saline County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.8%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
