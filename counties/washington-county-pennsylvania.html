Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 7.7% above the Pennsylvania state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $949/month is 4.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washington County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
