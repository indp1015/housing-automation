Market Analysis

The median home value in Ward County is 51.9% above the North Dakota state average and 17.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,048/month is 35.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Ward County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.8%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
