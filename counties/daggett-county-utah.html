Market Analysis

The median home value in Daggett County is 35.9% below the Utah state average and 14.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $775/month is 29.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Daggett County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Daggett County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.8%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
