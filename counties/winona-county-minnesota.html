Market Analysis

The median home value in Winona County is 1.2% below the Minnesota state average and 3.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $907/month is 3.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Winona County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.5%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
