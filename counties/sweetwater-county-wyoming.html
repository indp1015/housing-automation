Market Analysis

The median home value in Sweetwater County is 27.5% below the Wyoming state average and 10.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $911/month is 3.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sweetwater County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.6%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
