Market Analysis

The median home value in Clinton County is 24.0% below the Kentucky state average and 46.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $617/month is 22.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Clinton County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Clinton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.1%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
