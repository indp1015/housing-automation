Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 52.1% below the South Carolina state average and 59.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $663/month is 29.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.9, buying a home in Marion County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
