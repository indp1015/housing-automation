Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnson County is 13.0% below the Tennessee state average and 17.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $639/month is 25.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Johnson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.6%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
