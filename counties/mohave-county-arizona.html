Market Analysis

The median home value in Mohave County is 5.8% above the Arizona state average and 23.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,091/month is 1.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Mohave County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.1%, compared to 70.7% in Arizona and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
