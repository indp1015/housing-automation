Market Analysis

The median home value in El Paso County is 3.2% below the Texas state average and 20.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,079/month is 8.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.9, buying a home in El Paso County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

El Paso County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.2%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
