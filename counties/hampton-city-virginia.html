Market Analysis

The median home value in Hampton city is 16.9% below the Virginia state average and 7.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,427/month is 22.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Hampton city is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Hampton city has an owner-occupancy rate of 56.9%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
