Market Analysis

The median home value in Yuma County is 18.0% below the Arizona state average and 4.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $993/month is 7.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Yuma County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Yuma County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.0%, compared to 70.7% in Arizona and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
