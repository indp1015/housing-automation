Market Analysis

The median home value in Valley County is 6.1% below the Nebraska state average and 32.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $626/month is 21.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Valley County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.8%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
