Market Analysis

The median home value in Rolette County is 41.0% below the North Dakota state average and 54.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $505/month is 34.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Rolette County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Rolette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.8%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
