Market Analysis

The median home value in Kinney County is 56.0% below the Texas state average and 64.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,272/month is 27.6% above the state average.

Kinney County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.9%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
