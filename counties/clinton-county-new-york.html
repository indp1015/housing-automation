Market Analysis

The median home value in Clinton County is 35.8% below the New York state average and 20.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,003/month is 14.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Clinton County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Clinton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.4%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
