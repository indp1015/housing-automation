Market Analysis

The median home value in Issaquena County is 18.4% below the Mississippi state average and 51.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $339/month is 57.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.2, Issaquena County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Issaquena County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.0%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
