Market Analysis

The median home value in Issaquena County is 28.9% below the Mississippi state average and 57.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $253/month is 67.6% below the state average.

Issaquena County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.3%, compared to 71.7% in Mississippi and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
