Market Analysis

The median home value in Cleveland County is 15.0% below the North Carolina state average and 12.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $914/month is 6.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Cleveland County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Cleveland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.9%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
