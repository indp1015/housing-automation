Market Analysis

The median home value in Caldwell County is 18.3% below the North Carolina state average and 15.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $744/month is 23.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Caldwell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.5%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
