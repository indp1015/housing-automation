Market Analysis

The median home value in Hart County is 8.7% above the Georgia state average and 5.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $844/month is 12.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hart County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.8%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
