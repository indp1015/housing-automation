Market Analysis

The median home value in Pinal County is 31.3% above the Arizona state average and 53.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,537/month is 43.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Pinal County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Pinal County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.7%, compared to 70.7% in Arizona and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
