Market Analysis

The median home value in Cedar County is 12.4% above the Nebraska state average and 18.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $716/month is 10.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cedar County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
