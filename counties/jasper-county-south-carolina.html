Market Analysis

The median home value in Jasper County is 63.0% above the South Carolina state average and 42.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,328/month is 35.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Jasper County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
