Market Analysis

The median home value in Marshall County is 22.1% above the Tennessee state average and 16.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $924/month is 7.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Marshall County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
