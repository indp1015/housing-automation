Market Analysis

The median home value in Laurens County is 14.6% below the South Carolina state average and 25.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $880/month is 10.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Laurens County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Laurens County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.5%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
