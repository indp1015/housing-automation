Market Analysis

The median home value in Hood River County is 70.7% above the Oregon state average and 189.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,417/month is 19.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.8, Hood River County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Hood River County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.4%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
