Market Analysis

The median home value in Bannock County is 14.3% below the Idaho state average and 26.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $879/month is 4.4% below the state average.

Bannock County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
