Market Analysis

The median home value in Somerset County is 34.4% below the Maine state average and 26.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $887/month is 8.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Somerset County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Somerset County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.3%, compared to 76.3% in Maine and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
