Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnson County is 6.2% below the Wyoming state average and 39.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,081/month is 15.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Johnson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.6%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
