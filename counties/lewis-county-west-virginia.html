Market Analysis

The median home value in Lewis County is 2.6% above the West Virginia state average and 33.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $730/month is 6.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lewis County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lewis County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.2%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
