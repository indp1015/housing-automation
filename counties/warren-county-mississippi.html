Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 13.2% above the Mississippi state average and 31.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $889/month is 13.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Warren County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.2%, compared to 71.7% in Mississippi and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
