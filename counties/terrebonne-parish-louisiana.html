Market Analysis

The median home value in Terrebonne Parish is 11.8% above the Louisiana state average and 17.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,065/month is 16.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Terrebonne Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Terrebonne Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.3%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
