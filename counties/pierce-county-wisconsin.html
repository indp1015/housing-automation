Market Analysis

The median home value in Pierce County is 41.7% above the Wisconsin state average and 45.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,025/month is 15.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Pierce County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Pierce County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.8%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
