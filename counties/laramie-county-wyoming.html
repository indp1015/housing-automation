Market Analysis

The median home value in Laramie County is 3.0% above the Wyoming state average and 53.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,080/month is 15.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Laramie County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Laramie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.8%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
