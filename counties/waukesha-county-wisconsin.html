Market Analysis

The median home value in Waukesha County is 71.5% above the Wisconsin state average and 76.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,300/month is 46.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Waukesha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
