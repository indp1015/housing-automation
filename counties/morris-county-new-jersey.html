Market Analysis

The median home value in Morris County is 35.5% above the New Jersey state average and 155.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,904/month is 16.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Morris County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Morris County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
