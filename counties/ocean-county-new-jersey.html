Market Analysis

The median home value in Ocean County is 7.4% below the New Jersey state average and 75.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,755/month is 7.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Ocean County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Ocean County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
