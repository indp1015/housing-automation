Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnson County is 48.3% above the Indiana state average and 25.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,271/month is 39.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Johnson County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Johnson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.7%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
