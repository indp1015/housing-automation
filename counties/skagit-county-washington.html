Market Analysis

The median home value in Skagit County is 22.3% above the Washington state average and 129.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,439/month is 19.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.2, Skagit County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Skagit County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.6%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
