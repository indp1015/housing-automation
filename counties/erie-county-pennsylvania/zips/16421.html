The housing market in ZIP code 16421 exhibits a volatile yet robust upward trajectory over the past five years. This near-perfect alignment with the parent county indicates that 16421 is not an outlier but rather a representative microcosm of the regional market. Nearby ZIPs, such as 16502 with an 11.29% increase, show that while growth is widespread, 16421 maintains a steady, moderate pace without the extreme volatility seen in adjacent areas. Characteristically, this sub-market appears to be middle-income rather than luxury or entry-level. With a county median home value of $177,000 and a median household income of $63,354, the area supports a mix of owner-occupied and rental homes, with owner occupancy at 68.1%.
