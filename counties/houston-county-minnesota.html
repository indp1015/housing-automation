Market Analysis

The median home value in Houston County is 1.7% above the Minnesota state average and 7.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $881/month is 3.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Houston County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.1%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
