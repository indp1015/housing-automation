Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin city is 13.3% below the Virginia state average and 12.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $988/month is 15.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin city has an owner-occupancy rate of 53.9%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
