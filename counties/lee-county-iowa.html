Market Analysis

The median home value in Lee County is 23.0% below the Iowa state average and 41.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $814/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.7, buying a home in Lee County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
