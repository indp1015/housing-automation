Market Analysis

The median home value in Chatham County is 89.6% above the North Carolina state average and 96.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,180/month is 20.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.5, Chatham County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Chatham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.6%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
