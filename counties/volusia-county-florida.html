Market Analysis

The median home value in Volusia County is 10.1% above the Florida state average and 37.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,467/month is 12.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Volusia County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
