Market Analysis

The median home value in Republic County is 45.5% below the Kansas state average and 66.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $664/month is 17.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.5, buying a home in Republic County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Republic County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
