Market Analysis

The median home value in Wright County is 30.1% below the Iowa state average and 47.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $790/month is 5.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.6, buying a home in Wright County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wright County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
