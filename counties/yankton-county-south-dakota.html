Market Analysis

The median home value in Yankton County is 25.3% above the South Dakota state average and 1.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $777/month is 2.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Yankton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.3%, compared to 72.7% in South Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
