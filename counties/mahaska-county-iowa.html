Market Analysis

The median home value in Mahaska County is 12.3% below the Iowa state average and 33.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $821/month is 1.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Mahaska County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Mahaska County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.8%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
