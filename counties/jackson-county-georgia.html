Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 74.3% above the Georgia state average and 51.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,126/month is 16.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Jackson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.3%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
