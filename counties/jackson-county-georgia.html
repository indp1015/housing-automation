Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 72.9% above the Georgia state average and 47.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,048/month is 13.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.8%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
