Market Analysis

The median home value in Clinton County is 14.5% below the Indiana state average and 27.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $920/month is 1.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Clinton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clinton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
