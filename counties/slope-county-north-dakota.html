Market Analysis

The median home value in Slope County is 3.3% below the North Dakota state average and 25.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $525/month is 32.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.0, Slope County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Slope County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
