Market Analysis

The median home value in Wakulla County is 9.2% below the Florida state average and 13.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,108/month is 15.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Wakulla County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Wakulla County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.9%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
