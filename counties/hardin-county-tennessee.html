Market Analysis

The median home value in Hardin County is 28.2% below the Tennessee state average and 29.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $775/month is 13.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Hardin County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Hardin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
