Market Analysis

The median home value in Geary County is 24.4% above the Kansas state average and 24.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,104/month is 37.7% above the state average.

Geary County has an owner-occupancy rate of 43.5%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
