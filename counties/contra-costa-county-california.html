Market Analysis

The median home value in Contra Costa County is 42.3% above the California state average and 280.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,375/month is 40.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.4, Contra Costa County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Contra Costa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.0%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
