Contra Costa County functions as a distinctively expensive owner-occupant market, characterized by a 67.7% ownership rate that significantly exceeds national norms. Market Analysis

The median home value in Contra Costa County is 45.5% above the California state average and 292.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,322/month is 43.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.8, Contra Costa County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Contra Costa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.7%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
