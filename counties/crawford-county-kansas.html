Market Analysis

The median home value in Crawford County is 4.5% below the Kansas state average and 41.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $849/month is 5.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.0, buying a home in Crawford County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Crawford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.8%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
