Market Analysis

The median home value in Midland County is 67.9% above the Texas state average and 37.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,407/month is 41.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Midland County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Midland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.6%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
