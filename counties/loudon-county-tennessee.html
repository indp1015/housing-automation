Market Analysis

The median home value in Loudon County is 55.8% above the Tennessee state average and 52.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,062/month is 19.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.3, Loudon County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Loudon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.3%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
