Market Analysis

The median home value in Steele County is 3.0% above the Minnesota state average and 7.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,061/month is 13.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Steele County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Steele County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.4%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
