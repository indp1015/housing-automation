Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin Parish is 27.5% below the Louisiana state average and 44.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $719/month is 17.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Franklin Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
