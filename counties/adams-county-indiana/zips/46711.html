📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 46711 exhibits a volatile price trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) rose from 149.0 in 2021 to a peak of 197.43 in 2023, followed by a contraction to 181.44 in 2024. However, the most recent data indicates a sharp correction upward, with the 2025 HPI reaching 250.41. This pattern suggests significant short-term volatility rather than steady, linear appreciation, characterized by a substantial dip in 2024 that was immediately followed by a robust rebound in the current period. This ZIP code demonstrates extreme divergence from broader regional metrics. While the state of Indiana recorded a modest year-over-year increase of 3.45% and Adams County grew by 7.07%, ZIP 46711 surged by 38.01%. This magnitude of outperformance is statistically anomalous for a single-year period in a stable market. Such a sharp deviation from county and state averages often signals limited transaction volume, where a small number of high-value sales disproportionately influences the index. Consequently, the data may reflect idiosyncratic local sales rather than broad-based market appreciation, distinguishing it from the more moderate trends observed in neighboring ZIPs 46733 and 46773. Despite the high relative HPI, the sub-market character aligns with the broader county profile. The parent county’s median home value stands at $178,400, and the median household income is $65,289, indicating an affordable, entry-level market with strong owner occupancy at 83.5%. The high HPI index likely reflects the accumulation of value over the 28-year data history rather than current luxury pricing. For prospective buyers, the primary consideration is the data’s volatility. The significant gap between the ZIP’s 38.01% growth and the county’s 7.07% growth suggests that recent price signals may be skewed. Buyers should rely on individual comparable sales rather than aggregate index trends to assess true market value in this specific micro-market.
