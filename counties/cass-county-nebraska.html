Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 54.8% above the Nebraska state average and 11.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $996/month is 25.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.4%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
