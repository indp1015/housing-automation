Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 61.7% above the Nebraska state average and 16.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $987/month is 26.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.
