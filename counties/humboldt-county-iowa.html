Market Analysis

The median home value in Humboldt County is 8.9% below the Iowa state average and 31.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $744/month is 10.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Humboldt County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Humboldt County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
