Market Analysis

The median home value in Cumberland County is 13.1% above the Tennessee state average and 7.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $829/month is 3.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cumberland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.2%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
