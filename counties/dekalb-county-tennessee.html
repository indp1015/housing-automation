Market Analysis

The median home value in DeKalb County is 2.5% above the Tennessee state average and 2.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $773/month is 10.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

DeKalb County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.9%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
