Market Analysis

The median home value in Stark County is 54.4% above the North Dakota state average and 23.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $966/month is 25.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Stark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.9%, compared to 74.1% in North Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
