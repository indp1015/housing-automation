Market Analysis

The median home value in Stark County is 54.6% above the North Dakota state average and 19.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $960/month is 24.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Stark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.8%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
