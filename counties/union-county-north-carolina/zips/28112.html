📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP 28112's FHFA HPI reached 273.0 in 2025, up from 191.36 in 2021 — a 42.7% cumulative gain over four years. The trajectory shows clear deceleration: annual appreciation ran roughly 20.8% into 2022 and 10.6% into 2023, then cooled to 3.6% and 3.0% in 2024 and 2025. The ZIP has therefore shifted from a post-pandemic surge phase into a slower, steadier growth pattern, though it has not reversed. At +3.00% year over year, 28112 slightly trails Union County's +3.49% but substantially outpaces North Carolina's +0.38%. That pattern — modestly below the county, far above the state — indicates a sub-market participating in regional demand without leading it. Within the county, 28112's HPI of 273.0 sits below ZIP 28173 (302.3, +4.74%) and ZIP 28110 (283.4, +3.27%), positioning it as the more moderately priced of the three. County ACS data frame the sub-market character: median home value of $417,300, median gross rent of $1,496, median household income of $102,900, and 81.6% owner occupancy. This is an owner-dominant, middle-to-upper-middle market rather than a renter-heavy one, with rent-to-value ratios implying relatively contained rental yields. For prospective buyers, the practical implication is a market where prices continue rising but at a decelerating pace against a 7.40% mortgage rate environment, and where entry pricing is comparatively favorable relative to adjacent Union County ZIPs.
