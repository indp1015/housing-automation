Market Analysis

The median home value in Bourbon County is 42.6% above the Kentucky state average and 0.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $822/month is 3.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Bourbon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.7%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
