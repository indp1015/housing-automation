Market Analysis

The median home value in Gunnison County is 50.0% above the Colorado state average and 182.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,191/month is 4.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 41.8, Gunnison County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Gunnison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.9%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
