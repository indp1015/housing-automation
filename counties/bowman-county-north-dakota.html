Market Analysis

The median home value in Bowman County is 6.2% below the North Dakota state average and 27.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $765/month is 0.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Bowman County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Bowman County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.3%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
