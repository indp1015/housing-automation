Market Analysis

The median home value in Okeechobee County is 32.1% below the Florida state average and 15.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,082/month is 17.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Okeechobee County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Okeechobee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.9%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
