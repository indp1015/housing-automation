Market Analysis

The median home value in Washtenaw County is 89.6% above the Michigan state average and 64.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,463/month is 61.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washtenaw County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.6%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
