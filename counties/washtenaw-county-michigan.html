Market Analysis

The median home value in Washtenaw County is 93.7% above the Michigan state average and 66.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,400/month is 60.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Washtenaw County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.6%, compared to 79.9% in Michigan and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
