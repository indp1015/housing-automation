Market Analysis

The median home value in Poinsett County is 27.2% below the Arkansas state average and 54.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $619/month is 20.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.9, buying a home in Poinsett County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Poinsett County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.4%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
