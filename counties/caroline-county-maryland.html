Market Analysis

The median home value in Caroline County is 22.3% below the Maryland state average and 28.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,070/month is 24.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Caroline County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.9%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
