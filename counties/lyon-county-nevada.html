Market Analysis

The median home value in Lyon County is 14.0% above the Nevada state average and 62.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,259/month is 10.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lyon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.6%, compared to 70.8% in Nevada and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
