📝 Editor's Insight

Franklin County operates as a distinct buyer’s market, evidenced by a price-to-rent ratio of 13.8, significantly below the national average of 18.0. This metric indicates that purchasing property offers superior long-term value compared to renting. The five-year trend reveals synchronized growth, with home values increasing at a 4.2% CAGR while rents rose at 3.5%, suggesting stable asset appreciation without excessive speculative inflation. Affordability remains robust locally; the price-to-income ratio of 2.29 is substantially lower than the national benchmark of 3.09. Additionally, the rent burden sits at 16.6%, slightly under the national average of 17.2%, confirming that housing costs do not disproportionately strain household budgets relative to the median income of $51,919. A critical insight for potential residents is the high owner occupancy rate of 74.0%, which limits available rental inventory. Consequently, while purchase affordability is high, renters face a tighter supply environment, potentially reducing negotiating leverage on lease terms despite moderate rent levels.

The median home value in Franklin County is 7.5% below the Arkansas state average and 42.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $719/month is 7.1% below the state average.
