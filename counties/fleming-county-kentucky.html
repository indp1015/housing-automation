Market Analysis

The median home value in Fleming County is 16.2% below the Kentucky state average and 40.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $715/month is 9.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Fleming County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Fleming County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.8%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
