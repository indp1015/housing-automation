📝 Editor's Insight

Crenshaw County functions as a distinct buyer’s market, evidenced by a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, significantly below the national average of 18.0. This metric indicates that purchasing equity is comparatively cheaper than long-term renting. Over the past five years, home values expanded at a 7.6% CAGR to reach $108,400, outpacing the 5.5% CAGR in median gross rent. This divergence suggests that asset appreciation is accelerating relative to rental costs, favoring those who secure ownership early. Affordability remains robust, with a price-to-income ratio of 2.21, well below the national benchmark of 3.09. Additionally, the 16.2% rent burden sits under the 17.2% national standard, indicating manageable housing costs for local earners. A critical insight lies in the county’s 74.2% owner-occupancy rate. This high concentration of homeowners suggests a stable, low-turnover market where inventory scarcity may limit competition, but also implies fewer transaction opportunities for new entrants compared to higher-churn urban areas.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Crenshaw County is 28.6% below the Alabama state average and 49.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $661/month is 19.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Crenshaw County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Crenshaw County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.2%, compared to 73.2% in Alabama and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
