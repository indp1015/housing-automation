Market Analysis

The median home value in Crawford County is 38.7% below the Indiana state average and 48.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $686/month is 24.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Crawford County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Crawford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
