Market Analysis

The median home value in Pulaski County is 8.0% above the Missouri state average and 16.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,112/month is 42.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Pulaski County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Pulaski County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.2%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
