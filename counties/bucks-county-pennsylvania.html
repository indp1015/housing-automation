Market Analysis

The median home value in Bucks County is 110.2% above the Pennsylvania state average and 95.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,605/month is 62.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Bucks County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.8%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
