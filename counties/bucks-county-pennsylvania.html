Market Analysis

The median home value in Bucks County is 110.5% above the Pennsylvania state average and 99.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,537/month is 60.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Bucks County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 73.9% in Pennsylvania and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
