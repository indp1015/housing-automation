Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 21.9% above the Ohio state average and 5.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $950/month is 5.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Knox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
