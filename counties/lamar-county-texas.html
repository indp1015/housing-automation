Market Analysis

The median home value in Lamar County is 7.7% above the Texas state average and 11.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $949/month is 4.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lamar County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lamar County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.1%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
