Market Analysis

The median home value in Elmore County is 10.9% below the Idaho state average and 39.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,243/month is 28.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Elmore County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
