Market Analysis

The median home value in Spokane County is 6.8% below the Washington state average and 75.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,200/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.7, Spokane County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Spokane County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
