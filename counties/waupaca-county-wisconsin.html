Market Analysis

The median home value in Waupaca County is 9.7% below the Wisconsin state average and 7.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $852/month is 6.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Waupaca County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.3%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
