Market Analysis

The median home value in Sweet Grass County is 19.2% above the Montana state average and 45.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,149/month is 36.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sweet Grass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.7%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
