Market Analysis

The median home value in Sarasota County is 44.8% above the Florida state average and 80.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,818/month is 39.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Sarasota County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Sarasota County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
