Market Analysis

The median home value in Elk County is 53.5% below the Kansas state average and 71.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $534/month is 32.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.5, buying a home in Elk County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
