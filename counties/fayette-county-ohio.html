Market Analysis

The median home value in Fayette County is 13.1% below the Ohio state average and 25.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $822/month is 9.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Fayette County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
