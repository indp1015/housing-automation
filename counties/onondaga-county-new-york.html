Market Analysis

The median home value in Onondaga County is 30.6% below the New York state average and 12.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,067/month is 5.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in Onondaga County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Onondaga County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.7%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
