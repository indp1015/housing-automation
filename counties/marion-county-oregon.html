Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 7.7% above the Oregon state average and 82.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,414/month is 18.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.3%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
