Market Analysis

The median home value in Pulaski County is 18.5% below the Indiana state average and 31.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $772/month is 15.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Pulaski County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Pulaski County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.6%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
