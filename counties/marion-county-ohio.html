Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 20.1% below the Ohio state average and 31.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $864/month is 4.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Marion County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.5%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
