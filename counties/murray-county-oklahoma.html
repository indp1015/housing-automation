Market Analysis

The median home value in Murray County is 5.1% above the Oklahoma state average and 29.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $881/month is 5.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Murray County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Murray County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.1%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
