Market Analysis

The median home value in Keweenaw County is about equal to the Michigan state average and 14.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $679/month is 22.2% below the state average.

Keweenaw County has an owner-occupancy rate of 91.7%, compared to 79.9% in Michigan and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
