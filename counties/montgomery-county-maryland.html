Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 73.0% above the Maryland state average and 181.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,068/month is 41.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.8, Montgomery County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.3%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
