Market Analysis

The median home value in Barry County is 33.9% above the Michigan state average and 16.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $972/month is 7.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Barry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.3%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
