Market Analysis

The median home value in Merced County is 35.7% below the California state average and 72.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,343/month is 20.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Merced County has an owner-occupancy rate of 54.0%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
