Market Analysis

The median home value in Payette County is about equal to the Idaho state average and 46.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $874/month is 4.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.6, Payette County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Payette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
