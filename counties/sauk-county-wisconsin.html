Market Analysis

The median home value in Sauk County is 10.5% above the Wisconsin state average and 13.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,018/month is 11.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sauk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.4%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
