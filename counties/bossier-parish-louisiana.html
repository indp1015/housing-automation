📝 Local Market Analysis

Bossier Parish operates as a balanced market with a distinct tilt toward buyer-favorable conditions, driven primarily by its price-to-rent ratio of 16.1, which sits well below the national average of 18.7. This metric suggests that purchasing is economically more efficient than renting, a structural advantage reinforced by the county’s 65.1% owner-occupancy rate. While the median home value of $227,200 is nearly identical to the national average, it commands a 35.7% premium over the Louisiana state mean, indicating strong local demand relative to the broader state context. The market’s affordability is moderate, with a price-to-income ratio of 3.26, marginally higher than the national benchmark of 3.20, while the rent burden of 20.2% exceeds the national average of 17.2%, signaling that rental costs are becoming a heavier financial strain for tenants. The trend data reveals a significant acceleration in home values, which surged 15.4% in 2022 before moderating to 5.6% in 2024. This recent deceleration aligns with the FHFA HPI’s 2025 year-over-year change of +1.43%, which trails the state’s +1.59% growth. This divergence suggests that the initial post-pandemic price spike has stabilized, with the county now tracking slightly below state-level momentum. A notable anomaly exists in the migration data, where net inflow of 3,158 filers between 2021 and 2022 was accompanied by outflowing residents earning slightly more ($75,611) than incoming residents ($74,530). This income parity, despite population growth, limits upward pressure on the median household income, which stands at $69,617, just 2.4% above the national average. Economic fundamentals provide a mixed backdrop for housing demand. Local employment totals 37,096 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,003, supporting a steady but not booming labor market. Housing supply remains constrained by low construction activity, with only 664 new single-family units authorized and zero multi-family permits in 2025, which likely sustains price resilience despite the high 7.40% national mortgage rate. The composite assessment reflects this tension: high vitality and momentum scores are offset by low stability and affordability metrics. For prospective buyers, the lower price-to-rent ratio offers a long-term value proposition, whereas renters face a market where rising home values are not yet fully transmitted to rental rates, creating a temporary window of relative rental affordability before potential market adjustments.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Bossier Parish is 35.7% above the Louisiana state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,174/month is 28.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Bossier Parish is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Bossier Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.1%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
