Market Analysis

The median home value in Marlboro County is 58.9% below the South Carolina state average and 64.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $689/month is 30.0% below the state average.

Marlboro County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.0%, compared to 73.2% in South Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
