Market Analysis

The median home value in Sullivan County is 4.5% below the Tennessee state average and 6.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $889/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sullivan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.1%, compared to 74.1% in Tennessee and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
