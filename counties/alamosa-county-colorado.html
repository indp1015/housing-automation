Market Analysis

The median home value in Alamosa County is 44.1% below the Colorado state average and 5.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $907/month is 30.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Alamosa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.9%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
