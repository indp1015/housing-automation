Market Analysis

The median home value in Garfield County is 45.2% below the Washington state average and 5.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $791/month is 36.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.2, Garfield County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Garfield County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
