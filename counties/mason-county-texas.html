Market Analysis

The median home value in Mason County is 59.1% above the Texas state average and 29.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $751/month is 22.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.5, Mason County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Mason County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.9%, compared to 73.0% in Texas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
