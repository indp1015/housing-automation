Market Analysis

The median home value in Shelby County is 6.0% above the Ohio state average and 8.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $922/month is 2.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Shelby County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
