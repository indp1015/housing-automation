Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 12.6% above the West Virginia state average and 26.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $932/month is 19.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.9, buying a home in Marion County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
