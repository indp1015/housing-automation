Market Analysis

The median home value in Cumberland County is 15.3% below the North Carolina state average and 12.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,222/month is 25.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Cumberland County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cumberland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.0%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
