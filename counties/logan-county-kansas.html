📝 Local Market Analysis

Logan County, Kansas, functions as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $117,900 and a price-to-income ratio of 1.56, which is less than half the national average of 3.20. This extreme affordability is reinforced by a rent burden of 13.3%, significantly lower than the 17.2% national benchmark, and a price-to-rent ratio of 11.7, well below the national 18.7. These metrics indicate that purchasing equity is substantially more cost-effective than long-term renting, positioning the local market firmly in favor of homeownership for qualified buyers. The county’s median household income of $75,662 exceeds the national average by 11.3%, further supporting purchasing power despite the low absolute price points. The multi-year trend reveals a sharp appreciation spike followed by stabilization. After a modest 2019 dip, home values surged 22.4% in 2021 to reach $109,700, before moderating to 5.8% growth in 2022 and slowing to just 1.1% in 2024. This deceleration from peak momentum suggests the market has absorbed the initial post-pandemic demand shock. Notably, home values are rising faster than rents, a divergence that typically signals strengthening buyer demand relative to the rental sector. The current 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40% presents a headwind for monthly payments, yet the low principal amount mitigates this impact compared to higher-cost markets. Regional context highlights Logan County’s competitive position, with home values sitting 15.1% below the Kansas state average and 48.2% below the national mean. The local economic base is small, with only 619 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $869, yet the influx of 1,104 filers in 2021-2022, averaging $79,399 in income, suggests an attractive quality of life or cost structure is drawing higher-earning residents. Net migration of +51 filers indicates a stable, slightly growing population of 2,707. For potential buyers, the market offers significant entry-level value with minimal rent burden risk, while the slowing price appreciation provides a more predictable environment for long-term investment. Renters face limited cost advantages, as the low price-to-rent ratio makes homeownership a financially superior alternative for those seeking stability. The combination of high relative income and low asset prices creates a unique niche where affordability remains robust despite national interest rate pressures.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Logan County is 15.1% below the Kansas state average and 48.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $840/month is 4.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.7, buying a home in Logan County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Logan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.9%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
