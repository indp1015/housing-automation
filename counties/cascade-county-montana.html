Market Analysis

The median home value in Cascade County is 3.3% below the Montana state average and 15.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $902/month is 9.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cascade County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.2%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
