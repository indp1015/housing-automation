Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 130.1% above the West Virginia state average and 53.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,103/month is 45.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.2%, compared to 78.0% in West Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
