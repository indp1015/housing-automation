Market Analysis

The median home value in Greenbrier County is 0.7% above the West Virginia state average and 34.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $868/month is 11.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Greenbrier County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Greenbrier County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.1%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
