Market Analysis

The median home value in Jones County is 48.2% below the Texas state average and 57.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $866/month is 13.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.3, buying a home in Jones County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jones County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
