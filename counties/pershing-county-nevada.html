Market Analysis

The median home value in Pershing County is 41.5% below the Nevada state average and 21.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $715/month is 38.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Pershing County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.0%, compared to 73.0% in Nevada and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
