Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 23.2% below the Ohio state average and 33.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $769/month is 14.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Jackson County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.9%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
