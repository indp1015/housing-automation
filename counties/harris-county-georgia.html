Market Analysis

The median home value in Harris County is 53.0% above the Georgia state average and 33.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,048/month is 8.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Harris County has an owner-occupancy rate of 88.8%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
