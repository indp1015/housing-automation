Market Analysis

The median home value in Utah County is 29.6% above the Utah state average and 131.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,441/month is 31.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.3, Utah County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Utah County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.1%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
