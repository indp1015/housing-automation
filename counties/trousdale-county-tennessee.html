Market Analysis

The median home value in Trousdale County is 40.9% above the Tennessee state average and 34.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $805/month is 6.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.4, Trousdale County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Trousdale County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
