Market Analysis

The median home value in Big Horn County is 48.2% below the Montana state average and 37.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $697/month is 17.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Big Horn County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
