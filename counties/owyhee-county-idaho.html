Market Analysis

The median home value in Owyhee County is 1.3% above the Idaho state average and 58.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $821/month is 15.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 36.5, Owyhee County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Owyhee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
