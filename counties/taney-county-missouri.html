Market Analysis

The median home value in Taney County is 24.5% above the Missouri state average and 3.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $926/month is 18.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Taney County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Taney County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.0%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
