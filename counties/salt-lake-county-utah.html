Market Analysis

The median home value in Salt Lake County is 28.3% above the Utah state average and 128.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,493/month is 35.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.0, Salt Lake County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Salt Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.0%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
