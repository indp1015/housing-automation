Market Analysis

The median home value in Fannin County is 51.9% above the Georgia state average and 29.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $876/month is 5.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.1, Fannin County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fannin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
