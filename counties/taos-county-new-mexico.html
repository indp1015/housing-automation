Market Analysis

The median home value in Taos County is 94.9% above the New Mexico state average and 67.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,069/month is 22.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.6, Taos County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Taos County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 73.3% in New Mexico and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
