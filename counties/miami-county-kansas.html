Market Analysis

The median home value in Miami County is 107.2% above the Kansas state average and 26.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,107/month is 38.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Miami County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
