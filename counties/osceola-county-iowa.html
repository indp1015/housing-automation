Market Analysis

The median home value in Osceola County is 28.3% below the Iowa state average and 46.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $797/month is 4.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Osceola County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
