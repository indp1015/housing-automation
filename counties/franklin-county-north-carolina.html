Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 12.5% above the North Carolina state average and 13.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $988/month is 5.3% above the state average.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.1%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
