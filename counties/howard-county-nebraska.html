Market Analysis

The median home value in Howard County is 27.0% above the Nebraska state average and 8.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $777/month is 2.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Howard County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
