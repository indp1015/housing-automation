📝 Local Market Analysis

Bienville Parish operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.84, which sits below the national average of 3.20. Despite a median household income of $35,011, significantly lower than the national benchmark, the median home value of $99,500 remains accessible. This affordability is reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 13.7, well under the national average of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing offers a more favorable long-term value proposition than renting, where the rent burden stands at 20.8% of income compared to the 17.2% national norm. The market’s momentum is exceptionally strong, with the FHFA Home Price Index surging 28.11% year-over-year in 2025, vastly outpacing the Louisiana state average of 1.59%. This rapid appreciation follows a three-year upward trajectory, including an 18.7% jump in 2022, indicating a sustained correction from previous stagnation. The economic context reveals a small but stabilizing labor market with 3,214 jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,058. Housing supply remains tight, with only 18 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family construction, which likely contributes to the sharp price increases. Migration patterns show a net inflow of 138 filers, with incoming residents earning slightly less than those leaving, suggesting the market is attracting budget-conscious buyers rather than high-income professionals. The divergence between the modest ACS five-year estimates and the aggressive FHFA quarterly data highlights a recent acceleration in transaction prices that may not yet be fully reflected in broader demographic averages. The overall market assessment reflects high momentum but low stability and vitality. While the 99.1 momentum score is driven by the exceptional 28.11% price growth, the stability score of 24.3 and vitality score of 33.2 reflect the limited economic base and low population of 12,638. The affordability score of 36.5 indicates that while entry prices are low, the rapid price appreciation is eroding purchasing power relative to stagnant local incomes. For potential buyers, the current environment offers low entry costs but requires caution regarding the pace of appreciation. Renters face a relatively low cost of living, with median gross rent at $606, but the low price-to-rent ratio suggests that ownership remains the more financially efficient strategy for those with stable local employment. The market is currently driven more by investment interest and supply constraints than by robust local wage growth.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Bienville Parish is 40.6% below the Louisiana state average and 56.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $606/month is 33.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.7, buying a home in Bienville Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Bienville Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.2%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
