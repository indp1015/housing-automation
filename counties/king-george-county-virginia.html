Market Analysis

The median home value in King George County is 42.4% above the Virginia state average and 87.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,426/month is 25.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

King George County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
