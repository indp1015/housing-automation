Market Analysis

The median home value in Lawrence County is 12.4% below the Tennessee state average and 16.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $736/month is 14.4% below the state average.

Lawrence County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 73.7% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
