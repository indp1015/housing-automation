📝 Local Market Analysis

Allegany County, New York, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $101,700 and a price-to-income ratio of 1.62, which is less than half the national average of 3.20. The rent burden stands at 14.6% of median household income, significantly below the national benchmark of 17.2%, further reinforcing affordability for both owners and renters. While the county’s median income of $62,869 trails the national average by 7.5%, the substantial discount in housing costs—home values are 55.3% below the national mean and 63.8% below the state average—creates a favorable equity environment. The high owner-occupancy rate of 79.3% indicates a stable residential base, though the low vitality score of 39.9 reflects limited economic dynamism compared to other metrics. Price trends reveal a period of accelerated appreciation followed by normalization. Median home values rose from $73,300 in 2017 to $101,700 in 2024, with a peak year-over-year increase of 15.2% in 2022. Recent FHFA data shows a 4.42% annual gain, outperforming the New York state average of 3.55% by 0.87 percentage points. This momentum is supported by net positive migration of 762 filers between 2021 and 2022, despite modest local employment of 9,379 jobs and an average weekly wage of $944. Housing supply remains constrained, with only 97 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits, suggesting that demand outpaces new construction. The divergence between strong price momentum and moderate rent growth, where home values are rising faster than rents, highlights a shift in asset appreciation rather than broad-based rental inflation. The composite assessment of this market is driven primarily by exceptional affordability and positive momentum, which offset lower vitality and stability concerns. The price-to-rent ratio of 11.1, well below the national average of 18.7, indicates that purchasing is significantly more economical than renting over time. For potential buyers, the current environment offers low entry costs relative to income, though the 7.40% national mortgage rate impacts monthly payment calculations. Renter’s face a less pressured market, with gross rents averaging $763 per month. The data suggests a stable, low-cost housing ecosystem where the primary constraint is not price but rather the limited availability of new inventory and the modest scale of the local labor market.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Allegany County is 63.8% below the New York state average and 55.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $763/month is 34.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.1, buying a home in Allegany County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Allegany County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.3%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
