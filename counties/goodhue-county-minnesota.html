Market Analysis

The median home value in Goodhue County is 19.6% above the Minnesota state average and 25.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $966/month is 3.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Goodhue County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
