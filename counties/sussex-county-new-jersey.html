📝 Local Market Analysis

Sussex County, New Jersey, functions as a balanced market characterized by strong momentum but constrained affordability. The price-to-income ratio of 3.18 aligns almost perfectly with the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden of 16.4% sits slightly below the national norm of 17.2%. However, the price-to-rent ratio of 19.4 exceeds the national average of 18.7, suggesting that owning remains marginally more expensive relative to renting than in the broader national context. This equilibrium is supported by a high owner-occupancy rate of 83.7% and a median household income of $116,186, which is 70.9% above the national average. The market’s stability is further evidenced by the 81.0 stability index, reflecting consistent demand without extreme volatility. The primary driver of the current market dynamic is robust price momentum, with the FHFA Home Price Index rising 6.57% year-over-year, outperforming the state’s 4.53% growth by 2.04 percentage points. This upward trajectory follows a peak annual appreciation of 14.0% in 2022, moderating to 7.8% in 2024. A notable divergence exists between asset values and rental costs; while home values have surged, median gross rent remains at $1,585, indicating that price growth is outpacing rental inflation. This pattern suggests that equity accumulation is accelerating faster than the cost of living for renters, potentially widening the wealth gap between owners and tenants. Economic fundamentals provide a mixed backdrop for this appreciation. Local employment stands at 31,741 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,139, yet housing supply remains tight, with only 96 new units authorized in 2025. This limited supply, combined with positive net migration of 2,645 filers, creates structural pressure on prices. The 7.40% national mortgage rate further complicates affordability, as high borrowing costs offset the region’s strong income levels. Consequently, while the market exhibits high vitality in terms of transaction activity and price growth, the low vitality score of 42.2 reflects the friction between high demand and restricted supply. Potential buyers face a competitive environment where income strength supports purchasing power, but rising rates and limited inventory maintain upward pressure on median home values, which currently sit 62.2% above the national average.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Sussex County is 14.1% below the New Jersey state average and 62.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,585/month is 3.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Sussex County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Sussex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.7%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
