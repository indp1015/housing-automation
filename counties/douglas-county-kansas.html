Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 122.9% above the Kansas state average and 35.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,099/month is 37.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 50.1%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
