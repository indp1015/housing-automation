Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 114.8% above the Kansas state average and 33.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,066/month is 35.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 49.5%, compared to 74.0% in Kansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
