Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 17.9% above the Minnesota state average and 24.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $889/month is 2.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.9%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
