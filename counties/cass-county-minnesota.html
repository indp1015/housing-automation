Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 21.0% above the Minnesota state average and 26.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $863/month is 8.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.8, Cass County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.2%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
