Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 1.9% above the Oklahoma state average and 31.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $865/month is 6.3% above the state average.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.3%, compared to 72.1% in Oklahoma and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
