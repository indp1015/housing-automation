Market Analysis

The median home value in Bonneville County is 4.8% above the Idaho state average and 54.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,054/month is 14.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.9, Bonneville County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Bonneville County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.6%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
