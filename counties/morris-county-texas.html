Market Analysis

The median home value in Morris County is 35.4% below the Texas state average and 47.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $859/month is 13.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.7, buying a home in Morris County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Morris County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.9%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
