Market Analysis

The median home value in Lyon County is 34.2% above the Iowa state average and 1.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $802/month is 1.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lyon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.3%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
