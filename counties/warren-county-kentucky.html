Market Analysis

The median home value in Warren County is 60.4% above the Kentucky state average and 13.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,002/month is 26.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Warren County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.6%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
