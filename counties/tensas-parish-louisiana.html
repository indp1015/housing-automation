📝 Local Market Analysis

Tensas Parish operates as a distinctively affordable market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 2.36, significantly below the national average of 3.20. This affordability is reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 12.0, well under the national benchmark of 18.7, suggesting that ownership costs remain relatively low compared to rental alternatives. The market exhibits a buyer’s advantage due to these low entry barriers, although the 74.2% owner-occupancy rate indicates a stable, established housing stock. With a median home value of $86,300, the parish sits 62.1% below the national average and 48.4% below the state average, reflecting a deep discount driven by limited local economic vitality. Trend analysis reveals volatility rather than steady growth, with home values fluctuating from a 2017 low of $62,200 to the current $86,300. The 2020 spike of 18.8% was followed by a 7.0% correction in 2022, before stabilizing with a 7.5% increase in 2024. This pattern suggests that price movements are reactive to broader macroeconomic shifts rather than driven by consistent local demand. Notably, the rent burden stands at 19.7% of median income, slightly above the national average of 17.2%, indicating that while absolute rents are low at $601 per month, they consume a disproportionate share of the modest $36,615 median household income. This divergence highlights that affordability metrics, while favorable in absolute terms, face pressure from stagnant income growth. The economic context further explains the market’s constrained vitality, with only 520 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $885. Migration data shows a net gain of just 47 filers, with inflow and outflow averages nearly identical at approximately $55,000, indicating a lack of high-income demographic shifts that could drive sustained price appreciation. The 7.40% national mortgage rate adds friction to purchasing power, though the low home prices mitigate this impact. Consequently, the market’s stability is undermined by its minimal economic base, while momentum remains moderate due to recent price recovery. For potential entrants, the data suggests a low-risk entry point relative to national standards, but limited upside potential due to the parish’s small population of 3,935 and weak local wage growth.

The median home value in Tensas Parish is 48.4% below the Louisiana state average and 62.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $601/month is 34.2% below the state average.
