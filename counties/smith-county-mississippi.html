Market Analysis

The median home value in Smith County is 17.1% below the Mississippi state average and 50.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $664/month is 17.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Smith County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Smith County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.4%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
