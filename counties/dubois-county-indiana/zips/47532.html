📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 47532, located in Dubois County, Indiana, has demonstrated a consistent, albeit moderating, upward price trajectory over the past five years. However, the pace of growth has decelerated significantly. After a sharp 11.8% increase between 2021 and 2022, the index remained nearly flat in 2024 at 209.51 before registering a modest 1.74% year-over-year gain in 2025. This recent stabilization suggests that the initial post-pandemic surge has cooled, resulting in a more normalized price environment for the local market. This disparity indicates that demand and price momentum are concentrated in specific pockets within Dubois County, rather than being uniformly distributed across all areas. The sub-market character of ZIP 47532 aligns with the broader county profile, which features a median home value of $230,100 and a median household income of $75,979. With an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, the area maintains a solid base of homeownership, though it is not exclusively a luxury market. The HPI level of 213.15, while higher than the 2000 baseline, remains below the levels seen in adjacent ZIPs like 47542 (243.9).
