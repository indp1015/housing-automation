Market Analysis

The median home value in Nassau County is 143.5% above the New York state average and 200.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,252/month is 92.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.3, Nassau County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Nassau County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.9%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
