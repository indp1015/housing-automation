Market Analysis

The median home value in Nassau County is 146.6% above the New York state average and 211.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,195/month is 93.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.0, Nassau County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Nassau County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.9%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
