Market Analysis

The median home value in Napa County is 42.7% above the California state average and 281.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,237/month is 32.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.4, Napa County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Napa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.4%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
