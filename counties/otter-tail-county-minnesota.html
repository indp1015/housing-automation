Market Analysis

The median home value in Otter Tail County is 14.5% above the Minnesota state average and 19.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $868/month is 7.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Otter Tail County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Otter Tail County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.5%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
