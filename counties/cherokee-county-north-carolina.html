Market Analysis

The median home value in Cherokee County is 2.6% below the North Carolina state average and 1.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $800/month is 14.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cherokee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.6%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
