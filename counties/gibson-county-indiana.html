Market Analysis

The median home value in Gibson County is 6.2% below the Indiana state average and 21.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $935/month is 2.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Gibson County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Gibson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
