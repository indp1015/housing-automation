Market Analysis

The median home value in Kane County is 7.1% below the Utah state average and 65.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,120/month is 1.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.1, Kane County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Kane County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.7%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
