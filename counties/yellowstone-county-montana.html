Market Analysis

The median home value in Yellowstone County is 26.2% above the Montana state average and 53.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,131/month is 34.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.8, Yellowstone County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Yellowstone County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.6%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
