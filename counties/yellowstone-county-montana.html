Market Analysis

The median home value in Yellowstone County is 26.3% above the Montana state average and 50.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,084/month is 31.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Yellowstone County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.3%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
