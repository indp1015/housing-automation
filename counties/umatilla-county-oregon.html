Market Analysis

The median home value in Umatilla County is 28.3% below the Oregon state average and 21.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $962/month is 15.4% below the state average.

Umatilla County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.1%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
