Market Analysis

The median home value in York County is 17.2% above the Pennsylvania state average and 9.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,193/month is 20.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in York County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

York County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.3%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
