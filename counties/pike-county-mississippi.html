Market Analysis

The median home value in Pike County is 18.4% below the Mississippi state average and 51.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $919/month is 14.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.0, buying a home in Pike County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Pike County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.8%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
