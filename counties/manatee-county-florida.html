Market Analysis

The median home value in Manatee County is 37.7% above the Florida state average and 71.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,671/month is 28.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Manatee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Manatee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
