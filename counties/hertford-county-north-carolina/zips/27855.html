📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 27855, located within Hertford County, North Carolina, has experienced a period of significant appreciation followed by a sharp correction. The FHFA House Price Index (HPI) for this area rose from 144.84 in 2018 to a peak of 214.32 in 2023, representing a cumulative gain of approximately 48% over five years. This trajectory reflects the broader national surge in home values during the early 2020s, driven by low interest rates and high demand. However, the most recent data indicates a reversal, with the county’s year-over-year change standing at -3.54%. This decline contrasts with the state of North Carolina, which saw a modest increase of +0.38% over the same period. The divergence suggests that local market dynamics in Hertford County are decoupling from statewide trends, likely due to localized economic factors or a correction from previous overheating. In terms of sub-market character, ZIP 27855 aligns with an entry-level, price-sensitive segment. The parent county’s median home value is $119,200, and the median household income is $45,537, indicating a market where affordability is a primary constraint for residents. With an owner occupancy rate of 67.4%, the area maintains a balanced mix of ownership and rental housing, though the median gross rent of $844 per month remains relatively low compared to national averages. The HPI level of 214.32, while high relative to its 2000 baseline, must be contextualized against the low absolute price points; the index reflects percentage changes rather than nominal value, meaning the actual dollar increase remains modest in absolute terms. For prospective buyers and residents, the current environment presents both opportunity and caution.
