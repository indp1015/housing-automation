Market Analysis

The median home value in Pike County is 28.5% above the Pennsylvania state average and 19.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,378/month is 39.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Pike County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Pike County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.0%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
