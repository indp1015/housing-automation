Market Analysis

The median home value in James City County is 51.2% above the Virginia state average and 96.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,552/month is 32.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

James City County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
