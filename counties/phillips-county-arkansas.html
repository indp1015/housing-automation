Market Analysis

The median home value in Phillips County is 44.7% below the Arkansas state average and 65.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $737/month is 4.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 8.8, buying a home in Phillips County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Phillips County has an owner-occupancy rate of 51.2%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
