Market Analysis

The median home value in Brown County is 14.7% below the Texas state average and 30.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $919/month is 7.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Brown County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Brown County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.3%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
