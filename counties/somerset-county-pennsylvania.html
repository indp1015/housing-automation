Market Analysis

The median home value in Somerset County is 34.8% below the Pennsylvania state average and 38.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $749/month is 21.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in Somerset County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Somerset County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.3%, compared to 73.9% in Pennsylvania and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
