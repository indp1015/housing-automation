Market Analysis

The median home value in Lucas County is 27.2% below the Iowa state average and 44.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $738/month is 9.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.2, buying a home in Lucas County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lucas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.1%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
