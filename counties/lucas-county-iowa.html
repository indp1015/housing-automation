📝 Local Market Analysis

Lucas County, Iowa, functions as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-income ratio of 1.95, significantly below the national average of 3.20. The median home value of $120,200 stands 47.2% below the national mean and 29.7% below the state average, while the median household income of $61,793 trails the national figure by only 9.1%. This disparity creates a substantial affordability cushion, further evidenced by a rent burden of 13.8%, which is well under the 17.2% national benchmark. The price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, compared to the national 18.7, reinforces the economic advantage for homeownership over renting in this small rural jurisdiction. The market exhibits strong recent momentum, with home values appreciating from $84,900 in 2017 to $120,200 in 2024, representing a cumulative gain of approximately 41.6%. This trajectory was driven by a peak year-over-year increase of 16.1% in 2022, which has since moderated to 2.7% in 2024. A notable divergence exists between asset classes, as home values are rising faster than rents, suggesting that equity accumulation is outpacing rental cost inflation. This trend aligns with the high owner occupancy rate of 78.8%, indicating a stable, established housing stock rather than a speculative rental-heavy environment. Economic fundamentals support this stability despite limited new supply. With only two new units authorized in 2025, the housing inventory is effectively static, which likely contributes to the sustained price appreciation observed in recent years. The local economy, comprising 2,530 jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,020, provides a modest but sufficient income base for the current home values. Migration data shows a net inflow of 160 filers, with incoming residents earning an average of $62,403, slightly less than the $62,819 average of those leaving, suggesting a stable demographic profile without significant wealth-driven influx. Although the national 30-year mortgage rate sits at 7.40%, the low absolute price point in Lucas County mitigates the impact of high interest rates on monthly payments. For potential buyers, the combination of low entry costs and strong historical appreciation offers a favorable risk-reward profile, while renters benefit from low relative housing costs, though limited new multi-family construction may constrain rental availability in the long term.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Lucas County is 29.7% below the Iowa state average and 47.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $709/month is 14.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Lucas County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lucas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
