Market Analysis

The median home value in Clarke County is 5.8% below the Iowa state average and 29.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $931/month is 11.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Clarke County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Clarke County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.3%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
