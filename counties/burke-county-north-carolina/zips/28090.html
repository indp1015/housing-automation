📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 28090 exhibits a distinct price trajectory characterized by rapid appreciation followed by a recent correction. The Home Price Index (HPI) surged from 154.44 in 2021 to a peak of 239.38 in 2024, reflecting a period of intense demand. However, the index has since retreated to 231.36 in 2025, indicating a cooling phase after the previous three years of expansion. This recent dip suggests that the sub-market is adjusting to broader economic conditions, specifically the sustained high cost of borrowing. A notable divergence exists between ZIP 28090 and the wider regional trends. While the parent county recorded a year-over-year increase of +2.45% and the state posted a modest +0.38% gain, ZIP 28090 experienced a decline of -3.35%. This negative performance relative to the county and state averages signals that local inventory or buyer sentiment in this specific area is under unique pressure. The contrast highlights that while the broader market remains stable or slightly positive, this particular ZIP code is undergoing a sharper recalibration of values compared to its immediate neighbors and the state at large. The sub-market character of ZIP 28090 appears to be mid-range, situated above the county median home value of $185,400 but below the levels seen in nearby ZIP 28168. With a median household income of $58,592 and an owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%, the area supports a solid base of homeowners rather than a renter-heavy demographic. The median gross rent of $884 per month further underscores a market where ownership is the primary housing strategy. For prospective buyers, the current environment presents a potential entry point following the 2024 peak, though the 7.40% national mortgage rate continues to constrain purchasing power. Residents should note that while the area remains stable in terms of ownership rates, the recent price correction offers a different dynamic than the rapid appreciation observed in the preceding years.
