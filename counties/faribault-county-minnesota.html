Market Analysis

The median home value in Faribault County is 43.2% below the Minnesota state average and 40.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $799/month is 14.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Faribault County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Faribault County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.2%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
