Market Analysis

The median home value in Hendricks County is 60.3% above the Indiana state average and 35.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,436/month is 57.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Hendricks County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Hendricks County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.2%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
