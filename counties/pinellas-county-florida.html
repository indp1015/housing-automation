Market Analysis

The median home value in Pinellas County is 24.9% above the Florida state average and 55.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,642/month is 26.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Pinellas County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Pinellas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.5%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
