Market Analysis

The median home value in St. Lawrence County is 56.7% below the New York state average and 46.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $818/month is 30.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.4, buying a home in St. Lawrence County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

St. Lawrence County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.9%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
