Market Analysis

The median home value in Lane County is 11.4% above the Oregon state average and 89.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,355/month is 13.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.5, Lane County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lane County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.7%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
