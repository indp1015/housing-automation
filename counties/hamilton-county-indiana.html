Market Analysis

The median home value in Hamilton County is 112.9% above the Indiana state average and 79.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,468/month is 66.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hamilton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.3%, compared to 76.0% in Indiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
