Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 34.4% below the New York state average and 18.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $928/month is 20.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Madison County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.3%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
