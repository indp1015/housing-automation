Market Analysis

The median home value in Cook County is 108.9% above the Illinois state average and 44.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,381/month is 65.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Cook County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Cook County has an owner-occupancy rate of 57.5%, compared to 75.7% in Illinois and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
