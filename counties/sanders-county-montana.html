Market Analysis

The median home value in Sanders County is 28.7% above the Montana state average and 53.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $774/month is 6.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 34.9, Sanders County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sanders County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.0%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
