Market Analysis

The median home value in Sanders County is 29.7% above the Montana state average and 57.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $820/month is 2.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 36.5, Sanders County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Sanders County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
