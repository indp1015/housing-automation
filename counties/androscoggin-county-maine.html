Market Analysis

The median home value in Androscoggin County is 1.9% below the Maine state average and 9.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $966/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Androscoggin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.4%, compared to 76.3% in Maine and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
