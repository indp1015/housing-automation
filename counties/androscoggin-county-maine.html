📝 Local Market Analysis

Androscoggin County, Maine, exhibits characteristics of a renter’s market, driven by a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, which exceeds the national average of 18.7. This metric suggests that purchasing is relatively more expensive compared to renting, yet the 67.9% owner-occupancy rate indicates a substantial existing homeowner base. The market is currently classified as stressed due to affordability constraints, with a price-to-income ratio of 3.72 compared to the national benchmark of 3.20. While the median gross rent of $1,010 represents a manageable 17.9% of the median household income, the median home value of $252,800 places a significant burden on potential buyers, particularly given the 7.40% national mortgage rate. Trend data reveals a period of accelerated appreciation that has recently moderated. Home values surged 21.0% year-over-year in 2022, followed by 9.5% in 2023 and 9.0% in 2024. This trajectory contrasts with the state’s broader 0.98% year-over-year growth, indicating that Androscoggin County is outperforming the state by 6.00 percentage points. The FHFA House Price Index confirms this momentum, showing a 6.98% year-over-year increase in 2025. This divergence is notable because home values are rising significantly faster than rents, suggesting that equity growth is outpacing rental cost inflation. Such a pattern often signals a transition from a renter-favorable environment toward a buyer’s market, although current affordability metrics have not yet fully reflected this shift. The local economic context supports sustained demand. Net migration of +2,299 filers between 2021 and 2022, coupled with an average weekly wage of $1,159, provides a stable income base. However, housing supply remains tight, with only 453 new units authorized in 2025, including 269 single-family homes. This limited new construction, against a backdrop of 42,761 total jobs, contributes to the high momentum and vitality scores of the market. Despite the strong momentum, the overall market assessment remains constrained by low stability and affordability scores. The 3.72 price-to-income ratio and the premium paid over national averages for home values (+11.0%) weigh heavily on the composite evaluation. For potential participants, the data indicates a market with strong underlying demand and price appreciation, but entry costs remain elevated relative to local income levels, requiring careful financial planning for both purchase and rental strategies.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Androscoggin County is 3.0% below the Maine state average and 11.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,010/month is 1.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Androscoggin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.9%, compared to 76.6% in Maine and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
