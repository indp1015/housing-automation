Market Analysis

The median home value in Le Sueur County is 29.3% above the Minnesota state average and 36.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $885/month is 3.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.1, Le Sueur County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Le Sueur County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.0%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
