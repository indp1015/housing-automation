Market Analysis

The median home value in Emanuel County is 52.2% below the Georgia state average and 58.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $783/month is 18.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.1, buying a home in Emanuel County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Emanuel County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.8%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
