Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 48.1% below the Massachusetts state average and 44.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,177/month is 27.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.1%, compared to 67.2% in Massachusetts and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
