Market Analysis

The median home value in Pickens County is 60.2% above the Georgia state average and 36.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,079/month is 16.5% above the state average.

Pickens County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.9%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
