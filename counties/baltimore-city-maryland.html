Market Analysis

The median home value in Baltimore city is 37.9% below the Maryland state average and 0.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,331/month is 8.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Baltimore city is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Baltimore city has an owner-occupancy rate of 47.5%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
