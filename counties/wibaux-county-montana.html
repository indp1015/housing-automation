Market Analysis

The median home value in Wibaux County is 64.4% below the Montana state average and 56.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $963/month is 14.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 8.5, buying a home in Wibaux County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
