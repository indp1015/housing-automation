Market Analysis

The median home value in New Castle County is about equal to the Delaware state average and 54.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,448/month is 7.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

New Castle County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.0%, compared to 74.4% in Delaware and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
