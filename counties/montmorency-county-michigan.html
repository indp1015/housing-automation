Market Analysis

The median home value in Montmorency County is 19.6% below the Michigan state average and 30.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $686/month is 24.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Montmorency County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Montmorency County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.9%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
