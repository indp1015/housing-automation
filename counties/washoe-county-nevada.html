Market Analysis

The median home value in Washoe County is 65.2% above the Nevada state average and 134.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,507/month is 32.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.5, Washoe County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Washoe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 59.5%, compared to 70.8% in Nevada and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
