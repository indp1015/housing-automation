Market Analysis

The median home value in Richmond County is 146.5% above the New York state average and 211.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,689/month is 49.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.5, Richmond County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Richmond County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.9%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
