Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 17.0% below the Iowa state average and 37.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $769/month is 5.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Cass County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.3%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
