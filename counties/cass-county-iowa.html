📝 Local Market Analysis

Cass County, Iowa, functions as a distinctly buyer’s market, characterized by significant affordability advantages relative to national norms. With a median home value of $150,300 and a median household income of $63,013, the price-to-income ratio stands at 2.39, well below the national average of 3.20. Similarly, the rent burden is 14.8% of income, underperforming the national benchmark of 17.2%. These metrics indicate that housing costs remain manageable for local residents, creating a stable foundation for ownership despite broader economic headwinds. The owner-occupancy rate of 71.6% further underscores the community’s preference for equity accumulation over renting, supported by a price-to-rent ratio of 16.1, which is lower than the national average of 18.7. The market exhibits a complex trend pattern where recent price appreciation has outpaced rental growth. Home values surged 12.8% year-over-year in 2024, following a similar 12.8% jump in 2022, while the median gross rent remains modest at $777. This divergence suggests that capital gains in the owner-occupied sector are not yet fully reflected in rental yields, potentially signaling an emerging imbalance. However, the FHFA HPI index indicates a more subdued recent momentum, with a 1% year-over-year change in 2025, underperforming the state average by 1.03 percentage points. This deceleration contrasts with the sharp Census-based estimates, highlighting the difference between long-term valuation trends and short-term transactional pricing. Economic fundamentals provide limited support for sustained rapid growth. Local employment totals 4,273 jobs with an average weekly wage of $963, and new housing supply is constrained to just 11 authorized units in 2025. Although net migration was positive at 329 filers, the inflow average income of $75,223 does not significantly exceed the outflow average of $76,705, suggesting limited demographic wealth injection. Consequently, the market’s vitality is moderate, with affordability remaining the primary strength. The overall assessment reflects a challenging environment for high-growth expectations, as stability is tempered by low economic vitality and slowing momentum. Potential buyers benefit from low entry costs, but investors should note that the 7.40% national mortgage rate increases carrying costs, making the low price-to-income ratio the central competitive advantage in this rural Iowa market.

The median home value in Cass County is 12.1% below the Iowa state average and 34.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $777/month is 6.6% below the state average.
