Market Analysis

The median home value in Floyd County is 23.8% below the Virginia state average and 1.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $878/month is 24.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Floyd County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.9%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
