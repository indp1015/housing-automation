Market Analysis

The median home value in Floyd County is 22.5% below the Virginia state average and 1.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $826/month is 27.5% below the state average.

Floyd County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.0%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
