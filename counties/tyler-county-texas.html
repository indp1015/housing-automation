Market Analysis

The median home value in Tyler County is 15.6% below the Texas state average and 30.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,049/month is 5.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.5, buying a home in Tyler County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Tyler County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.2%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
