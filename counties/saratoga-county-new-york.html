Market Analysis

The median home value in Saratoga County is 24.0% above the New York state average and 53.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,394/month is 19.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Saratoga County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.3%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
