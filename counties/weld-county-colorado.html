Market Analysis

The median home value in Weld County is 11.7% above the Colorado state average and 110.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,469/month is 17.2% above the state average.

Weld County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
