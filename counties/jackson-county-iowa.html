Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 2.3% above the Iowa state average and 23.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $807/month is 3.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Jackson County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
