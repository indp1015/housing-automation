Market Analysis

The median home value in Williamsburg city is 44.7% above the Virginia state average and 88.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,407/month is 20.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.4, Williamsburg city is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Williamsburg city has an owner-occupancy rate of 53.2%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
