Market Analysis

The median home value in Mathews County is 25.4% above the Virginia state average and 64.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,108/month is 2.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Mathews County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Mathews County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.6%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
