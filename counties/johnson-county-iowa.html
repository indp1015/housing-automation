Market Analysis

The median home value in Johnson County is 82.5% above the Iowa state average and 38.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,104/month is 36.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Johnson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 58.8%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
