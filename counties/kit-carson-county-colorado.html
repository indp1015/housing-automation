Market Analysis

The median home value in Kit Carson County is 44.4% below the Colorado state average and 4.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $916/month is 26.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Kit Carson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.3%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
