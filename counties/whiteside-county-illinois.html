📝 Local Market Analysis

Whiteside County operates as a highly affordable, balanced housing market, characterized by significant value relative to national standards. The county’s median home value of $127,100 sits 44.2% below the national average and 16.9% below the Illinois state average, while the median household income of $67,500 remains nearly identical to the national mean. This disparity creates a price-to-income ratio of 1.88, substantially lower than the national benchmark of 3.20, and a rent burden of 15.2%, which undercuts the national average of 17.2%. These metrics indicate that both homeownership and renting are financially accessible, supporting a stable owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%. The market is not driven by speculative demand but by fundamental affordability, resulting in a price-to-rent ratio of 12.4 compared to the national 18.7, suggesting that buying offers a strong long-term value proposition over renting. Trend analysis reveals a period of rapid appreciation followed by stabilization. After modest single-digit growth between 2017 and 2021, home values surged 11.5% in 2022 to $122,200, before moderating to 2.9% in 2023 and 1.0% in 2024. Recent FHFA HPI data indicates a 3.98% year-over-year increase, which lags behind the Illinois state average of 5.54%, signaling that the county is underperforming relative to broader state trends. This divergence suggests that the initial post-2021 spike has normalized, with price growth now aligning more closely with general economic inflation rather than experiencing outsized local demand. The consistency between rising home values and rents further supports a balanced dynamic where neither side holds a distinct advantage in negotiating power. Economic fundamentals provide a mixed backdrop for future stability. Local employment stands at 15,492 jobs with an average weekly wage of $989, while net migration of +788 filers indicates sustained population growth. However, new housing supply is constrained, with only 23 single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family units, which may limit inventory expansion. The current 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.40% adds financing friction, yet the low entry price point mitigates this impact for qualified buyers. The overall market vitality is constrained by limited economic expansion, reflected in a low vitality metric, while high affordability and steady momentum drive the composite assessment. Consequently, the market remains attractive for income-qualified buyers seeking equity accumulation in a low-cost environment, though limited new construction may keep inventory tight.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Whiteside County is 16.9% below the Illinois state average and 44.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $856/month is 1.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.4, buying a home in Whiteside County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Whiteside County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.9%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
