Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 26.9% below the Illinois state average and 50.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $788/month is 9.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.8, buying a home in Knox County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Knox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.8%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
