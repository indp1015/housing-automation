📝 Local Market Analysis

Brunswick County presents a distinct buyer’s market characterized by significant affordability constraints despite robust economic vitality. The price-to-income ratio of 4.54 exceeds the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden stands at 21.0% compared to the 17.2% national norm. These metrics indicate that housing costs are disproportionately high relative to local earning power. However, the market is underpinned by strong stability and vitality, driven by a median household income of $77,024, which is 13.3% above the national average. The high owner-occupancy rate of 84.6% further signals a settled, long-term residential base rather than a transient rental-heavy environment. The multi-year trend reveals a period of accelerated appreciation that has recently moderated. Home values surged 19.0% in 2022 and 11.2% in 2024, rising from $194,700 in 2017 to $349,800 in 2024. This trajectory contrasts with the FHFA HPI’s recent 2.98% year-over-year change, which outpaces the state’s 0.38% growth by 2.60 percentage points. This divergence between recent quarterly momentum and long-term annual averages suggests a cooling but still positive trajectory. A notable anomaly is the price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, significantly above the 18.7 national average, indicating that buying is less favorable relative to renting than in typical markets, yet the data shows home values rising faster than rents, compressing the gap slightly. Regional context highlights Brunswick County’s premium status, with home values 48.7% above the state average and 53.6% above the national mean. This premium is supported by substantial net migration, with 8,818 net new filers in 2021-2022, and an average inflow income of $90,010. Local employment, totaling 36,894 jobs with an average weekly wage of $1,034, provides a stable economic foundation. However, new housing supply remains heavily skewed toward single-family units, with 4,454 permits authorized versus only 3 for multi-family, limiting rental inventory expansion. For potential buyers, the 7.40% mortgage rate combined with the high price-to-income ratio creates significant entry barriers, while renters face above-average cost burdens. The market’s high vitality and stability are offset by poor affordability, creating a challenging environment for first-time buyers despite strong regional economic performance.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Brunswick County is 48.7% above the North Carolina state average and 53.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,351/month is 38.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Brunswick County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.6%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
