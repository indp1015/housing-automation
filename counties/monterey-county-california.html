Market Analysis

The median home value in Monterey County is 28.2% above the California state average and 243.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,055/month is 21.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 31.7, Monterey County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Monterey County has an owner-occupancy rate of 52.1%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
