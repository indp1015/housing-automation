Market Analysis

The median home value in Monterey County is 26.7% above the California state average and 241.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,995/month is 23.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.2, Monterey County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Monterey County has an owner-occupancy rate of 52.3%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
