Market Analysis

The median home value in Williamson County is 234.2% above the Tennessee state average and 218.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,895/month is 120.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.6, Williamson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Williamson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.8%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
