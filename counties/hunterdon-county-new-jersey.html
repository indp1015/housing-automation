Market Analysis

The median home value in Hunterdon County is 20.3% above the New Jersey state average and 127.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,687/month is 3.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Hunterdon County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Hunterdon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.1%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
