Market Analysis

The median home value in Yavapai County is 60.0% above the Arizona state average and 86.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,351/month is 25.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Yavapai County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Yavapai County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.1%, compared to 70.7% in Arizona and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
