Market Analysis

The median home value in Union County is 75.7% above the Ohio state average and 51.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,247/month is 37.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Union County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.2%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
