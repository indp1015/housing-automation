Market Analysis

The median home value in Ada County is 44.2% above the Idaho state average and 125.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,577/month is 62.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.1, Ada County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Ada County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.8%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
