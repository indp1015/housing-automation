Market Analysis

The median home value in Ada County is 52.6% above the Idaho state average and 125.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,465/month is 59.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.1, Ada County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Ada County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.4%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
