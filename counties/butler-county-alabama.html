Market Analysis

The median home value in Butler County is 37.5% below the Alabama state average and 56.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $725/month is 11.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.5, buying a home in Butler County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Butler County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.8%, compared to 73.2% in Alabama and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
