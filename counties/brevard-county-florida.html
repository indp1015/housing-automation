Market Analysis

The median home value in Brevard County is 19.4% above the Florida state average and 49.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,556/month is 19.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.2, the buy-vs-rent decision in Brevard County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Brevard County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
