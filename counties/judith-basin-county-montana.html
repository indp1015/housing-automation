Market Analysis

The median home value in Judith Basin County is 13.9% below the Montana state average and 4.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $495/month is 41.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 40.1, Judith Basin County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Judith Basin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.7%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
