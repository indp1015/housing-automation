Market Analysis

The median home value in Sheboygan County is about equal to the Wisconsin state average and 3.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $889/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sheboygan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
