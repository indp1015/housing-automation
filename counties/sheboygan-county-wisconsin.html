Market Analysis

The median home value in Sheboygan County is 0.6% below the Wisconsin state average and 2.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $934/month is 2.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Sheboygan County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.5%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
