Market Analysis

The median home value in Ransom County is 9.8% above the North Dakota state average and 15.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $691/month is 10.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Ransom County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
