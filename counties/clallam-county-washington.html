Market Analysis

The median home value in Clallam County is 3.0% below the Washington state average and 82.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,110/month is 7.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.9, Clallam County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clallam County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.8%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
