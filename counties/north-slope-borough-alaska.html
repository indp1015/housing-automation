Market Analysis

The median home value in North Slope Borough is 23.0% below the Alaska state average and 4.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,254/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.5, buying a home in North Slope Borough is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
