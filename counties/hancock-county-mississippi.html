Market Analysis

The median home value in Hancock County is 65.1% above the Mississippi state average and 1.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $977/month is 21.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Hancock County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Hancock County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.2%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
