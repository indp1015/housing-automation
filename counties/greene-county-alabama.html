📝 Local Market Analysis

Greene County, Alabama, presents a distinct buyer’s market characterized by extreme affordability relative to both state and national benchmarks. With a median home value of $81,200, the county sits 49.1% below the Alabama average and 64.3% below the national median. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.78, significantly lower than the national average of 3.20, indicating that housing costs are manageable despite a median household income of $29,200. This affordability is further reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 15.7, which is below the national average of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing is financially more advantageous than renting for qualified buyers. The rent burden is 17.8% of income, closely tracking the national average of 17.2%, confirming that rental costs are proportionate to local earnings. The market trend reveals a volatile but stabilizing pattern. After a peak year-over-year increase of 21.1% in 2019, home values experienced a correction, declining by 6.0% in 2024 to reach $81,200. This recent downward momentum contrasts with the broader national environment where 30-year fixed mortgage rates stand at 7.40%, a factor that typically suppresses demand in high-priced markets but has less impact in this low-cost region. The divergence between price and rent growth, noted as values rising faster than rents in the trend signal, is offset by the absolute decline in home values over the last two years. This suggests a cooling of speculative or rapid appreciation pressures, leading to a more stable, albeit lower, valuation baseline. Regional context highlights the county’s economic isolation and limited growth drivers. Local employment data shows zero reported jobs and an average weekly wage of $0, reflecting a lack of significant industrial or corporate presence, despite 133 existing establishments. Housing supply is negligible, with only one new unit authorized in 2025, indicating minimal new construction activity. Migration patterns show a net gain of 27 filers, with inflows and outflows nearly balanced at approximately 2,600 individuals each, suggesting a static population base. The primary implication for potential buyers is that Greene County offers entry-level pricing far below state averages, but the lack of job growth and new supply limits long-term appreciation potential. Renter’s options remain affordable, with a median gross rent of $432, but the low price-to-rent ratio favors homeownership for those with stable income sources outside the local economy.
