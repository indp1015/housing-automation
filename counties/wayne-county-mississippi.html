Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 32.9% below the Mississippi state average and 60.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $784/month is 2.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 9.7, buying a home in Wayne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.3%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
