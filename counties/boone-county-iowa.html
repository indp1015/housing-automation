Market Analysis

The median home value in Boone County is 24.2% above the Iowa state average and 6.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $845/month is 1.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Boone County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.0%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
