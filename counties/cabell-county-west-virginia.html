Market Analysis

The median home value in Cabell County is 11.1% above the West Virginia state average and 27.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $923/month is 18.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Cabell County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cabell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.2%, compared to 78.4% in West Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
