Market Analysis

The median home value in Hughes County is 33.7% above the South Dakota state average and 6.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $990/month is 26.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hughes County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.0%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
