Market Analysis

The median home value in Ashland County is 4.9% below the Ohio state average and 18.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $888/month is 1.8% below the state average.

Ashland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.9%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
