Market Analysis

The median home value in Cherokee County is 10.8% above the Oklahoma state average and 26.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $831/month is 1.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Cherokee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Cherokee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.7%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
