Market Analysis

The median home value in Berkeley County is 55.4% above the South Carolina state average and 36.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,654/month is 68.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Berkeley County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
