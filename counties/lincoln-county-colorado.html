Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 41.7% below the Colorado state average and 9.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $854/month is 31.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.1%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
