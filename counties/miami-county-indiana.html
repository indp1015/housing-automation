Market Analysis

The median home value in Miami County is 34.2% below the Indiana state average and 44.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $838/month is 7.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.5, buying a home in Miami County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Miami County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.2%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
