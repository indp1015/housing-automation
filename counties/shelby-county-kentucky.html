Market Analysis

The median home value in Shelby County is 84.8% above the Kentucky state average and 31.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $998/month is 29.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Shelby County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.1%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
