Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 28.3% above the South Dakota state average and 2.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $770/month is 1.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Clay County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 50.8%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
