Market Analysis

The median home value in Clay County is 33.5% above the South Dakota state average and 4.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $736/month is 2.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Clay County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Clay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 51.8%, compared to 72.7% in South Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
