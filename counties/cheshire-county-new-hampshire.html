Market Analysis

The median home value in Cheshire County is 19.9% below the New Hampshire state average and 23.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,276/month is 3.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Cheshire County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Cheshire County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.7%, compared to 74.3% in New Hampshire and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
