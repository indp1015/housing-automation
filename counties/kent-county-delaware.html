Market Analysis

The median home value in Kent County is 9.7% below the Delaware state average and 39.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,354/month is 0.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.5, the buy-vs-rent decision in Kent County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Kent County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.5%, compared to 74.4% in Delaware and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
