Market Analysis

The median home value in Parker County is 101.8% above the Texas state average and 65.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,500/month is 50.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Parker County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.7%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
