Market Analysis

The median home value in San Bernardino County is 17.1% below the California state average and 121.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,801/month is 6.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

San Bernardino County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.1%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
