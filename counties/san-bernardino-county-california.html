Market Analysis

The median home value in San Bernardino County is 16.8% below the California state average and 124.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,706/month is 5.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

San Bernardino County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.5%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
