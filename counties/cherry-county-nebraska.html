Market Analysis

The median home value in Cherry County is 7.6% below the Nebraska state average and 33.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $916/month is 15.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.8, buying a home in Cherry County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cherry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.4%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
