The housing market in ZIP code 30248 exhibits a distinct pattern of recent correction following a period of rapid appreciation. This local contraction aligns closely with broader regional trends, as Butts County recorded a similar year-over-year decline of 3.31%. This divergence suggests that the central Georgia sub-market is currently undergoing a price adjustment distinct from the broader state’s continued growth. Compared to nearby ZIP 30233, which has a higher HPI of 244.2, ZIP 30248 remains a more accessible entry point into homeownership.
