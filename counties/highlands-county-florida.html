Market Analysis

The median home value in Highlands County is 33.9% below the Florida state average and 17.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,035/month is 20.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.1, the buy-vs-rent decision in Highlands County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Highlands County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.6%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
