Market Analysis

The median home value in Chisago County is 47.6% above the Minnesota state average and 54.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,186/month is 26.4% above the state average.

Chisago County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.9%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
