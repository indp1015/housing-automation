Market Analysis

The median home value in Story County is 51.3% above the Iowa state average and 13.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,018/month is 22.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Story County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.5%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
