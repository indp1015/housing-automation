Market Analysis

The median home value in Tunica County is 29.5% above the Mississippi state average and 22.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $977/month is 21.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Tunica County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Tunica County has an owner-occupancy rate of 39.5%, compared to 72.4% in Mississippi and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
