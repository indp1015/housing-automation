Market Analysis

The median home value in Mississippi County is 13.4% below the Arkansas state average and 46.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $830/month is 7.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, buying a home in Mississippi County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Mississippi County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.0%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
