📝 Local Market Analysis

Mississippi County, Arkansas, functions as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by metrics that significantly undercut national benchmarks. The median home value of $122,500 sits 46.2% below the national average and 13.4% below the state average, creating a price-to-income ratio of 2.33 compared to the national standard of 3.20. This affordability is reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, well below the national 18.7, indicating that purchasing offers superior long-term value relative to renting. While the rent burden stands at 19.0% of income, slightly above the national 17.2%, the overall cost of entry remains low. The market’s stability is compromised by high volatility, reflected in a low stability metric, yet this is offset by strong momentum driven by consistent price appreciation. The multi-year trend reveals a significant acceleration in home values, rising from $81,400 in 2017 to $122,500 in 2024, a 50.6% cumulative increase. This growth peaked at 14.3% in 2022 before moderating to 5.8% in 2024, suggesting a normalization of rapid inflationary pressures. Notably, home values are rising faster than rents, a divergence that strengthens the case for homeownership. This momentum is supported by positive net migration of 377 filers and an average inflow income of $56,667, which exceeds the county’s median household income of $52,548. However, local economic vitality remains constrained, with only 17,364 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $1,560, limiting the depth of the buyer pool. Housing supply remains tight, with only 69 new units authorized in 2025, predominantly single-family, which may sustain upward pressure on prices despite the current 7.32% national mortgage rate. For potential buyers, the primary implication is that while current affordability is high, the rapid appreciation trend reduces the margin of safety for those with limited income growth. The low vitality score, driven by modest employment figures and limited multi-family development, suggests that while the market is gaining momentum, it lacks the robust economic diversification found in higher-scoring regions. The combination of low entry costs and strong price momentum creates a favorable environment for those with stable incomes, but the high volatility and modest wage base require careful consideration of long-term financial resilience. The data indicates a market in transition, where affordability remains a significant advantage, but the pace of value growth is outstripping the local economic fundamentals, creating a dynamic environment for both buyers and investors.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Mississippi County is 13.4% below the Arkansas state average and 46.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $830/month is 7.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, buying a home in Mississippi County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Mississippi County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.0%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
