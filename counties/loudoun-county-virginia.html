Market Analysis

The median home value in Loudoun County is 151.4% above the Virginia state average and 226.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,386/month is 104.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Loudoun County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Loudoun County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.9%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
