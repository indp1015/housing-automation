Market Analysis

The median home value in Bayfield County is 2.9% above the Wisconsin state average and 5.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $812/month is 11.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Bayfield County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.9%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
