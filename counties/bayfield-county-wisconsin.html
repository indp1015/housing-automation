Market Analysis

The median home value in Bayfield County is 4.7% above the Wisconsin state average and 7.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $789/month is 11.3% below the state average.

Bayfield County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.5%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
