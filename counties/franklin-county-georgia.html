Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 9.6% below the Georgia state average and 21.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $844/month is 12.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Franklin County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.2%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
