Market Analysis

The median home value in Grand County is 40.7% above the Colorado state average and 164.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,493/month is 14.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.6, Grand County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Grand County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.4%, compared to 71.9% in Colorado and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
