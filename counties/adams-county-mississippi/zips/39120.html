📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 39120 exhibits a robust upward trajectory, with the FHFA Home Price Index rising from 158.38 in 2021 to 201.16 in 2025. This four-year expansion represents a cumulative gain of approximately 26.8%, indicating sustained appreciation despite a temporary dip in 2023. The most recent year-over-year increase of 6.70% suggests that momentum has not only recovered but has accelerated, outpacing the broader regional trends. This pattern reveals a sub-market that is currently experiencing significant price pressure, distinct from the more modest corrections seen in other periods. When compared to broader geographic benchmarks, ZIP 39120 demonstrates notable divergence. The ZIP’s 6.70% annual growth marginally exceeds the Adams County average of 6.63%, but it significantly outperforms the Mississippi state average of 0.60%. This substantial gap signals that local demand is driving prices far above the state-level equilibrium. While the state market remains relatively flat, this specific area is undergoing a rapid revaluation. Such divergence typically indicates localized factors, such as limited inventory or specific infrastructural developments, are influencing value independent of the wider state economy. The sub-market character aligns with entry-level affordability, as evidenced by the parent county’s median home value of $108,700 and median household income of $43,644. With an owner occupancy rate of 68.1%, the area maintains a strong base of homeownership, though the median gross rent of $734 per month suggests a viable rental alternative. For prospective buyers, the combination of a 7.40% national mortgage rate and a 6.70% local price increase creates a challenging entry point. Residents should note that while the area remains affordable relative to national standards, the rapid appreciation rate in ZIP 39120 means that equity accumulation is occurring quickly, but purchasing power is being eroded by simultaneous price hikes and high borrowing costs.
