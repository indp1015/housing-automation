Market Analysis

The median home value in Lee County is 27.4% above the Florida state average and 59.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,712/month is 31.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Lee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Lee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.3%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
