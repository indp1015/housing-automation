Market Analysis

The median home value in Matagorda County is 7.2% below the Texas state average and 24.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $999/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Matagorda County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Matagorda County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.1%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
