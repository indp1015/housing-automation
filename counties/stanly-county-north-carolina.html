Market Analysis

The median home value in Stanly County is 3.0% above the North Carolina state average and 6.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $861/month is 11.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Stanly County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.5%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
