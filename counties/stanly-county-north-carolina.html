Market Analysis

The median home value in Stanly County is about equal to the North Carolina state average and 1.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $830/month is 11.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Stanly County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.6%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
