Market Analysis

The median home value in Plymouth County is 12.3% below the Massachusetts state average and 144.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,747/month is 8.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.5, Plymouth County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Plymouth County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.6%, compared to 67.2% in Massachusetts and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
