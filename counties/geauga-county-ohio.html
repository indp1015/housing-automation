Market Analysis

The median home value in Geauga County is 68.6% above the Ohio state average and 45.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,083/month is 19.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.5, Geauga County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Geauga County has an owner-occupancy rate of 87.2%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
