Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 32.9% below the South Dakota state average and 47.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $460/month is 39.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.8%, compared to 72.7% in South Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
