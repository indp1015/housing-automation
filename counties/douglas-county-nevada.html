Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 100.7% above the Nevada state average and 170.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,593/month is 36.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.2, Douglas County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 73.0% in Nevada and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
