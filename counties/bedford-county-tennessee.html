Market Analysis

The median home value in Bedford County is 26.6% above the Tennessee state average and 20.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $917/month is 6.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Bedford County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.2%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
