📝 Local Market Analysis

Red River Parish presents a distinct buyer’s market, characterized by a price-to-rent ratio of 13.7, significantly below the national average of 18.7, and a price-to-income ratio of 2.43 compared to the national benchmark of 3.20. These metrics indicate that purchasing remains substantially more accessible than renting, with a rent burden of 17.8% only marginally exceeding the national average of 17.2%. The market’s affordability profile is further reinforced by the median home value of $119,700, which sits 47.4% below the national mean and 28.5% below the Louisiana state average. This deep discount reflects the parish’s limited economic vitality, evidenced by only 1,946 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $955, which constrains broader demand despite low entry prices. Recent price appreciation reveals a volatile trajectory rather than steady growth. After a prolonged decline from 2017 to 2021, where values dropped from $85,900 to $77,900, the market experienced a sharp correction in 2022 with a 25.6% year-over-year increase to $99,100. This momentum continued into 2024, with values rising 16.0% to $119,700. However, the composite stability metric of 44.4 highlights this volatility as a significant risk factor. While momentum scores remain moderate at 58.8, the vitality score of 11.2 underscores the structural weakness of the local economy, driven by minimal employment base and stagnant population growth, with a net migration increase of only 51 filers between 2021 and 2022. The divergence between the sharp recent price gains and the relatively flat income levels, where the median household income stands at $49,167, creates a narrowing gap between asset values and local purchasing power. With the 30-year fixed mortgage rate at 7.40%, monthly carrying costs are rising, potentially tempering the affordability advantage that previously defined the parish. For potential buyers, the low price-to-income ratio offers a hedge against national inflation, provided that local employment does not contract further. Renter’s are currently protected by low gross rents of $729, but the rising price-to-rent ratio suggests that long-term rental costs may eventually converge with the rapidly appreciating ownership costs. The market remains fundamentally driven by external migration inflows, averaging $65,751 in income, rather than local wage growth, making it sensitive to broader regional economic shifts.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Red River Parish is 28.5% below the Louisiana state average and 47.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $729/month is 20.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.7, buying a home in Red River Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Red River Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.3%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
