Market Analysis

The median home value in Fauquier County is 95.5% above the Virginia state average and 157.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,561/month is 37.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.0, Fauquier County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fauquier County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.1%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
