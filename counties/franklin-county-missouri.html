Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 32.3% above the Missouri state average and 1.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $874/month is 14.0% above the state average.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 73.6% in Missouri and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
