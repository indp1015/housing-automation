Market Analysis

The median home value in Franklin County is 29.5% above the Missouri state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $893/month is 14.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Franklin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.5%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
