📝 Local Market Analysis

LaSalle Parish, Louisiana, functions as a highly affordable buyer’s market, characterized by significant price-to-income advantages relative to national benchmarks. The median home value of $119,500 stands at 47.5% below the national average, resulting in a price-to-income ratio of just 1.74 compared to the national norm of 3.20. This affordability is reinforced by a rent burden of 11.5% of income, well below the 17.2% national average, and a price-to-rent ratio of 15.2. These metrics indicate that both purchasing and renting are financially accessible, with the low entry barrier outweighing the limited local economic vitality. The market is not defined by speculative momentum but by structural affordability, making it a distinct outlier in the broader housing landscape. Recent trends reveal a volatile yet ultimately stable trajectory. After peaking at $137,200 in 2023, median values corrected by 12.9% in 2024 to reach $119,500. Despite this recent dip, the FHFA House Price Index indicates a 10.18% year-over-year increase in 2025, significantly outperforming the Louisiana state average of 1.59%. This divergence suggests that while Census-based estimates show a correction, transaction-based data reflects strong recent appreciation. The multi-year trend from 2017 to 2024 shows overall growth, albeit with a notable 2021 contraction. The current environment is shaped by tight housing supply, with only 14 new units authorized in 2025, all single-family, and a high owner-occupancy rate of 80.6%. The local economic context provides limited support for sustained high growth. With total employment at 3,332 jobs and an average weekly wage of $966, the labor market is small and modest. However, positive net migration of 138 filers between 2021 and 2022 suggests sustained demographic interest. The high affordability score drives the overall market assessment, balancing the low vitality score caused by limited job diversity and small population size. For potential buyers, the 7.40% national mortgage rate remains a headwind, but the low home prices mitigate monthly payment burdens. Renter’s are similarly positioned favorably, with gross rents averaging $657 per month. The market’s primary strength lies in its low cost of entry relative to income, making it a resilient option for those prioritizing affordability over rapid capital appreciation or robust local economic expansion.

The median home value in LaSalle Parish is 28.6% below the Louisiana state average and 47.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $657/month is 28.1% below the state average.
