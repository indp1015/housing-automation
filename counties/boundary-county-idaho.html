Market Analysis

The median home value in Boundary County is 10.3% above the Idaho state average and 62.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $867/month is 5.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 33.1, Boundary County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Boundary County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
