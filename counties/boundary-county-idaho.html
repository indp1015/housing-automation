Market Analysis

The median home value in Boundary County is 15.5% above the Idaho state average and 80.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $877/month is 9.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 39.0, Boundary County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Boundary County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.3%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
