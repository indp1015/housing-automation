Market Analysis

The median home value in Clark County is 12.5% below the South Dakota state average and 30.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $782/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Clark County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Clark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.5%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
