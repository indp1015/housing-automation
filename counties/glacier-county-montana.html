📝 Local Market Analysis

Glacier County exhibits a distinct buyer’s market profile, characterized by a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, significantly above the national average of 18.7. This metric indicates that purchasing remains substantially more cost-efficient than renting, despite the county’s median home value of $165,100 sitting 27.5% below the national mean. The affordability landscape is mixed; while the price-to-income ratio of 3.52 slightly exceeds the national benchmark of 3.20, the rent burden stands at just 14.0% of household income, well under the national 17.2% threshold. This divergence suggests that while homeownership requires a higher income relative to asset value, rental costs remain exceptionally low, reinforcing the structural advantage for prospective buyers over long-term renters. Recent momentum reveals a complex trajectory. After a period of stagnation between 2019 and 2020, home values surged 21.9% in 2021, moderating to 7.6% growth by 2024. However, the FHFA House Price Index indicates a recent 1.99% year-over-year decline in 2025, underperforming the Montana state average by 4.58 percentage points. This recent cooling contrasts with the multi-year appreciation trend, suggesting a potential correction or stabilization phase. The economic foundation is fragile, with only 1,692 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $905, which limits local purchasing power. Although net migration added 209 filers in 2021-2022, the small population base of 13,637 residents makes the market highly sensitive to demographic shifts. The market’s overall stability is compromised by low vitality, driven by the limited employment sector of 263 establishments and a median household income of $46,875, which trails the national average by 31.1%. The high price-to-rent ratio, combined with the recent negative price momentum, signals that while entry barriers are lower than in major metros, the local economy lacks the depth to sustain rapid appreciation. The 7.40% national mortgage rate further pressures affordability for buyers, though the low absolute price points mitigate this impact compared to higher-cost regions. Consequently, the market is defined by relative affordability against national standards but constrained by local economic weakness and recent price softening.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Glacier County is 40.4% below the Montana state average and 27.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $548/month is 34.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.1, Glacier County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Glacier County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.7%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
