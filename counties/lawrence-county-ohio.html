Market Analysis

The median home value in Lawrence County is 26.7% below the Ohio state average and 36.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $848/month is 6.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Lawrence County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lawrence County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.3%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
