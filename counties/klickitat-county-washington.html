Market Analysis

The median home value in Klickitat County is 2.3% below the Washington state average and 83.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,119/month is 6.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.9, Klickitat County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Klickitat County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
