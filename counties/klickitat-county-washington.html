Market Analysis

The median home value in Klickitat County is 2.5% below the Washington state average and 86.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,105/month is 11.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 32.1, Klickitat County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Klickitat County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.7%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
