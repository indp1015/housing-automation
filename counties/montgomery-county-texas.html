Market Analysis

The median home value in Montgomery County is 85.9% above the Texas state average and 52.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,532/month is 53.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Montgomery County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Montgomery County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.8%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
