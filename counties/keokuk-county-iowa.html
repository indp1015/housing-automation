Market Analysis

The median home value in Keokuk County is 30.6% below the Iowa state average and 47.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $828/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.9, buying a home in Keokuk County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Keokuk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 75.8% in Iowa and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
