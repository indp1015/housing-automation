Market Analysis

The median home value in Pendleton County is 0.6% below the Kentucky state average and 29.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $793/month is about equal to the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Pendleton County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Pendleton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
