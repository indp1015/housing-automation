Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 47.0% above the Kentucky state average and 4.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $851/month is 10.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.4%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
