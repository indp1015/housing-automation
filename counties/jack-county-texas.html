Market Analysis

The median home value in Jack County is 34.9% above the Texas state average and 10.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $960/month is 3.7% below the state average.

Jack County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.2%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
