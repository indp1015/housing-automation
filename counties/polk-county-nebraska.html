Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 16.3% above the Nebraska state average and 15.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $692/month is 11.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
