Market Analysis

The median home value in Mahoning County is 21.2% below the Ohio state average and 32.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $791/month is 12.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Mahoning County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Mahoning County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.3%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
