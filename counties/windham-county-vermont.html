Market Analysis

The median home value in Windham County is 2.9% below the Vermont state average and 25.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,056/month is 5.5% below the state average.

Windham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.8%, compared to 76.9% in Vermont and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
