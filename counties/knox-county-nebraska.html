📝 Local Market Analysis

Knox County, Nebraska, operates as a distinctively buyer-friendly market, characterized by extreme affordability relative to national benchmarks. The price-to-income ratio stands at 1.98, significantly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is only 12.0% of median household income compared to the national 17.2%. With a median home value of $127,700, properties are priced 43.9% below the national mean and 22.4% below the state average. This affordability is reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 16.4, which is lower than the national 18.7, suggesting that purchasing remains a more financially efficient option than renting for long-term residents. The market’s stability is further supported by a high owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, indicating a settled demographic rather than a transient rental population. Recent market dynamics reveal a period of accelerated appreciation that has outpaced broader regional trends. Home values increased from $85,100 in 2017 to $127,700 in 2024, representing a cumulative gain of nearly 50%. The most significant surge occurred in 2022, with a 14.1% year-over-year increase, followed by a 9.2% rise in 2024. This upward trajectory is corroborated by the FHFA House Price Index, which shows a 3.32% year-over-year increase in 2025, outperforming the Nebraska state average of 1.96% by 1.36 percentage points. This divergence suggests that local demand drivers are stronger than statewide conditions. However, the economic foundation remains modest, with total employment at 1,727 jobs and an average weekly wage of $861. Housing supply is constrained, with only 32 new units authorized in 2025, comprising 18 single-family and 14 multi-family homes. Despite a net migration gain of 164 filers between 2021 and 2022, the small population base of 8,363 limits the scale of demand. The market’s vitality is tempered by the current macroeconomic environment, specifically the 7.40% national 30-year fixed mortgage rate, which increases carrying costs for new buyers. While the affordability metrics provide a strong buffer against high interest rates, the limited employment base and low establishment count of 275 suggest that local economic resilience is moderate. The high affordability score is the primary driver of the market’s overall strength, offsetting lower vitality scores resulting from the small labor market. For potential buyers, the data indicates a low entry barrier with strong historical appreciation, whereas renters face a less competitive pricing environment relative to their income levels. The sustained outperformance of state price indices signals that local fundamentals are currently supporting higher valuations than the broader Nebraska market.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 22.4% below the Nebraska state average and 43.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $647/month is 18.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Knox County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Knox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.4%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
