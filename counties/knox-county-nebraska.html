Market Analysis

The median home value in Knox County is 23.5% below the Nebraska state average and 44.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $692/month is 11.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Knox County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Knox County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.6%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
