Market Analysis

The median home value in Dearborn County is 27.5% above the Indiana state average and 7.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $848/month is 3.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dearborn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.7%, compared to 76.0% in Indiana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
