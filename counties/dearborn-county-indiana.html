Market Analysis

The median home value in Dearborn County is 26.8% above the Indiana state average and 6.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $845/month is 7.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dearborn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 85.2%, compared to 76.3% in Indiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
