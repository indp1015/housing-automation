Market Analysis

The median home value in McHenry County is 14.1% below the North Dakota state average and 33.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $713/month is 7.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in McHenry County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

McHenry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.4%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
