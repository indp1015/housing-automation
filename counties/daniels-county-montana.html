Market Analysis

The median home value in Daniels County is 29.3% below the Montana state average and 15.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $588/month is 28.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.2, Daniels County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Daniels County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.4%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
