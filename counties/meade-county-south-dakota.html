Market Analysis

The median home value in Meade County is 71.7% above the South Dakota state average and 36.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,264/month is 61.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Meade County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.9%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
