Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 38.8% below the New York state average and 22.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $929/month is 17.9% below the state average.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.9%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
