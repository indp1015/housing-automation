Market Analysis

The median home value in Elbert County is 67.0% above the Colorado state average and 214.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,436/month is 14.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 38.6, Elbert County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Elbert County has an owner-occupancy rate of 94.4%, compared to 72.1% in Colorado and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
