Market Analysis

The median home value in Philadelphia County is 16.0% above the Pennsylvania state average and 9.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,323/month is 38.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Philadelphia County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Philadelphia County has an owner-occupancy rate of 52.3%, compared to 73.9% in Pennsylvania and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
