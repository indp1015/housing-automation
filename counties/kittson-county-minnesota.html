Market Analysis

The median home value in Kittson County is 43.4% below the Minnesota state average and 40.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $757/month is 19.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Kittson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Kittson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.9%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
