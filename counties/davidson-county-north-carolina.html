Market Analysis

The median home value in Davidson County is 6.9% below the North Carolina state average and 3.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $901/month is 7.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Davidson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.4%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
