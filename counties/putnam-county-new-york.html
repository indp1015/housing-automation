Market Analysis

The median home value in Putnam County is 67.6% above the New York state average and 107.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,775/month is 52.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Putnam County has an owner-occupancy rate of 83.8%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
