📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 48427, located in Sanilac County, Michigan, exhibits a robust upward price trajectory over the recent five-year period. The Home Price Index (HPI) for this area has surged from 117.03 in 2019 to 246.06 in 2025, representing a cumulative increase of approximately 110%. This steep appreciation indicates significant capital gains for existing homeowners and a tightening market for prospective buyers. The acceleration in value growth, particularly between 2021 and 2022, suggests that local demand has outpaced supply, driving prices well above historical baselines. Current year-over-year performance reveals a divergence from broader regional trends. While the county’s overall YoY growth stands at +5.53% and the state average is +3.68%, ZIP 48427’s implied momentum aligns with the higher end of county performance, though it trails the extreme spikes seen in neighboring ZIP 48454 (+41.54%) and ZIP 48419 (+27.80%). This relative stability compared to adjacent areas suggests that 48427 is a mature sub-market rather than a speculative hotspot. The HPI level of 246.1, which is 2.46 times the 2000 baseline, reflects a market that has fully adjusted to post-pandemic inflationary pressures. In terms of sub-market character, the area functions as an entry-level to mid-range residential zone. The parent county’s median home value of $168,300 and median household income of $58,863 indicate a market accessible to lower-income demographics, despite the recent HPI surge. With an owner-occupancy rate of 82.6%, the area is predominantly residential rather than renter-heavy. For prospective buyers, the combination of a 7.40% national mortgage rate and elevated local prices creates a challenging entry point. However, the high owner-occupancy rate signals a stable community with long-term residency potential, distinct from the more volatile investment-driven markets observed in nearby ZIP codes.
