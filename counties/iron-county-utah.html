Market Analysis

The median home value in Iron County is 9.2% below the Utah state average and 62.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,008/month is 8.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.3, Iron County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Iron County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.4%, compared to 77.0% in Utah and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
