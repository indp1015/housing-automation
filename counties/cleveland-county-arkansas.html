Market Analysis

The median home value in Cleveland County is 2.7% below the Arkansas state average and 39.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $536/month is 30.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Cleveland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 82.6%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
