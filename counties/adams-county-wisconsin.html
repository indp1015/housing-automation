Market Analysis

The median home value in Adams County is 14.2% below the Wisconsin state average and 11.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $828/month is 9.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Adams County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.2%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
