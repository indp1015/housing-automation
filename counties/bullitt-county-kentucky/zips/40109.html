📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 40109 exhibits a robust upward trajectory, with the Home Price Index (HPI) surging from 154.31 in 2019 to 246.37 in 2025. This six-year period reflects a cumulative appreciation of approximately 59.7%, indicating sustained demand and limited supply dynamics. The steepest gains occurred between 2020 and 2022, where the index rose from 161.4 to 199.14, suggesting that the pandemic-era housing boom had a pronounced local impact. The continued rise to 246.37 in 2025 confirms that price momentum has persisted beyond the initial speculative surge, maintaining a strong baseline for asset value. Regarding market alignment, ZIP 40109’s year-over-year growth of 3.38% trails the broader Bullitt County average, which is also 3.38%, but falls short of the Kentucky state average of 4.26%. This divergence suggests that while the local market remains healthy, it is not outpacing state-wide trends. The performance is closely mirrored by nearby ZIP 40150, which recorded a 1.98% increase, indicating a relatively stable regional corridor. The alignment with the county average implies that 40109 is moving in lockstep with its immediate neighbors, lacking the extreme volatility seen in faster-growing state segments. The sub-market character is defined by a blend of owner-occupancy and moderate affordability. With an owner-occupancy rate of 84.9% and a median household income of $80,558, the area functions as a standard middle-class residential zone rather than a luxury enclave. The median home value of $247,200 aligns closely with the HPI level, positioning the market as accessible for local income earners. However, the current 30-year mortgage rate of 7.40% significantly impacts carrying costs, making entry-level pricing a critical factor for new entrants. Prospective buyers should note that while appreciation has been strong, the market’s growth rate is now moderating to align with broader state trends, suggesting a stabilization phase rather than a speculative peak.
