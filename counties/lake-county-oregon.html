Market Analysis

The median home value in Lake County is 43.2% below the Oregon state average and 3.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $878/month is 26.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Lake County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.3%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
