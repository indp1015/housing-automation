Market Analysis

The median home value in Uinta County is 16.4% below the Wyoming state average and 27.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $900/month is 4.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.9, Uinta County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Uinta County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.6%, compared to 73.6% in Wyoming and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
