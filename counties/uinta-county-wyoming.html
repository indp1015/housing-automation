Market Analysis

The median home value in Uinta County is 19.1% below the Wyoming state average and 20.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $882/month is 5.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Uinta County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.5%, compared to 73.7% in Wyoming and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
