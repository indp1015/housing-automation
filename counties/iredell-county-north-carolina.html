Market Analysis

The median home value in Iredell County is 36.6% above the North Carolina state average and 41.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,252/month is 28.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.4, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Iredell County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.9%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
