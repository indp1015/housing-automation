Market Analysis

The median home value in Independence County is 0.8% above the Arkansas state average and 37.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $845/month is 9.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.1, buying a home in Independence County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Independence County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.2%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
