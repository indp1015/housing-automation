Market Analysis

The median home value in Grand Forks County is 43.8% above the North Dakota state average and 11.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $992/month is 28.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Grand Forks County has an owner-occupancy rate of 51.8%, compared to 73.6% in North Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
