Market Analysis

The median home value in Benton County is 32.7% above the Oregon state average and 125.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,410/month is 18.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.3, Benton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Benton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 56.9%, compared to 68.4% in Oregon and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
