Market Analysis

The median home value in Carson County is 18.5% below the Texas state average and 33.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $933/month is 3.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.6, buying a home in Carson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Carson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.8%, compared to 73.0% in Texas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
