Market Analysis

The median home value in Spencer County is 98.2% above the Kentucky state average and 40.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $954/month is 23.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Spencer County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Spencer County has an owner-occupancy rate of 86.6%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
