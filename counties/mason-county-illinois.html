Market Analysis

The median home value in Mason County is 32.4% below the Illinois state average and 54.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $802/month is 7.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.7, buying a home in Mason County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Mason County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.8%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
