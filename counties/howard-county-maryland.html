Market Analysis

The median home value in Howard County is 64.4% above the Maryland state average and 172.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,038/month is 44.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Howard County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.7%, compared to 72.2% in Maryland and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
