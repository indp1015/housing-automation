Market Analysis

The median home value in Howard County is 61.6% above the Maryland state average and 162.6% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,099/month is 43.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Howard County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.5%, compared to 72.7% in Maryland and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
