Market Analysis

The median home value in Pittsburg County is 2.8% above the Oklahoma state average and 30.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $857/month is 5.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Pittsburg County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
