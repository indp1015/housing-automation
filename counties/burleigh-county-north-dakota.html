Market Analysis

The median home value in Burleigh County is 86.6% above the North Dakota state average and 48.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $996/month is 29.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.3, Burleigh County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Burleigh County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.6%, compared to 74.1% in North Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
