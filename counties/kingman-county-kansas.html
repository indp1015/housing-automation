Market Analysis

The median home value in Kingman County is 20.0% below the Kansas state average and 51.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $846/month is 5.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.9, buying a home in Kingman County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Kingman County has an owner-occupancy rate of 66.3%, compared to 74.2% in Kansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
