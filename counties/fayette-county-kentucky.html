Market Analysis

The median home value in Fayette County is 82.5% above the Kentucky state average and 28.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,164/month is 46.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Fayette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 53.8%, compared to 73.4% in Kentucky and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
