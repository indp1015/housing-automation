Market Analysis

The median home value in Okfuskee County is 23.8% below the Oklahoma state average and 49.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $716/month is 14.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.5, buying a home in Okfuskee County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Okfuskee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.8%, compared to 72.3% in Oklahoma and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
