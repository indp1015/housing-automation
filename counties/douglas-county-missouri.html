Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 4.3% below the Missouri state average and 26.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $700/month is 10.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.6%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
