Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 30.5% below the Arkansas state average and 56.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $668/month is 13.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.3, buying a home in Jackson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.5%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
