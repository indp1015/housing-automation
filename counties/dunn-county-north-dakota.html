Market Analysis

The median home value in Dunn County is 44.7% above the North Dakota state average and 15.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $941/month is 22.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dunn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.8%, compared to 74.1% in North Dakota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
