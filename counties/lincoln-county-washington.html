Market Analysis

The median home value in Lincoln County is 31.5% below the Washington state average and 28.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $872/month is 27.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.0, Lincoln County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lincoln County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.5%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
