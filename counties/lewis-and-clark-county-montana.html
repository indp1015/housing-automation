Market Analysis

The median home value in Lewis and Clark County is 42.4% above the Montana state average and 69.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,062/month is 28.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.1, Lewis and Clark County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Lewis and Clark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.7%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
