Market Analysis

The median home value in Polk County is 5.1% below the Minnesota state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $843/month is 7.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Polk County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.1%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
