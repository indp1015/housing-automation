Market Analysis

The median home value in Shoshone County is 28.6% below the Idaho state average and 5.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $880/month is 4.2% below the state average.

Shoshone County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.8%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
