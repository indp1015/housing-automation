Market Analysis

The median home value in Shoshone County is 24.7% below the Idaho state average and 17.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $943/month is 2.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Shoshone County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.0%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
