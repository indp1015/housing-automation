Market Analysis

The median home value in Fairfax County is 147.7% above the Virginia state average and 221.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,276/month is 94.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.8, Fairfax County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fairfax County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.6%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
