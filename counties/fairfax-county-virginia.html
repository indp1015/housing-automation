Market Analysis

The median home value in Fairfax County is 151.6% above the Virginia state average and 230.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,230/month is 95.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.1, Fairfax County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Fairfax County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.3%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
