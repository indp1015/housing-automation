Market Analysis

The median home value in Dade County is about equal to the Georgia state average and 12.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $829/month is 14.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Dade County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.0%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
