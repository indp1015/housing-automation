Market Analysis

The median home value in Yamhill County is 24.0% above the Oregon state average and 109.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,377/month is 21.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.8, Yamhill County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Yamhill County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.9%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
