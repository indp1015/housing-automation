Market Analysis

The median home value in Bay County is 22.9% below the Michigan state average and 33.2% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $860/month is 5.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.7, buying a home in Bay County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Bay County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.0%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
