Market Analysis

The median home value in Rensselaer County is 8.1% below the New York state average and 13.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,217/month is 4.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.7, the buy-vs-rent decision in Rensselaer County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Rensselaer County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.8%, compared to 69.3% in New York and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
