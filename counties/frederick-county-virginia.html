Market Analysis

The median home value in Frederick County is 27.7% above the Virginia state average and 67.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,402/month is 23.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Frederick County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.0%, compared to 70.7% in Virginia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
