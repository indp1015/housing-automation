Market Analysis

The median home value in Frederick County is 30.5% above the Virginia state average and 69.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,507/month is 28.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Frederick County has an owner-occupancy rate of 79.3%, compared to 70.8% in Virginia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
