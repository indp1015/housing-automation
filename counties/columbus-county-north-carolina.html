Market Analysis

The median home value in Columbus County is 38.0% below the North Carolina state average and 35.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $824/month is 15.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Columbus County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Columbus County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.1%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
