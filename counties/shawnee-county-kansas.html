Market Analysis

The median home value in Shawnee County is 30.0% above the Kansas state average and 19.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $970/month is 23.3% above the state average.

Shawnee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.0%, compared to 74.0% in Kansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
