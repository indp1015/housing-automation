Market Analysis

The median home value in Madison County is 47.5% above the Iowa state average and 11.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $938/month is 15.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Madison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.3%, compared to 75.7% in Iowa and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
