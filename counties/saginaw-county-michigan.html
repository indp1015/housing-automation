Market Analysis

The median home value in Saginaw County is 22.6% below the Michigan state average and 32.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $947/month is 4.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.4, buying a home in Saginaw County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Saginaw County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.1%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
