Market Analysis

The median home value in Ventura County is 35.0% above the California state average and 261.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $2,317/month is 37.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 29.6, Ventura County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Ventura County has an owner-occupancy rate of 64.6%, compared to 63.4% in California and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
