Market Analysis

The median home value in Brown County is 22.1% above the South Dakota state average and 2.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $777/month is 0.9% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Brown County has an owner-occupancy rate of 67.6%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
