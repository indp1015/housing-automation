Market Analysis

The median home value in Providence County is 21.9% below the Rhode Island state average and 60.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,242/month is 8.7% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Providence County has an owner-occupancy rate of 56.6%, compared to 69.3% in Rhode Island and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
