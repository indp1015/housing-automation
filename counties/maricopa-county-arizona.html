Market Analysis

The median home value in Maricopa County is 70.4% above the Arizona state average and 98.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,708/month is 59.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Maricopa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.2%, compared to 70.7% in Arizona and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
