Market Analysis

The median home value in Monroe County is 181.4% above the Florida state average and 242.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,959/month is 58.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.8, Monroe County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Monroe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.4%, compared to 74.0% in Florida and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
