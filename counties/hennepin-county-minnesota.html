Market Analysis

The median home value in Hennepin County is 69.2% above the Minnesota state average and 78.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,439/month is 57.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.8, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Hennepin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.0%, compared to 78.1% in Minnesota and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
