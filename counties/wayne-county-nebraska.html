Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 34.6% above the Nebraska state average and 2.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $800/month is 0.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.3%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
