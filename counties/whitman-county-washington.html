Market Analysis

The median home value in Whitman County is 17.6% below the Washington state average and 57.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,049/month is 16.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.6, Whitman County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Whitman County has an owner-occupancy rate of 46.8%, compared to 71.3% in Washington and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
