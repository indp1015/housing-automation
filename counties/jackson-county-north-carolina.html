Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 21.0% above the North Carolina state average and 25.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $904/month is 7.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Jackson County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 60.8%, compared to 71.8% in North Carolina and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
