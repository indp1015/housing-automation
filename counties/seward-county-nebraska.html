Market Analysis

The median home value in Seward County is 46.6% above the Nebraska state average and 6.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $980/month is 23.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Seward County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.0%, compared to 74.0% in Nebraska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
