Market Analysis

The median home value in Douglas County is 1.3% above the Washington state average and 90.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,234/month is 2.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.2, Douglas County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Douglas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.4%, compared to 70.9% in Washington and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
