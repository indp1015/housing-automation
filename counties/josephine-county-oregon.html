Market Analysis

The median home value in Josephine County is 7.3% above the Oregon state average and 81.1% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,157/month is 1.8% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 27.6, Josephine County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Josephine County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.7%, compared to 68.3% in Oregon and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
