Market Analysis

The median home value in York County is 51.8% above the Maine state average and 73.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,339/month is 31.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

York County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.3%, compared to 76.6% in Maine and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
