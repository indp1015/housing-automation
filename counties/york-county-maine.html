Market Analysis

The median home value in York County is 51.0% above the Maine state average and 68.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,239/month is 27.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 24.0, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

York County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.1%, compared to 76.3% in Maine and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
