Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 36.8% below the Pennsylvania state average and 40.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $746/month is 22.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.2, buying a home in Jefferson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.2%, compared to 73.9% in Pennsylvania and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
