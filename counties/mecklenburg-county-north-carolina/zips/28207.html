📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 28207 exhibits a robust upward trajectory, with the Home Price Index (HPI) rising from 191.32 in 2020 to 280.74 in 2025. This five-year expansion represents a cumulative increase of approximately 47%, indicating sustained demand and significant equity buildup for existing homeowners. The pace of appreciation has remained consistent, suggesting that this sub-market has not experienced the volatility seen in other areas. Currently, the ZIP code’s year-over-year appreciation of 2.45% outpaces the broader Mecklenburg County average, which is also at 2.45%, and significantly exceeds the North Carolina state average of 0.38%. This alignment with the county but divergence from the state suggests that local economic fundamentals are driving performance independently of statewide trends. In contrast, nearby ZIP codes 28134 and 28278 are experiencing negative growth of -3.30% and -6.71%, respectively. This sharp contrast highlights that 28207 is currently a relative strength zone within the county, while adjacent areas face cooling demand or oversupply issues. Based on the parent county’s median home value of $406,800 and median household income of $87,005, ZIP 28207 functions as a mixed-income, owner-occupied sub-market. With an owner occupancy rate of 55.1%, the area balances residential stability with a notable rental sector, where the median gross rent stands at $1,627 per month. The HPI level of 280.7 indicates that home prices are nearly three times their 2000 baseline, placing this neighborhood in the mid-to-upper range of affordability for the county. Prospective buyers should note that while appreciation has been strong, the current 30-year mortgage rate of 7.32% significantly impacts monthly carrying costs. For residents, the data signals a stable, appreciating asset class, whereas new entrants must account for higher entry price points relative to the state average.
