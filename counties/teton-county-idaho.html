Market Analysis

The median home value in Teton County is 94.4% above the Idaho state average and 203.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,319/month is 36.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 43.6, Teton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Teton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.8%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
