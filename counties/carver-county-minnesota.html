Market Analysis

The median home value in Carver County is 90.3% above the Minnesota state average and 99.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,514/month is 61.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.0, Carver County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Carver County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.5%, compared to 77.9% in Minnesota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
