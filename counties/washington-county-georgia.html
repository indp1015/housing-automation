Market Analysis

The median home value in Washington County is 43.2% below the Georgia state average and 50.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $858/month is 11.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.9, buying a home in Washington County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.
