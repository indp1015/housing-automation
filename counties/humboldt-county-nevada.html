Market Analysis

The median home value in Humboldt County is 9.2% below the Nevada state average and 22.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $994/month is 15.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 23.3, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Humboldt County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.0%, compared to 73.0% in Nevada and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
