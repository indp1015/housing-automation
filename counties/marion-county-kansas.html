Market Analysis

The median home value in Marion County is 12.7% below the Kansas state average and 45.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $727/month is 7.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.1, buying a home in Marion County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Marion County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.8%, compared to 74.0% in Kansas and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
