📝 Local Market Notes

ZIP 74429 has exhibited robust price appreciation over the last five years, with the FHFA House Price Index rising from 182.1 in 2021 to 239.9 in 2025—a cumulative increase of 31.8 percent. Annual gains accelerated from 13.0 percent in 2022 to 5.1 percent in 2023 before moderating to 6.6 percent in 2024 and 4.1 percent in 2025, reflecting a normalization from pandemic-era peaks while maintaining positive momentum. This divergence is further underscored by the neighboring ZIP 74467, which posted a higher 4.70 percent YoY gain but from a lower index base of 208.5, suggesting 74429 occupies a relatively stronger price tier within the county.
