Market Analysis

The median home value in Denton County is 134.7% above the Texas state average and 92.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,728/month is 73.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Denton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 65.5%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
