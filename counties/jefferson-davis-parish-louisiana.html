📝 Local Market Analysis

Jefferson Davis Parish functions as a balanced market with distinct affordability advantages, evidenced by a price-to-income ratio of 2.63 compared to the national average of 3.20. The low price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, well below the national benchmark of 18.7, indicates that purchasing is significantly more cost-effective than renting, supporting a high owner-occupancy rate of 77.1%. While the median home value of $150,800 sits 9.9% below the state average and 33.8% below the national mean, the median household income of $57,341 remains 15.7% below the national standard. This relative valuation suggests that while absolute costs are low, local purchasing power is constrained, resulting in a rent burden of 17.7% that slightly exceeds the national average of 17.2%. The multi-year trend reveals a volatile recovery rather than steady growth. Home values surged 19.6% in 2021 and 13.1% in 2022 before correcting 3.2% in 2023, stabilizing with a 4.0% increase in 2024. Recent FHFA data confirms this stabilization, showing a 2.94% year-over-year increase that outpaces the state’s 1.59% growth by 1.35 percentage points. This momentum aligns with a positive net migration of 291 filers, where incoming residents reported slightly lower average incomes than those departing, suggesting an influx of price-sensitive households. However, the local economy remains fragile, with only 6,072 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $898, which limits broader economic vitality. Supply constraints further shape the landscape, with 2025 building permits authorizing only 62 new single-family units and zero multi-family developments. This limited supply, coupled with a 7.32% national mortgage rate, creates a challenging entry point for new buyers despite the low absolute price tags. The market’s overall assessment is driven by strong momentum and relative affordability, yet it is weighed down by low economic vitality and moderate stability. The divergence between robust price appreciation and stagnant wage growth highlights a structural tension: while asset values are rising, the local labor market lacks the dynamism to support sustained demand increases. Potential buyers benefit from below-national price levels, but renters face limited options due to the absence of new multi-family construction, reinforcing the long-term dominance of the single-family owner-occupier segment in this rural parish.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson Davis Parish is 9.9% below the Louisiana state average and 33.8% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $848/month is 7.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Jefferson Davis Parish is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jefferson Davis Parish has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.1%, compared to 72.2% in Louisiana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
