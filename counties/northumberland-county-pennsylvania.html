Market Analysis

The median home value in Northumberland County is 23.4% below the Pennsylvania state average and 28.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $806/month is 18.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 16.8, the buy-vs-rent decision in Northumberland County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Northumberland County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 74.2% in Pennsylvania and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
