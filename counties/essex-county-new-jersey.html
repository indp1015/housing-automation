Market Analysis

The median home value in Essex County is 21.9% above the New Jersey state average and 130.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,531/month is 6.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 28.5, Essex County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Essex County has an owner-occupancy rate of 44.9%, compared to 68.3% in New Jersey and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
