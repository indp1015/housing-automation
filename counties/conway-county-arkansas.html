📝 Editor's Insight

Conway County exhibits a balanced market leaning toward affordability, evidenced by a price-to-income ratio of 2.68, which sits below the national average of 3.09. This divergence indicates that equity accumulation is outpacing rental cost increases, favoring long-term ownership over renting. This suggests a stable, entrenched housing stock where high ownership correlates with slower inventory turnover.
