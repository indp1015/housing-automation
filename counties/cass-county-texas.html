Market Analysis

The median home value in Cass County is 21.4% below the Texas state average and 35.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $852/month is 14.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.3, buying a home in Cass County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Cass County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.6%, compared to 73.2% in Texas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
