Market Analysis

The median home value in Anchorage Municipality is 39.8% above the Alaska state average and 77.7% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,453/month is 19.1% above the state average.

Anchorage Municipality has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.7%, compared to 64.3% in Alaska and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
