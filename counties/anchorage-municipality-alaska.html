Market Analysis

The median home value in Anchorage Municipality is 39.7% above the Alaska state average and 73.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,489/month is 19.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Anchorage Municipality has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.9%, compared to 64.7% in Alaska and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
