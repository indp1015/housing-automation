Market Analysis

The median home value in Gallatin County is 45.8% below the Illinois state average and 63.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $541/month is 37.6% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.8, buying a home in Gallatin County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Gallatin County has an owner-occupancy rate of 77.4%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
