📝 Local Market Analysis

Baraga County presents a balanced market characterized by strong affordability but limited economic vitality. With a price-to-income ratio of 2.50, significantly below the national average of 3.20, and a rent burden of 13.4% compared to the national 17.2%, the area remains highly accessible for residents. The price-to-rent ratio of 18.7 aligns perfectly with the national benchmark, indicating that neither buying nor renting offers a distinct structural advantage. However, the market’s stability is tempered by a low vitality score, driven by a small population of 8,191 and total local employment of just 1,337 jobs. This constrained economic base limits the depth of the buyer pool, creating a market where affordability is the primary driver of demand rather than speculative investment. The trend data reveals a volatile yet upward trajectory for home values, which surged 13.6% in 2022 and another 11.0% in 2024, reaching a median value of $138,100. This recent acceleration is corroborated by the FHFA HPI, which shows a 3.95% year-over-year increase, slightly outpacing the Michigan state average of 3.68%. A notable anomaly exists in the divergence between asset growth and rental costs; while home values have risen sharply, the median gross rent remains modest at $616. This suggests that the housing stock is aging or that rental supply is not keeping pace with the rising value of owned properties. The local economy supports this observation, as only 12 new single-family units were authorized in 2025, indicating a severe supply constraint that may sustain price pressure despite the high 7.40% national mortgage rate. Regional context further defines the market’s position. Home values are 39.4% below the national average and 30.0% below the Michigan state average, reflecting the county’s lower cost of living. Median household income of $55,327 is 18.6% below the national norm, yet the affordability metrics remain robust due to the lower price point. Net migration of +162 filers between 2021 and 2022, with inflowing residents earning slightly less than outflowing counterparts, suggests a demographic shift toward lower-income households or retirees rather than high-earning professionals. For potential buyers, the current environment offers significant entry-level value, but the limited job market and low wage average of $1,030 per week necessitate careful consideration of long-term financial stability. The market’s momentum is driven by scarcity and affordability rather than broad economic expansion, making it a niche but stable option for those prioritizing low cost over career opportunities.

The median home value in Baraga County is 30.0% below the Michigan state average and 39.4% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $616/month is 31.9% below the state average.
