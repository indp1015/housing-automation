Market Analysis

The median home value in Blaine County is 112.8% above the Idaho state average and 213.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,217/month is 32.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 45.5, Blaine County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Blaine County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
