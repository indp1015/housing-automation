Market Analysis

The median home value in Blaine County is 107.0% above the Idaho state average and 222.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,338/month is 38.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 45.8, Blaine County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Blaine County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.5%, compared to 75.2% in Idaho and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
