Market Analysis

The median home value in Benton County is 38.8% below the Tennessee state average and 41.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $788/month is 8.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.0, buying a home in Benton County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Benton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 73.8% in Tennessee and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
