📝 Local Market Analysis

Mercer County, Illinois, operates as a distinctly buyer’s market, characterized by exceptional affordability relative to national standards. The median home value of $146,700 stands 35.6% below the national average, while the price-to-income ratio of 1.98 is significantly lower than the national benchmark of 3.20. This affordability is reinforced by a rent burden of just 12.7% of income, well below the national average of 17.2%. Consequently, the county exhibits high stability and affordability metrics, driving its strong overall positioning despite lower vitality indicators. The economic foundation supports this resilience, with a median household income of $74,182, which is 9.1% above the national mean, providing robust purchasing power for local residents. The market trend reveals a period of accelerated appreciation that has recently moderated. Home values rose from $105,100 in 2017 to $146,700 in 2024, with peak growth of 12.6% observed in 2022. This appreciation has outpaced rent growth, where the median gross rent remains at $786, resulting in a price-to-rent ratio of 15.6, below the national average of 18.7. Recent FHFA data indicates a 4.23% year-over-year price increase, which lags behind the state’s 5.54% growth, suggesting Mercer County is currently underperforming relative to broader Illinois trends. This divergence highlights a shift from rapid local gains to a more measured pace that aligns closer with, but slightly trails, state-level dynamics. Economic context presents a complex picture for long-term vitality. Local employment is limited to 2,365 jobs with an average weekly wage of $832, and new housing supply is minimal, with only 18 single-family units authorized in 2025. Despite this constrained supply, net migration is positive at 262 filers, indicating sustained demographic interest. However, the low vitality score reflects the limited job base and establishment count of 250. For potential buyers, the current 7.40% mortgage rate increases carrying costs, yet the low entry price remains a significant advantage. Renter’s equity is less pronounced due to the strong price-to-rent differential, favoring ownership. The market’s strength is derived primarily from its affordability and stability, offsetting the weaker economic vitality and slowing momentum relative to the state.

The median home value in Mercer County is 4.1% below the Illinois state average and 35.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $786/month is 9.3% below the state average.
