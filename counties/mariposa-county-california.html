Market Analysis

The median home value in Mariposa County is 37.3% below the California state average and 69.2% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,268/month is 21.6% below the state average.

Mariposa County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.7%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
