Market Analysis

The median home value in Clark County is 40.6% above the Nevada state average and 89.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,626/month is 39.1% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.1, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Clark County has an owner-occupancy rate of 57.8%, compared to 73.0% in Nevada and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
