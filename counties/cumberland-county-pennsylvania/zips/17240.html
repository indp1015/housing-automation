📝 Local Market Notes

The housing market in ZIP code 17240, located in Cumberland County, Pennsylvania, exhibits a robust upward price trajectory over the past five years. The Home Price Index (HPI) has expanded significantly, rising from 180.34 in 2019 to 269.69 in 2025. This represents a cumulative gain of approximately 49.6% over the period, indicating sustained appreciation that outpaces many regional benchmarks. The steepest portion of this growth occurred between 2021 and 2022, where the index jumped nearly 18%, reflecting the broader national surge in home values during that timeframe. Currently, the ZIP code’s year-over-year growth of 3.92% aligns closely with the county’s 3.92% increase and the state’s 3.89% rise. This tight alignment suggests that ZIP 17240 is moving in lockstep with the broader Cumberland County and Pennsylvania markets, rather than diverging as a distinct outlier. In contrast, nearby ZIP 17053 has experienced a 5.48% decline, highlighting that market performance in this region is highly localized. The stability of ZIP 17240 relative to its immediate geographic neighbors indicates a resilient sub-market that is not currently subject to the correction seen in adjacent areas. The sub-market character of ZIP 17240 appears to be a mid-tier residential area within a stronger county context. With a median home value of $277,600 in Cumberland County and a median household income of $87,494, the area supports a substantial owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%. The HPI level of 269.7 suggests that home prices have more than doubled since the 2000 baseline, positioning the ZIP as a solid middle-class market rather than a luxury enclave. For prospective buyers, the market remains active but is currently constrained by the 7.40% national 30-year mortgage rate. Residents should note that while prices have risen sharply, the current growth rate has normalized to match state-wide trends, suggesting a market that has stabilized after its rapid expansion.
