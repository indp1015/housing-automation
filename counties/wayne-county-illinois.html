Market Analysis

The median home value in Wayne County is 25.1% below the Illinois state average and 48.3% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $632/month is 24.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.4, buying a home in Wayne County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Wayne County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.9%, compared to 75.7% in Illinois and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
