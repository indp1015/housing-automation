📝 Local Market Analysis

Monroe County, Illinois, functions as a distinctively strong buyer’s market, characterized by exceptional affordability metrics that diverge sharply from national norms. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.74, significantly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is only 12.2% of income compared to the national 17.2%. This affordability is reinforced by a median household income of $102,880, which exceeds the national average by 51.3%. Consequently, the market exhibits high stability, reflected in a composite stability metric of 92.7, driven by consistent income growth and low debt-to-income pressures for residents. The high owner-occupancy rate of 84.7% further underscores the area’s residential permanence and economic resilience. Recent momentum indicates accelerating appreciation, with the FHFA House Price Index rising 6.54% year-over-year in 2025, outpacing the state’s 5.54% growth. This trend follows a peak annual increase of 12.3% in 2022, moderating to 6.2% in 2024. However, a notable anomaly exists in the price-to-rent ratio of 22.6, which exceeds the national average of 18.7. This divergence suggests that home price appreciation is outpacing rental market adjustments, potentially signaling that long-term ownership offers superior value retention relative to renting. The market is supported by positive net migration of 511 filers, with inflow earners averaging $109,658, indicating an influx of high-income residents that sustains demand despite limited new supply, as only 65 new units were authorized in 2025. The economic context remains supportive, with average weekly wages at $1,000 and total employment at 7,232 jobs. However, the vitality metric lags at 38.8, reflecting a smaller local job base relative to the population. For potential buyers, the current 7.40% mortgage rate must be weighed against the county’s premium positioning; home values are 84.4% above the state average, making Monroe an outlier within Illinois. The combination of high income levels, low rent burdens, and sustained price momentum creates a robust environment for equity accumulation. Nevertheless, the limited housing supply and high price-to-rent ratio suggest that while entry costs are manageable relative to income, the premium paid for ownership is substantial compared to broader regional benchmarks. The market’s strength is fundamentally driven by demographic inflow and income superiority rather than expansive construction, maintaining a tight supply-demand balance that favors existing homeowners.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Monroe County is 84.4% above the Illinois state average and 23.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,042/month is 20.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 22.6, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Monroe County has an owner-occupancy rate of 84.7%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
