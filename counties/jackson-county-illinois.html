Market Analysis

The median home value in Jackson County is 10.3% below the Illinois state average and 39.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $782/month is 9.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Jackson County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Jackson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 50.0%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
