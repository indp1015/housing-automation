Market Analysis

The median home value in Teton County is 6.4% below the Montana state average and 11.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $750/month is 9.1% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.2, Teton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Teton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.9%, compared to 72.7% in Montana and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
