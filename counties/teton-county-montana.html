Market Analysis

The median home value in Teton County is 4.7% below the Montana state average and 15.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $730/month is 13.3% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 30.1, Teton County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Teton County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 73.0% in Montana and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
