Market Analysis

The median home value in Perry County is 2.7% below the Ohio state average and 16.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $777/month is 14.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Perry County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.4%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
