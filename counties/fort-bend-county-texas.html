Market Analysis

The median home value in Fort Bend County is 101.0% above the Texas state average and 64.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,807/month is 81.2% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 17.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in Fort Bend County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.
