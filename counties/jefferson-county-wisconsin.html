Market Analysis

The median home value in Jefferson County is 16.5% above the Wisconsin state average and 20.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,012/month is 13.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.9, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Jefferson County has an owner-occupancy rate of 72.9%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
