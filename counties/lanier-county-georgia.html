Market Analysis

The median home value in Lanier County is 18.0% below the Georgia state average and 28.7% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $913/month is 5.4% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.8, buying a home in Lanier County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lanier County has an owner-occupancy rate of 73.2%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
