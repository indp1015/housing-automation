Market Analysis

The median home value in Desha County is 35.7% below the Arkansas state average and 60.1% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $718/month is 7.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 10.6, buying a home in Desha County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Desha County has an owner-occupancy rate of 56.8%, compared to 71.1% in Arkansas and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
