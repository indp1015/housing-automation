Market Analysis

The median home value in Oconee County is 135.1% above the Georgia state average and 101.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,347/month is 45.5% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 26.3, Oconee County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Oconee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.6%, compared to 70.2% in Georgia and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
