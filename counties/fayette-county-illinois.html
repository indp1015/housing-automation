📝 Local Market Analysis

Fayette County, Illinois, presents a distinct buyer’s market characterized by high affordability but limited economic vitality. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.14, significantly below the national average of 3.20, while the rent burden is only 15.7% of income compared to the national benchmark of 17.2%. These metrics indicate that housing costs remain manageable relative to local earning power, with a median household income of $60,944 supporting a median home value of $130,700. However, the market’s low vitality is evident in the minimal housing supply, with only three new units authorized in 2025, all single-family. This scarcity contrasts with a positive net migration of 355 filers, suggesting demand is outpacing new construction, yet the local economy supports only 3,689 total jobs with an average weekly wage of $819. The recent price trajectory reveals significant volatility rather than steady growth. After modest increases between 2017 and 2021, home values surged 16.6% in 2022 and another 13.9% in 2024, reaching the current median. Despite this upward trend in census data, the FHFA House Price Index indicates a recent 3.66% year-over-year decline, underperforming the state by 9.20 percentage points. This divergence suggests that while long-term census estimates show appreciation, recent transaction data reflects a cooling or correction phase. The price-to-rent ratio of 13.6 remains well below the national average of 18.7, reinforcing that purchasing remains financially superior to renting for qualified buyers. Regional context highlights Fayette County’s position as a low-cost alternative, with home values 42.6% below the national average and 14.6% below the state average. The composite assessment reflects strong affordability and moderate stability, driven by the low cost of entry and consistent owner occupancy rate of 80.6%. However, momentum is weak, as indicated by the recent negative HPI change and the lack of new multi-family development. For potential buyers, the current environment offers high value relative to income, but the recent price correction and limited job base warrant caution regarding long-term appreciation potential. Renters benefit from low costs, but the minimal new supply may limit options for those seeking to transition into homeownership in a market where the 7.40% mortgage rate adds to monthly carrying costs.

The median home value in Fayette County is 14.6% below the Illinois state average and 42.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $798/month is 8.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 13.6, buying a home in Fayette County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Fayette County has an owner-occupancy rate of 80.6%, compared to 75.8% in Illinois and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
