Market Analysis

The median home value in St. Lucie County is 22.1% above the Florida state average and 52.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,585/month is 21.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.3, the buy-vs-rent decision in St. Lucie County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

St. Lucie County has an owner-occupancy rate of 78.6%, compared to 74.6% in Florida and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
