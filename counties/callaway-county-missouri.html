Market Analysis

The median home value in Callaway County is 14.7% above the Missouri state average and 11.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $887/month is 13.4% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 18.9, the buy-vs-rent decision in Callaway County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Callaway County has an owner-occupancy rate of 75.5%, compared to 73.7% in Missouri and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
