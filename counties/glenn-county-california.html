Market Analysis

The median home value in Glenn County is 40.7% below the California state average and 60.0% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,103/month is 31.8% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 25.6, Glenn County is a strongly rent-favored market. Home prices are high relative to rents, meaning renting is typically far cheaper than owning in the near term.

Glenn County has an owner-occupancy rate of 63.1%, compared to 63.6% in California and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
