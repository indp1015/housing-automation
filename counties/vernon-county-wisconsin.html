Market Analysis

The median home value in Vernon County is 3.3% below the Wisconsin state average and about equal to the U.S. national average. The median rent of $826/month is 7.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 21.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Vernon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 81.1%, compared to 75.8% in Wisconsin and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
