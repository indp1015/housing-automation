Market Analysis

The median home value in La Crosse County is 9.8% above the Wisconsin state average and 12.9% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,059/month is 15.7% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.2, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

La Crosse County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.2%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
