📝 Local Market Analysis

Bacon County, Georgia, operates as a distinctively affordable buyer’s market, characterized by a median home value of $101,400 and a median household income of $44,694. The price-to-income ratio stands at 2.27, significantly below the national average of 3.20, indicating high relative affordability. This is reinforced by a price-to-rent ratio of 11.4, well under the national benchmark of 18.7, suggesting that purchasing equity is more cost-effective than long-term renting. The rent burden is 19.9% of income, modestly higher than the national 17.2%, yet remains manageable within the local economic context. The market exhibits strong recent momentum despite a slight 2.6% year-over-year dip in 2024 ACS data. FHFA HPI data for 2025 reveals a 12.01% year-over-year increase, substantially outperforming the Georgia state average of 1.83% by 10.18 percentage points. This divergence highlights a robust recovery in transaction-based prices following the 2019 peak growth of 11.1%. However, this momentum contrasts with the local economic vitality, which is constrained by only 3,051 total jobs and an average weekly wage of $985. Housing supply remains extremely tight, with just 14 new single-family units authorized in 2025 and zero multi-family permits, limiting new inventory to meet demand. Regional context places Bacon County at a significant discount, with home values 55.5% below the national average and 48.8% below the state average. Migration patterns show a net inflow of 194 filers between 2021 and 2022, with incoming residents earning slightly less on average ($62,993) than those leaving ($64,396). The primary driver of the composite assessment is the high momentum score, offset by low stability and vitality metrics due to the small employment base and limited housing supply. For potential buyers, the 7.40% national mortgage rate increases carrying costs, yet the low entry price mitigates this risk. Renters face a less favorable ratio, as the price-to-rent differential favors ownership. The data suggests a market where low absolute prices and strong recent price appreciation create a compelling, albeit economically constrained, investment environment.

Market Analysis

The median home value in Bacon County is 48.8% below the Georgia state average and 55.5% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $743/month is 23.0% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 11.4, buying a home in Bacon County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Bacon County has an owner-occupancy rate of 68.6%, compared to 70.6% in Georgia and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
