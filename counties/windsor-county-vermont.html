Market Analysis

The median home value in Windsor County is 2.1% below the Vermont state average and 26.4% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,089/month is 2.5% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.5, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Windsor County has an owner-occupancy rate of 74.9%, compared to 76.9% in Vermont and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
