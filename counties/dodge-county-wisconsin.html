Market Analysis

The median home value in Dodge County is 0.9% below the Wisconsin state average and 1.8% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $988/month is 8.0% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.6, the buy-vs-rent decision in Dodge County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Dodge County has an owner-occupancy rate of 71.3%, compared to 75.9% in Wisconsin and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
