Market Analysis

The median home value in Davison County is 13.7% above the South Dakota state average and 9.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $847/month is 8.0% above the state average.

Davison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 61.5%, compared to 72.6% in South Dakota and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
