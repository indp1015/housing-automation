Market Analysis

The median home value in Lucas County is 16.5% below the Ohio state average and 28.0% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $934/month is 3.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 14.6, buying a home in Lucas County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Lucas County has an owner-occupancy rate of 62.0%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
