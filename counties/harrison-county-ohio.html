Market Analysis

The median home value in Harrison County is 38.1% below the Ohio state average and 46.6% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $785/month is 13.2% below the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 12.9, buying a home in Harrison County is generally more favorable than renting for those planning to stay long-term. A ratio below 15 typically signals that purchasing is financially advantageous over a multi-year horizon.

Harrison County has an owner-occupancy rate of 76.3%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more homeowner-heavy market than the U.S. average.
