Market Analysis

The median home value in Durham County is 64.2% above the North Carolina state average and 66.3% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,415/month is 50.9% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 20.7, renting is generally more cost-effective than buying in the short to medium term. The market favors buyers only for long-term holds.

Durham County has an owner-occupancy rate of 55.0%, compared to 71.4% in North Carolina and 73.0% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
