Market Analysis

The median home value in Butler County is 31.7% above the Ohio state average and 13.5% above the U.S. national average. The median rent of $1,135/month is 25.6% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 19.0, the buy-vs-rent decision in Butler County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Butler County has an owner-occupancy rate of 69.9%, compared to 73.7% in Ohio and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
