Market Analysis

The median home value in Genesee County is 7.5% below the Michigan state average and 19.9% below the U.S. national average. The median rent of $989/month is 9.3% above the state average.

With a price-to-rent ratio of 15.4, the buy-vs-rent decision in Genesee County is balanced. Whether buying or renting makes more sense depends on your time horizon, down payment capacity, and local market conditions.

Genesee County has an owner-occupancy rate of 70.7%, compared to 80.2% in Michigan and 73.3% nationally. This indicates a more renter-heavy market than the U.S. average.
