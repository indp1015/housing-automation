Family, Investor, Budget, or Young Pro: Which Livability Preset Is Right for You?

Guides · Published 2026-10-21 · 6 min read

A first-time homebuyer on a tight budget has different priorities than a real estate investor chasing appreciation. Our four Livability presets give you a smart starting point — then you can fine-tune.

🏡 Family Preset

Weights: Affordability 25%, Stability 20%, Safety 20%, Health 15%, Amenities 10%, Momentum 10%

Prioritizes stable neighborhoods with great schools, hospitals, and low crime. Ideal for growing families planning to stay ten or more years.

Top 3 with this preset: Woodford County, IL, Monroe County, IL, LaMoure County, ND

📈 Investor Preset

Weights: Momentum 35%, Amenities 25%, Affordability 15%, Safety 10%, Stability 10%, Health 5%

Focused on appreciation potential. Favors fast-growing Sun Belt metros. Be aware that high momentum can signal bubbles — pair with stability analysis before committing capital.

Top 3 with this preset: Los Alamos County, NM, Catoosa County, GA, Sarpy County, NE

💰 Budget Preset

Weights: Affordability 50%, Stability 15%, Safety 15%, Amenities 10%, Momentum 5%, Health 5%

Maximum weight on price-to-income and rent burden. Great for first-time buyers or remote workers relocating from expensive cities.

Top 3 with this preset: Woodford County, IL, Cass County, IL, Gallatin County, IL

🎓 Young Pro Preset

Weights: Amenities 30%, Affordability 25%, Momentum 15%, Safety 15%, Health 10%, Stability 5%

Urban-friendly: high amenity density, walkability, restaurant and nightlife options. Ideal for young professionals in tech, finance, or creative industries.

Top 3 with this preset: Los Alamos County, NM, Wabash County, IN, Dearborn County, IN

Same Data, Four Very Different Answers

Compare the top-ranked counties above. The Family preset favors quiet Midwestern and Northeastern counties. The Investor preset leans toward high-growth Sun Belt markets. The Budget preset rewards rural counties where housing costs relative to income are lowest. And the Young Pro preset champions dense, walkable suburbs.

All four draw from the same underlying data. The difference is purely about what you value. That's the point of a customizable ranking: it doesn't tell you where to live — it tells you where you would most enjoy living, given your own priorities.

Customize Beyond the Presets

Every preset is just a starting point. On the Livability Score page, you can drag any slider and the total weight always re-balances to 100%. Every configuration is shareable via URL, so you can compare scenarios with a partner or realtor.

The Bottom Line

There is no objectively 'best' preset — only the one that best matches your priorities. Start with a preset, adjust the sliders, and see how the ranking shifts. It takes less than a minute to discover which U.S. counties align with what matters most to you.
