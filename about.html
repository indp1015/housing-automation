About US Housing Data

US Housing Data is an independent, data-driven resource that provides free housing market information for every U.S. state and county. Our goal is simple: make authoritative housing data accessible, understandable, and useful for homebuyers, renters, researchers, and curious citizens.

What We Cover

We publish county-level and state-level housing metrics for all 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia, covering more than 3,100 counties:

  • Median Home Value — the midpoint of all owner-occupied housing values
  • Median Gross Rent — the midpoint of monthly rental costs, including utilities
  • Owner Occupancy Rate — the percentage of occupied units that are owner-occupied
  • Price-to-Rent Ratio — a key indicator of whether buying or renting is more favorable
  • Historical Price Trends — multi-year price and rent trajectories
  • FHFA House Price Index — recent market momentum data

Data Sources

All data on US Housing Data comes from official, publicly available government sources. We do not generate or estimate housing values ourselves.

  • U.S. Census Bureau — American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates
    Provides median home values, median gross rent, and tenure data. The ACS is the largest household survey in the United States and is updated annually. Learn more at census.gov.
  • Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) — House Price Index (HPI)
    Provides a quarterly index of single-family home price changes based on millions of mortgage transactions. Learn more at fhfa.gov.

Our Methodology

We aggregate raw data from these sources and compute derived metrics (such as price-to-rent ratio and year-over-year changes) using transparent, reproducible formulas. We clearly label the vintage (year) of all data and never claim to provide real-time prices.

Where FHFA county-level data is unavailable (typically due to limited mortgage transaction volume), we indicate this explicitly rather than substituting estimates.

Editorial Standards

Some pages include an "Editor's Insight" section that summarizes key patterns in the underlying data. These insights are generated using AI language models based on the actual data points, and are reviewed for accuracy. They are analytical commentary, not investment advice.

What We Are Not

US Housing Data is not a real estate brokerage, lender, or financial advisor. We do not sell, buy, or list properties. Nothing on this Site constitutes financial, legal, or investment advice. Always consult a qualified professional before making major financial decisions.

Independence

US Housing Data is independently operated and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Census Bureau, the FHFA, or any government agency. This product uses FHFA Data but is neither endorsed nor certified by FHFA.
